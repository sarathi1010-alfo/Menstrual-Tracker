# AI Overview & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO/SGE) Framework

To dominate Google's AI Overviews (SGE), ChatGPT citations, and Perplexity, LunaCycle's programmatic pages must be designed for **Machine Extractability** and **Semantic Retrieval**.

## 1. The AI Citation Formatting Layer (Extractability)
AI models do not "read" pages; they chunk and retrieve. Every tool and guide page must follow a strict, scannable architecture:
- **The Answer Block (First 100 words):** Directly answer the core query using an `<p>` tag immediately following the H1. E.g., *"The fertile window is the 6 days leading up to and including ovulation."*
- **The "What This Means" Section:** A brief summary explaining the output of the tool in plain language.
- **Structured Summaries:** Use `<ul>` and `<ol>` tags heavily. AI systems prefer extracting lists over dense paragraphs.
- **Definition Paragraphs:** Bold the entity being defined. (e.g., "**Basal Body Temperature (BBT)** is...")

## 2. Entity Reinforcement (Semantic Consistency)
For our Fertility/Pregnancy niche, we must consistently map to known Knowledge Graph entities.
- **Medical Entities:** Ovulation, Menstruation, Luteal Phase, HCG.
- **Timing Entities:** Trimester, Gestational Age, Cycle Length.
- **Implementation:** Ensure these exact terms are used in H2s and within `Article` / `FAQPage` schema `about` or `mentions` properties.

## 3. Confidence & Trust Signals (YMYL Necessity)
AI engines are highly cautious with YMYL queries. To be cited, we must prove authority.
- **Methodology Explanations:** Include a section on *how* the math works (e.g., "Calculated using Naegele's Rule").
- **Limitations/Disclaimers:** "This tool provides an estimate. It is not medical advice..."
- **Update Timestamps:** Display "Last updated: [Date]" prominently near the top of guides.

## 4. Conversational Query Optimization
Optimize for natural language queries that users type into ChatGPT or voice search:
- Structure H2s and FAQs as explicit questions:
  - *"Can I get pregnant 3 days after my period?"*
  - *"Is it normal to spot during ovulation?"*
  - *"How many weeks pregnant am I if my last period was [Date]?"*

## 5. Multi-Intent Compression
A single page must satisfy multiple AI retrieval intents simultaneously:
1. **Calculation Intent:** The interactive tool.
2. **Quick Lookup Intent:** The "Answer Block" at the top.
3. **Educational Intent:** "How it works" section.
4. **Reassurance Intent:** FAQ addressing common worries (e.g., "Is my cycle length normal?").
