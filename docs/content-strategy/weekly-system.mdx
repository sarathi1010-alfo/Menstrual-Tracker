## 2. Content Templates

### Template A: Cluster/Pillar (700-1000 words)
- **H1:** [PRIMARY_KEYWORD]: Complete Guide for [TARGET_AUDIENCE]
- **Definition:** 30-word summary for AI Overviews.
- **H2:** Why [PRIMARY_KEYWORD] Matters.
- **H2:** Step-by-Step [ACTION].
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways.
- **Schema:** Article + FAQPage.

### Template B: Comparison (500-700 words)
- **H1:** LunaCycle vs [COMPETITOR]: Best for [USE_CASE]?
- **Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary.
- **Comparison Table:** Privacy, Features, Cost, Winner.
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]".

### Template C: Micro-Answer (300-500 words)
- **H1:** What is [CYCLE_TERM]?
- **Definition:** 40-60 word snippet.
- **H2:** How it works.
- **Table:** [TERM] vs [RELATED_TERM].
