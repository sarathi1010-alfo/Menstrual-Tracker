# What is PMS?

Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) refers to a group of physical and emotional symptoms that many women experience in the days leading up to their period.

## When Does it Occur?
PMS symptoms typically appear during the **luteal phase** of the cycle (the time between ovulation and the start of your period) and usually disappear within a few days after bleeding begins.

## Common Symptoms
- **Physical:** Bloating, breast tenderness, headaches, and fatigue.
- **Emotional:** Irritability, mood swings, anxiety, and changes in appetite or sleep patterns.

## Tracking PMS
By logging your symptoms in LunaCycle, you can see if they consistently occur during your luteal phase, which helps in differentiating PMS from other health conditions.
