# What is an Irregular Cycle?

An irregular menstrual cycle is one where the length of the cycle (the number of days between the start of one period and the start of the next) falls outside the "normal" range or varies significantly month to month.

## The "Normal" Range
Most health experts define a regular cycle as being between **21 and 35 days** long.

## When is a Cycle Considered Irregular?
- It is shorter than 21 days.
- It is longer than 35 days.
- The length varies by more than 7-9 days from one month to the next.
- You skip one or more periods entirely.

## Is Irregularity Always a Problem?
Not necessarily. It is common for cycles to be irregular during puberty, after childbirth, during breastfeeding, or during the transition to menopause (perimenopause).

*Disclaimer: If your cycle becomes suddenly irregular, consult a healthcare provider.*
