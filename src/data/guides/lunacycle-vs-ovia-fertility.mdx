# LunaCycle vs Ovia: Comparison for TTC

When you are Trying to Conceive (TTC), you need a reliable way to track your cycle and identify your fertile window. Both LunaCycle and Ovia Fertility offer tools for this, but they take very different approaches to user data.

## Feature Breakdown

### LunaCycle: The Minimalist Utility
LunaCycle focuses on speed and privacy. It provides a beautiful visual calendar and clear predictions for your next period and fertile window, all without requiring an account.
- **TTC Focus:** Visualizing the fertile window and average cycle length.
- **Privacy:** 100% local storage.

### Ovia Fertility: The Data-Heavy App
Ovia is known for its extensive data logging, including cervical mucus, position, mood, and even blood pressure.
- **TTC Focus:** Highly detailed health logging and "fertility scores."
- **Privacy:** Data is stored on Ovia's servers and may be shared with partners or researchers (refer to their privacy policy).

## Comparison at a Glance

| Feature | LunaCycle | Ovia Fertility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Privacy** | Local Only | Cloud / Account Based |
| **Ads** | None | Ad-supported / Partner Content |
| **Setup Time** | Instant (No Account) | Moderate (Profile Creation) |
| **Complexity** | Low (Essential Features) | High (Comprehensive Health) |

## Which Should You Use?

- **Use LunaCycle if:** You want a clean, fast way to see your fertile window and you don't want your health data being sold or stored in a cloud database. It's perfect for those who want to keep their TTC journey private.
- **Use Ovia if:** You have a complex health situation (like PCOS) and want to track dozens of variables daily, and you are comfortable with your data living on an external server.

## Summary: Best Pick for Scenario
- **Privacy Advocates:** LunaCycle
- **Health Data Enthusiasts:** Ovia
- **Fast Daily Logging:** LunaCycle
