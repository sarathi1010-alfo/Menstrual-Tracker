# What is Menorrhagia?

Menorrhagia is the medical term for menstrual periods with abnormally heavy or prolonged bleeding. While many people have heavy flow days, menorrhagia specifically refers to bleeding that interferes with daily life.

## How Does Menorrhagia Work?
It can be caused by hormonal imbalances, uterine fibroids, or other medical conditions that cause the uterine lining to build up excessively or the blood not to clot normally during menstruation.

## Real-World Example of Menorrhagia
A person with menorrhagia might need to change their pad or tampon every hour for several hours in a row, or experience a period that lasts longer than 7 days consistently.

## Why is Understanding Menorrhagia Important?
Heavy bleeding can lead to iron-deficiency anemia, causing fatigue and weakness. Identifying menorrhagia through cycle tracking allows individuals to advocate for themselves when seeking medical treatment.

## Normal Flow vs. Menorrhagia

| Feature | Normal Flow | Menorrhagia |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Duration | 3-7 Days | > 7 Days |
| Product Change | Every 3-6 hours | Every 1-2 hours |
| Impact | Manageable | Disrupts daily tasks |

> ### Core Principles
> - Quantifiable Flow: Tracking the number of products used helps diagnosis.
> - Anemia Risk: Heavy bleeding often correlates with low iron.
> - Clinical Support: Effective treatments are available once diagnosed.
