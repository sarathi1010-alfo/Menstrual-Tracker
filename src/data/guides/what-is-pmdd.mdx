# What is PMDD?

Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD) is a severe, sometimes disabling extension of premenstrual syndrome (PMS). It affects a small percentage of women of childbearing age.

## Key Differences from PMS
While both PMS and PMDD cause physical and emotional symptoms, PMDD is characterized by severe emotional distress that significantly interferes with daily life and relationships.

## Severe Symptoms
- **Extreme Mood Shifts:** Sudden sadness, hopelessness, or intense irritability and anger.
- **Cognitive Issues:** Difficulty concentrating or feeling "out of control."
- **Physical Impact:** Severe joint pain, bloating, or breast tenderness that prevents normal activity.

## Diagnosis Through Tracking
To be diagnosed with PMDD, most doctors require at least two months of daily symptom tracking to confirm that the symptoms are strictly tied to the luteal phase of the cycle.

*Disclaimer: PMDD is a serious medical condition. Please seek help from a healthcare provider if you believe you are experiencing these symptoms.*
