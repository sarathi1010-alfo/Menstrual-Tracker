# Cycle Tracking for Avoiding Pregnancy: 2026 Guide

Using cycle tracking to avoid pregnancy is a method used by millions who prefer hormone-free contraception. However, it requires a high degree of diligence and an understanding of the biological limitations of calendar-based predictions.

## How it Works
The goal is to identify your "fertile window" and abstain from intercourse or use barrier methods (like condoms) during that time.

### The Standard Rules of Pregnancy Prevention
1. **Identify Ovulation:** Use markers like cervical mucus and BBT to confirm when ovulation has passed.
2. **The 3-Day Rule:** Most methods suggest waiting at least 3 days after a confirmed temperature shift before assuming the fertile window has closed.
3. **Assume Fertility Early:** Because sperm can live for 5 days, you must consider yourself fertile well before you expect to ovulate.

## Key Features to Prioritize
If you are tracking to avoid pregnancy, you need a tool that is:
- **Private:** You don't want your reproductive intentions being tracked by advertisers.
- **Visual:** A clear calendar view helps you see the upcoming fertile window at a glance.
- **Simple:** The more difficult an app is to use, the more likely you are to miss a log.

## Tips for Success
- **Log Daily:** Don't rely on memory. Log your period start and end dates as they happen.
- **Watch for Stress:** Stress can delay ovulation, making your standard "safe days" inaccurate.
- **Use Backup:** Always have a backup method of contraception ready for your fertile window.
