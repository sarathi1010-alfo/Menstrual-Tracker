# Natural Family Planning: Using Tracking for Contraception

Natural Family Planning (NFP) and Fertility Awareness-Based Methods (FABMs) involve tracking your body's natural signs of fertility to prevent pregnancy. These methods are popular for those seeking hormone-free contraception options.

## How NFP Works

NFP relies on identifying the "fertile window"—the time when conception is possible. By abstaining from intercourse or using barrier methods during this window, users can reduce the risk of pregnancy.

### Core Principles
1. **Daily Observation:** Monitoring temperature and cervical mucus every day.
2. **Standard Rules:** Following specific guidelines (like the 3-over-6 rule in the symptothermal method) to confirm ovulation.
3. **Consistency:** Success depends heavily on diligent and accurate record-keeping.

## Popular NFP Methods

- **Standard Days Method:** For cycles between 26-32 days, considers days 8-19 as fertile.
- **Billings Ovulation Method:** Focuses exclusively on cervical mucus patterns.
- **Symptothermal Method:** The most researched NFP method, combining temperature and mucus tracking.

## Effectiveness and Risks

When used perfectly, the symptothermal method can be over 99% effective. However, "typical use" effectiveness is often much lower (around 76-88%) due to the complexities of tracking and the variability of human cycles.

### Factors that Affect NFP Accuracy
- Stress or illness
- Irregular sleep patterns (affects BBT)
- Recent use of hormonal birth control
- Travel across time zones
