# What is Amenorrhea?

Amenorrhea is the medical term for the absence of menstruation. It is defined as missing one or more menstrual periods or never having started a period by age 15.

## How Does Amenorrhea Work?
Amenorrhea occurs when the complex interplay of hormones between the brain, ovaries, and uterus is disrupted. This can be caused by lifestyle factors, hormonal imbalances, or structural issues.

## Real-World Example of Amenorrhea
An elite athlete might experience secondary amenorrhea (stopping their period) due to the combination of high physical stress and low caloric intake, which signals the brain to pause the reproductive cycle.

## Why is Understanding Amenorrhea Important?
Amenorrhea is often a sign of an underlying health issue. While it is normal during pregnancy and breastfeeding, at other times it may indicate hormonal imbalances like PCOS or significant nutritional deficiencies that require medical attention.

## Primary vs. Secondary Amenorrhea

| Type | Definition | Common Causes |
|------|------------|---------------|
| Primary | Never started period by 15 | Genetics, Anatomy |
| Secondary | Period stops for 3+ months | Stress, Weight, Pregnancy |

> ### Core Principles
> - Cycle Pause: The body's way of signaling stress or imbalance.
> - Medical Consultation: Always see a doctor for unexplained amenorrhea.
> - Reversibility: Many forms of secondary amenorrhea are reversible with lifestyle changes.
