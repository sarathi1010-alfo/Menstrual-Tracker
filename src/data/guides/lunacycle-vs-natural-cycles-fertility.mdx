# LunaCycle vs Natural Cycles: Which is Best for Fertility?

Choosing a fertility tracker is a personal decision that often comes down to two main factors: accuracy and privacy. Today we are comparing **LunaCycle**, a privacy-first utility, with **Natural Cycles**, the first FDA-cleared birth control app.

## Quick Verdict
- **Choose Natural Cycles if:** You want an FDA-cleared medical device and are comfortable with cloud-based data storage and a monthly subscription.
- **Choose LunaCycle if:** You prioritize 100% data privacy, want a free tool, and prefer to manage your own fertility data locally on your device.

## Comparison Table

| Feature | LunaCycle | Natural Cycles |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Privacy** | 100% Local (Private) | Cloud-Based (Encrypted) |
| **Cost** | Free | Subscription (~$100/yr) |
| **Medical Status** | Tracking Utility | FDA-Cleared Medical Device |
| **Data Access** | No Account Needed | Account Required |
| **Platform** | Web/PWA | iOS / Android / Web |

## LunaCycle Pros & Cons
**Pros:**
- Completely private (your data never leaves your device).
- Zero cost to use.
- Simple, beautiful visual interface.
- No accounts or emails required.

**Cons:**
- Not a medical device.
- Does not provide "Green Days" or "Red Days" for contraception.

## Natural Cycles Pros & Cons
**Pros:**
- FDA-cleared for use as birth control.
- Sophisticated algorithm that accounts for temperature shifts.
- Integration with Oura Ring and Apple Watch.

**Cons:**
- Requires sharing sensitive health data with their servers.
- Ongoing subscription cost.
- History of data privacy concerns in the industry.

## Best Pick for Privacy
If your primary concern is who has access to your health data, **LunaCycle** is the clear winner. By storing everything in your browser's local storage, LunaCycle ensures that your fertility journey remains your business alone.

*Disclaimer: LunaCycle is a tracking utility and does not provide medical advice or contraception services. Natural Cycles is a regulated medical device.*
