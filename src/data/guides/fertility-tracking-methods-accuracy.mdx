# Fertility Tracking Methods: Accuracy and Comparison

Tracking your fertility involves monitoring various biological markers to identify your fertile window. Understanding the accuracy of different methods can help you make informed decisions about your reproductive health.

## Common Fertility Tracking Methods

### 1. Basal Body Temperature (BBT)
BBT is your body's temperature when you are at complete rest. After ovulation, your temperature slightly increases due to the hormone progesterone.
- **Accuracy:** Good for confirming ovulation occurred, but not for predicting it in advance.
- **Pros:** Low cost, helps identify luteal phase defects.
- **Cons:** Requires consistent daily measurement before getting out of bed.

### 2. Cervical Mucus Monitoring
As you approach ovulation, your cervical mucus changes in consistency, becoming clear, stretchy, and slippery (like raw egg whites).
- **Accuracy:** High. It is one of the most reliable predictors of the opening of the fertile window.
- **Pros:** Real-time indicator of rising estrogen levels.
- **Cons:** Requires learning how to categorize different types of mucus.

### 3. Ovulation Predictor Kits (OPKs)
These kits detect the surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH) in your urine, which typically happens 24-48 hours before ovulation.
- **Accuracy:** Very high for detecting the LH surge.
- **Pros:** Easy to use and provides a clear "yes/no" result.
- **Cons:** Can be expensive and may give false positives in conditions like PCOS.

### 4. Symptothermal Method
This method combines BBT, cervical mucus, and sometimes cervical position for a more comprehensive view of fertility.
- **Accuracy:** Highest among natural methods when used correctly.
- **Pros:** Multiple markers provide cross-verification.
- **Cons:** Requires significant education and consistent charting.
