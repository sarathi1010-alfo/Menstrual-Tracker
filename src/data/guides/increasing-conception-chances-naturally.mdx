# How to Increase Your Chances of Conception Naturally

Trying to conceive (TTC) can be a journey of patience. By understanding your body and making specific lifestyle adjustments, you can optimize your environment for a successful pregnancy.

## 1. Optimize Your Timing
As discussed in our [Fertile Window Guide](/guides/fertile-window-conception-guide), timing is the most critical factor. Use tracking to ensure you are having intercourse during your most fertile days.

## 2. Prioritize Nutrition
- **Folic Acid:** Start taking a prenatal vitamin with at least 400mcg of folic acid before you start trying.
- **Healthy Fats:** Include Omega-3s from fish or flaxseed.
- **Limit Processed Sugars:** High insulin levels can interfere with ovulation.

## 3. Manage Stress
High levels of cortisol (the stress hormone) can disrupt the signaling between your brain and your ovaries, potentially delaying ovulation.
- Practices like yoga, meditation, or regular walking can help.

## 4. Maintain a Healthy Weight
Both being underweight and overweight can impact hormone production and ovulation frequency. Aiming for a BMI in the healthy range is often beneficial for fertility.

## 5. Limit Alcohol and Caffeine
Excessive intake of alcohol and caffeine has been linked to decreased fertility in both men and women. Consider limiting caffeine to 200mg per day (about one cup of coffee).

## When to See a Doctor
Most doctors recommend seeking a fertility evaluation if you have been trying to conceive for:
- One year if you are under 35.
- Six months if you are over 35.
