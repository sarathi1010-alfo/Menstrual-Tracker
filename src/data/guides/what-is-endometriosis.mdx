# What is Endometriosis?

Endometriosis is a condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus (the endometrium) grows outside the uterus, most commonly on the ovaries, fallopian tubes, and the tissue lining the pelvis.

## Core Definition
This displaced tissue continues to act as it would inside the uterus—thickening, breaking down, and bleeding with each menstrual cycle. Because this tissue has no way to exit the body, it becomes trapped, leading to inflammation and pain.

## Common Symptoms
- **Painful Periods (Dysmenorrhea):** Pelvic pain and cramping that may begin before and extend several days into a period.
- **Pain with Intercourse:** Pain during or after sex is common.
- **Heavy Bleeding:** Occasionally experiencing heavy menstrual periods or bleeding between periods.

## Why Tracking Matters
Logging the intensity and location of your pain throughout your cycle can help healthcare providers determine if endometriosis is a likely cause.
