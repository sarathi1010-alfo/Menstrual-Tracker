# What is Cycle Syncing?

Cycle syncing is the practice of adjusting your diet, exercise, and lifestyle habits to match the specific hormonal changes that occur during the four phases of your menstrual cycle.

## How Does Cycle Syncing Work?
It works by identifying which phase of your cycle you are currently in—Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulatory, or Luteal—and then selecting activities and foods that support the hormones dominant in that phase (such as estrogen or progesterone).

## Real-World Example of Cycle Syncing
An individual might plan a high-intensity HIIT workout during their **Ovulatory phase** when energy is high, but switch to gentle yoga during their **Menstrual phase** when the body requires more rest and recovery.

## Why is Cycle Syncing Important?
Cycle syncing is important because it allows you to work *with* your body's natural rhythms rather than against them. This can lead to reduced PMS symptoms, improved mood, better energy levels, and more effective fitness results.

## Cycle Syncing vs. Traditional Training

| Feature | Traditional Training | Cycle Syncing |
|---------|----------------------|---------------|
| Intensity | Constant/Linear | Variable/Cyclical |
| Recovery | Scheduled rest days | Phase-based recovery |
| Goal | Consistent output | Optimized output |

> ### Core Principles
> - Hormonal Awareness: Knowing your current phase.
> - Lifestyle Flexibility: Adjusting plans based on energy.
> - Bio-Individual Focus: Listening to your own body's cues.
