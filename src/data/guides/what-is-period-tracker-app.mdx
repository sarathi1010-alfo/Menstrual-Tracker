# What is a Period Tracker App?

A period tracker app is a digital tool designed to help individuals record their menstrual cycles, symptoms, and other health data to predict future periods and fertile windows.

## How Does a Period Tracker App Work?
The app uses mathematical algorithms based on your historical data (like the start date and length of past periods) to calculate averages and estimate when your next cycle will begin.

## Real-World Example of a Period Tracker App
Using LunaCycle, a user logs the first day of their period on the calendar. The app immediately highlights the estimated fertile days for the coming month and provides a countdown to their next expected period.

## Why is a Period Tracker App Important?
It removes the guesswork from reproductive health. By providing a clear visualization of your cycle history, these apps help you prepare for your period, understand your moods, and provide accurate data to healthcare providers.

## Digital Apps vs. Paper Tracking

| Feature | Paper Calendar | Period App (LunaCycle) |
|---------|----------------|------------------------|
| Prediction | Manual calculation | Automatic & Dynamic |
| Accessibility | Physical location | Everywhere (on device) |
| Privacy | Visible to others | Secure/Local Storage |

> ### Core Principles
> - Data-Driven Insights: Using past dates to predict the future.
> - Health Awareness: Spotting irregularities early.
> - Convenience: Instant calculations on the go.
