# What is an Irregular Cycle?

An irregular cycle is defined as a menstrual cycle that is consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days, or one that varies in length by more than 7-9 days from one month to the next.

## How Does Cycle Irregularity Work?

Irregularity is usually a symptom of delayed or absent ovulation. Since the time from ovulation to your period is constant, a "late" period is almost always caused by a late release of the egg in the first half of the cycle.

## Real-World Example of an Irregular Cycle

If your last three cycles were 28 days, 42 days, and 31 days, your cycle is considered irregular. This level of variance makes it difficult to use simple calendar-based prediction tools.

## Why is an Irregular Cycle Important?

While occasional irregularity due to stress or travel is normal, persistent irregularity can be a sign of underlying issues such as thyroid dysfunction, PCOS, or perimenopause.
