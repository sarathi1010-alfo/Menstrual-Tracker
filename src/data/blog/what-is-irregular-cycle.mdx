# What is an Irregular Cycle?

An **irregular cycle** is defined as a menstrual cycle that frequently falls outside the &quot;normal&quot; range of 21 to 35 days, or one where the cycle length varies by more than 7–9 days from month to month.

## How Does Cycle Irregularity Work?

The timing of your period is controlled by a complex interplay of hormones (estrogen, progesterone, LH, and FSH). If ovulation is delayed or does not occur (anovulation), the cycle will be longer than usual. Factors like stress, illness, or travel can temporarily disrupt these hormones, leading to a one-off irregular cycle.

## Real-World Example of an Irregular Cycle

Using [LunaCycle](/tracker), you might see a history that looks like this:
- Month 1: 28 days
- Month 2: 42 days
- Month 3: 31 days
- Month 4: 25 days
The lack of a consistent pattern makes it difficult to predict the next period start date.

## Why is Tracking Irregular Cycles Important?

Identifying that your cycles are irregular is the first step toward finding the root cause.
- **For Fertility:** Irregular cycles often mean irregular ovulation, making it harder to time intercourse for conception.
- **For Health:** Persistent irregularity can be a symptom of underlying conditions like PCOS or thyroid dysfunction.
- **For Peace of Mind:** Knowing your patterns helps you prepare and reduces the anxiety of a &quot;late&quot; period.

## Regular vs. Irregular Cycles

| Feature | Regular Cycle | Irregular Cycle |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Length | 21–35 days | < 21 or > 35 days |
| Variation | 1–3 days difference | 8+ days difference |
| Predictability | High | Low |
