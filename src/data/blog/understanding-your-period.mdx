"Is this normal?" is the most common question in menstrual health. Because every body is different, the best way to answer it is to track your own data and look for your baseline.

## The "Normal" Range

### Cycle Length
The number of days from the first day of one period to the first day of the next.
  - "**Normal:** 21–35 days."
  - "**Average:** 28 days."

### Period Duration
The number of days you are actually bleeding.
  - "**Normal:** 3–7 days."

### Flow Volume
How much blood you lose.
  - "**Normal:** Needing to change a pad or tampon every 3–6 hours."
  - "**Warning Sign:** Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several hours."

## Period Color Decoded
  - "**Bright Red:** Fresh blood, usually seen during the heaviest days."
  - "**Dark Red/Brown:** Older blood, often seen at the very start or very end of the period."
  - "**Pink:** Often seen during very light flow or spotting."

## When to Consult a Professional
While variations are normal, you should talk to a healthcare provider if you experience:
1. Periods that suddenly stop for more than 90 days.
2. Periods that become erratic after being regular.
3. Bleeding that lasts more than 7 days.
4. Severe pain that isn't helped by over-the-counter medication.
5. Bleeding between periods (intermenstrual bleeding).
