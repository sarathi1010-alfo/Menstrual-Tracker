Knowing what constitutes a "normal" period helps you identify when something might be wrong and when to seek medical advice.

## Why Period Awareness Matters for Your Health

Your period is a vital sign. Changes in the color, consistency, or frequency of your flow can be early indicators of underlying health conditions, stress levels, or nutritional deficiencies.

## How to Evaluate Your Period (Step-by-Step)

1. **Measure the Length**: A typical period lasts between 3 and 7 days.
2. **Observe the Color**: Healthy period blood ranges from bright red to dark brown.
3. **Track the Volume**: Note how often you change your pad, tampon, or cup.
4. **Assess the Pain**: Track where and when you feel cramps and how intense they are.

## What is "Normal"?

### Cycle Length
A cycle from 21 to 35 days is considered normal for most adults.

### Flow Consistency
Some small clots (smaller than a quarter) are normal, especially on heavy days.

### Symptom Severity
Mild bloating, breast tenderness, and mood shifts are common and usually not a cause for concern.

## Period Red Flags: When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Description | Potential Issue |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Menorrhagia | Soaking a pad/tampon every hour | Fibroids, anemia |
| Dysmenorrhea | Debilitating, life-altering pain | Endometriosis |
| Amenorrhea | No period for 3+ months | PCOS, high stress, thyroid |

### 5 Key Takeaways
- **Normal is a range**: Everyone's "normal" looks a bit different.
- **Color tells a story**: Bright red is fresh; brown is older blood.
- **Pain shouldn't stop your life**: Severe pain needs medical investigation.
- **Track for patterns**: One weird period is usually fine; three is a trend.
- **Listen to your gut**: If something feels wrong, it's worth checking out.
