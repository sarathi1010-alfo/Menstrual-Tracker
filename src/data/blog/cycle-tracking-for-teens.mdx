The teenage years are a time of significant change, and your menstrual cycle is a big part of that. Starting to track early helps you understand what's normal for *you*.

## Why cycle tracking matters for Teens

When you first start your period, it's often irregular. Your body is still learning how to balance hormones. Tracking helps you see these patterns, know when to expect your period, and feel more in control of your body.

## Key features to prioritize for Teens

- **Simplicity**: Easy logging without complex medical jargon.
- **Privacy**: Knowing your data is safe and just for you.
- **Symptom Tracking**: Noticing how your mood and skin change during the month.

## Tips and best practices for Teens

1. **Be Patient**: It can take a few years for your cycle to become regular.
2. **Log Everything**: Even if it's just a little spotting.
3. **Talk to Someone**: If you're worried, talk to a parent, school nurse, or doctor.
