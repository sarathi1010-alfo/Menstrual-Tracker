Starting your period is a big milestone. Tracking your cycle from the beginning can help you feel more in control and less surprised by your body's changes.

## Why Should Teens Track Their Cycle?

1. **Be Prepared:** Knowing when your period is coming means you can have supplies ready in your bag.
2. **Understand Irregularity:** For the first 2-3 years, cycles are often irregular. Tracking shows you that this is normal.
3. **Connect the Dots:** You might notice you get more breakouts or feel more tired at certain times. Tracking helps you see these patterns.
4. **Privacy First:** Your health data is personal. Using a tracker like LunaCycle ensures your data isn't being stored on a server or shared with anyone.

## How to Get Started

### 1. Mark the First Day
Whenever you start your period, just tap that date on the LunaCycle calendar. That's Day 1.

### 2. Note the "Vibe"
You don't just have to track blood. Note if you felt extra tired, had a headache, or felt really happy. This helps you learn your own "cycle signature."

### 3. Check Your Predictions
After you've logged a few periods, LunaCycle will show you a prediction for your next one. Since you're just starting, it might not be perfect, but it will get better over time.

## What's "Normal" for Teens?
  - "**Cycle Length:** Can be anywhere from 21 to 45 days."
  - "**Period Length:** Usually 2 to 7 days."
  - "**Irregularity:** It's very common to skip a month or have two periods close together when you're first starting."
