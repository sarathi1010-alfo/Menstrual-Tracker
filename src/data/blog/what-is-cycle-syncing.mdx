Cycle syncing is a wellness practice where you adjust your daily habits—including what you eat, how you exercise, and even how you work—to match the four distinct phases of your menstrual cycle.

## How Does Cycle Syncing Work?
The menstrual cycle is driven by the rise and fall of hormones like estrogen and progesterone. These changes affect your metabolism, energy levels, brain chemistry, and immune system. Cycle syncing involves choosing activities that complement these internal shifts rather than working against them.

## Real-World Example of Cycle Syncing
During your **Ovulatory Phase**, when estrogen and testosterone are at their peak, you might schedule a high-stakes work presentation or a challenging HIIT workout. Conversely, during your **Menstrual Phase**, you might opt for a quiet evening at home and gentle yoga to honor your body's need for rest.

## Why is Cycle Syncing Important?
For many, cycle syncing helps reduce the severity of PMS symptoms, improves energy stability throughout the month, and fosters a more positive relationship with their menstrual cycle. It moves away from the idea that we should be identical every day and embraces our natural fluctuations.

## Cycle Syncing vs. Traditional Wellness

| Feature | Traditional Wellness | Cycle Syncing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consistency** | Same routine every day. | Routine changes by phase. |
| **Exercise** | Push through regardless of phase. | Match intensity to energy. |
| **Nutrition** | Fixed diet or macros. | Nutrients adjusted for hormonal needs. |

**Core Principles:**
- Listen to your body's internal cues.
- Prioritize rest during the second half of your cycle.
- Use tracking data from LunaCycle to identify your current phase.
- Start with small, manageable changes in one area (like exercise).
