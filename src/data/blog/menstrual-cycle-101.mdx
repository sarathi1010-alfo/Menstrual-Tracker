Welcome to the world of cycle tracking. Whether you are tracking for health, fertility, or just to stay prepared, understanding the basics is the first step toward body literacy.

## What is the Menstrual Cycle?

The menstrual cycle is a series of changes your body goes through each month to prepare for a potential pregnancy. It is controlled by hormones like estrogen and progesterone. While many people refer to their "period" as their cycle, the period is actually just the beginning of a much larger process.

## Why Track Your Cycle?

Tracking your cycle offers several benefits:
  - "**Predictability:** Know when your next period is likely to arrive."
  - "**Health Monitoring:** Changes in your cycle can be early indicators of overall health."
  - "**Symptom Management:** Connect mood or energy changes to specific phases of your cycle."
  - "**Privacy:** With LunaCycle, you can track everything without worrying about your data being shared or sold."

## How to Start Tracking

1. **Identify Day 1:** The first day of your period (red flow) is Day 1 of your cycle.
2. **Log Every Day of Flow:** Record each day you experience bleeding.
3. **Be Patient:** It takes a few cycles for any tracker to understand your unique rhythm.
