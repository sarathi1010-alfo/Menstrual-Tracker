## Why the Menstrual Cycle Matters for Your Health

Understanding your menstrual cycle is about more than just knowing when your next period will arrive. It is often referred to as the 'fifth vital sign' because it provides critical insights into your overall hormonal health and well-being. Whether you are looking to conceive, avoid pregnancy, or simply understand your body better, the menstrual cycle is the foundation of reproductive health.

## How to Track Your Cycle with LunaCycle (Step-by-Step)

Tracking your cycle is a simple habit that pays off in significant insights. Here is how to get started:

1.  **Mark Day 1:** The moment you notice your period has started, open the [LunaCycle Tracker](/tracker) and tap the corresponding date on the calendar.
2.  **Log Your Flow:** Note the intensity of your flow (light, medium, or heavy) to help the algorithm understand your patterns.
3.  **Track Symptoms:** Log any moods, energy levels, or physical symptoms like cramps or bloating.
4.  **Stay Consistent:** Continue logging for at least three cycles to allow the app to calculate your personal averages.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why it matters | How to fix it |

| Starting on Day 0 | There is no Day 0; the first day of bleeding is Day 1. | Always log the first day of flow as Day 1. |
