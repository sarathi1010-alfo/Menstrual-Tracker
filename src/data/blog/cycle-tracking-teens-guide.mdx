## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens
The first few years of having a period can be confusing and unpredictable. Cycle tracking is a powerful way for teens to build "body literacy"—an understanding of how their own body works. By tracking, they can start to predict when their period will arrive, reducing the anxiety of unexpected bleeding at school. It also validates their experiences, showing them that mood swings or fatigue are real, natural hormonal responses.

## Key Features to Prioritize
When choosing a tracking method, teens should look for:
- **Simplicity:** It shouldn't feel like homework.
- **Privacy:** Many popular apps sell health data. Teens need a secure space.
- **Educational Content:** Access to medically accurate information without fear-mongering.
- **No Accounts:** Avoiding tools that require email sign-ups or social logins.

## Tips and Best Practices
Here is how to get started:
1. **Start Simple:** For the first few months, just log the start date and end date of the period.
2. **Add Symptoms Later:** Once they are used to logging dates, they can add symptoms like cramps, mood, or acne.
3. **Be Patient:** Teen cycles can range anywhere from 21 to 45 days. It's completely normal for it to be irregular.
