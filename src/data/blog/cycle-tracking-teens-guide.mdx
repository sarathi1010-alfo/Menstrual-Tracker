## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

Starting your period can be confusing and unpredictable. Cycle tracking helps teens build body literacy—an understanding of how their own body works. It reduces the anxiety of an unexpected period and builds confidence in managing menstrual health.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When teens look for a tracking tool, they should prioritize:
- **Simplicity:** It shouldn't be overwhelming.
- **Privacy:** Data should be kept safe, ideally without needing an account.
- **Educational Content:** Easy access to accurate information about what is normal.

## Tips and Best Practices

Start small. Don't worry about tracking every single symptom at first. Begin by just noting the start and end dates of your period. Once you are comfortable, you can add notes about cramps, mood, or energy levels.
