## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

For teenagers experiencing their first few years of menstruation, cycles can be irregular and confusing. Tracking helps build body literacy, allowing teens to understand what is normal for their unique bodies. It also reduces anxiety by helping to predict when the next period will start.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When choosing a tracker, teens should look for:
- **Simplicity:** An easy-to-use interface without overwhelming medical jargon.
- **Privacy:** Protection from data selling or parental tracking.
- **Educational Content:** Reliable information to demystify the cycle.

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Start Simple:** Begin by just logging the start and end dates.
2. **Add Context:** As you get comfortable, start noting symptoms like cramps or mood swings.
3. **Be Patient:** Teen cycles can be irregular for the first few years.
