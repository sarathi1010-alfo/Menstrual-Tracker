## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens
Cycle tracking matters for teens because it builds body literacy and confidence. Understanding when to expect a period reduces anxiety and helps manage symptoms.

## Key Features to Prioritize
When choosing a tracking method, prioritize:
-   **Simplicity:** It should be easy to use.
-   **Privacy:** It shouldn't require personal information or share data.
-   **Educational Content:** It should help you learn about your body.

## Tips and Best Practices
-   **Start Simple:** Begin by just logging the start and end dates of your period.
-   **Add Symptoms Later:** Once you're comfortable tracking dates, start noting how you feel (cramps, mood swings).
-   **Be Consistent:** Try to track every month to get accurate predictions.
