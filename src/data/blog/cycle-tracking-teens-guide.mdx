## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

Starting your period can be overwhelming. Cycle tracking is a powerful way for teens to build body literacy and confidence. By understanding when their next period is likely to arrive, teens can reduce anxiety, avoid surprises at school, and begin to recognize patterns in their moods and energy levels.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When teens look for a cycle tracker, they should prioritize:
- **Simplicity**: The tool should be easy to use without overwhelming medical jargon.
- **Privacy**: Teens deserve digital privacy. Avoid apps that share data or require accounts.
- **Educational Content**: Access to reliable information about what is normal.

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Start Simple**: Just log the first and last day of your period.
2. **Add Symptoms Later**: Once you are comfortable, start logging cramps or mood changes.
3. **Be Patient**: Teen cycles are often irregular for the first few years.
