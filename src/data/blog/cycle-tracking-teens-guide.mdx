## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

Starting your period can be confusing and sometimes stressful. Cycle tracking is a powerful way for teens to build body literacy and confidence. By understanding your unique rhythm, you can predict when your period will arrive, manage symptoms, and reduce the anxiety of being caught off guard.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When choosing how to track, teens should look for:
- **Simplicity:** The tool shouldn't be overwhelming.
- **Educational Content:** It should help explain what is happening in the body.
- **Privacy:** Data should be kept completely secure and private from parents, peers, or tech companies.

## Tips and Best Practices

The best approach is to start small:
1. **Just the basics:** Begin by only logging the first and last day of your period.
2. **Add flow:** Once you're comfortable, start noting if the flow is light, medium, or heavy.
3. **Note feelings:** Eventually, you can add notes about cramps or mood changes.
