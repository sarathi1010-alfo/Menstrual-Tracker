Why cycle tracking helps teens understand their bodies, predict periods, and reduce anxiety.

## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

For teens, navigating the early years of menstruation can be confusing. Tracking builds essential body literacy and confidence, helping them feel more in control of their health.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When teens look for a tracking method, they should prioritize simplicity, robust privacy (to keep data safe from parents or peers), and access to reliable educational content.

## Tips and Best Practices

A good approach is to start simple. Begin by logging just the start and end dates of the period. Once comfortable, add symptoms like cramps or mood changes to see broader patterns.
