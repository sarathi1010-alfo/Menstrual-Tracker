## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens
Cycle tracking builds body literacy and confidence, helping teens understand their changing bodies, predict periods, and reduce anxiety.

## Key Features to Prioritize
Look for simplicity, strict privacy without data selling, and helpful educational content.

## Tips and Best Practices
Start with just logging the start and end dates. As you get comfortable, add symptoms like cramps or mood swings to spot patterns over time.
