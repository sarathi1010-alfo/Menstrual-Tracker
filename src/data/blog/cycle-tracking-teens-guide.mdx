## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

Cycle tracking helps teens understand their bodies, predict periods, and reduce anxiety. It builds body literacy, helping young women feel confident and prepared rather than surprised or stressed by natural bodily changes.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When teens track their cycles, they should look for:
- **Simplicity:** Easy-to-use interfaces that aren't overwhelming.
- **Privacy:** Apps that don't require accounts or sell data.
- **Educational Content:** Resources to explain what is happening.

## Tips and Best Practices

Start simple. Begin by logging just the start and end dates of the period. Once that becomes a habit, slowly introduce tracking for symptoms like cramps, mood swings, or energy levels.
