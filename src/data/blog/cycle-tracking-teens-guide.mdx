Starting your period can be confusing and sometimes stressful. Cycle tracking is a simple way to take control, understand your body, and reduce the anxiety of a surprise period.

## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

For teenagers, the body is going through rapid changes. Tracking your cycle builds **body literacy**—the ability to understand your body's signals. It helps you:
- Predict when you need to carry pads or tampons.
- Understand why you might feel moody or tired on certain days.
- Communicate better with doctors if you have very painful periods.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When choosing how to track, teens should look for:
- **Simplicity:** It shouldn't feel like homework.
- **Educational Content:** Easy access to reliable information about what's normal.
- **Privacy:** Your health data is yours alone.

## Tips and Best Practices

- **Start Simple:** Just log the start and end dates. Don't worry about tracking every single symptom right away.
- **Be Consistent:** Logging every month gives the app better data to predict your next period.
- **Don't Panic About Irregularity:** It's completely normal for teen cycles to skip months or vary in length.
