## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens
Starting your period can be overwhelming. Cycle tracking is a powerful way for teens to build body literacy and confidence. Knowing when your next period is due reduces the anxiety of being caught off guard at school or during sports.

## Key Features to Prioritize
For teens, the best tracking method focuses on:
- **Simplicity**: No overwhelming charts or fertility data.
- **Privacy**: No accounts, no data sharing with parents or companies without permission.
- **Education**: Clear information about what is normal.

## Tips and Best Practices
When just starting out, don't worry about tracking everything.
1. **Start small**: Log just the first and last day of bleeding.
2. **Add symptoms slowly**: Notice when you feel tired or get cramps, and log those.
3. **Be patient**: Teen cycles can be irregular, so predictions might not be perfect at first.
