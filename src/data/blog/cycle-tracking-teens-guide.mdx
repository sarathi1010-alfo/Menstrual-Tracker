Cycle tracking helps teens understand their bodies, predict periods, and reduce anxiety.

## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

Starting menstruation can be overwhelming. Cycle tracking builds body literacy and confidence, helping teens understand that the changes they are experiencing are natural and predictable. It provides a sense of control and helps reduce anxiety about unexpected periods.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When choosing a tracking method, teens should look for:
- **Simplicity:** An easy-to-use interface that isn't overly complicated with fertility jargon.
- **Privacy:** Assurance that their sensitive health data won't be shared or sold.
- **Educational Content:** Access to reliable information about menstrual health.

## Tips and Best Practices

- **Start Simple:** Begin by just logging the start and end dates of the period.
- **Add Symptoms Gradually:** Once comfortable, start noting physical symptoms like cramps or mood changes.
- **Don't Panic Over Irregularity:** Remember that cycles can be highly irregular during the teen years.
