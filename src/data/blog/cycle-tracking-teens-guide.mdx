## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

Starting your period can be overwhelming. Cycle tracking is one of the best ways for teens to build "body literacy"—a deep understanding of how their own body works. Tracking helps demystify the menstrual cycle, reduces the anxiety of unpredictable periods, and helps differentiate between normal hormonal changes and potential health issues.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When looking for a tracking method, teens should look for:
- **Simplicity:** It shouldn't feel like homework.
- **Educational Content:** Built-in information to explain what's happening.
- **Absolute Privacy:** Data should never be sold or shared.

## Tips and Best Practices

If you are just starting out, don't try to track everything at once.
1. **Start Simple:** Just log the start and end dates of your bleeding.
2. **Add Symptoms Later:** Once you are comfortable, start noting days you feel cramps, acne breakouts, or mood swings.
3. **Be Patient:** Teen cycles can be erratic. It may take months to see a pattern.
