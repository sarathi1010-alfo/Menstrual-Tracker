Why cycle tracking helps teens understand their bodies, predict periods, and reduce anxiety.

## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

Starting menstruation can be overwhelming. Tracking helps teens build body literacy and confidence by removing the mystery of when their period will arrive.

## Key Features to Prioritize

- **Simplicity:** Don't get bogged down in basal body temperatures yet.
- **Privacy:** Data shouldn't be shared or sold.
- **Educational Content:** Understanding what is happening to the body is empowering.

## Tips and Best Practices

Start simple. Log the start and end dates of the period. Once comfortable, add one or two symptoms, like cramps or mood swings, to see how they align with the cycle.
