## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens

For teens, the menstrual cycle can be unpredictable and confusing. Tracking helps build body literacy, giving them the confidence to understand what is happening and why. It demystifies the process and helps them advocate for their own health.

## Key Features to Prioritize

When choosing a tracker for a teenager, consider the following:
*   **Simplicity:** The interface should be easy to use without overwhelming medical jargon.
*   **Privacy:** This is paramount. Teens need a safe space where their data isn't being sold or tracked by third parties.
*   **Educational Content:** Access to accurate, age-appropriate information about menstrual health.

## Tips and Best Practices

1.  **Start Simple:** Begin by just logging the start and end dates of the period.
2.  **Add Symptoms Later:** Once comfortable, start tracking symptoms like cramps or mood changes to see patterns.
3.  **Don't Panic Over Irregularity:** Remind them that it's normal for cycles to fluctuate in the first few years.
