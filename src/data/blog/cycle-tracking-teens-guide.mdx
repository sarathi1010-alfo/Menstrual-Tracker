## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens
Navigating puberty and the start of menstruation can be stressful. Cycle tracking demystifies the process, helping teens understand what is happening to their bodies. It builds body literacy, fosters confidence, and reduces the anxiety of unpredictable periods.

## Key Features to Prioritize
When teens look for a tracking tool, they should prioritize:
*   **Simplicity:** Easy to use without overwhelming data points.
*   **Privacy:** Protection from data selling or parental surveillance.
*   **Educational Content:** Accessible information explaining cycle changes.

## Tips and Best Practices
1.  **Start Simple:** Begin by just logging the start and end dates of the period.
2.  **Add Symptoms Later:** Once comfortable, start noting symptoms like cramps or mood changes.
3.  **Use a Private App:** Avoid apps that require complex accounts or share data.
