## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens
Entering the years of menstruation can be overwhelming. Cycle tracking is a powerful tool that helps teens build body literacy and confidence. By understanding what to expect, tracking reduces the anxiety of unpredictable periods and helps you learn your body's unique rhythms.

## Key Features to Prioritize
When choosing a tracking method, teens should look for:
*   **Simplicity:** It shouldn't be overly complicated or focus heavily on fertility features.
*   **Educational Content:** Access to medically accurate, easy-to-understand information.
*   **Privacy:** Complete control over personal data.

## Tips and Best Practices
1.  **Start simple:** Don't worry about tracking every single symptom at first. Just log the start and end dates of your period.
2.  **Add details later:** As you get used to tracking, start noticing how your mood and energy change throughout the month.
3.  **Be patient:** Teen cycles can be notoriously irregular. Don't worry if your cycle doesn't fit a perfect 28-day mold.
