Why cycle tracking helps teens understand their bodies, predict periods, and reduce anxiety.

## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Teens
Navigating puberty is challenging, and the unpredictability of early menstrual cycles can cause significant anxiety. Cycle tracking helps teens build body literacy and confidence. By logging their periods, teens can start to see patterns, making the arrival of a period less of a surprise. It also helps them recognize how their cycle affects their mood, energy, and physical symptoms like acne or cramps.

## Key Features to Prioritize
When a teen selects a tracking app, certain features are paramount:
- **Simplicity:** The interface should be intuitive without overwhelming medical jargon.
- **Privacy:** Data should be kept secure, ideally without needing an email address or account.
- **Educational Content:** Access to accurate, age-appropriate information about reproductive health.

## Tips and Best Practices
Starting to track should be a low-pressure activity.
- **Start Simple:** Initially, just log the start and end dates of the period.
- **Add Details Gradually:** As they get comfortable, teens can start logging symptoms like cramps or mood swings.
- **Be Patient:** Emphasize that it takes a few months to establish a pattern, and irregularity is normal early on.
