# What is PCOS?

**Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)** is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age. Women with PCOS may have infrequent or prolonged menstrual periods or excess male hormone (androgen) levels. The ovaries may develop numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) and fail to regularly release eggs.

## How Does PCOS Work?

In a typical cycle, the ovaries produce a mature egg that is released during ovulation. In individuals with PCOS, the hormonal imbalance interferes with this process. The &quot;cysts&quot; referred to in the name are actually immature follicles that never developed enough to release an egg.

## Real-World Example of PCOS in Cycle Tracking

A person with PCOS might use [LunaCycle](/tracker) and notice that their cycles are very long—sometimes 40, 60, or even 90 days apart. They might also notice &quot;patchy&quot; cervical mucus that never quite becomes clear and stretchy, indicating that the body is trying but failing to ovulate.

## Why is PCOS Important?

PCOS is more than just an irregular period. It is linked to:
- Insulin resistance and Type 2 diabetes
- High blood pressure and heart health issues
- Infertility
- Sleep apnea and mood disorders

## PCOS vs. Endometriosis

| Feature | PCOS | Endometriosis |
|---------|------|---------------|
| Primary Issue | Hormonal/Ovulatory | Tissue growth outside uterus |
| Period Type | Infrequent/Irregular | Extremely painful/Heavy |
| Common Sign | Weight gain/Acne | Chronic pelvic pain |
