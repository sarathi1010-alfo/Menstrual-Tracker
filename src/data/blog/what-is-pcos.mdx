# What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal condition that affects how a woman's ovaries work. It is one of the most common causes of female infertility, affecting up to 10% of women of childbearing age.

## How Does PCOS Work?

In PCOS, the ovaries may develop numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) and fail to regularly release eggs. This is often driven by an imbalance in reproductive hormones, particularly higher levels of androgens (often called "male" hormones).

## Real-World Example of PCOS

A person with PCOS might notice that their periods are very unpredictable, sometimes skipping several months at a time, or they may experience heavy bleeding when a period finally arrives.

## Why is PCOS Important?

Early diagnosis and management of PCOS are crucial because the condition is linked to a higher risk of long-term health issues, including type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, and heart problems.
