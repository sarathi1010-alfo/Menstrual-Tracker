An **irregular cycle** can be frustrating, especially when you are trying to plan your life or conceive. However, your cycle is often referred to as a &quot;fifth vital sign,&quot; and irregularity is your body&apos;s way of communicating that something might be out of balance.

## What an Irregular Cycle is and How it Affects You

A &quot;normal&quot; cycle is usually between 21 and 35 days. An irregular cycle is one that frequently falls outside this range or varies significantly from month to month. This irregularity can make it difficult to:
- Predict when your next period will start.
- Identify your fertile window.
- Understand if you are ovulating.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help Manage Irregularity

When your cycle doesn&apos;t follow a predictable pattern, the &quot;calendar method&quot; used by many apps fails. Instead, you must rely on real-time biological data. By using [LunaCycle](/tracker), you can log:
- **Period Start and End Dates:** To calculate your actual cycle lengths over time.
- **Cervical Mucus Changes:** To see if your body is attempting to ovulate.
- **Basal Body Temperature:** To confirm if and when ovulation actually occurred.

## What to Look for in Your Cycle Data

If you notice your cycles are consistently irregular, look for these patterns in your [LunaCycle logs](/tracker):
- **Anovulatory Cycles:** Cycles where you never see a temperature shift or fertile mucus.
- **Short Luteal Phases:** Ovulating late in the cycle, leaving less than 10 days before the next period.
- **Mid-Cycle Spotting:** Which can indicate hormonal fluctuations or other underlying issues.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should share your [LunaCycle](/tracker) data with a doctor if:
- Your cycles are consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.
- Your periods suddenly stop for more than three months.
- You have heavy bleeding that lasts more than 7 days.
- Your cycle length varies by more than 10 days from month to month.

## Quick Reference: Irregular Cycles

| Condition | Symptoms | Cycle Impact | When to See a Doctor |
|-----------|----------|--------------|----------------------|
| PCOS | Acne, hair growth, weight gain | Very long, infrequent cycles | If cycles are > 35 days consistently. |
| Thyroid Issue | Fatigue, weight changes, feeling cold | Sudden change in cycle length/flow | If irregularity is new and persistent. |
| Stress | Anxiety, poor sleep | One-off long or skipped cycle | If stress is chronic and cycles stay irregular. |
| Perimenopause | Hot flashes, night sweats | Shorter cycles or skipped periods | If you are over 40 and patterns change. |
