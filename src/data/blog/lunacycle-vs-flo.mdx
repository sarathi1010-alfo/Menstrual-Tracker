# LunaCycle vs Flo: 2026 Comparison

When choosing a period tracker, the biggest difference between LunaCycle and Flo comes down to where your data lives.

## Quick Verdict
**Choose LunaCycle if:** You want 100% data privacy, no accounts, and a simple tool that stays on your device.
**Choose Flo if:** You want a large library of educational videos and don't mind your data being stored in the cloud.

## Comparison Table

| Feature | LunaCycle | Flo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Storage** | 100% Local (Device) | Cloud-Based (Servers) |
| **Account Required** | No | Yes |
| **Privacy Model** | Zero-Knowledge | Privacy-Enhanced (Anonymous Mode) |
| **Offline Mode** | Full Support | Limited |
| **Price** | Free | Freemium / Subscription |

## Deep Dive: LunaCycle
LunaCycle is built on a "Privacy-First" philosophy. By using browser local storage, it ensures that your health history is never vulnerable to server hacks or data brokers. It is a utility-focused tool for those who value digital sovereignty.

## Deep Dive: Flo
Flo is one of the most popular apps in the world. It provides a highly social and content-rich experience with AI-driven insights. However, being a cloud-based service means you must trust their infrastructure with your personal data.
