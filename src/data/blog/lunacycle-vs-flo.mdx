# LunaCycle vs Flo: Which is Better for You?

When it comes to tracking your period, you have many options. Today, we're comparing the privacy-first **LunaCycle** with the popular **Flo** app.

## Comparison at a Glance

| Feature | LunaCycle | Flo |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Data Storage | Local (Your Device) | Cloud (Their Servers) |
| Account Required | No | Yes |
| Privacy Focus | 100% (No Data Collection) | Mixed (Past Privacy Concerns) |
| Cost | Free | Freemium |
| Offline Use | Yes | Limited |

## Why LunaCycle Wins on Privacy

LunaCycle was built from the ground up to be a private tool. Unlike Flo, which stores your data on its servers, LunaCycle keeps every log, symptom, and date strictly on your own device.

## When to Choose Flo

Flo offers a wide range of social features and AI-driven insights that some users may find helpful if they are less concerned about cloud data storage.

### Best Pick for Privacy: LunaCycle
If you want to ensure your health data is yours and yours alone, LunaCycle is the clear winner.
