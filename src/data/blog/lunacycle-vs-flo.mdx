# LunaCycle vs Flo: Which Period Tracker is Better for Privacy?

**Quick Verdict:** If privacy is your top priority, **LunaCycle** is the clear winner due to its local-only storage and no-account architecture.

## Comparison Table

| Feature | LunaCycle | Flo | Winner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Storage** | Local (Your Device) | Cloud (Flo Servers) | LunaCycle |
| **Account Required?** | No | Yes | LunaCycle |
| **Cost** | 100% Free | Freemium | LunaCycle |
| **AI Insights** | Local Algorithmic | Advanced Cloud AI | Flo |
| **Privacy Focus** | High (Zero-Knowledge) | Standard (Cloud) | LunaCycle |

## Deep Dive: LunaCycle
LunaCycle was built as a direct response to privacy concerns in the period tracking industry. By utilizing browser `localStorage`, LunaCycle ensures that your sensitive health data never touches a server.
-   **Pros:** Total privacy, no ads tracking you, completely free.
-   **Cons:** No cross-device sync (unless you manually export/import data).

## Deep Dive: Flo
Flo is one of the most popular apps in the world, offering a wide range of features from pregnancy tracking to advanced AI health reports.
-   **Pros:** Extensive educational content, cross-device sync, health community.
-   **Cons:** History of privacy concerns, aggressive upselling, requires personal data for account creation.

## When to Choose Each
-   **Choose LunaCycle** if you believe your health data should be private and under your sole control.
-   **Choose Flo** if you need cross-device synchronization and don't mind your data being stored on a corporate server.
