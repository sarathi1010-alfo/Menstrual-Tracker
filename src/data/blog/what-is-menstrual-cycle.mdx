## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The menstrual cycle is governed by hormones like estrogen and progesterone. It consists of four phases: Menstruation (shedding the uterine lining), the Follicular phase (preparing an egg), Ovulation (releasing the egg), and the Luteal phase (preparing the uterus for a potential fertilized egg).

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle: Days 1-5 might be menstruation. Days 1-13 are the follicular phase. Day 14 is ovulation. Days 15-28 are the luteal phase. You can track this easily in [LunaCycle](/tracker).

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

It is a crucial indicator of reproductive health and fertility. Regular cycles generally suggest that hormonal systems are functioning properly.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Concept | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| Menstrual Cycle | The entire 21-35 day process of hormonal changes. |
| Ovulation | A single, short event (12-24 hours) within the cycle when an egg is released. |
