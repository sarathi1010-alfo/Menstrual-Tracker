## Definition
The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for possible pregnancy. It lasts about 28 days on average, but anywhere from 21 to 35 days is considered normal.

## How Does It Work?
It involves four distinct phases: menstruation (shedding the uterine lining), the follicular phase (preparing an egg), ovulation (releasing the egg), and the luteal phase (preparing the body for potential pregnancy).

## Real-World Example
In a typical 28-day cycle, menstruation occurs on days 1-5, the follicular phase overlaps and continues until ovulation around day 14, followed by the luteal phase from days 15-28.

## Why Is It Important?
It is a vital indicator of overall reproductive health, hormonal balance, and fertility.
