## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?
Briefly describe the four phases: menstruation, follicular, ovulation, luteal.

## Real-World Example
A 28-day cycle with menstruation day 1-5, follicular phase, ovulation day 14, luteal phase days 15-28.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?
Reproductive health, fertility indicator.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation
| Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---|---|
| Entire monthly process | Specific event within the cycle |
