## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle works through a feedback loop of hormones. It starts with menstruation (shedding the uterine lining), followed by the follicular phase (growing an egg), ovulation (releasing the egg), and the luteal phase (preparing the uterus for a potential fertilized egg).

## Real-World Example

In a standard 28-day cycle, a person might experience bleeding from days 1-5. The follicular phase overlaps and continues until ovulation around day 14. Days 15-28 make up the luteal phase, ending just before the next period begins.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

It is often considered a "fifth vital sign" because regular, healthy cycles indicate that the body's hormonal and reproductive systems are functioning correctly.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Function** | Entire process of preparation and shedding | Specifically the release of the egg |
