# What is a Menstrual Cycle?

The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for possible pregnancy. It lasts about 28 days on average, but anywhere from 21 to 35 days is considered normal.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is driven by hormones and is split into four phases:
1. **Menstruation:** Shedding the uterine lining.
2. **Follicular Phase:** Preparing an egg to be released.
3. **Ovulation:** Releasing the egg.
4. **Luteal Phase:** Preparing the uterus for a potential fertilized egg.

## Real-World Example

A typical 28-day cycle looks like this: Menstruation occurs on days 1-5, the follicular phase overlaps and continues until ovulation around day 14. The luteal phase then spans days 15-28.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

It is a vital sign of reproductive health. A regular cycle indicates balanced hormones and healthy reproductive organs.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| Process | Series of changes | Release of a single egg |
| Purpose | Prepare for pregnancy | Make an egg available for fertilization |
