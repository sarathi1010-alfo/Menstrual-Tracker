## What is a Menstrual Cycle?

The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for possible pregnancy. It involves fluctuating hormones that trigger the release of an egg and the thickening of the uterine lining. It lasts about 28 days on average, but anywhere from 21 to 35 days is considered normal.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is driven by hormones and divided into four phases:
1.  **Menstruation:** Shedding the uterine lining.
2.  **Follicular Phase:** Preparing an egg to be released.
3.  **Ovulation:** Releasing the mature egg.
4.  **Luteal Phase:** Preparing the uterus for a fertilized egg.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle:
*   Days 1-5: Menstruation.
*   Days 1-13: Follicular phase.
*   Day 14: Ovulation.
*   Days 15-28: Luteal phase.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

It is vital for reproductive health and acts as a "fifth vital sign," offering clues about hormonal balance, nutrition, and overall well-being.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| Function | Prepares body for pregnancy | Releases an egg for fertilization |
| Scope | The entire monthly process | A single event within the cycle |
