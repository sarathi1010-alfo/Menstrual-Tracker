## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is driven by hormones and is divided into four main phases:
1. **Menstruation**: The shedding of the uterine lining (your period).
2. **Follicular Phase**: The ovary prepares to release an egg.
3. **Ovulation**: The release of the mature egg.
4. **Luteal Phase**: The body prepares for a potential pregnancy; if none occurs, the cycle restarts.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle, menstruation might last from Day 1 to Day 5. The follicular phase runs concurrently and continues until ovulation, which often occurs around Day 14. The luteal phase then spans from Day 15 to Day 28, leading up to the next period.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

Beyond reproductive capabilities, the menstrual cycle is often considered a "fifth vital sign." Regular, healthy cycles indicate that your hormones are balanced and your body is functioning correctly. Irregularities can be the first sign of underlying health issues like thyroid problems, high stress, or PCOS.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The entire monthly process | A single event within the cycle |
| **Duration** | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Purpose** | Prepare body for pregnancy | Release an egg for fertilization |
