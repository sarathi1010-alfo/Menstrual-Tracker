## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The menstrual cycle is driven by hormones and divided into four distinct phases. It begins with **menstruation**, where the uterine lining is shed. Following this is the **follicular phase**, where an egg matures in the ovary. Then, **ovulation** occurs, releasing the mature egg. Finally, the **luteal phase** prepares the uterus for a potential pregnancy. If pregnancy doesn't happen, the cycle starts over.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle:
- **Days 1-5:** Menstruation (bleeding).
- **Days 1-13:** Follicular phase (overlapping with menstruation).
- **Day 14:** Ovulation (release of the egg).
- **Days 15-28:** Luteal phase (preparation for potential pregnancy).

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

Your cycle is a key indicator of your reproductive and overall health. It signals that your body is functioning correctly and provides a regular rhythm to your hormonal fluctuations.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| Definition | The entire monthly sequence of changes. | A specific event within the cycle. |
| Duration | 21-35 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| Purpose | Preparation for potential pregnancy. | Release of a mature egg. |
