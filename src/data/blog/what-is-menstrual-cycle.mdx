## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is divided into four phases: menstruation (the shedding of the uterine lining), the follicular phase (egg preparation), ovulation (egg release), and the luteal phase (body prepares for potential pregnancy).

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle:
- **Days 1-5:** Menstruation (your period).
- **Days 1-13:** Follicular phase.
- **Day 14:** Ovulation.
- **Days 15-28:** Luteal phase.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

Beyond fertility, the menstrual cycle is often considered a "fifth vital sign." Regular cycles indicate that your hormones are balanced and your reproductive system is functioning correctly.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Duration** | 21-35 days (entire cycle) | 12-24 hours (event) |
| **Purpose** | Preparation for pregnancy | Release of the egg |
| **When it happens** | Continuous | Usually mid-cycle |
