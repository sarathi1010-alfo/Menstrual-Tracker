## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is driven by hormones and consists of four main phases:
1.  **Menstruation:** The shedding of the uterine lining (your period).
2.  **Follicular Phase:** The body prepares to release an egg.
3.  **Ovulation:** The release of the egg.
4.  **Luteal Phase:** The body prepares for a potential pregnancy; if none occurs, the cycle restarts.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle, menstruation might occur from days 1-5. The follicular phase runs concurrently, leading up to ovulation around day 14. The luteal phase follows, lasting from day 15 until day 28, preparing the body for the next cycle.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

It is a vital indicator of reproductive health and overall well-being. Regular cycles suggest that your body's hormone levels are balanced and functioning correctly.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The entire monthly process (21-35 days) | The single event of releasing an egg |
| **Duration** | Weeks | 12-24 hours |
| **Purpose** | Prepare body for pregnancy | Release egg for fertilization |
