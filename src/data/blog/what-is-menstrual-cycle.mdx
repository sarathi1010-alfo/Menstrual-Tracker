## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?
The cycle is driven by hormones and is divided into four main phases: Menstruation (shedding the uterine lining), the Follicular phase (preparing an egg), Ovulation (releasing the egg), and the Luteal phase (preparing for potential implantation).

## Real-World Example
In a typical 28-day cycle, a person might experience menstruation from days 1 to 5, ovulate around day 14, and be in the luteal phase from days 15 to 28, before the cycle restarts.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?
Beyond reproduction, the menstrual cycle is often considered a "fifth vital sign." Regular cycles indicate balanced hormones and good overall health, while irregularities can signal underlying issues like stress, nutritional deficiencies, or medical conditions.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Definition | The entire monthly process | A single event within the cycle |
| Duration | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| Focus | Overall reproductive preparation | Release of the egg |
