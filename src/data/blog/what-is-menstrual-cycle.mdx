The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for possible pregnancy. It lasts about 28 days on average, but anywhere from 21 to 35 days is considered normal.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?
It operates in a continuous loop governed by hormones, transitioning through menstruation, the follicular phase, ovulation, and the luteal phase.

## Real-World Example
In a typical 28-day cycle, menstruation might last days 1-5, followed by the follicular phase. Ovulation occurs around day 14, leading into the luteal phase for days 15-28.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?
It is a key indicator of reproductive health and overall well-being.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation
| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| Purpose | Preparation for pregnancy | Release of the egg |
