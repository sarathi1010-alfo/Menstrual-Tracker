The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for possible pregnancy. It lasts about 28 days on average, but anywhere from 21 to 35 days is considered normal.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?
The cycle is controlled by hormones and involves four main phases: the menstrual phase (bleeding), the follicular phase (egg maturation), ovulation (egg release), and the luteal phase (preparation for pregnancy or next period).

## Real-World Example
In a typical 28-day cycle, menstruation might last from days 1-5. The follicular phase runs concurrently and up to day 13. Ovulation happens around day 14. The luteal phase occurs from days 15-28.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?
It is a crucial indicator of reproductive health and fertility. Regular cycles generally suggest healthy ovulation.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Definition | Monthly hormonal cycle | Release of an egg |
| Duration | ~28 days | ~12-24 hours |
