The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for possible pregnancy. It lasts about 28 days on average, but anywhere from 21 to 35 days is considered normal.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is divided into four main phases: menstruation (the shedding of the uterine lining), the follicular phase (preparation of an egg), ovulation (release of the egg), and the luteal phase (preparation for potential implantation). These phases are controlled by fluctuations in hormones like estrogen and progesterone.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle:
- **Days 1-5:** Menstruation occurs.
- **Days 1-13:** The follicular phase prepares an egg.
- **Day 14:** Ovulation happens.
- **Days 15-28:** The luteal phase prepares the uterus.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

It is a key indicator of reproductive health and overall well-being. A regular cycle suggests balanced hormones and healthy reproductive organs.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| ------- | --------------- | --------- |
| Duration | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| Function | Prepares body for pregnancy | Releases an egg |
