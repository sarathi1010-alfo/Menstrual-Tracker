## What is a Menstrual Cycle?

**Definition:** The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for possible pregnancy. It lasts about 28 days on average, but anywhere from 21 to 35 days is considered normal.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?
The cycle is driven by hormones and is divided into four main parts: the menstrual phase (bleeding), the follicular phase (egg preparation), ovulation (egg release), and the luteal phase (uterine lining preparation).

## Real-World Example
In a typical 28-day cycle, menstruation might last from days 1-5. The follicular phase runs concurrently but continues until ovulation around day 14. The luteal phase then spans days 15-28.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?
It is a crucial indicator of reproductive health and overall wellbeing. Irregularities can point to underlying conditions like PCOS or thyroid issues.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation
| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| Function | Prepares for pregnancy | Releases an egg |
