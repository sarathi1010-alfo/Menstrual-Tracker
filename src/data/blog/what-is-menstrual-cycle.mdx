## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?
The cycle is driven by hormones and is divided into four phases:
1. **Menstruation:** The shedding of the uterine lining (your period).
2. **Follicular Phase:** The body prepares to release an egg.
3. **Ovulation:** The release of the mature egg.
4. **Luteal Phase:** The body prepares the uterus for a potential fertilized egg.

## Real-World Example
In a typical 28-day cycle: Menstruation might last from Day 1 to Day 5. The follicular phase overlaps, ending around Day 13. Ovulation occurs roughly on Day 14. The luteal phase spans from Day 15 to Day 28, ending right before the next period starts.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?
Your menstrual cycle is a vital sign of your overall reproductive health. Regular cycles indicate that your body is producing hormones normally and functioning as expected. Tracking it with [LunaCycle](/tracker) helps you understand these patterns.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | The entire monthly process | A single event within the cycle |
| Duration | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| Purpose | Preparation for potential pregnancy | Release of an egg |
