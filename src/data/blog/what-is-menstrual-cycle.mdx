## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is driven by a complex interaction of hormones (like estrogen and progesterone) and consists of four main phases:
1.  **Menstruation:** The shedding of the uterine lining (your period).
2.  **Follicular Phase:** The body prepares to release an egg.
3.  **Ovulation:** The release of the egg.
4.  **Luteal Phase:** The body prepares the uterus for a potential fertilized egg.

## Real-World Example

Imagine a typical 28-day cycle:
*   **Days 1-5:** Menstruation occurs.
*   **Days 6-13:** The follicular phase continues, building up estrogen.
*   **Day 14:** Ovulation happens.
*   **Days 15-28:** The luteal phase takes over until the cycle begins again.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

It is often considered a "fifth vital sign." A regular cycle indicates that your reproductive system is functioning correctly and that your hormones are relatively balanced. Tracking it with [LunaCycle](/tracker) provides critical insights into your overall health.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The entire month-long process. | A single event within the cycle. |
| **Duration** | 21-35 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Purpose** | Overall reproductive preparation. | Release of an egg for fertilization. |
