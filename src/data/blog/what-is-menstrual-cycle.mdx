# What is a Menstrual Cycle?

The menstrual cycle is a repeating series of changes in a woman's body, specifically involving the ovaries and the uterus, driven by hormones. Its primary purpose is to prepare the body for pregnancy each month.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is controlled by the complex interaction of hormones like estrogen and progesterone. It involves two main processes:
1.  **The Ovarian Cycle:** Preparing and releasing an egg (ovulation).
2.  **The Uterine Cycle:** Building up and then shedding the uterine lining (menstruation).

## Real-World Example of the Menstrual Cycle

Imagine you log your period start date on the [LunaCycle Tracker](/tracker) on July 1st. If your next period starts on July 29th, your menstrual cycle was 28 days long. During those 28 days, your body went through follicular growth, ovulation, and the luteal phase.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

Your cycle is a vital sign of your overall health. Regular cycles indicate that your hormonal systems are functioning correctly. Irregularities can sometimes signal underlying health conditions that may need attention.

## Menstrual Cycle vs. Period

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Period (Menstruation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 21–35 days | 3–7 days |
| **Main Event** | Egg maturation & Ovulation | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Starts on** | Day 1 of bleeding | Day 1 of bleeding |
