## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The menstrual cycle operates through a complex interplay of hormones that trigger four main phases:

1.  **Menstruation:** The shedding of the uterine lining (your period).
2.  **Follicular Phase:** The body prepares to release an egg.
3.  **Ovulation:** The release of the egg from the ovary.
4.  **Luteal Phase:** The body prepares for a potential pregnancy.

## Real-World Example

Imagine a typical 28-day cycle:
*   **Days 1-5:** Menstruation occurs.
*   **Days 1-13:** The follicular phase is ongoing (overlapping with menstruation).
*   **Day 14:** Ovulation typically happens around this day.
*   **Days 15-28:** The luteal phase occurs, ending when the next period begins if pregnancy doesn't happen.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?

Beyond its role in reproduction, the menstrual cycle is a vital sign of overall health. Regular cycles indicate that your hormonal systems are functioning correctly, while irregular cycles can be the first sign of underlying health issues. Tracking it with [LunaCycle](/) helps you stay informed.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Duration** | 21-35 days (entire month) | 12-24 hours (a specific event) |
| **Purpose** | Overall preparation for pregnancy | Release of the egg for fertilization |
| **Scope** | Includes bleeding, egg development, and uterine preparation | Only the release of the egg |
