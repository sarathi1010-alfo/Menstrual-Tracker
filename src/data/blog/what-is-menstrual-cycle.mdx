## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?
The cycle is driven by hormones (estrogen and progesterone) and consists of four main phases: Menstruation (your period), the Follicular Phase (egg maturation), Ovulation (egg release), and the Luteal Phase (preparation for pregnancy).

## Real-World Example
In a typical 28-day cycle:
- **Days 1-5:** Menstruation (bleeding).
- **Days 1-13:** Follicular phase (overlapping with menstruation).
- **Day 14:** Ovulation.
- **Days 15-28:** Luteal phase.

## Why is the Menstrual Cycle Important?
It is a crucial indicator of overall reproductive health and fertility. Regular cycles generally indicate balanced hormones. Tracking it with [LunaCycle](/) helps you understand your body.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation
| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | The entire monthly process | The single event of releasing an egg |
| Duration | 21-35 days | 12-24 hours |
| Purpose | Prepare body for pregnancy | Make fertilization possible |
