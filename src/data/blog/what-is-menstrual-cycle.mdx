# What is a Menstrual Cycle?

The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a body goes through in preparation for the possibility of pregnancy.

## How it Works
Each month, one of the ovaries releases an egg—a process called ovulation. At the same time, hormonal changes prepare the uterus for pregnancy. If ovulation takes place and the egg isn't fertilized, the lining of the uterus sheds through the vagina. This is a menstrual period.

## The Two Main Halves
1. **The Follicular Phase:** Before the egg is released.
2. **The Luteal Phase:** After the egg is released.

## Why We Track It
Tracking your cycle helps you understand what is "normal" for you, allowing you to notice when something changes and helps you plan your life around your energy and fertility.
