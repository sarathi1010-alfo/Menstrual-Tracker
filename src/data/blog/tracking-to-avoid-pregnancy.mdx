*Medical Disclaimer: Using cycle tracking to avoid pregnancy (Natural Family Planning) has a higher risk of unintended pregnancy than hormonal birth control, IUDs, or implants. This guide is for educational purposes. Consult a healthcare provider for contraceptive advice.*

In 2026, many individuals are looking for hormone-free ways to manage their fertility. **Cycle tracking for avoiding pregnancy**, often called Fertility Awareness-Based Methods (FABMs), is a powerful tool when practiced with precision and privacy.

## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Avoiding Pregnancy

Choosing to avoid pregnancy without hormones allows you to remain in touch with your natural cycle. However, unlike tracking for &quot;general knowledge,&quot; tracking for contraception requires a strict set of rules and daily commitment. Using a privacy-first tool like [LunaCycle](/tracker) is essential, as your contraception data is among your most sensitive personal information.

## Key Features to Prioritize for Avoiding Pregnancy

If you are using [LunaCycle](/tracker) to avoid pregnancy, you should focus on:
1. **Daily Log:** Recording your cervical mucus observations every single day.
2. **Temperature Tracking:** Using a basal thermometer to log your BBT every morning.
3. **Cycle History:** Reviewing past cycles to understand your earliest possible ovulation day.

## Tips and Best Practices for Success

- **Get Educated:** Read a comprehensive book like *Taking Charge of Your Fertility* or take a course from a certified NFP instructor.
- **Be Conservative:** If you aren&apos;t sure if you&apos;re fertile, assume you are.
- **Use Backup:** Have high-quality barrier methods (like condoms) ready for use during your fertile window.
- **Stay Private:** Avoid apps that store your fertility data in the cloud. [LunaCycle](/tracker) keeps everything on your phone or computer.
