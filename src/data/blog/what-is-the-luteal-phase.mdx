## The Role of Progesterone

After an egg is released, the empty follicle becomes the corpus luteum, which secretes progesterone. This hormone thickens the uterine lining, making it receptive to a fertilized egg.

## Symptoms and PMS

If pregnancy does not occur, the corpus luteum breaks down, causing a rapid drop in progesterone and estrogen. This hormonal shift triggers menstruation and is responsible for Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) symptoms such as mood swings, bloating, and fatigue.
