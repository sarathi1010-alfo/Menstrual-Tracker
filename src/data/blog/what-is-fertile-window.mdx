# What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the specific timeframe during a menstrual cycle when a person can conceive. It represents the days when intercourse is most likely to result in pregnancy.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window is determined by the lifespan of both the sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (specifically, in the presence of fertile cervical mucus). The egg, however, only survives for 12 to 24 hours after it is released during ovulation. Therefore, the fertile window typically spans the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## Real-World Example in Cycle Tracking

If you are using a period tracker like [LunaCycle](/), the app will highlight your estimated fertile window. If your cycle is typically 28 days and you ovulate on day 14, your fertile window would roughly be days 9 through 14. This visualization helps users plan or avoid intercourse depending on their family planning goals.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days. | 1 day (12-24 hours). |
| **Significance** | The entire period when pregnancy is possible. | The peak day of fertility, when the egg is released. |
| **Factors** | Depends on both sperm lifespan and egg lifespan. | Depends solely on the release of the egg. |
