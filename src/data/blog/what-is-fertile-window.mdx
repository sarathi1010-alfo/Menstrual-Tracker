## Definition
The fertile window refers to the days in a woman's menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does It Work?
Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days, while an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after release. Thus, having intercourse *before* ovulation maximizes the chance of fertilization.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation is predicted on Day 14, the fertile window spans from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse on Days 12, 13, or 14 yields the highest chance of conception.

## Why Is It Important?
Identifying this window is critical for anyone actively trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy.
