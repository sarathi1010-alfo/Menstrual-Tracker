## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the specific timeframe during a woman's menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans approximately six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

This window exists due to the differing lifespans of sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (fertile cervical mucus). However, once an egg is released during ovulation, it only survives for 12 to 24 hours. Therefore, having sex in the days prior to ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window is approximately Days 9 through 14. Day 15 would carry a very low chance of conception, and by Day 16, the window is closed until the next cycle.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is the most critical piece of information if you are Trying to Conceive (TTC). Conversely, understanding this window is also the basis for natural family planning methods to avoid pregnancy (though this requires strict, multi-symptom tracking and should be discussed with a doctor).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Purpose** | Total timeframe conception is possible | The actual release of the egg |
| **Timing** | Leads up to and includes ovulation | The peak of the fertile window |
