## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (fertile cervical mucus), intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation can result in fertilization when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window is roughly Days 9 through 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Identifying this window using tools like [LunaCycle](/) is the foundation of natural family planning and fertility awareness methods.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase
| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Before and during ovulation | After ovulation |
| Fertility | High (pregnancy possible) | Low (pregnancy extremely unlikely) |
