## Definition
The fertile window is the specific period during a menstrual cycle when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It is dictated by the lifespan of sperm (up to 5 days) and the lifespan of the egg (12-24 hours).

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Because sperm can survive in the reproductive tract for several days, having intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation means sperm are waiting when the egg is released. Once the egg is released (ovulation), it must be fertilized within 24 hours.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation occurs on Day 14 of a cycle, the fertile window spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Day 12, for example, could result in pregnancy because the sperm can wait for the egg.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Understanding the fertile window is critical for anyone trying to conceive (to time intercourse correctly) and for those practicing natural family planning methods (to know when to abstain or use protection).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation
| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Focus | Sperm and Egg lifespan | Egg release only |
| Pregnancy chance | High | Highest |
