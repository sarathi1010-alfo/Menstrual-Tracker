The fertile window is the timeframe during the menstrual cycle when unprotected intercourse can lead to conception. It typically includes the five days preceding ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Pregnancy is only possible if sperm are present in the fallopian tubes when an egg is released. Because an egg lives for only 12-24 hours, but sperm can survive in the reproductive tract for up to five days, the "window" of fertility spans about six days.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window generally spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse during these days carries a higher chance of resulting in pregnancy, with the probability peaking on Days 12, 13, and 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is essential for family planning. Whether you are trying to conceive or practicing natural birth control, knowing these days is crucial. [LunaCycle](/) helps predict this window based on your cycle history.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Concept | Days when intercourse can lead to pregnancy | The physical release of the egg |
| Focus | Sperm survival + Egg release | Egg release only |
