**Definition:** The fertile window is the specific timeframe during the menstrual cycle when sexual intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It typically spans about six days: the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (specifically, in the presence of fertile cervical mucus). Therefore, having intercourse *before* ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If you expect to ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is roughly Days 9 through 14. Predicting this window requires tracking your cycle length and symptoms accurately over time, which you can do effortlessly with [LunaCycle](/).

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is critical whether you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy using natural family planning methods.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 Days | 12-24 Hours |
| What it is | The time pregnancy is possible | The actual release of the egg |
| When it happens | Leading up to and including ovulation | A single point in time |
