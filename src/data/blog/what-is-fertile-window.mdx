Definition: The fertile window is the period of time during the menstrual cycle when intercourse is most likely to result in pregnancy. It spans the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days, having intercourse in the days prior to ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window generally runs from Day 9 through Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is essential for natural family planning—whether trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy. Use [LunaCycle](/) to monitor your signs.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Function | Maximizes chances of conception | The actual release of the egg |
