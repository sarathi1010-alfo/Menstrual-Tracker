The fertile window refers to the specific days during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after release, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, intercourse in the days preceding ovulation can lead to fertilization when the egg is finally released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on day 14, the fertile window would be from days 9 through 14. The highest chances of conception are on days 12, 13, and 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is crucial for family planning—whether you are actively trying to conceive (TTC) or practicing natural pregnancy prevention.

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
