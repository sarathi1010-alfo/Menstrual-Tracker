## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window refers to the specific days during a woman's menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself. This roughly six-day period is critical because sperm can survive for up to five days in fertile cervical mucus, waiting for the egg.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

As ovulation approaches, rising estrogen levels change the cervical mucus to become stretchy and hospitable to sperm. This environment allows sperm to survive and travel up the fallopian tubes, where they can wait for the egg to be released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window spans from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse on any of these days can result in pregnancy, though the highest chances are 1-2 days before ovulation.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing this window is the most effective way to time intercourse for conception.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Timing | Before and during ovulation | Peak of fertile window |
| Biology | Based on sperm lifespan | Based on egg release |
