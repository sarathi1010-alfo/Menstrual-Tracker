The fertile window is the roughly six-day timeframe during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, having intercourse in the days *leading up* to ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is roughly Days 9 through 14. Day 15 would have a very low chance of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is crucial whether you are trying to conceive (to time intercourse) or trying to avoid pregnancy. Apps like [LunaCycle](/) can help estimate these days.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Focus | Sperm survival + Egg release | Egg release only |
