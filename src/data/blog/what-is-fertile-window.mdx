## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the approximately six-day interval in a woman's menstrual cycle when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It comprises the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (specifically, in the presence of fertile cervical mucus). This discrepancy creates a "window" of fertility.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 15 of her cycle, her fertile window technically begins on Day 10 and ends on Day 15. The highest probability of conception occurs if intercourse happens one to two days *before* ovulation.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is critical whether you are actively Trying To Conceive (TTC) or trying to avoid pregnancy using natural methods. It directs when intercourse should or should not occur depending on your family planning goals.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Timing | Leads up to and includes ovulation | A specific event |
| Cause | Sperm lifespan + egg lifespan | LH surge |
