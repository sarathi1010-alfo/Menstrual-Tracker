## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window exists because of the different lifespans of the egg and sperm. An egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after ovulation. However, healthy sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract (specifically in fertile cervical mucus) for up to 5 days. Therefore, having intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is predicted to happen on Day 14 of the cycle:
*   The fertile window opens around Day 9.
*   The chances of conception increase daily, peaking on Day 13 and Day 14.
*   By Day 15, the fertile window has closed.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is critical whether you are trying to conceive (by targeting intercourse during these days) or practicing natural family planning to avoid pregnancy (by abstaining or using barrier methods during this time).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | About 6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Definition** | The period when conception is possible. | The exact moment the egg is released. |
| **Key Factor** | Sperm survival time. | Egg release. |
