The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during a woman's menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It includes the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, the fertile window opens days *before* the egg is actually released, maximizing the chances of fertilization.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window is from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse on Day 12 could lead to pregnancy because the sperm can wait in the fallopian tubes for the egg to arrive on Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is essential for family planning. Couples trying to conceive should aim to have intercourse during these specific days. Those wishing to avoid pregnancy must abstain or use protection during this time (though tracking alone is not reliable contraception).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Concept** | Timeframe pregnancy is possible | Specific release of the egg |
