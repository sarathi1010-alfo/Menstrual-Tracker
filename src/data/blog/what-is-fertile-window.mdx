Definition: The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The window is defined by the lifespan of two cells. Sperm can live inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (fertile cervical mucus). An egg, however, only survives for 12 to 24 hours after being released. Therefore, having intercourse *before* ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg arrives.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Day 12 or 13 carries the highest statistical probability of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is critical whether you are actively trying to conceive or practicing fertility awareness.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Before and during ovulation | Strictly after ovulation |
| Pregnancy Chance | High | Near zero (once egg dissolves) |
| Hormone Focus | High Estrogen | High Progesterone |
