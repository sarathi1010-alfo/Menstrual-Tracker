## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the specific timeframe during the menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It comprises the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself. This roughly six-day window exists because of the differing lifespans of sperm and the egg.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg survives for only 12 to 24 hours after being released (ovulation), sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract (in fertile cervical mucus) for up to five days. Therefore, intercourse that occurs several days *before* ovulation can still result in pregnancy, as the sperm wait for the egg to be released.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window is approximately Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on Day 10 could lead to pregnancy because the sperm can survive until the egg is released on Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is crucial whether you are Trying To Conceive (TTC) or practicing natural family planning to avoid pregnancy. Timing intercourse within this window maximizes the chances of conception.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Definition** | The period when intercourse can lead to pregnancy | The actual release of the egg |
| **Includes** | Days leading up to and including ovulation | Just the day the egg is released |
