## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days, intercourse before ovulation can result in conception once the egg is released. The window closes about 24 hours after ovulation.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation happens on Day 14, the fertile window is generally from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse during these days carries the highest probability of pregnancy.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Identifying this window is critical for those trying to conceive, as timing intercourse during these days maximizes the chances of success.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Timing | Precedes and includes ovulation | Single event |
| Purpose | Timeframe for viable sperm | Release of the egg |
