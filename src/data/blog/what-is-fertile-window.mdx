**The fertile window is the specific period within the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It encompasses the day of ovulation and the five days preceding it.**

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window relies on the lifespan of both sperm and egg. While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (specifically, in the presence of fertile cervical mucus).

## Real-World Example

If a woman expects to ovulate on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window typically begins around Day 9 and ends roughly 12-24 hours after ovulation on Day 14 or 15. Having intercourse during days 11-14 offers the highest chance of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is critical for anyone trying to conceive (TTC) to time intercourse effectively. Conversely, for those using natural family planning, avoiding intercourse during this window is essential to prevent pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | Up to 6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Timing** | Before and during ovulation | Release of the egg |
| **Focus** | Optimal time for intercourse | The biological event |
