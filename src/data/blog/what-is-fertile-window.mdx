## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the specific timeframe during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It is determined by the lifespan of both the sperm and the egg.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (fertile cervical mucus). An egg, however, only survives for 12 to 24 hours after ovulation. Therefore, the fertile window spans the five days *before* ovulation plus the day of ovulation.

## Real-World Example

If you expect to ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window would be from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse on any of these days could potentially lead to conception, with the highest chances being the days immediately preceding ovulation.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is the core concept behind both trying to conceive (TTC) and fertility awareness methods for natural contraception.

## Fertile Window vs Menstruation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Conception | Shedding unneeded uterine lining |
| **Fertility** | High | Extremely low (but not impossible if cycles are very short) |
| **Hormones** | High estrogen, peaking LH | Low hormones |
