## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the timeframe during a woman's menstrual cycle when she has the highest chance of conceiving. It spans roughly six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. This window exists because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days, waiting for the egg to be released.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

As ovulation approaches, the body produces more estrogen, which changes the cervical mucus to become stretchy and clear. This fertile mucus helps nourish and transport sperm. Once the egg is released (ovulation), it survives for only 12 to 24 hours. Therefore, intercourse before ovulation maximizes the chances of sperm being present when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is expected on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window roughly spans Days 9 through 14. Having intercourse on Day 12 or 13 offers the highest probability of conception.
