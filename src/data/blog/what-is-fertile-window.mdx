## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window is determined by the lifespan of both sperm and the egg. While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after release, healthy sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days in the presence of fertile cervical mucus, waiting to fertilize the egg upon its release.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is expected on Day 14 of a cycle, having unprotected intercourse on Day 11, 12, or 13 can result in pregnancy, because the sperm can survive in the reproductive tract until the egg is released.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is crucial for family planning. Those trying to conceive must time intercourse during this window, while those avoiding pregnancy must abstain or use protection during these days.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | ~1 day |
| Definition | Window of possible conception | Exact moment of egg release |
