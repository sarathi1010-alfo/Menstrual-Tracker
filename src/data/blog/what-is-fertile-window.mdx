The fertile window is the timeframe in a woman's menstrual cycle when intercourse is most likely to result in conception. It typically spans the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
The window exists because of the differing lifespans of the egg and sperm. Sperm can survive in fertile cervical mucus for up to 5 days, while the egg is only viable for 12-24 hours. Intercourse during this window ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation occurs on day 14 of a cycle, the fertile window spans from day 9 to day 14. Intercourse on day 12 or 13 carries the highest chance of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Understanding this window is crucial for family planning—both for those trying to conceive and those using fertility awareness to prevent pregnancy. A simple tracker like [LunaCycle](/) can assist in estimating these dates based on your cycle history.
