# What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the timeframe during a menstrual cycle when intercourse is most likely to result in pregnancy. This window is determined by the lifespan of the egg and the sperm.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

An egg lives for about 12-24 hours after ovulation. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, the fertile window spans the five days prior to ovulation plus the day of ovulation itself.
