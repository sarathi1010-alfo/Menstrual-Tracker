The fertile window is the specific timeframe during the menstrual cycle when a woman has a chance of getting pregnant. It includes the days immediately leading up to and the day of ovulation.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window exists because of the lifespan of both sperm and the egg. While an egg only lives for 12 to 24 hours after [ovulation](/what-is-ovulation), healthy sperm can survive inside the female body (specifically in fertile cervical mucus) for up to 5 days. Therefore, if a woman has intercourse in the days *before* she ovulates, the sperm can wait in the fallopian tubes to fertilize the egg as soon as it is released.

## Real-World Example

If a woman predicts she will ovulate on Day 15 of her cycle, her fertile window technically begins on Day 10. The window remains open until Day 16 (accounting for the 24-hour lifespan of the egg). Intercourse on Day 13 or 14 would provide the highest probability of conception, as the sperm would be ready and waiting for the egg on Day 15.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is the most critical aspect of fertility awareness. Whether you are actively Trying To Conceive (TTC) and want to time intercourse optimally, or you are practicing natural family planning to avoid pregnancy, knowing these 6 days is essential. Tracking tools like [LunaCycle](/) use your historical cycle data and symptom logs to help predict this window.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The period when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. | The specific event of the egg being released. |
| **Duration** | Approximately 6 days. | 12 to 24 hours. |
| **Biological Basis** | Based on the lifespan of both sperm (5 days) and egg (1 day). | Based solely on the release and lifespan of the egg. |
| **Tracking Focus** | The goal of most fertility tracking apps. | The biological trigger that closes the window shortly after. |

- The fertile window is the only time in a cycle you can get pregnant.
- It spans about 6 days: 5 days before ovulation plus the day of ovulation.
- Sperm can live up to 5 days; the egg only lives up to 24 hours.
- Cycle variations mean the window's exact dates can shift each month.
