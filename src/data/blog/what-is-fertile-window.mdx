# What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window refers to the days in your menstrual cycle when pregnancy is biologically possible.

## Why is it 6 Days?
An egg only lives for about 12 to 24 hours after being released. However, sperm can live inside the reproductive tract for up to 5 days. This means if you have sex in the days before ovulation, sperm can "wait" for the egg.

## Calculation
Typically, the window is calculated as:
**[Ovulation Day] minus 5 days = Start of Fertile Window.**

## Accuracy
Predicting the window depends on knowing when ovulation will happen. Because ovulation can shift due to stress, illness, or travel, the window is always an estimation.
