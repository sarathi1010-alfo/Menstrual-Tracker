The fertile window is the brief period during a menstrual cycle when a woman has the highest chance of getting pregnant. It typically lasts about six days.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
The fertile window is determined by the lifespan of both sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, while an egg survives for only 12-24 hours after being released. Therefore, the fertile window includes the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## Real-World Example
If you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window begins on Day 9 and ends on Day 14. Having intercourse on Day 12 or 13 offers the highest probability of conception, as sperm will be waiting when the egg is released.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Knowing your fertile window is essential for family planning. If you are Trying To Conceive (TTC), this is when you should have intercourse. Conversely, if you are practicing Natural Family Planning (NFP) to avoid pregnancy, this is the time to abstain or use barrier methods.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
|---------|----------------|--------------|
| **Timing** | Before and during ovulation | After ovulation |
| **Fertility** | High | Low (once the egg has died) |
| **Hormone** | Rising Estrogen, LH surge | High Progesterone |
