# What is the Fertile Window?

**The fertile window is the approximately six-day period in a woman's menstrual cycle when conception is most likely to occur. It encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.**

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The length of the fertile window is determined by the lifespan of sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under optimal conditions (aided by fertile cervical mucus). An egg, however, only survives for 12 to 24 hours after being released. Therefore, for pregnancy to occur, intercourse must happen within the 5 days before ovulation or on the day of ovulation.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window is roughly from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Day 12 could lead to pregnancy because the sperm can survive until the egg is released on Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is crucial for family planning. If you are trying to conceive, timing intercourse during this window maximizes your chances. Conversely, avoiding intercourse (or using barrier methods) during this time is the basis for fertility awareness-based methods of contraception.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Purpose** | The timeframe when conception is possible | The specific event of releasing the egg |
| **Key Factor** | Lifespan of sperm (up to 5 days) | Lifespan of egg (up to 24 hours) |
