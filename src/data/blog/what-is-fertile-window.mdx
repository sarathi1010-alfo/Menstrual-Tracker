The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during the [menstrual cycle](/what-is-menstrual-cycle) when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It includes the five days before ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after [ovulation](/what-is-ovulation), sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days in the presence of fertile cervical mucus. Therefore, intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation can result in conception because the sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window is approximately Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on Day 11, for example, could result in pregnancy if the sperm survive until Day 14. By Day 15, the fertile window has closed.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is crucial for family planning. Couples trying to conceive (TTC) should aim to have intercourse during this time. Conversely, those avoiding pregnancy use fertility awareness methods to abstain or use protection during these days.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Duration | ~6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| Definition | The time when sex can lead to conception. | The actual release of the egg. |
| Key Factor | Dependent on sperm lifespan + egg lifespan. | Dependent solely on egg release. |
