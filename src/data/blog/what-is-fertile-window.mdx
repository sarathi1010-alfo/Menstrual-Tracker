The fertile window is the specific timeframe during the menstrual cycle when sexual intercourse can result in pregnancy. It lasts for approximately six days: the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The timing of the fertile window is determined by the lifespans of both sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract (specifically in fertile cervical mucus) for up to 5 days. The egg, however, only survives for 12 to 24 hours after being released. Therefore, having intercourse before ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg arrives.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window is from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Day 11 could result in pregnancy because the sperm can survive until the egg is released on Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is essential for family planning. For those Trying to Conceive (TTC), timing intercourse during these 6 days maximizes the chances of pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The period when pregnancy is possible. | The specific event of egg release. |
| **Duration** | ~6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Key Factor** | Driven by sperm lifespan. | Driven by the egg lifespan. |
