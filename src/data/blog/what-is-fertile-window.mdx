## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Because sperm can survive inside the body in fertile cervical mucus for up to five days, having intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation can result in pregnancy. Once ovulation occurs, the egg must be fertilized within 12-24 hours. After this window closes, the chance of conception drops to near zero until the next cycle.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is predicted to occur on Day 14, the fertile window would be from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse on Days 12, 13, or 14 yields the highest probability of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is critical for anyone trying to conceive (TTC). Conversely, understanding when this window occurs is essential knowledge for those wishing to avoid pregnancy, though calendar methods alone are not reliable contraception.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Purpose** | The entire period conception is possible | The specific day the egg is released |
| **Sperm Role** | Sperm can wait for the egg | Sperm must meet the egg |
