The fertile window refers to the days in a woman's menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans about six days, comprising the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The timing is based on the lifespan of sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (fertile cervical mucus), while the egg only survives for 12-24 hours after release.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is expected on Day 14 of a cycle, the fertile window would roughly be Days 9 through 14. Intercourse during these days carries a higher chance of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is the most critical piece of information for couples who are actively trying to conceive (TTC) or for those using fertility awareness methods for contraception.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Optimal time for conception | Preparation for implantation |
| **Timing** | Before and during ovulation | After ovulation |
