The fertile window is the timeframe during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It generally spans about six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

This window is determined by two biological facts:
1.  **Sperm Lifespan:** Sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract (specifically in fertile cervical mucus) for up to five days.
2.  **Egg Lifespan:** An egg survives for only 12 to 24 hours after being released.

Therefore, if intercourse occurs several days before ovulation, sperm can wait in the fallopian tubes for the egg to be released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window would roughly be Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on Day 10 could result in pregnancy if the sperm survives until the egg is released on Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is critical for both achieving pregnancy and avoiding it naturally (Fertility Awareness Methods).

| Feature | Fertile Window | Menstruation |
