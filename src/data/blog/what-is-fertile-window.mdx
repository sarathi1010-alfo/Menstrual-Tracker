# What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window represents the days in a woman's cycle when she can become pregnant from unprotected intercourse. It is determined by the combined longevity of sperm and the human egg.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While the egg only survives for about a day, sperm can survive in 'fertile' cervical mucus for up to five days. Therefore, if intercourse occurs several days before ovulation, sperm may still be present and capable of fertilizing the egg when it is finally released.

## Real-World Example of the Fertile Window

If you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window likely spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Using a [Safe Days Calculator](/tools/safe-days-calculator) can help you visualize this window based on your historical cycle data.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

For those trying to conceive, timing intercourse within this window is critical. For those avoiding pregnancy, knowing this window helps identify 'high-risk' days when extra precautions are necessary.

## Fertile Window vs. Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Length** | ~6 days | ~24 hours |
| **Conception Possible?** | Yes | Yes (Peak) |
| **Logic** | Sperm survival + Egg survival | Egg release only |
