## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window depends on the lifespan of both sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in fertile cervical mucus for up to 5 days, waiting for the egg to be released. Once released, the egg only lives for 12-24 hours.

## Real-World Example

If you ovulate on day 14, your fertile window is roughly days 9 through 14. Intercourse on day 12 or 13 offers the highest probability of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is critical for anyone trying to conceive (to time intercourse) or trying to avoid pregnancy (to abstain or use protection).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
|---------|----------------|---------------|
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Probability of Pregnancy** | Ranges from 10-33% per day | Drops quickly after 12-24 hrs |
| **Focus** | Timing intercourse | The actual release of the egg |
