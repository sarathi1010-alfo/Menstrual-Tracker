## Definition

The fertile window is the roughly six-day timeframe in the menstrual cycle when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It is comprised of the five days leading up to ovulation plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Pregnancy relies on two lifespans: sperm (which can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days under the right conditions) and the egg (which survives for 12-24 hours). The fertile window opens when sperm can survive long enough to meet the soon-to-be-released egg. For more on the phases involved, see [Cycle Phases Explained](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal).

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Day 10 could lead to pregnancy because the sperm can wait in the fallopian tubes until the egg is released on Day 14. Use [LunaCycle](/) to automatically calculate this window based on your data.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is essential whether you are Trying To Conceive (TTC) and want to time intercourse optimally, or trying to avoid pregnancy by knowing when to use protection or abstain.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Nature** | A period of time when conception is possible. | The specific event of the egg being released. |
| **Calculation** | 5 days prior + day of ovulation. | Usually 14 days before the next period starts. |
