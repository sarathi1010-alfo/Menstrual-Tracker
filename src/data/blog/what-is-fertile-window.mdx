The fertile window is the timeframe during the menstrual cycle when a woman has the highest chance of becoming pregnant. It spans roughly six days: the five days preceding ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, having intercourse in the days *before* ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window roughly spans from Day 9 through Day 14. Having intercourse during these days maximizes the chances of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is crucial whether you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy. Apps like [LunaCycle](/) can help predict this window based on your cycle history.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **What happens** | Sperm can survive, waiting for the egg | The egg is actually released |
| **Purpose** | The timeframe for possible conception | The singular event required for conception |
