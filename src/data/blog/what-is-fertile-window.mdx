## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can live inside the female body for up to 5 days if fertile cervical mucus is present. Therefore, having intercourse in the days *before* ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window is approximately Days 9 through 14. You can monitor your unique window using [LunaCycle](/tracker).

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is essential for anyone trying to conceive (to time intercourse) or trying to avoid pregnancy (to abstain or use protection).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Concept | Duration | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fertile Window | ~6 days | The entire timeframe conception is possible. |
| Ovulation Day | 12-24 hours | The specific day the egg is released. |
