## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Sperm can survive in the reproductive tract for up to 5 days, waiting for the egg, which lives for 12-24 hours.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation is on day 14, the fertile window is roughly days 9 to 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Timing intercourse during this window maximizes chances of conception.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation
| Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|
| 6-day timeframe | 1-day event |
