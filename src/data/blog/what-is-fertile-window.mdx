## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12 to 24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract (in fertile cervical mucus) for up to five days. Therefore, the fertile window opens roughly five days before ovulation and closes about one day after ovulation.

## Real-World Example

If a person ovulates on Day 15 of their cycle, their fertile window roughly spans from Day 10 to Day 15. Having intercourse on Day 12 could result in pregnancy because the sperm can survive until the egg is released on Day 15.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is critical whether you are trying to conceive (TTC) or trying to avoid pregnancy using natural family planning methods. Timing intercourse during this specific window maximizes the chances of fertilization.

## Fertile Window vs Menstruation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conception Chance** | Highest | Extremely low (but not impossible) |
| **Hormonal State** | High estrogen, peaking LH | Low estrogen and progesterone |
| **Physical Sign** | Stretchy, clear cervical mucus | Uterine bleeding |
