## Definition

The fertile window represents the days during a menstrual cycle when a person is most likely to become pregnant from unprotected sexual intercourse. It spans approximately six days.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The biology of the fertile window is based on the lifespan of both sperm and egg:
1. **Sperm Lifespan:** Sperm can survive in the optimal conditions of the female reproductive tract (aided by fertile cervical mucus) for up to 5 days.
2. **Egg Lifespan:** Once an egg is released during ovulation, it is viable for fertilization for only 12 to 24 hours.
Therefore, if intercourse occurs in the five days before ovulation, sperm can be waiting in the fallopian tubes ready to fertilize the egg as soon as it is released.
