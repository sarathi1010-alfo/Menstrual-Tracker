## Definition

The fertile window refers to the days during a woman's menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It typically spans a six-day period: the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself. This window exists because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions, while the egg only survives for 12-24 hours after release.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

As ovulation approaches, the body produces cervical mucus that is friendly to sperm (often described as resembling raw egg whites). This mucus nourishes the sperm and helps them travel through the cervix and into the fallopian tubes, where they wait for the egg to be released.

## Real-World Example in Cycle Tracking

Using the [LunaCycle Tracker](/tracker) to log your periods helps estimate your fertile window. If you have a regular 28-day cycle and ovulate on Day 14, your fertile window is roughly Days 9 through 14. If you are trying to conceive, having intercourse during these days maximizes your chances. If avoiding pregnancy, you would use our [Safe Days Calculator](/tools/safe-days-calculator) and avoid intercourse (or use protection) during this time.

## Fertile Window vs. Safe Days

| Feature | Fertile Window | Safe Days |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pregnancy Chance** | High | Low |
| **Timing** | ~5 days before and day of ovulation. | Days before the fertile window begins, and after the egg has died. |
| **Cervical Mucus** | Wet, slippery, clear. | Dry, sticky, or absent. |
