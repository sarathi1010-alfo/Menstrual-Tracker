The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during the menstrual cycle when sexual intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It includes the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, intercourse before ovulation can still result in conception.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on any of these days carries a chance of pregnancy.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is critical for couples trying to conceive (TTC) or for those using natural family planning methods to avoid pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | 1 day (12-24 hours) |
| Conception probability | Low to High | Highest |
