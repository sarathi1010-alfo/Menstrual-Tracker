The fertile window refers to the days during a woman's menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans approximately six days: the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The timing is based on the lifespan of both sperm and the egg. While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after being released (ovulation), sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days in the presence of fertile cervical mucus.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window typically runs from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Day 10 could result in pregnancy because the sperm can survive until the egg is released on Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is critical whether you are actively trying to conceive or trying to prevent pregnancy naturally.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
|---|---|---|
| **What is it?** | The total time conception is possible. | The specific day the egg is released. |
| **Length** | ~6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
