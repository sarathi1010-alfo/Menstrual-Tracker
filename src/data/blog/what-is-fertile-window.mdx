The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during the menstrual cycle when a woman is most likely to get pregnant from unprotected sex. It includes the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can live inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days in the presence of fertile cervical mucus.

## Real-World Example
If you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is roughly from Day 9 through Day 14. Having intercourse during this timeframe maximizes the chances that sperm will be waiting when the egg is released.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Identifying this window is critical for family planning. If you are trying to conceive, timing intercourse during these days is essential. If you are avoiding pregnancy, you must use protection or abstain during this time.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Timing | Leads up to and includes ovulation | A single mid-cycle event |
| Purpose | Timeframe when sperm can survive to fertilize | Release of the egg |
