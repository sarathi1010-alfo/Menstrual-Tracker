## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
The fertile window is determined by the lifespan of both sperm and the egg. While an egg survives for only 12-24 hours after ovulation, healthy sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, the fertile window spans the five days prior to ovulation plus the day of ovulation.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation occurs on Day 14 of a cycle, the fertile window would begin on Day 9. Intercourse occurring on days 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, or 14 could potentially lead to pregnancy.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Understanding the fertile window is critical for family planning. It allows individuals to time intercourse to either maximize the chances of conception or, conversely, to avoid unprotected intercourse if trying to prevent pregnancy naturally.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Fertile Window |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~28 days (21-35 days) | ~6 days |
| Purpose | Monthly reproductive cycle | The specific days conception is possible |
| Focus | Overall health and preparation | Timing for pregnancy |
