The fertile window is the timeframe during the menstrual cycle when a woman can get pregnant. It spans roughly six days: the five days leading up to [ovulation](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal) and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

This window exists because of the lifespan of sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in fertile cervical mucus for up to 5 days. The egg, however, only lives for 12-24 hours after release. Thus, having sperm ready and waiting before the egg is released maximizes the chances of conception.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window is approximately Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on any of these days could potentially result in pregnancy, with the highest probability on Days 12, 13, and 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is essential whether you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy using natural family planning. You can track this window securely using [LunaCycle](/).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Concept** | The timeframe when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. | The specific event of the egg being released. |
| **Dependence** | Relies on sperm lifespan + ovulation timing. | Relies solely on hormonal triggers (LH surge). |
