The fertile window is the period of time during a menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans approximately six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12-24 hours after release, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days in the presence of fertile cervical mucus. This means that intercourse occurring several days before ovulation can still lead to pregnancy.

## Real-World Example of the Fertile Window

If you ovulate on Day 14, your fertile window includes Day 14, but also Days 13, 12, 11, 10, and 9. The most "high-fertility" days are usually the two days immediately preceding ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Whether you are trying to conceive (TTC) or trying to avoid pregnancy, knowing this window is critical. It allows you to time intercourse or use contraception more effectively based on your body's biological reality.

| Day | Pregnancy Probability |
|-----|-----------------------|
| 3+ days before ovulation | Low to Moderate |
| 1-2 days before ovulation | High |
| Day of ovulation | Peak |
| 1 day after ovulation | Very Low |
