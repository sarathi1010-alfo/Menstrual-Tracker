## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after [ovulation](/what-is-ovulation), sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (specifically, in fertile cervical mucus). Therefore, the "window" of fertility opens several days before the egg is actually released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is expected on Day 14 of a cycle, the fertile window opens around Day 9 and closes approximately 24 hours after Day 14 (Day 15). Intercourse on Days 12, 13, or 14 yields the highest chances of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying your fertile window is critical whether you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy using natural family planning methods. Accurately tracking it using your cycle history with an app like [LunaCycle](/) provides valuable insight into your reproductive timing.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| **Duration** | About 6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Definition**| The period when intercourse can lead to pregnancy | The physical release of the egg |
| **Timing** | Before and during ovulation | A single event |
