# What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the timeframe during the menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans approximately six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, waiting for the egg to be released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window is roughly from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse on Day 12 can result in pregnancy because sperm survive until Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying it is crucial for couples trying to conceive, as well as for those practicing natural family planning to avoid pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Concept | Days intercourse can lead to pregnancy | The event of egg release |
