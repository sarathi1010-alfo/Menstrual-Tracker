## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Because sperm can survive in the female body for up to five days, intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation can result in conception. The egg only survives for 12-24 hours after release, closing the window shortly after ovulation.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14 of a cycle, the fertile window spans from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse during this timeframe has the highest probability of resulting in pregnancy.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is crucial for couples trying to conceive, as timing intercourse during these days maximizes the chances of success.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
