## What is the Fertile Window?

**Definition:** The fertile window is the specific timeframe during the menstrual cycle when a person is most likely to get pregnant. It comprises the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Because sperm can survive in fertile cervical mucus for up to 5 days, intercourse occurring before the egg is released can still result in conception once ovulation happens.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation occurs on cycle day 14, the fertile window would be days 9 through 14. Day 15 would have a very low chance of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Whether you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy, knowing your fertile window is critical for timing intercourse.
