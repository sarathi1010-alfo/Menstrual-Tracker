The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It includes the five days before ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, healthy sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, if intercourse occurs in the days leading up to ovulation, the sperm can be waiting in the fallopian tubes when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If a person ovulates on Day 14 of their cycle, their fertile window spans roughly from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Day 12 or 13 carries the highest statistical chance of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Understanding this window is the core of Natural Family Planning (NFP), whether the goal is to achieve pregnancy or avoid it.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Length** | ~6 Days | 12-24 Hours |
| **Timing** | Leads up to and includes ovulation | Occurs at the end of the fertile window |
| **Conception Chance** | Possible on any day, peaks just before ovulation | The final opportunity for conception |
