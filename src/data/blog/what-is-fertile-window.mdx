The fertile window is the timeframe during the menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans approximately six days: the five days leading up to ovulation plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions, having intercourse before the egg is released can result in conception.

## Real-World Example
If you expect to ovulate on day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window would roughly span from day 9 through day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Identifying it is crucial for anyone trying to conceive (or trying to avoid pregnancy using natural family planning).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day
| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | Days sperm can wait for the egg | Day the egg is released |
| Length | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
