The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during the menstrual cycle when sexual intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It is determined by the lifespan of the egg and the lifespan of sperm within the female reproductive tract.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after ovulation, sperm can live inside the female body (specifically in fertile cervical mucus) for up to five days. Therefore, the fertile window encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## Real-World Example

If an egg is released on Friday (ovulation day), the fertile window opened on the preceding Sunday. If intercourse occurred on Monday, the sperm could survive in the fallopian tubes waiting for the egg to be released on Friday, resulting in conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this specific timeframe is crucial for anyone trying to conceive (TTC) to maximize their chances. Conversely, knowing this window is equally vital for those using natural family planning methods to avoid pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Mid-cycle, ending at ovulation | Post-ovulation until period starts |
| Fertility | High (pregnancy possible) | Extremely low (pregnancy highly unlikely) |
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-14 days |
