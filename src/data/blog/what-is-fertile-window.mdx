The fertile window is the roughly six-day period leading up to and including the day of ovulation when a woman is most likely to get pregnant.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days. The egg lives for up to 24 hours after ovulation.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is on day 14, the fertile window is approximately days 9 through 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Key for those trying to conceive or avoiding pregnancy (using natural family planning).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| -------------- | --------- |
| 6 days long | 1 day long |
