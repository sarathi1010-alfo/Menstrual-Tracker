## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window represents the days in a woman's menstrual cycle when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It spans approximately six days: the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after being released, sperm can survive in the favorable environment of fertile cervical mucus for up to five days. Therefore, having intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is roughly Day 9 through Day 14. Your highest chances of conception are on Days 12, 13, and 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is critical for anyone trying to conceive (TTC) or trying to avoid pregnancy naturally.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | When conception is possible. | When the egg is released. |
| **Duration** | ~6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Key Factor** | Sperm survival time + egg release. | Egg release only. |
