The fertile window is the timeframe during the menstrual cycle when unprotected sex is most likely to result in pregnancy. It spans the days leading up to and including the day of ovulation, typically lasting about 6 days.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days in the presence of fertile cervical mucus. Therefore, the fertile window includes the 5 days before ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## Real-World Example

If a woman is expected to ovulate on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window would roughly begin on Day 9 and end on Day 14. Having intercourse during these days, particularly on Days 12, 13, or 14, maximizes the chances of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is critical whether your goal is to conceive (by timing intercourse for these days) or to avoid pregnancy (by avoiding unprotected sex during this time, though a strict tracking method is required for this to be effective).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Timing | Leads up to and includes ovulation | The specific day the egg is released |
| Role | The period when conception is possible | The event that makes conception possible |
