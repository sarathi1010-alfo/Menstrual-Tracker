## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in optimal cervical mucus for up to 5 days. Therefore, the "window" of fertility opens several days *before* the egg is actually released.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window is approximately Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on Day 10 could still result in pregnancy if sperm survive until Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is crucial whether you are trying to conceive (TTC) or practicing natural family planning to avoid pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Concept** | The time pregnancy is possible | The actual release of the egg |
| **Sperm Life** | Relies on 5-day sperm lifespan | Relies on 1-day egg lifespan |
