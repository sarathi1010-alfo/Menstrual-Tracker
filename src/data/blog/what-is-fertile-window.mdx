## What is the Fertile Window?
The fertile window refers to the days in a menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans the five days preceding ovulation plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Because sperm can live for up to 5 days, intercourse during this window can lead to conception when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation is on Day 14, the fertile window is Day 9 through Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Crucial for family planning (TTC) and avoiding pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation
| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| Duration | ~6 days | ~24 hours |
| Focus | Conception opportunity | Egg release |
