## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
The fertile window exists because of two distinct biological facts: a woman's egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, but sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days under the right conditions (fertile cervical mucus).

## Real-World Example
If you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is roughly Day 9 through Day 14. Having intercourse on Day 10 could result in pregnancy because the sperm can survive until the egg is released on Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Understanding your fertile window is essential whether you are trying to conceive (TTC) or trying to avoid pregnancy. Tracking tools like [LunaCycle](/tracker) can help you estimate this window based on your cycle history.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Focus | Sperm survival + Egg presence | Egg release only |
| Purpose | Identifying all possible days for conception | The specific event required for conception |
