## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during the menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It includes the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself, because sperm can live inside the female body for up to five days.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Your chances of getting pregnant increase steadily during the fertile window, peaking on the two days before ovulation and the day of ovulation. Once ovulation occurs and the egg dies (after 12-24 hours), the fertile window closes until the next cycle.

## Real-World Example

If you expect to ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window roughly spans Days 9 through 14. Having intercourse during these days provides the highest chance of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Whether you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy, knowing your fertile window is critical for timing intercourse appropriately.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The days pregnancy is possible. | The release of the egg. |
| **Duration** | About 6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Relationship** | Leads up to and includes ovulation. | Is the final day of the fertile window. |
