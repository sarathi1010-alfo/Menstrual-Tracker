## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female body (supported by fertile cervical mucus) for up to 5 days. Therefore, having intercourse in the days *before* ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is roughly Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on Day 12 or 13 yields the highest probability of conception, while Day 15 is generally past the window.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is the foundation of both natural family planning (avoiding pregnancy) and fertility optimization (achieving pregnancy).

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Pregnancy Chance** | High throughout the window | Highest on this specific day |
| **Indicator** | Increasing cervical mucus | BBT spike the day *after* |
