The fertile window is the time during the menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It typically spans six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12 to 24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, having intercourse in the days just *before* ovulation maximizes the chance that sperm will be present when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example

If you expect to ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window roughly spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Having intercourse during this window gives you the highest probability of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Whether you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy, knowing your fertile window is crucial for family planning.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Duration | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Timing | Precedes and includes ovulation | Middle of the cycle |
| Focus | Sperm survival + egg release | Egg release only |
