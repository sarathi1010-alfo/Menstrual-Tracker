## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window exists because of the different lifespans of sperm and the egg. While the egg survives for only 12 to 24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days under the right conditions (aided by fertile cervical mucus).

## Real-World Example

If you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is roughly from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse on any of these days could potentially result in pregnancy, with the highest probability on Days 12, 13, and 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is crucial whether you are trying to conceive (TTC) or practicing natural family planning to avoid pregnancy. Identifying these specific days allows you to time intercourse accordingly.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | About 6 days. | 12 - 24 hours. |
| **Definition** | The timeframe when conception is possible. | The specific event of the egg releasing. |
| **Biological Basis** | Combined lifespan of sperm and egg. | Lifespan of the egg alone. |
