## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the specific period during the menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It typically spans the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Pregnancy is only possible if sperm is present when the egg is released. Because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days, intercourse in the days preceding ovulation can lead to fertilization.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is predicted for day 14, the fertile window would roughly encompass days 9 through 14. Intercourse during this time maximizes the chances of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is critical for individuals or couples who are trying to conceive (TTC) as well as those practicing natural family planning methods to avoid pregnancy.
