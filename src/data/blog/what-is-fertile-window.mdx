## Definition
The fertile window is the timeframe during a menstrual cycle when a woman can conceive. It spans roughly six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days, intercourse in the days *before* ovulation means sperm are waiting when the egg is released. The egg itself only lives for 12-24 hours.

## Real-World Example
If you ovulate on Day 14, your fertile window is roughly Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on Day 12 or 13 yields a high chance of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Identifying this window is critical for anyone trying to conceive (TTC). It's the only time during the cycle when pregnancy can occur.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| Timeframe | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| Role | The period when intercourse can lead to pregnancy | The actual release of the egg |
