The fertile window is the roughly 6-day period in a menstrual cycle when sexual intercourse can result in pregnancy. It includes the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
While an egg only lives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days if fertile cervical mucus is present. Thus, intercourse days before ovulation can lead to conception.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle with ovulation on Day 14, the fertile window would typically be Days 9 through 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Identifying this window is essential for anyone Trying to Conceive (TTC) to time intercourse effectively. It is also a core concept for natural family planning methods.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~6 Days | 12-24 Hours |
| Scope | Includes sperm survival time | Only the egg's lifespan |
