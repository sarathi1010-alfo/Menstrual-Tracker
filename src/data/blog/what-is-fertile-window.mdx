**Definition:** The fertile window refers to the specific days during a woman's menstrual cycle when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It spans approximately six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract (with the help of fertile cervical mucus) for up to five days. The fertile window combines these lifespans.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is predicted to happen on Day 14 of the cycle, the fertile window would roughly be Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on Day 12 could result in conception because sperm can wait for the egg.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is critical for anyone trying to conceive (TTC) to time intercourse correctly. It's equally important for those practicing natural family planning to avoid pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | About 6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Focus** | Sperm survival + egg lifespan. | Egg release. |
