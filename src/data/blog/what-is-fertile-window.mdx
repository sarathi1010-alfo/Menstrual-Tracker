# What is the Fertile Window?

**Definition:** The fertile window refers to the roughly six days during a menstrual cycle when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It includes the five days before ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, intercourse leading up to ovulation can result in fertilization.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is expected on Day 14, the fertile window would roughly be Days 9 through 14. Intercourse on Day 10 could result in pregnancy if sperm survive until the egg is released.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is the most critical factor for Trying to Conceive (TTC). Timing intercourse within this window maximizes the chances of conception.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Timing** | Before and during ovulation | After ovulation |
| **Pregnancy Chance** | High | Extremely Low |
