The fertile window is the roughly six-day period during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. It comprises the five days leading up to ovulation plus the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
Because sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days, intercourse before the egg is released can still result in fertilization once ovulation occurs.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation happens on Day 14, the fertile window would be from Day 9 to Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Identifying it is essential for anyone trying to conceive (TTC) or trying to avoid pregnancy using fertility awareness methods.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Timing | Pre-ovulation & Ovulation | Post-ovulation |
| Pregnancy Chance | High | Very Low |
