## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window refers to the roughly six days during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself.

<MedicalDisclaimer />

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window is determined by the lifespans of sperm and the egg:
- **Sperm Lifespan:** Sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract (specifically in fertile cervical mucus) for up to 5 days.
- **Egg Lifespan:** Once released during ovulation, an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours.
Therefore, if intercourse occurs several days *before* ovulation, sperm can wait in the fallopian tubes for the egg to be released.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14 of the cycle:
- The fertile window begins around Day 9.
- The most fertile days are Days 12, 13, and 14.
- By Day 15 (24 hours after ovulation), the fertile window has closed.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is the cornerstone of natural family planning. For those Trying to Conceive (TTC), timing intercourse during these days maximizes the chances of pregnancy. For those avoiding pregnancy, knowing when to use barrier methods or abstain is critical.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Concept** | Timeframe for potential conception. | The biological event of egg release. |
| **Duration** | ~6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Key Factor** | Sperm survival time. | Egg release and survival time. |
