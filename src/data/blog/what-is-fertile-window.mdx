**Definition:** The fertile window is the specific timeframe during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It spans the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days. Therefore, having intercourse during the days *before* ovulation maximizes the chance of fertilization.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on Day 14, the fertile window is generally from Day 9 to Day 14. Day 13 and Day 14 typically have the highest probability of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is the most critical step for anyone actively trying to conceive (TTC) or for those using natural family planning methods to avoid pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|
| Time of high fertility | Post-ovulation, low fertility |
| Driven by rising estrogen | Driven by high progesterone |
| Lasts ~6 days | Lasts ~11-17 days |
