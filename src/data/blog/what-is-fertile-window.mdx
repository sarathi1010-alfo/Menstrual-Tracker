The fertile window is the roughly six-day period ending on the day of ovulation when a woman has the highest chance of conceiving.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12 to 24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (aided by fertile cervical mucus). Therefore, intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation can result in pregnancy.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is predicted for Day 14, the fertile window would generally span from Day 9 to Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is essential whether you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy using fertility awareness methods.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
|----------------|--------------|
| High chance of pregnancy. | Post-ovulation, very low chance of pregnancy. |
| Includes days leading to ovulation. | Follows ovulation. |
