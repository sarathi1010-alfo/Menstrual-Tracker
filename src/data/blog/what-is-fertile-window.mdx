## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window exists because of the different lifespans of the egg and sperm. An egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation. However, healthy sperm can survive in the favorable environment of cervical mucus for up to 5 days. Therefore, intercourse leading up to ovulation can result in pregnancy.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is expected on Day 14 of a cycle, the fertile window generally begins on Day 9 and ends roughly 24 hours after ovulation on Day 15. Having intercourse on Day 12 or 13 yields the highest probability of conception.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is the cornerstone of fertility awareness. Whether you are actively trying to conceive (TTC) and timing intercourse, or practicing natural family planning to avoid pregnancy, pinpointing these specific days is essential.

## Fertile Window vs Menstruation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Conception possible | Uterine lining sheds |
| **Hormonal Driver** | High Estrogen / LH Surge | Low levels of all hormones |
| **Physical Sign** | Stretchy, clear mucus | Bleeding |
