## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window exists because of the lifespan of sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days in optimal conditions (fertile cervical mucus), while an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation.

## Real-World Example

If you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is roughly from Day 9 through Day 14. Intercourse on any of these days could potentially result in pregnancy, with the highest chances on days 12, 13, and 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window is essential if you are trying to conceive, as timing intercourse is crucial. Conversely, it is also important information for those practicing fertility awareness methods to avoid pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Role in Pregnancy** | The timeframe when intercourse can lead to conception | The release of the egg needed for conception |
