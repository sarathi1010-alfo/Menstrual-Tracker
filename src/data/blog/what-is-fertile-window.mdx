The fertile window is the period during the menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It spans approximately six days, culminating on the day of ovulation.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

This window exists because of two key biological facts: sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (fertile cervical mucus), and the egg survives for only 12-24 hours after ovulation.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation occurs on day 14 of a cycle, the fertile window would roughly begin on day 9 and close around day 15. Intercourse on day 11 could result in pregnancy if the sperm survives until the egg is released on day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is the most crucial step for anyone actively trying to conceive, as timing intercourse during these days maximizes the chances of fertilization.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~6 Days | 12-24 Hours |
| **Definition** | The time pregnancy is possible | The exact moment the egg is released |
| **Key Factor** | Sperm survival time + Egg life | Egg release |
