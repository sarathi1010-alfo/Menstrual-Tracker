The fertile window is the specific period during the menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It spans about six days: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?
The fertile window exists because of the different lifespans of sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days if fertile cervical mucus is present. However, the egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation. Therefore, having intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation ensures sperm are waiting when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example
If ovulation occurs on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window is from Day 9 through Day 14. Day 13 and Day 14 are typically your most fertile days.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?
Knowing your fertile window is critical for family planning. Couples trying to conceive need to time intercourse during this window. Conversely, those trying to avoid pregnancy using natural family planning methods must abstain from unprotected sex during this time.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | About 6 days | 12-24 hours |
| **Concept** | The days pregnancy is possible | The release of the egg |
| **Determined By** | Sperm lifespan + egg lifespan | LH Surge |
