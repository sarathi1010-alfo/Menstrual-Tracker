## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window exists because of the lifespan of sperm and the egg. Sperm can survive in optimal cervical fluid for up to 5 days, while the egg survives for only 12-24 hours after it is released (ovulation). Therefore, intercourse in the days leading up to and on the day of ovulation can result in pregnancy.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window generally spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Day 12 could lead to pregnancy because the sperm can wait in the fallopian tubes for the egg to be released.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Knowing your fertile window gives you the highest chance of timing intercourse correctly if you are trying to conceive. Conversely, if you are avoiding pregnancy, knowing this window helps you understand when you are most at risk.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| Definition | The days when conception is possible. | The exact moment the egg is released. |
| Duration | ~6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
