## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Because sperm can survive in hospitable cervical mucus for up to 5 days, and an egg lives for up to 24 hours after release, the fertile window spans the days leading up to and including ovulation.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is expected on Day 14, the fertile window opens around Day 9 and closes approximately 24 hours after Day 14. Intercourse on Day 11 could result in pregnancy if sperm survive until Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying this window is crucial both for those trying to conceive (to time intercourse) and those practicing natural family planning (to abstain).

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Timing** | Precedes and includes ovulation | Follows ovulation |
| **Fertility** | High | Extremely low |
