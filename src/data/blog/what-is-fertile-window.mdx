## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The fertile window is defined by the lifespan of both the sperm and the egg. While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, healthy sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract (in fertile cervical mucus) for up to 5 days. Therefore, sex occurring in the days leading up to ovulation can result in conception.

## Real-World Example

If ovulation is predicted to happen on Friday (Day 14), the fertile window opens on Sunday (Day 9). Sex on Monday or Tuesday could result in sperm waiting in the fallopian tubes for the egg to be released on Friday.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Identifying the fertile window is crucial for both trying to conceive (TTC) and natural family planning (NFP). Tracking apps like [LunaCycle](/) help estimate this window based on your cycle history, allowing you to time intercourse effectively or avoid it depending on your goals.

## Fertile Window vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| **Timing** | Pre-ovulation and day of ovulation | Post-ovulation |
| **Pregnancy Chance** | High | Near zero |
| **Hormone** | Rising Estrogen, LH surge | High Progesterone |
| **Duration** | ~6 days | 12-14 days |
