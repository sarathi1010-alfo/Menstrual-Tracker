# Period Tracker Apps: What to Look For in 2026

With hundreds of menstrual tracking apps available, finding the right one can feel overwhelming. In a world where health data privacy is increasingly under the spotlight, the criteria for a "good" app have shifted from just features to security and ethics.

## 1. Data Privacy and Sovereignty
This is the most important feature. In 2026, you should ask:
- **Where is my data stored?** (Local vs. Cloud)
- **Do I need an account?** (Accounts usually mean your data is on a server)
- **What is the business model?** (If the product is free and cloud-based, you might be the product)

## 2. Prediction Accuracy
A tracker is only as good as its algorithm. Look for tools that:
- Allow you to customize your luteal phase length.
- Account for cycle variability.
- Provide a clear 'fertile window' based on your specific history rather than a generic 28-day model.

## 3. User Interface (UI) and Experience (UX)
You will likely use this app every day (or at least every month). It should be:
- **Fast:** You should be able to log a period in under 5 seconds.
- **Visual:** A clear calendar view is essential for spotting patterns.
- **Calm:** Many users prefer a medically neutral or calming aesthetic over bright, stereotypical designs.

## 4. Export and Backup Options
Since your health data is yours, you should be able to take it with you. Ensure the app allows you to export your data (usually as JSON or CSV) so you can share it with a doctor or move it to another service.

## Comparison Checklist

| Feature | Importance | Why it Matters |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| Local Storage | High | Ensures data cannot be leaked from a server. |
| No Account Required | High | Minimizes your digital footprint. |
| Customizable Cycles | Medium | Essential for irregular periods. |
| Export Data | Medium | Important for medical consultations. |
