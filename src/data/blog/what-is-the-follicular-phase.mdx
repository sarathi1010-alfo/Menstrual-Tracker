The follicular phase is the first half of your menstrual cycle. It officially begins on the first day of bright red bleeding (your period) and ends when you ovulate. During this time, your body is hard at work preparing to release a mature egg.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The pituitary gland releases Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH), prompting the ovaries to develop several follicles. Typically, one follicle becomes dominant and matures. As it grows, it produces estrogen, which helps rebuild the uterine lining shed during your period.

## Real-World Example of Follicular Phase Tracking

If your cycle length varies from month to month, it is almost always because the length of your follicular phase varies. By logging your start dates in [LunaCycle](/), you can observe these natural fluctuations and better understand your body's response to environmental factors like stress.

## Follicular Phase vs. Menstrual Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Menstrual Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Timing | Day 1 of bleeding until Ovulation. | Day 1 to the end of bleeding. |
| Overlap | Yes. The menstrual phase is the *beginning* of the follicular phase. | Contained entirely within the early follicular phase. |
