## The Build-Up to Ovulation

The follicular phase is characterized by the body's preparation for ovulation. Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH) encourages the growth of follicles in the ovaries. As these follicles grow, they produce increasing amounts of estrogen.

## Energy and Mood Shifts

Rising estrogen levels often bring a sense of renewal. Many individuals report feeling more energetic, focused, and sociable during the mid-to-late follicular phase compared to the luteal phase.

## The Most Variable Phase

Unlike the luteal phase, which remains relatively constant in length, the follicular phase is highly variable. Stress, illness, or travel can delay ovulation, extending the follicular phase. Using a tracker like [LunaCycle](/) helps you observe these variations over time.
