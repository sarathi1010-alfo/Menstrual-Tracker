## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormonal awareness is crucial because hormones act as chemical messengers, influencing nearly every aspect of your well-being. Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals, explaining why you might feel energetic one week and fatigued the next.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You don't need daily blood tests to track hormonal changes. By logging daily symptoms, you can infer hormonal shifts. Use [LunaCycle](/) to record mood, energy levels, skin changes, and cervical mucus. Over a few months, patterns will emerge aligning with the typical rise and fall of estrogen and progesterone.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstruation: Rest and Reflect
As all hormones are at their lowest, focus on rest, gentle movement, and iron-rich foods to replenish what is lost.

### Follicular Phase: Harness the Energy
Rising estrogen brings increased energy and cognitive function. This is the ideal time for challenging workouts and starting new projects.

### Ovulation: Socialize and Connect
Peak estrogen and a surge in LH and testosterone often lead to increased sociability and libido. Capitalize on this communicative phase.

### Early Luteal: Sustained Focus
Progesterone begins to rise, offering a grounding effect. It's a great time for detail-oriented tasks and steady work.

### Late Luteal: Self-Care and Comfort
As progesterone and estrogen drop (if not pregnant), PMS symptoms may arise. Prioritize sleep, stress management, and comforting routines.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

If your hormones are out of sync, your body will tell you. Look out for these signs:

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Irregular or missing periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or extreme stress |
| Severe PMS or PMDD | Estrogen dominance or low progesterone |
| Unexplained weight gain | Insulin resistance or thyroid dysfunction |
| Hormonal acne (jawline/chin) | Excess androgens |
