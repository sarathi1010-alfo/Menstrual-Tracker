Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals. Track how these hormones make you feel using the secure [LunaCycle](/) app.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones do more than just regulate reproduction. They influence your mood, sleep, appetite, and energy levels. By understanding the hormonal ebb and flow, you can give yourself grace on low-energy days and capitalize on high-energy phases. For a foundational overview of the whole process, see [Menstrual Cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide), and to tie this into broader tracking goals, visit the [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide).

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

Since you can't see your hormones, you have to track their effects. Using a tool like [LunaCycle](/), log your daily symptoms: energy levels, skin changes, mood, and cervical fluid. Over a few months, patterns will emerge that correspond to the phases detailed in [Cycle Phases Explained](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal).

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Rest and restore. Stick to light exercises like yoga or walking.

### Late Follicular (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on high energy. Tackle difficult projects and engage in high-intensity workouts.

### Ovulation (Estrogen & LH Peak)
You are likely feeling your most social and communicative.

### Early Luteal (Rising Progesterone)
Focus on steady tasks. Progesterone can make you feel calmer but also slightly more sluggish.

### Late Luteal (Dropping Hormones)
Prioritize self-care. This is when PMS strikes as hormone levels crash.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Extreme sensitivity to hormone drops |
| Irregular Cycles | PCOS (often high androgens) |
| Heavy Bleeding | Estrogen dominance |
| Spotting before period | Low progesterone |
