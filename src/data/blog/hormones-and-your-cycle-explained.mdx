## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

The menstrual cycle is a complex symphony orchestrated by hormones. When these chemical messengers are in harmony, your cycle runs smoothly. When they are out of balance, you may experience a range of symptoms from acne and fatigue to heavy bleeding and irregular periods.

Awareness of what these hormones do—Estrogen, Progesterone, Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), and Luteinizing Hormone (LH)—demystifies your body's signals. It helps explain why you might feel outgoing and energetic one week, and introspective and tired the next, empowering you to manage your reproductive health more proactively.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure your hormone levels daily without blood tests, you can track their *effects*. By logging physical and emotional symptoms in [LunaCycle](/), you can start to see patterns that correspond to hormonal shifts.

*   **High Estrogen signs:** Clear skin, high energy, increased libido, clear/stretchy cervical mucus.
*   **High Progesterone signs:** Higher body temperature, breast tenderness, slower digestion, sleepiness.
*   **Dropping Hormone signs:** Cramps, mood swings, headaches, fatigue.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. The Menstrual Phase Strategy
As all hormones drop, prioritize rest, gentle movement like yoga, and iron-rich foods to replenish what is lost through bleeding.

### 2. The Follicular Phase Strategy
With estrogen rising, this is a great time to tackle challenging tasks, socialize, and engage in higher-intensity workouts.

### 3. The Ovulatory Phase Strategy
Peak estrogen and a surge in testosterone mean peak energy. Capitalize on communication skills and focus on fertility goals if TTC.

### 4. The Luteal Phase Strategy
As progesterone dominates, switch to strength training or moderate exercise. Focus on complex carbs and magnesium to combat cravings and PMS.

### 5. The Late Luteal (Pre-menstrual) Strategy
Wind down. As hormones plummet, practice extreme self-care, prioritize sleep, and reduce stress.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Sign | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| **Severe PMS / PMDD** | Extreme sensitivity to normal hormonal fluctuations. |
| **Irregular/Missing Periods** | Often linked to PCOS, stress, or thyroid issues affecting ovulation. |
| **Mid-cycle Spotting** | Can be related to a sudden drop in estrogen around ovulation. |
| **Very Heavy Bleeding** | May indicate estrogen dominance or low progesterone. |
