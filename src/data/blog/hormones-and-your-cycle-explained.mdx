## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is driven by a delicate balance of hormones. Understanding how estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) fluctuate throughout the month can help you make sense of changes in your mood, energy levels, and physical symptoms.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure your exact hormone levels at home without specialized tests, you can infer hormonal shifts by tracking your symptoms. Using a private tracker like [LunaCycle](/tracker) allows you to log daily changes in mood, energy, and physical signs like cervical mucus and basal body temperature.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest, hydration, and gentle exercise as estrogen and progesterone drop.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on increasing energy for challenging workouts and complex tasks.

### 3. Ovulatory Phase (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
Enjoy peak energy and sociability; a great time for networking or socializing.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
Shift towards moderate exercise and steady tasks as progesterone promotes a calming effect.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase (Hormones Dropping)
Prioritize self-care, stress reduction, and healthy eating to manage PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Hormonal Imbalance |
| --- | --- |
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Estrogen dominance or low progesterone. |
| Irregular Cycles | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress affecting LH/FSH. |
| Heavy Bleeding | Excess estrogen or low progesterone. |
| Absent Periods (Amenorrhea) | Low estrogen, stress, or extreme weight loss. |
