## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones do more than just regulate your period; they affect your mood, energy levels, metabolism, and reproductive health. Understanding these hormonal shifts can help you make sense of how you feel throughout the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

The best way to track hormonal changes is by logging your symptoms and cycle days using a tool like [LunaCycle](/). Over time, you'll see patterns that correlate with hormonal fluctuations.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstrual Phase Strategy
Focus on rest and gentle movement as hormone levels are at their lowest.

### Follicular Phase Strategy
Take advantage of rising estrogen by engaging in high-energy activities and starting new projects.

### Ovulation Strategy
Peak estrogen and testosterone mean you're likely feeling your most energetic and social.

### Early Luteal Phase Strategy
As progesterone rises, focus on steady, moderate activities and nutrient-dense foods.

### Late Luteal Phase Strategy (PMS)
Prioritize self-care, reduce stress, and ensure adequate sleep as hormones drop before your period.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Possible Cause |
|---|---|
| Irregular periods | PCOS, stress, thyroid issues |
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Extreme sensitivity to normal hormonal changes |
| Hormonal acne | Elevated androgens (testosterone) |
| Hair loss | Thyroid imbalance or PCOS |
