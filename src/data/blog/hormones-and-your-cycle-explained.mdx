Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormonal fluctuations affect more than just your period. They impact mood, energy levels, appetite, and sleep. Understanding these shifts helps you optimize your daily life.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests give precise levels, you can track hormonal effects by logging daily symptoms like mood, energy, and physical changes in an app like [LunaCycle](/).

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase
Rest and recharge. Hormone levels are low.

### 2. Early Follicular Phase
Focus on complex carbs and lean proteins to support rising estrogen.

### 3. Late Follicular/Ovulation
Capitalize on high energy levels for intense workouts or challenging tasks.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase
Prioritize stable blood sugar as progesterone rises, which can increase cravings.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase
Practice stress reduction techniques to manage PMS symptoms as hormones drop.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Cause |
|---|---|
| Severe acne | High androgens |
| Irregular periods | PCOS or thyroid issues |
| Extreme fatigue | Thyroid imbalance or low iron |
