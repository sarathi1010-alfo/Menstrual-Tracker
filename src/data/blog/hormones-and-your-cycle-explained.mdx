Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

For mood, energy, and reproductive health, understanding your hormones is crucial. It helps explain the physical and emotional shifts you experience throughout the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

Using symptoms and cycle logging, you can infer hormonal shifts. For example, increased cervical mucus indicates rising estrogen, while a temperature shift confirms progesterone is present.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Rest, stay hydrated, and focus on gentle movement.

### 2. Early Follicular (Rising Estrogen)
Engage in creative tasks and light exercise as energy builds.

### 3. Late Follicular/Ovulation (Peak Estrogen/LH)
High energy—great for intense workouts and social events.

### 4. Early Luteal (Rising Progesterone)
Focus on organization and steady work; energy starts to slow.

### 5. Late Luteal (Dropping Hormones)
Prioritize self-care, sleep, and managing PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Irregular cycles | PCOS, Thyroid issues |
| Severe PMS | Estrogen dominance / Low progesterone |
| Hot flashes | Perimenopause / Low estrogen |
