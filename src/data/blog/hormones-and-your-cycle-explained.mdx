## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is entirely driven by hormones. Understanding how these chemical messengers work provides a window into your overall health. By learning about the roles of estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH), you can better anticipate changes in your mood, energy, and physical state.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure your hormone levels daily without blood tests, you can track the *effects* of these hormones. By logging symptoms such as mood swings, energy dips, breast tenderness, and changes in cervical fluid using a tool like [LunaCycle](/), you can infer what is happening hormonally in your body.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest and gentle movement. Your body needs recovery time.

### Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on increasing energy. This is a great time for challenging workouts and creative projects.

### Ovulation (Estrogen & LH Peak)
You may feel most social and energetic. Take advantage of this peak for networking or important tasks.

### Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
As progesterone rises, you may feel more inward-focused. Gentle exercise and self-care are beneficial.

### Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
Prepare for your period. Prioritize sleep, manage stress, and expect potential PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
| --- | --- |
| Irregular or absent periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress |
| Severe PMS or PMDD | Estrogen dominance or low progesterone |
| Heavy or prolonged bleeding | Fibroids, polyps, or hormonal imbalance |
| Painful cramps | Endometriosis or elevated prostaglandins |
