Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

For mood, energy, and reproductive health, understanding the ebb and flow of your hormones is crucial. These chemicals act as messengers, telling your body when to release an egg, when to build up the uterine lining, and when to shed it. They also influence your brain chemistry, affecting how you feel emotionally and physically throughout the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

Using symptoms and cycle logging is the most accessible way to track hormonal changes. By noting when you feel energetic (often linked to rising estrogen) or sluggish/bloated (often linked to rising progesterone and its subsequent drop), you can map out your body's internal hormonal landscape. Using an app like [LunaCycle](/) makes this easy and private.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. The Menstrual Phase
Rest and recharge. Hormones are at their lowest. Focus on gentle movement and nourishing foods.

### 2. The Follicular Phase
As estrogen rises, harness that increasing energy for more intense workouts and complex tasks.

### 3. The Ovulatory Phase
With peak estrogen and a surge of LH, you're likely feeling your most sociable and energetic. Capitalize on this for social events or presentations.

### 4. The Luteal Phase (Early)
As progesterone takes over, you might feel more focused but less outwardly energetic. Good for detail-oriented work.

### 5. The Luteal Phase (Late)
Hormones drop, potentially leading to PMS. Prioritize self-care, sleep, and stress reduction.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Hormonal Issue |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Heavy or painful periods | High estrogen or low progesterone |
| Irregular or missed cycles | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress impacting LH/FSH |
| Severe mood swings | Extreme fluctuations in estrogen/progesterone |
| Unexplained weight gain | Insulin resistance or thyroid imbalance |
