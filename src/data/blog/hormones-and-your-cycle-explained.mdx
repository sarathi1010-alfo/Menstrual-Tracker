Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones do more than just regulate your menstrual cycle; they impact your entire body. Hormonal fluctuations can influence your mood, energy levels, sleep quality, and even your metabolism. By understanding how estrogen and progesterone rise and fall, you can anticipate these changes and manage your health more effectively.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You can infer hormonal changes by tracking your symptoms and cycle phases using [LunaCycle](/). For example, increased energy and clear skin often correlate with rising estrogen, while bloating and mood swings might indicate rising progesterone in the luteal phase.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase Strategy
Focus on rest and gentle movements, like yoga, as hormone levels are at their lowest.

### 2. Follicular Phase Strategy
Take advantage of rising estrogen by engaging in high-intensity workouts and tackling complex projects.

### 3. Ovulatory Phase Strategy
With estrogen and testosterone peaking, this is a great time for social events and communication.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase Strategy
As progesterone rises, shift towards moderate exercise and focus on finishing tasks.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase Strategy
Prioritize self-care, sleep, and stress reduction to manage potential PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Hormonal Issue |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Extreme sensitivity to normal hormonal fluctuations. |
| Irregular or missing periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or extreme stress. |
| Heavy bleeding | Estrogen dominance or low progesterone. |
| Persistent acne | Elevated androgens. |
