## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters
Hormonal awareness matters for your mood, energy, and reproductive health. Understanding these chemical messengers helps explain *why* you feel the way you do at different times of the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes
You can track hormonal changes using symptom journaling and cycle logging apps like [LunaCycle](/). Look for patterns in headaches, skin changes, and mood swings.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies
### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Rest and focus on gentle movement and nutrient-dense foods.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on rising energy with more intense workouts and creative tasks.

### 3. Ovulation (Peak Estrogen & LH)
Enjoy peak social energy and physical stamina.

### 4. Early Luteal (Rising Progesterone)
Focus on steady-state activities and organization.

### 5. Late Luteal (Dropping Hormones)
Prioritize self-care, sleep, and managing PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs
| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Irregular or missing periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress |
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Estrogen dominance or low progesterone |
| Hormonal acne (jawline/chin) | Excess androgens |
