## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is governed by a delicate dance of hormones. By understanding the roles of Estrogen, Progesterone, Luteinizing Hormone (LH), and Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), you can better navigate changes in your mood, energy, and reproductive health. See the [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) for more details.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You can't see hormones, but you can feel their effects. Tracking physical and emotional symptoms with [LunaCycle](/) is an excellent way to infer hormonal shifts.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation: Rest and Reflect
As hormones drop to their lowest levels, prioritize rest and gentle activities.

### 2. Follicular Phase: Harness Estrogen
Take advantage of rising estrogen by tackling challenging tasks and engaging in higher-intensity workouts. See [cycle phases explained](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal).

### 3. Ovulation: Peak Energy
Estrogen and testosterone peak. This is often when you feel your most energetic and sociable.

### 4. Early Luteal: Smooth the Transition
As progesterone rises, focus on complex carbohydrates and steady-state cardio.

### 5. Late Luteal: Prepare for PMS
Support your body with magnesium-rich foods and stress-reduction techniques.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Indication |
|---|---|
| Persistent severe acne | High androgens |
| Extremely heavy periods | Estrogen dominance |
| Short luteal phase | Low progesterone |
