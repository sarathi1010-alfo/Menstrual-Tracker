## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is a complex symphony of hormones. The main players—Estrogen, Progesterone, Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), and Luteinizing Hormone (LH)—dictate not just reproductive events, but also influence your mood, metabolism, skin, and sleep. Awareness of these hormonal fluctuations demystifies the physical and emotional changes you experience throughout the month, allowing you to work with your body rather than against it.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You don't need a lab test every day to track your hormones. By observing and logging your body's signals—such as basal body temperature (BBT), cervical mucus changes, acne flare-ups, and mood swings—you can infer what your hormones are doing. Using a privacy-focused app like [LunaCycle](/) allows you to safely record these daily symptoms and spot patterns over time.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation Strategy (Low Hormones)
With estrogen and progesterone at their lowest, focus on rest, gentle movement (like walking or restorative yoga), and iron-rich foods to replenish lost blood.

### 2. Follicular Strategy (Rising Estrogen)
As estrogen rises, energy and creativity often increase. This is an excellent time for brainstorming, starting new projects, and engaging in more intense workouts.

### 3. Ovulation Strategy (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
Estrogen peaks, and testosterone may slightly rise. You might feel your most sociable and energetic. Capitalize on this for important meetings, social events, and high-intensity interval training (HIIT).

### 4. Early Luteal Strategy (Rising Progesterone)
Progesterone takes over, which has a calming, sometimes sedating effect. Focus on steady-state cardio, strength training, and tasks that require detail-oriented focus.

### 5. Late Luteal Strategy (Dropping Hormones)
As both hormones drop (if no pregnancy occurs), PMS can set in. Prioritize self-care, sleep, complex carbohydrates, and boundary-setting.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Irregular or absent periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, high stress, low body weight. |
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Extreme sensitivity to normal hormonal drops. |
| Heavy, painful periods | Endometriosis, fibroids, estrogen dominance. |
| Persistent cyclical acne | Elevated androgens (testosterone) or sensitivity to hormonal shifts. |
