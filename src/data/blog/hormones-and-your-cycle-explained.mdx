## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormonal awareness is crucial for understanding changes in mood, energy, and reproductive health. By recognizing how hormones fluctuate, you can better manage symptoms and optimize your lifestyle choices throughout the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You can infer hormonal changes by tracking physical and emotional symptoms alongside your cycle dates using [LunaCycle](/). For example, a rise in basal body temperature indicates the progesterone dominance of the luteal phase.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstrual Phase (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest, hydration, and gentle movement.

### Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on increasing energy with more intense workouts and creative projects.

### Ovulation (Peak Estrogen & LH)
Engage in social activities and high-energy tasks. This is your most fertile time.

### Early Luteal Phase (High Progesterone)
Focus on steady-state activities and organization.

### Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
Practice self-care to manage PMS symptoms as estrogen and progesterone levels fall.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Sign | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Irregular or missing periods | Low estrogen or PCOS |
| Severe PMS or PMDD | Estrogen dominance or low progesterone |
| Heavy, painful periods | Excess estrogen |
