Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormonal awareness matters because these chemical messengers control more than just your period. They influence your mood, energy levels, metabolism, and overall reproductive health. By understanding these fluctuations, you can anticipate changes in how you feel and function throughout the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure exact hormone levels at home without tests, you can track their effects:
* **Log Symptoms:** Keep detailed notes on mood, energy, and physical symptoms using [LunaCycle](/tracker).
* **Observe Physical Signs:** Monitor cervical mucus changes and basal body temperature shifts, which are direct results of hormonal actions.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase Rest
Hormones are low. Prioritize rest, gentle movement, and iron-rich foods.

### 2. Follicular Phase Energy Utilization
As estrogen rises, harness the increased energy for challenging tasks and higher-intensity workouts.

### 3. Ovulation Phase Socializing
Peak estrogen often brings peak sociability and communication skills.

### 4. Luteal Phase Winding Down
As progesterone rises, focus on completion tasks, self-care, and stress management to mitigate PMS.

### 5. Managing Stress
Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which can suppress the production of reproductive hormones, disrupting your cycle.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Irregular or missing periods | PCOS, stress, thyroid issues |
| Severe PMS or PMDD | Estrogen dominance or rapid progesterone drop |
| Unexplained weight gain | PCOS, thyroid issues, insulin resistance |
| Persistent acne | High androgens (common in PCOS) |
