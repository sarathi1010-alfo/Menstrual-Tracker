Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your mood, energy levels, and overall reproductive health are heavily influenced by your hormones. Awareness allows you to work with your body rather than against it.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

Use [LunaCycle](/) to log daily symptoms like mood, energy, and physical changes, which can provide insights into your hormonal fluctuations.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstrual Phase Rest
Focus on rest and gentle movement while hormones are low.

### Follicular Phase Energy
Capitalize on rising estrogen with high-intensity workouts.

### Ovulation Peak
Utilize peak communication and energy levels.

### Luteal Phase Wind Down
Transition to lower-impact activities as progesterone rises.

### Pre-menstrual Care
Prioritize self-care and nutrient-dense foods.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Sign | Possible Issue |
| ---- | -------------- |
| Severe acne | Androgen excess |
| Irregular periods | PCOS or thyroid issues |
