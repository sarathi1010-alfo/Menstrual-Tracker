## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is driven by a delicate balance of hormones. Understanding the roles of estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) can demystify the physical and emotional changes you experience throughout the month. This awareness is vital for managing mood, energy, and reproductive health.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests provide precise measurements, you can infer hormonal changes by tracking symptoms and cycle length. Observing physical signs like cervical mucus consistency and basal body temperature can offer clues about your hormonal shifts.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstruation
Prioritize rest and gentle movement to support your body during this phase.

### Follicular Phase
Take advantage of rising energy levels by tackling demanding tasks or intense workouts.

### Ovulation
Embrace social activities and communication, as estrogen peaks.

### Luteal Phase (Early)
Focus on steady energy and routine tasks.

### Luteal Phase (Late)
Practice self-care and stress management to navigate potential PMS symptoms.
