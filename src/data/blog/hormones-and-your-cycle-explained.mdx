## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

The menstrual cycle is a complex symphony of hormones. The primary players—Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), Estrogen, Luteinizing Hormone (LH), and Progesterone—dictate not just your reproductive health, but also your mood, energy, and overall well-being. Understanding these hormones helps you make sense of the physical and emotional changes you experience throughout the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure exact hormone levels without medical tests, you can track their effects. Using a tool like [LunaCycle](/), log your daily symptoms, energy levels, and physical changes (like cervical mucus and basal body temperature) to infer which hormones are dominating your cycle at any given time.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Rest and focus on gentle movements. Eat iron-rich foods to replenish lost stores.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on rising energy and focus. This is a great time for challenging workouts and starting new projects.

### 3. Ovulation (Estrogen & LH Peak)
Your energy and social drive are likely at their highest. Engage in high-intensity workouts and social activities.

### 4. Early Luteal (Rising Progesterone)
As progesterone rises, you may feel calmer. Focus on strength training and steady-state cardio.

### 5. Late Luteal (Falling Hormones)
Energy drops as hormones decline. Prioritize self-care, yoga, and magnesium-rich foods to ease PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Sign | Potential Cause |
|------|-----------------|
| Irregular periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, stress |
| Severe PMS / PMDD | Exaggerated response to normal hormonal changes |
| Heavy bleeding | Estrogen dominance, fibroids |
| Fatigue and hair loss | Thyroid imbalances, low iron |
