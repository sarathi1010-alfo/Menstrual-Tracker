## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones do more than just regulate your period; they influence your mood, metabolism, energy levels, and reproductive health. Being aware of how estrogen and progesterone fluctuate throughout your cycle empowers you to understand why you feel a certain way and helps you make informed choices about your diet, exercise, and lifestyle.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure your exact hormone levels at home daily, you can track the symptoms they produce. By consistently logging your mood, energy, skin condition, and physical symptoms in a tracker like [LunaCycle](/), you create a personal map of your hormonal fluctuations.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest, hydration, and gentle movement. Iron-rich foods can help replenish what is lost during bleeding.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on rising energy levels. This is a great time for challenging workouts, starting new projects, and socializing.

### 3. Ovulation (Estrogen & LH Peak)
You may feel your best now. It's an optimal time for high-intensity exercise and important communications, as energy and mood often peak.

### 4. Early Luteal (Rising Progesterone)
As progesterone rises, you may feel more relaxed or slightly more tired. Shift towards steady-state cardio and focus on completing tasks.

### 5. Late Luteal (Hormones Dropping)
Prepare for potential PMS. Prioritize self-care, sleep, and complex carbohydrates to stabilize blood sugar and mood.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Indication |
| :--- | :--- |
| Irregular or missed periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress |
| Severe pelvic pain | Endometriosis or fibroids |
| Extreme mood swings/depression | PMDD (Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder) |
