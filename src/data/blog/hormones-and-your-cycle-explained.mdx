## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your cycle is a complex symphony of hormones. Understanding the key players—Estrogen, Progesterone, Luteinizing Hormone (LH), and Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH)—helps you decode the signals your body sends every day. Knowing when certain hormones peak and drop explains fluctuations in your mood, energy, and physical symptoms, allowing for greater self-compassion and better health management. Learn more about the phases they control in [Cycle Phases Explained](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal).

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't see your hormones, you can track their effects. By logging your symptoms—like acne, mood swings, breast tenderness, or changes in cervical mucus—in a private tool like [LunaCycle](/), you can start to see patterns that correspond with your hormonal shifts.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. The Menstrual Phase (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest and gentle movement. Iron-rich foods can help replenish what's lost during bleeding.

### 2. The Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on increasing energy for challenging workouts or new projects.

### 3. Ovulation (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
This is typically when energy and sociability are highest.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
As progesterone rises, you may feel more inward-focused. It's a good time for steady-state cardio and organization.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
To manage PMS, prioritize sleep, hydration, and stress reduction as hormones drop sharply.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Extremely heavy or painful periods | Estrogen dominance |
| Very short luteal phase | Low progesterone |
| Irregular or missing periods | High androgens (e.g., PCOS) |
| Severe mood swings (PMDD) | Sensitivity to normal hormone fluctuations |
