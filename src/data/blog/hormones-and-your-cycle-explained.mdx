## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters
Awareness of your hormones matters for managing mood, energy, and overall reproductive health. When you know why you feel a certain way, it’s easier to give your body what it needs.

For a foundational understanding, read [Menstrual Cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide) and explore our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide).

## How to Track Hormonal Changes
You can't test your hormones at home every day, but using symptoms and cycle logging in apps like [LunaCycle](/) provides powerful proxy data. By tracking mood, skin changes, and energy levels, you map your hormonal landscape.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies
### 1. Menstrual Phase Rest
Focus on restorative activities when hormones drop.

### 2. Follicular Phase Fuel
Eat fresh, vibrant foods as estrogen builds.

### 3. Ovulatory Phase Energy
Utilize the estrogen peak for high-intensity activities.

### 4. Luteal Phase Support
Incorporate complex carbs to support progesterone production. Learn more about the [Luteal Phase](/what-is-luteal-phase).

### 5. Stress Management
Keep cortisol in check, as high stress disrupts reproductive hormones.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs
| Symptom | Potential Meaning |
|---|---|
| Unpredictable cycles | General hormonal imbalance or anovulation |
| Extreme PMS | Estrogen dominance or low progesterone |
