## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is entirely driven by a complex interplay of hormones. When you understand what hormones like estrogen and progesterone are doing during different phases of your cycle, the physical and emotional changes you experience begin to make sense. This awareness empowers you to advocate for your reproductive health and make informed lifestyle choices that support hormonal balance.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you cannot measure exact hormone levels at home without specialized tests, you can track their *effects*.

Use [LunaCycle](/) to log:
*   **Energy levels:** High energy often correlates with rising estrogen.
*   **Mood:** Irritability or sadness might align with the progesterone drop before your period.
*   **Physical symptoms:** Breast tenderness, bloating, or skin changes.
*   **Cervical mucus:** Changes in discharge are direct responses to estrogen and progesterone.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest, gentle movement like yoga, and iron-rich foods to replenish what is lost during bleeding.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on rising energy levels. This is a great time for more intense workouts, starting new projects, and socializing.

### 3. Ovulation (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
You may feel your best right now. Focus on communication and challenging physical activities.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
Progesterone has a calming effect. Transition to steady-state cardio and focus on completing existing tasks rather than starting new ones.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
As hormones drop to trigger your period, prioritize self-care, reduce caffeine and sugar to manage PMS, and favor light exercise.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Dropping progesterone, estrogen dominance. |
| Irregular or absent periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, high stress (cortisol). |
| Heavy or painful periods | High estrogen levels, fibroids. |
| Unexplained weight gain | Insulin resistance, thyroid imbalance. |
