## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is an intricate dance orchestrated by hormones. Awareness of these hormonal fluctuations is crucial for understanding changes in your mood, energy, and reproductive health. When you know which hormone is dominant, you can better interpret your body's signals, from the burst of energy mid-cycle to the need for rest before your period.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure exact hormone levels at home without specialized tests, you can track their effects using apps like [LunaCycle](/). By logging your symptoms, mood, and physical signs (like cervical mucus or basal body temperature), you create a map of your hormonal activity.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation Strategy
*Focus on Rest.* Hormone levels are at their lowest. Prioritize gentle movement, rest, and nourishing foods.

### 2. Early Follicular Strategy
*Focus on Planning.* As estrogen slowly begins to rise, clarity and creativity often increase. It's a good time for planning and starting new projects.

### 3. Late Follicular / Ovulation Strategy
*Focus on High-Energy Tasks.* Estrogen peaks and testosterone briefly rises. You may feel your most social and energetic.

### 4. Early Luteal Strategy
*Focus on Completion.* Progesterone starts to rise, promoting a sense of calm. Focus on wrapping up tasks rather than starting new ones.

### 5. Late Luteal (Pre-menstrual) Strategy
*Focus on Self-Care.* Both estrogen and progesterone drop. Energy may wane, and you might prefer solitude. Prioritize self-care and stress reduction.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Very heavy or prolonged periods | Estrogen dominance |
| Short luteal phase / pre-menstrual spotting | Low progesterone |
| Severe acne or unusual hair growth | High androgens (like testosterone) |
| Missing or highly irregular periods | Various (PCOS, thyroid issues, high stress) |
