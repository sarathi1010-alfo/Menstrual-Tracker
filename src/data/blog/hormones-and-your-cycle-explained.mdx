## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormonal awareness matters for your mood, energy, and overall reproductive health. When you know which hormone is dominant, you can better understand why you might feel energetic one week and fatigued the next. Dive deeper into tracking in our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide).

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You can't see hormones, but you can see their effects. By logging symptoms like breast tenderness, mood swings, acne, and energy levels in [LunaCycle](/), you create a picture of your hormonal landscape.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase Rest
Focus on rest and recovery when all hormone levels are at their lowest.

### 2. Follicular Phase Energy
Utilize rising estrogen levels to tackle challenging tasks and intense workouts.

### 3. Ovulation Socialization
Estrogen peaks here; it's a great time for socializing and communication.

### 4. Luteal Phase Preparation
As progesterone rises, shift towards lower-intensity workouts and preparation for your period.

### 5. PMS Mitigation
Acknowledge the hormone drop at the end of the luteal phase and practice self-care.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Issue |
|---------|-----------------|
| Irregular periods | PCOS or thyroid issues |
| Severe pelvic pain | Endometriosis |
| Excessive hair growth | High androgens |
| Intense mood swings | PMDD |
