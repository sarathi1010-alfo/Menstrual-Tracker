## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormones govern everything from your mood to your energy and reproductive health. Being aware of how estrogen and progesterone fluctuate throughout your cycle can explain sudden shifts in how you feel, allowing you to give yourself grace during low-energy days.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure hormones directly without blood tests, you can track their effects by logging symptoms, mood, and physical changes in an app like [LunaCycle](/tracker).

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase Rest
Focus on restorative activities and rest as hormone levels are at their lowest.

### 2. Follicular Phase Energy
Take advantage of rising estrogen by tackling difficult projects and high-intensity workouts.

### 3. Ovulatory Phase Socializing
Peak estrogen makes you more outgoing and communicative.

### 4. Luteal Phase Winding Down
As progesterone rises, focus on self-care and gentle movement to manage PMS.

### 5. Managing Pre-Menstrual Drops
Eat nutrient-dense foods and prioritize sleep to mitigate the effects of dropping hormone levels.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Cause |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Severe Acne** | Often linked to excess androgens or hormonal fluctuations. |
| **Intense Mood Swings** | Rapid changes in estrogen and progesterone. |
| **Irregular Periods** | Can be caused by PCOS, stress, or thyroid issues. |
| **Excessive Fatigue** | Often a sign of low progesterone or thyroid imbalance. |
