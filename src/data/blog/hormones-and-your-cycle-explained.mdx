## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

For a deeper dive, check out our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) and learn more about [Cycle Phases Explained](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal) and [Menstrual Cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

Your cycle is a hormonal dance. Understanding the key players—Estrogen, Progesterone, Luteinizing Hormone (LH), and Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH)—helps you understand why you feel the way you do throughout the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests provide exact levels, you can infer hormonal changes by tracking your symptoms, mood, and basal body temperature with a tool like [LunaCycle](/).

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase
Rest and recharge. Your hormone levels are at their lowest.
### 2. Follicular Phase
Take on new projects. Rising estrogen boosts energy and creativity.
### 3. Ovulation Phase
Socialize and connect. High estrogen and testosterone make you feel your most outgoing.
### 4. Early Luteal Phase
Focus on detail-oriented tasks as progesterone begins to rise.
### 5. Late Luteal Phase
Prioritize self-care. Dropping hormones can lead to fatigue and PMS.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Severe PMS | High estrogen/low progesterone |
| Irregular cycles | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress |
| Heavy bleeding | Estrogen dominance |
