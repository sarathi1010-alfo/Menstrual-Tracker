## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Understanding the hormones that govern your menstrual cycle is key to understanding your own body. Estrogen, progesterone, Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), and Luteinizing Hormone (LH) work together in a complex, carefully timed symphony. When these hormones are balanced, you generally feel well. When they fluctuate or fall out of balance, they can dramatically affect your mood, energy levels, and reproductive health.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure your hormone levels directly without a blood test, you can track the *effects* of these hormones. By consistently logging your symptoms, mood, and physical signs (like basal body temperature or cervical mucus) in [LunaCycle](/), you can begin to see patterns that correspond with expected hormonal shifts.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. The Menstrual Phase (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest, gentle movement, and iron-rich foods to replenish what is lost during bleeding.

### 2. The Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Take advantage of rising energy levels for more intense workouts and creative projects.

### 3. The Ovulatory Phase (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
This is often when communication and social energy are highest. Capitalize on this for important meetings or social events.

### 4. The Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
As progesterone rises, you may feel a calming effect. Focus on sustained energy tasks and strength training.

### 5. The Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
Prepare for potential PMS symptoms. Prioritize sleep, stress management, and complex carbohydrates.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Unexplained weight gain | Potential thyroid issue or PCOS |
| Severe acne, excess hair growth | High androgens (often seen in PCOS) |
| Very heavy or painful periods | Estrogen dominance or endometriosis |
| Short cycles or spotting | Low progesterone |
