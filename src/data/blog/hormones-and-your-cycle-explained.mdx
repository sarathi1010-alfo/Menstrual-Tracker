Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

<MedicalDisclaimer />

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones are chemical messengers that affect almost every system in your body. Understanding how they fluctuate throughout your menstrual cycle is vital because it impacts:
- **Mood and Emotion:** Fluctuations can cause everything from euphoria to irritability.
- **Energy Levels:** Hormones dictate when you feel energized and when you need rest.
- **Reproductive Health:** They are the drivers of fertility and the menstrual cycle itself.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure exact hormone levels without a blood test, you can track the *symptoms* they cause. Using a tool like [LunaCycle](/), you can log:
- **Basal Body Temperature (BBT):** A sustained rise indicates that ovulation has occurred and progesterone is present.
- **Cervical Mucus:** Changes in texture and volume correspond to rising estrogen and the approach of ovulation.
- **Mood and Physical Symptoms:** Logging these helps you anticipate the effects of hormonal shifts.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
With estrogen and progesterone at their lowest, focus on rest, gentle movement like yoga, and iron-rich foods to replenish what is lost during bleeding.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
As estrogen rises, you may feel more energetic and social. This is a great time for challenging workouts, brainstorming, and starting new projects.

### 3. Ovulation (Estrogen Peak & LH Surge)
At peak estrogen, energy and libido are often highest. It's the optimal time for high-intensity exercise and important social or professional events.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
Progesterone has a calming effect. Transition to moderate exercise and focus on completing tasks. Prioritize complex carbohydrates to support serotonin production.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
As hormones drop before your period, energy wanes. Prioritize sleep, self-care, and stress management to mitigate PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Hormone | Sign of Imbalance | Potential Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Estrogen (Too High)** | Heavy periods, severe cramps, mood swings. | Exacerbated PMS, weight gain. |
| **Estrogen (Too Low)** | Light periods, hot flashes, vaginal dryness. | Irregular cycles, low libido. |
| **Progesterone (Too Low)** | Spotting before period, anxiety, sleep issues. | Short luteal phase, fertility challenges. |
| **Androgens (Too High)** | Acne, excessive facial hair, irregular cycles. | Often associated with PCOS. |
