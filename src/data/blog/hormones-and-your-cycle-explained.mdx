Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals and helps you understand the 'why' behind how you feel throughout the month.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones do more than just regulate your period; they affect your energy, metabolism, mood, and sleep. Understanding these fluctuations allows you to work with your body rather than against it, optimizing your reproductive health and overall well-being.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure exact hormone levels at home without tests, you can track the symptoms they produce. For example, increased cervical mucus indicates rising estrogen, while a post-ovulation temperature shift indicates rising progesterone. Use [LunaCycle](/) to log these daily symptoms privately.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest and gentle movement. Keep warm and hydrate.
### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on rising energy with more intense workouts and creative tasks.
### 3. Ovulation (Peak Estrogen & LH Surge)
This is typically when energy and sociability are highest.
### 4. Early Luteal (Rising Progesterone)
Focus on steady tasks. Progesterone can have a calming effect.
### 5. Late Luteal (Dropping Hormones)
Prepare for your period. Practice self-care to manage potential PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Sign | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Severe cramps | Elevated prostaglandins |
| Irregular/missed periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress |
| Heavy bleeding | Estrogen dominance |
