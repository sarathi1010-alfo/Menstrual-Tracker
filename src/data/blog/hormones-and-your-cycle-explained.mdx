## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones are chemical messengers that impact your mood, energy, appetite, and reproductive health. Understanding their fluctuations helps you make sense of how you feel throughout the month.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests provide exact levels, you can infer hormonal shifts by tracking symptoms in a tool like [LunaCycle](/). Note changes in energy, skin, mood, and cervical mucus.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Days 1-5)
**Hormones:** Estrogen and progesterone are low.
**Strategy:** Focus on rest, hydration, and gentle movement.

### 2. Early Follicular (Days 6-10)
**Hormones:** Estrogen begins to rise.
**Strategy:** Capitalize on increasing energy for brainstorming and socializing.

### 3. Late Follicular / Ovulation (Days 11-14)
**Hormones:** Estrogen peaks; LH surges.
**Strategy:** Great time for high-intensity workouts and important conversations.

### 4. Early Luteal (Days 15-21)
**Hormones:** Progesterone rises; estrogen dips then rises slightly.
**Strategy:** Focus on tasks requiring focus and organization.

### 5. Late Luteal (Days 22-28)
**Hormones:** Both estrogen and progesterone drop.
**Strategy:** Prioritize self-care, as PMS symptoms may appear.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Hormonal Issue |
| :--- | :--- |
| Severe acne | High androgens (like testosterone) |
| Extreme fatigue | Low thyroid or imbalances in estrogen/progesterone |
| Irregular periods | PCOS, stress, or thyroid issues |
