## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters
Hormones are the chemical messengers that control your menstrual cycle. Being aware of their natural ebbs and flows is crucial for understanding changes in your mood, energy, sleep patterns, and overall reproductive health.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes
While you can't measure your hormone levels directly without a blood or urine test, you can track the *symptoms* of their fluctuations. Logging your daily mood, physical symptoms (like breast tenderness or acne), and energy levels in an app like LunaCycle helps you map these hormonal shifts.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest, gentle movement, and iron-rich foods.

### Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Utilize the increasing energy for challenging workouts or starting new projects.

### Ovulation (Peak Estrogen & LH Surge)
Enjoy peak energy and sociability; a great time for high-intensity activities.

### Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
Shift towards moderate exercise, focus on completing tasks, and prioritize stress management as energy may dip.

### Late Luteal (Dropping Hormones)
Practice self-care to manage potential PMS symptoms; prioritize sleep and healthy comfort foods.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs
| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Severe, debilitating cramps | High prostaglandins or other conditions like endometriosis. |
| Consistently irregular cycles | Often linked to PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress. |
| Extreme mood swings/PMDD | Sensitivity to normal hormone fluctuations. |
| Hormonal cystic acne | Often related to androgen levels. |
