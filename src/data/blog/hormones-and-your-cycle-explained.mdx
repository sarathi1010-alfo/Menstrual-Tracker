Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones affect everything from your mood and energy to your reproductive health and metabolism. Understanding the hormonal shifts throughout your cycle empowers you to manage symptoms, optimize your lifestyle, and recognize when something might be amiss.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests provide exact measurements, you can track hormonal changes daily by logging your physical and emotional symptoms in [LunaCycle](/). Paying attention to energy levels, mood shifts, and physical signs like breast tenderness can provide clues about your hormonal landscape.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase Rest
Focus on restorative activities and rest as hormone levels drop.

### 2. Follicular Phase Activity
Take advantage of rising estrogen by engaging in more strenuous exercise and new projects.

### 3. Ovulation Energy
Utilize peak estrogen and testosterone for social activities and important tasks.

### 4. Luteal Phase Calm
As progesterone rises, shift towards lower-impact activities and prioritize stress management.

### 5. Premenstrual Care
Support your body through the hormone drop with self-care, good nutrition, and adequate sleep.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---------|---------------------|
| Irregular or missed periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress affecting various hormones. |
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Extreme sensitivity to normal hormonal fluctuations. |
| Excessive hair growth/acne | Elevated androgens (like testosterone). |
