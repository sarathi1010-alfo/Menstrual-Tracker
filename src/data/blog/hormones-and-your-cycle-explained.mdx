## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

For mood, energy, and reproductive health, understanding hormones is essential. They are the chemical messengers that tell your body what to do and when to do it.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You can track hormonal changes indirectly using symptoms and cycle logging. For example, breast tenderness often indicates rising progesterone, while a spike in energy might correlate with rising estrogen.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase Rest
Focus on light movement and rest while hormone levels are at their lowest.

### 2. Follicular Phase Energy
Capitalize on rising estrogen with more intense workouts or demanding tasks.

### 3. Ovulation Peak
Your communication skills and energy may be highest here; it's a great time for socializing.

### 4. Luteal Phase Winding Down
As progesterone rises, transition to calming activities and prioritize sleep.

### 5. Late Luteal Care
Support your body with nutrient-dense foods to help manage PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Sign | Potential Meaning |
|------|-------------------|
| Severe acne along jawline | Potential androgen excess |
| Extremely heavy periods | Possible estrogen dominance |
| Very short luteal phase | Potential low progesterone |
| Missing periods entirely | Could indicate PCOS or stress (hypothalamic amenorrhea) |
