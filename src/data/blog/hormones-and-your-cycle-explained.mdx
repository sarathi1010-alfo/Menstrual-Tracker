## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your cycle is a complex hormonal dance. Understanding this interplay is essential because these hormones don't just control reproduction; they significantly impact your mood, energy levels, metabolism, and overall reproductive health.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests provide exact levels, you can track the *effects* of hormonal changes daily. Using [LunaCycle](/), log physical symptoms, mood shifts, and energy levels. Over several months, patterns tied to your hormonal fluctuations will emerge.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest and gentle activities like stretching or yoga. Your body needs recovery time as hormone levels are at their lowest.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on increasing energy. This is a great time for more intense workouts, tackling challenging tasks, and socializing.

### 3. Ovulation (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
Your energy and communication skills are often at their highest. It's an optimal time for important meetings or intense physical activity.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
Transition to steady-state exercises. Progesterone has a calming effect, making this a good time for focused, independent work.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
Prioritize self-care and stress management as dropping hormones can lead to PMS symptoms. Listen to your body and rest if needed.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Estrogen/Progesterone imbalance |
| Very heavy or painful periods | Estrogen dominance |
| Irregular or missing periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress |
| Unexplained weight changes | Thyroid or insulin issues |
