## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is driven by a complex dance of hormones. Understanding this interplay is essential for managing your mood, energy, and reproductive health. When you know which hormone is dominant at what time, you can better understand why you feel energized one week and fatigued the next.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests provide exact levels, you can infer hormonal shifts by tracking symptoms and cycle days in [LunaCycle](/). Note changes in your skin, energy, mood, and digestion. These physical signs are direct reflections of your underlying hormonal activity.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstrual Phase (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest, hydration, and gentle movement as estrogen and progesterone drop.

### Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on rising energy to tackle complex tasks or intense workouts.

### Ovulation (Peak Estrogen & LH)
Your communication skills and social energy are likely at their highest. Schedule important meetings or social events.

### Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
As progesterone rises, you may feel more inward-focused and calm. Ideal for organizing and detail-oriented tasks.

### Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
Practice self-care to mitigate PMS symptoms caused by dropping hormone levels.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
| --- | --- |
| Severe PMS / PMDD | Estrogen dominance or rapid progesterone drop |
| Irregular/Absent Periods | Low estrogen, high androgens (e.g., PCOS), or thyroid issues |
| Mid-cycle spotting | Estrogen drop around ovulation |
