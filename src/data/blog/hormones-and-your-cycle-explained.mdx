Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormonal awareness is crucial for understanding fluctuations in your mood, energy levels, and overall reproductive health.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You can track hormonal changes indirectly by logging your daily symptoms, mood, and cycle events using [LunaCycle](/).

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstruation
Focus on rest and gentle movement as hormone levels are low.

### Follicular Phase
Take advantage of rising estrogen for higher energy tasks and workouts.

### Ovulation
Capitalize on peak estrogen and testosterone for social and communicative activities.

### Early Luteal Phase
Transition to more steady activities as progesterone begins to rise.

### Late Luteal Phase
Prioritize self-care and stress management as hormones decline before menstruation.
