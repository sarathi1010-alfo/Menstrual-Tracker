## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones act as chemical messengers, orchestrating the complex dance of your menstrual cycle. Awareness of these hormones matters because they do not just influence reproduction; they affect your brain chemistry, metabolism, and immune system. By understanding when specific hormones rise and fall, you can decode your body&apos;s signals, demystifying mood swings, cravings, and energy dips.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you cannot measure hormone levels at home without specialized tests, you can track their effects. Using [LunaCycle](/tracker), you can log daily symptoms that serve as proxies for hormonal activity. For example, charting an increase in clear cervical mucus points to rising estrogen, while tracking a post-ovulation temperature shift indicates progesterone production.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation Strategy (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest and gentle movement like yoga. Consume iron-rich foods to replenish lost blood.

### 2. Follicular Strategy (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on increasing energy by tackling difficult tasks or intense workouts.

### 3. Ovulation Strategy (Peak Estrogen & LH)
This is often a time of high confidence and sociability. Schedule important meetings or social events.

### 4. Early Luteal Strategy (Rising Progesterone)
As progesterone induces a calming effect, transition to steady-state cardio and focus on completing ongoing projects.

### 5. Late Luteal Strategy (Hormone Drop)
Prioritize self-care and stress reduction. Eat complex carbohydrates to stabilize blood sugar and mitigate PMS cravings.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---|---|
| Irregular or missing periods | Low estrogen, PCOS, or thyroid issues |
| Severe PMS or PMDD | Estrogen dominance or severe progesterone drop |
| Heavy or painful periods | High estrogen levels |
| Adult acne, especially on the jawline | High androgens (like testosterone) |
