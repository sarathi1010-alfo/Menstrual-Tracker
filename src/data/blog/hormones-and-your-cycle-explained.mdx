Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals and empowers you to make informed decisions about your health.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones do more than just regulate your period; they influence nearly every system in your body. From your mood and energy levels to your metabolism and sleep patterns, hormones are the master controllers. By understanding these hormonal shifts, you can better manage your daily life and identify when something might be off. For a beginner's look at the cycle as a whole, read [Menstrual Cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests provide precise measurements, you can track hormonal changes informally by observing your body's symptoms. Logging your mood, skin changes (like acne), energy levels, and physical sensations (like breast tenderness) using a tool like [LunaCycle](/) can reveal patterns that correlate with hormonal shifts. ## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest and recovery. Your body is working hard, and energy levels are typically at their lowest. Gentle exercise like walking or yoga is ideal.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
As estrogen rises, you may feel more energetic, creative, and outgoing. This is a great time to start new projects, socialize, and engage in higher-intensity workouts.

### 3. Ovulation (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
You are likely at your most energetic and sociable. Capitalize on this peak for important meetings or challenging physical activities.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
Progesterone has a calming effect. You might feel more inward-focused and relaxed. It's a good time for steady-state cardio and focused work tasks.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase (Hormone Drop)
As hormones drop before your period, you may experience PMS symptoms like mood swings or fatigue. Prioritize self-care, reduce caffeine, and ensure adequate sleep.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Severe, irregular periods** | PCOS, Thyroid issues, Perimenopause |
| **Intense PMS/PMDD** | Estrogen dominance, Low progesterone |
| **Excessive facial hair/acne** | High androgens (often associated with PCOS) |
| **Extreme fatigue/weight gain** | Hypothyroidism |
