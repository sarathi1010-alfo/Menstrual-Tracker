Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Understanding your hormones explains why you feel different throughout the month, aiding in mood management and overall reproductive health awareness.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

By logging symptoms like mood, energy, and physical changes in an app like LunaCycle, you can deduce your hormonal fluctuations over time.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstruation
Rest and hydrate as hormone levels drop.

### Follicular Phase
Utilize rising estrogen for high-energy tasks.

### Ovulation
Peak energy and sociability; use this to your advantage.

### Early Luteal Phase
Transition to lower-impact activities as progesterone rises.

### Late Luteal Phase (PMS)
Prioritize self-care and stress reduction.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Sign | Possible Issue |
|---|---|
| Severe acne | Androgen excess |
| Irregular periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or stress |
