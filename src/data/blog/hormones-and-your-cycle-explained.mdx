## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your cycle is driven by a complex interplay of hormones. Understanding how estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone (LH), and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) fluctuate can explain variations in your mood, energy, and overall health.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure your hormone levels directly at home without tests, you can track the symptoms they produce using an app like [LunaCycle](/). Note changes in your skin, energy, mood, and physical symptoms like breast tenderness.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstrual Phase: Rest
Focus on restorative activities as hormone levels are low.

### Follicular Phase: Energize
Take advantage of rising estrogen for challenging workouts and projects.

### Ovulation Phase: Connect
Peak estrogen and testosterone often boost sociability and energy.

### Early Luteal Phase: Wind Down
As progesterone rises, start transitioning to calmer activities.

### Late Luteal Phase: Self-Care
Prioritize self-care to manage potential PMS symptoms as hormones drop.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Sign | Potential Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Severe PMS/PMDD | Extreme sensitivity to normal hormonal shifts |
| Very irregular periods | Disruption in the delicate balance of cycle hormones |
