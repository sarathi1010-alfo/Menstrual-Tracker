## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Being aware of the hormones that govern your cycle empowers you to understand the physical and emotional changes you experience each month. It takes the mystery out of symptoms like bloating, mood swings, or energy crashes.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You can infer hormonal changes by tracking physical symptoms. For instance, increased cervical mucus often indicates rising estrogen, while a basal body temperature shift points to progesterone. Use [LunaCycle](/) to log these daily changes.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase Rest
Focus on rest and gentle movement while estrogen and progesterone are low.

### 2. Follicular Phase Energy
Leverage the energy boost from rising estrogen for more intense activities.

### 3. Ovulation Peak
Capitalize on peak estrogen for social activities and communication.

### 4. Luteal Phase Preparation
Prepare for the premenstrual drop in hormones with stress-management techniques.

### 5. Nutrition Support
Eat a balanced diet to support hormone production and metabolism throughout the cycle.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
|---------|---------------------|
| Severe PMS | High estrogen/Low progesterone |
| Irregular Cycles | PCOS or Thyroid issues |
| Heavy Bleeding | Estrogen dominance |
