## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormones are the chemical messengers that drive your menstrual cycle. Awareness of these hormonal shifts is crucial because they profoundly impact your mood, energy levels, and reproductive health. Understanding what estrogen and progesterone are doing can explain why you might feel energetic one week and fatigued the next.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you cannot measure exact hormone levels without a blood test, you can track their effects. Log your daily symptoms—like mood swings, breast tenderness, acne, or changes in cervical mucus—using a tool like [LunaCycle](/tracker). Over time, these symptom patterns will reflect your hormonal fluctuations.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstrual Phase (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest and light movement.

### Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on rising energy for challenging workouts or complex tasks.

### Ovulatory Phase (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
Great for social activities and communication.

### Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
Focus on steady energy tasks and balanced nutrition.

### Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
Prioritize self-care and stress management to mitigate PMS.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Imbalance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Very heavy or painful periods | Estrogen dominance |
| Short luteal phase (< 10 days) | Low progesterone |
| Irregular or absent periods | PCOS or thyroid issues |
