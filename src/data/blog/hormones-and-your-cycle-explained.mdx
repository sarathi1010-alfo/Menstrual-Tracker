## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Understanding the ebb and flow of your hormones is key to making sense of changes in your mood, energy, and overall reproductive health. When you know *why* you feel a certain way, you can better support your body's natural processes.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

You can't see hormones, but you can see their effects. By logging symptoms like breast tenderness, mood swings, changes in libido, and cervical mucus consistency, you are indirectly tracking your hormonal shifts.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Prioritize rest, hydration, and gentle movement like stretching or walking.

### Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on increasing energy for challenging workouts and creative projects.

### Ovulation (Peak Estrogen & LH)
This is often when you feel your best physically and socially. It is a great time for high-intensity activities.

### Early Luteal Phase (High Progesterone)
Focus on maintaining routine. You might feel calmer as progesterone exerts its relaxing effects.

### Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
Practice self-care to manage PMS symptoms. Opt for balanced meals to stabilize blood sugar.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Indicator |
| :--- | :--- |
| Severe, debilitating cramps | Endometriosis or fibroids (requires medical evaluation). |
| Unpredictable, skipped periods | PCOS, thyroid issues, or significant stress. |
| Extreme mood swings/depression | PMDD (Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder). |
| Unexplained weight changes | Thyroid dysfunction or insulin resistance. |
