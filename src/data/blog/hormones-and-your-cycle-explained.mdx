Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals.

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your hormones do much more than regulate your menstrual cycle; they influence nearly every system in your body. Hormonal awareness is key to understanding fluctuations in your mood, energy levels, and metabolism. By recognizing which hormones are dominant during different cycle phases, you can better manage your reproductive health, anticipate physical changes, and address symptoms before they become overwhelming.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure your hormone levels directly at home without tests, you can track the physical and emotional symptoms they cause. By consistently logging your mood, energy, physical symptoms (like acne or breast tenderness), and cervical fluid changes in [LunaCycle](/), you begin to see patterns that correlate with hormonal shifts. For example, a peak in energy often aligns with rising estrogen, while mood dips may follow a drop in progesterone.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
Focus on rest and gentle movement. Incorporate iron-rich foods to replenish lost nutrients.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Rising Estrogen)
Capitalize on increasing energy with more intense workouts or starting new projects.

### 3. Ovulation (Estrogen Peak, LH Surge)
This is typically a time of high energy and sociability. It's a great time for communication and strenuous exercise.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase (Rising Progesterone)
As progesterone rises, you may feel calmer. Focus on steady-state activities and organization.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase (Dropping Hormones)
If PMS occurs, prioritize self-care, reduce caffeine, and ensure adequate sleep.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Indication |
|---|---|
| Severe mood swings | Extreme fluctuations in estrogen/progesterone |
| Unexplained weight gain | Potential thyroid or insulin issues, common in PCOS |
| Irregular or missed periods | Disrupted ovulation, potential PCOS or stress |
| Excessive hair growth | Elevated androgens |

If you notice these signs, consult a healthcare provider.
