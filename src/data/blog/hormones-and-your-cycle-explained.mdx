## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Your menstrual cycle is essentially a complex hormonal dance. Being aware of how these hormones fluctuate can explain a lot about your daily life, from why you feel energetic and sociable one week to why you might crave rest and solitude the next. Understanding the roles of estrogen, progesterone, Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), and Luteinizing Hormone (LH) empowers you to work with your body rather than against it.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While blood tests provide the most accurate measure of hormone levels, you can estimate hormonal shifts by tracking physical and emotional symptoms. By logging your mood, energy, and physical signs (like cervical mucus or acne) using [LunaCycle](/), you can start to see patterns that align with specific [cycle phases](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal).

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstruation (Low Hormones)
With estrogen and progesterone at their lowest, focus on rest, gentle movement, and nutrient-dense foods to replenish iron.

### 2. Early Follicular (Rising Estrogen)
As estrogen begins to rise, you might feel a gradual return of energy. It's a great time to start new projects or engage in moderate exercise.

### 3. Late Follicular / Pre-Ovulation (Peak Estrogen)
High estrogen levels often bring peak energy, clear skin, and increased sociability. Maximize this time for intense workouts or social events.

### 4. Ovulation (LH Surge)
The brief LH surge triggers [ovulation](/what-is-ovulation). You may experience a slight temperature drop followed by a rise. Enjoy the lingering effects of high estrogen.

### 5. Luteal Phase (Progesterone Dominant)
Progesterone takes over, which has a calming effect but can also cause bloating or moodiness as it drops. Prioritize self-care, complex carbohydrates, and stress management.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Hormone | Sign of Imbalance |
|---------|-------------------|
| Estrogen (Too High) | Heavy periods, severe PMS, breast tenderness |
| Estrogen (Too Low) | Hot flashes, night sweats, irregular periods |
| Progesterone (Too Low) | Spotting before period, anxiety, sleep issues |
| Androgens (Too High) | Excess facial hair, severe acne, irregular cycles (often linked to [PCOS](/conditions/pcos-and-cycle-tracking-guide)) |
