Estrogen, progesterone, luteinizing hormone, and follicle-stimulating hormone orchestrate the menstrual cycle. Learning their roles demystifies your body's signals and helps you understand the foundation of [menstrual cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters

Hormones are chemical messengers that affect nearly every system in your body. Awareness of how they fluctuate throughout your cycle matters for managing mood, energy, and overall reproductive health. When you know which hormone is dominant, you can better anticipate and manage symptoms like fatigue, anxiety, or cravings.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes

While you can't measure exact hormone levels without a blood test, you can track the physical signs of their fluctuations by [logging your cycle](/blog/how-to-track-your-cycle-step-by-step). Use the [LunaCycle](/) app to record symptoms like changes in cervical mucus (driven by estrogen), basal body temperature shifts (driven by progesterone), and mood changes.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies

### 1. Menstrual Phase Rest
When estrogen and progesterone are at their lowest, prioritize rest, gentle movement, and iron-rich foods.

### 2. Follicular Phase Energy
As estrogen rises, you may feel more energetic. This is a good time for more intense workouts and starting new projects.

### 3. Ovulatory Window Focus
Estrogen peaks alongside an LH surge. You may feel your most social and communicative.

### 4. Early Luteal Phase Calm
As progesterone rises, transition to lower-intensity exercises and focus on completing tasks rather than starting new ones.

### 5. Late Luteal Phase Care
Before your period, progesterone and estrogen drop. Focus on self-care, stress reduction, and managing PMS symptoms.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs

| Symptom | Potential Indication |
| :--- | :--- |
| Severe Acne (Jawline) | Often related to androgen excess or estrogen dominance. |
| Irregular or Missed Periods | Can indicate issues with ovulation or conditions like PCOS. |
| Extreme Fatigue | May be related to low thyroid function or severe PMS. |
| Intense Mood Swings (PMDD) | Severe sensitivity to normal hormonal fluctuations. |

### Core Principles
*   Four main hormones drive the cycle: Estrogen, Progesterone, LH, and FSH.
*   Hormones impact mood, energy, and physical symptoms.
*   Tracking symptoms provides clues about hormonal health.
*   Lifestyle choices can help balance hormonal fluctuations.
*   Consult a doctor for persistent severe symptoms.
