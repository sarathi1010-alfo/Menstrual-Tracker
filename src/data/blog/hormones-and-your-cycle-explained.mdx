## Why Hormonal Awareness Matters
Hormones are the messengers that run the menstrual cycle. Understanding how estrogen and progesterone fluctuate can explain shifts in your mood, energy, and physical well-being. Using a tracker like [LunaCycle](/) helps correlate your feelings with these internal shifts.

## How to Track Hormonal Changes
While you can't see hormones, you can track their effects: basal body temperature, cervical mucus consistency, and mood changes are all outward signs of hormonal activity.

## Top 5 Hormonal Fluctuation Strategies
### Menstrual Phase Rest
Focus on restorative activities while hormone levels are low.
### Follicular Phase Energy
Take advantage of rising estrogen by tackling demanding tasks.
### Ovulatory Phase Communication
Peak estrogen and testosterone often make this a great time for socializing.
### Early Luteal Phase Productivity
Progesterone can have a calming, grounding effect.
### Late Luteal Phase Self-Care
As hormones drop, prioritize self-care to manage potential PMS.

## Common Hormonal Imbalance Signs
| Symptom | Potential Issue |
|---------|-----------------|
| Irregular periods | PCOS or thyroid issues |
| Severe mood swings | PMDD |
