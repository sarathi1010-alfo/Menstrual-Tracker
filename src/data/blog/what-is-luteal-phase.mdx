The luteal phase is the latter part of the menstrual cycle, occurring after ovulation and before the start of the next period, characterized by high progesterone levels.

## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?

The ruptured follicle becomes the corpus luteum, which secretes progesterone to thicken the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, the luteal phase typically spans days 15 to 28.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?

It prepares the body for potential implantation of a fertilized egg.

## Luteal Phase vs Follicular Phase

| Luteal Phase | Follicular Phase |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| Post-ovulation | Pre-ovulation |
| Progesterone dominant | Estrogen dominant |
