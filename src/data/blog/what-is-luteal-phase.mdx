## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?
After the egg is released, the empty follicle becomes the *corpus luteum*, which secretes progesterone. If the egg is not fertilized, the corpus luteum breaks down, progesterone drops, and menstruation begins.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle where ovulation happens on Day 14, the luteal phase spans Days 15 to 28. Symptoms like breast tenderness or mood changes (PMS) often occur here.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?
It is essential for early pregnancy support. Tracking its length with [LunaCycle](/) helps identify potential fertility issues.

## Luteal Phase vs Follicular Phase
| Feature | Luteal Phase | Follicular Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Post-ovulation | Pre-ovulation |
| Dominant Hormone | Progesterone | Estrogen |
| Goal | Support potential pregnancy | Mature an egg |
