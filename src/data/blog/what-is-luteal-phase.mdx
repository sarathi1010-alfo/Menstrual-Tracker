## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?

After an egg is released, the ruptured follicle transforms into the corpus luteum, which secretes progesterone. This hormone thickens the uterine lining to prepare for a fertilized egg.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, the luteal phase typically begins on Day 15 and ends on Day 28. During this time, a person might experience PMS symptoms like breast tenderness or mood changes due to fluctuating progesterone.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?

A sufficiently long luteal phase (at least 10-11 days) is necessary for a fertilized egg to successfully implant in the uterus.

## Luteal Phase vs Follicular Phase

| Feature | Luteal Phase | Follicular Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Timing** | Post-ovulation | Pre-ovulation |
| **Dominant Hormone** | Progesterone | Estrogen |
