## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?
After the egg is released, the ruptured follicle transforms into a structure called the corpus luteum. This structure secretes progesterone, which thickens and stabilizes the uterine lining, creating an ideal environment for a fertilized egg to implant.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle with ovulation on Day 14, the luteal phase spans from Day 15 to Day 28. If the egg is not fertilized, the corpus luteum breaks down, progesterone levels fall rapidly, and menstruation begins on Day 1 of the next cycle.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?
A sufficiently long and hormonally balanced luteal phase is vital for maintaining a pregnancy in its earliest stages. Imbalances here are also heavily associated with Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS).

## Menstrual Cycle vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~28 days | 12-14 days |
| Purpose | Full monthly preparation | Specific preparation for implantation |
| Key Hormone | Fluctuating Estrogen & Progesterone | Progesterone dominant |
