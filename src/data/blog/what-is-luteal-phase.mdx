## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?

After an egg is released, the empty follicle turns into the corpus luteum, which secretes progesterone. This hormone thickens the uterine lining. If the egg is not fertilized, the corpus luteum breaks down, progesterone drops, and menstruation begins.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle with ovulation on day 14, the luteal phase spans from day 15 to day 28. During this time, you might experience breast tenderness or mood changes.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?

A healthy luteal phase is essential for maintaining a pregnancy in its early stages. It's also the phase where premenstrual syndrome (PMS) occurs, so tracking it helps manage symptoms.

## Luteal Phase vs Follicular Phase

| Feature | Luteal Phase | Follicular Phase |
|---------|--------------|------------------|
| **Timing** | Post-ovulation | Pre-ovulation |
| **Dominant Hormone** | Progesterone | Estrogen |
| **Length** | Consistent (12-16 days) | Variable |
