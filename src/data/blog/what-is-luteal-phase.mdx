## What is the Luteal Phase?
The luteal phase is the second half of the menstrual cycle. It begins right after ovulation and ends the day before your next period starts, characterized by a rise in progesterone.

## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?
The ruptured follicle transforms into the corpus luteum, secreting progesterone to thicken the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, the luteal phase spans roughly Day 15 to Day 28.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?
A healthy luteal phase is necessary to sustain early pregnancy.

## Luteal Phase vs Follicular Phase
| Feature | Luteal Phase | Follicular Phase |
|---------|--------------|------------------|
| Timing | Post-ovulation | Pre-ovulation |
| Hormone | Progesterone | Estrogen |
