## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?

After releasing an egg, the empty follicle turns into the corpus luteum, which pumps out progesterone. This hormone thickens the uterine lining. If the egg isn't fertilized, the corpus luteum breaks down, progesterone drops, and menstruation begins.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle where ovulation happens on Day 14, the luteal phase runs from Day 15 to Day 28. Tracking these days in [LunaCycle](/tracker) helps predict your period.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?

A healthy luteal phase is vital for early pregnancy. It is also the phase responsible for Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) due to shifting hormone levels.

## Luteal Phase vs Follicular Phase

| Phase | Timing | Dominant Hormone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Luteal Phase | After ovulation | Progesterone |
| Follicular Phase | Before ovulation | Estrogen |
