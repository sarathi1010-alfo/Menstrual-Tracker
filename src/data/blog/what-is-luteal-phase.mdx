## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?

After releasing an egg, the empty follicle turns into the corpus luteum, which secretes progesterone. This hormone thickens the uterine lining. If the egg is not fertilized, the corpus luteum breaks down, progesterone drops, and menstruation begins.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle with ovulation on day 14, the luteal phase lasts from day 15 to day 28. During this time, many women experience premenstrual symptoms (PMS) due to hormonal shifts.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?

A healthy luteal phase is necessary for a fertilized egg to implant and grow. A phase shorter than 10 days (luteal phase defect) can make it difficult to maintain a pregnancy.

## Luteal Phase vs Follicular Phase

| Feature | Luteal Phase | Follicular Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Timing** | After ovulation | Before ovulation |
| **Dominant Hormone** | Progesterone | Estrogen |
| **Primary Function** | Prepare for implantation | Mature an egg |
