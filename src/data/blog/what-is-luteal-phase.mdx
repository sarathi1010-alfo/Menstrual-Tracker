## How Does the Luteal Phase Work?
The corpus luteum produces progesterone to thicken the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, it usually lasts from day 15 to day 28.

## Why is the Luteal Phase Important?
A healthy luteal phase is necessary to maintain an early pregnancy.

## Luteal Phase vs Follicular Phase
| Luteal Phase | Follicular Phase |
|---|---|
| Post-ovulation | Pre-ovulation |
