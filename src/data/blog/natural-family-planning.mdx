**Natural Family Planning (NFP)**, also known as Fertility Awareness-Based Methods (FABMs), is a way to manage contraception by tracking the body&apos;s natural signs of fertility. In an era where data privacy is increasingly under threat, many are choosing to use NFP in conjunction with privacy-first tools like [LunaCycle](/tracker).

## Why NFP Matters for Women in 2026

Many individuals are seeking alternatives to hormonal birth control due to side effects or personal health philosophies. NFP offers a way to avoid pregnancy by understanding exactly when you are fertile and either abstaining from intercourse or using a barrier method during that time.

## How to Practice NFP (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Choose a Specific Method
There are several established NFP protocols:
- **Sympto-Thermal Method:** Uses both BBT and cervical mucus.
- **Billings Ovulation Method:** Focuses exclusively on cervical mucus patterns.
- **Marquette Method:** Uses an electronic fertility monitor to track hormones in urine.

### 2. Learn the Rules of Your Method
Each method has specific rules for determining when the fertile window begins and ends. You must learn these rules from a certified instructor or a comprehensive manual.

### 3. Track Daily Symptoms
Every morning and throughout the day, you must observe and record your signs. Use [LunaCycle](/tracker) to maintain a private, digital record of these observations.

## Top Strategies for NFP Success

### Professional Instruction
The most common cause of NFP failure is improper application of the rules. Seeking guidance from a trained fertility awareness educator (FAE) is highly recommended.

### Consistency is Key
Missing just one or two days of temperature readings or mucus observations can make it impossible to determine your fertile status for that cycle.

### Conservative Decision Making
When in doubt about your fertility status, always assume you are fertile. This &quot;safety-first&quot; approach is essential for preventing unintended pregnancy.
