An **Ovulation Predictor Kit (OPK)** is an at-home test used to detect the surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH) in urine, which typically happens just before ovulation.

## How Does an OPK Work?
Your body produces a large amount of LH about 24 to 48 hours before you release an egg. The OPK uses antibodies to react with the LH in your urine. Most kits use a "test line" and a "control line"—the test line must be as dark as or darker than the control line to be considered "positive."

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, you might start testing on day 10. On day 14, you see a dark test line. This is your "surge," and you should expect to ovulate within the next day or two.

## Why is an OPK Important?
For those trying to conceive, an OPK provides a clear "green light" for timing intercourse during the most fertile part of the cycle.

## OPK vs. Pregnancy Test

| Feature | Ovulation Predictor Kit (OPK) | Pregnancy Test (HPT) |
|---------|------------------------------|----------------------|
| **Hormone Detected** | LH (Luteinizing Hormone) | hCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin) |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | After missed period |
| **Purpose** | Predict fertile window | Confirm conception |

*Medical Disclaimer: OPKs can give false positives in individuals with certain conditions like PCOS. Consult a doctor for personalized advice.*
