# What is an Ovulation Predictor Kit (OPK)?

An Ovulation Predictor Kit (OPK) is an at-home test used to determine when you are most fertile. They work by detecting a surge in **Luteinizing Hormone (LH)** in your urine.

## How Does an OPK Work?
Your brain sends a surge of LH to your ovaries to trigger the release of an egg. This "LH surge" typically happens 12 to 36 hours before ovulation. An OPK strip contains antibodies that react to LH, showing a dark line when the hormone level is high.

## Real-World Example
A user with a 28-day cycle starts testing with an OPK on Day 10. On Day 13, the test line is as dark as the control line (a positive result). They now know that they are in their peak fertile window and will likely ovulate on Day 14 or 15.

## Why is it Important?
- **Timing Intercourse:** For those trying to conceive, an OPK provides a clear "green light" for the best time to have intercourse.
- **Understanding Irregularity:** For those with irregular cycles, OPKs can help identify when ovulation is actually happening, regardless of what a calendar says.

## OPK vs. Pregnancy Test

| Feature | OPK | Pregnancy Test |
|---------|-----|----------------|
| Hormone Detected | LH (Luteinizing Hormone) | hCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin) |
| Timing | Middle of cycle | After missed period |
| Purpose | Predicting ovulation | Confirming pregnancy |

*Disclaimer: OPKs can give false positives in some conditions, such as PCOS. They are a tool, not a diagnosis.*
