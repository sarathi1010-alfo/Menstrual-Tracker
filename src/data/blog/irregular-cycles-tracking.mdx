## Understanding Irregular Cycles in 2026

For many women, the "28-day cycle" is a myth. In 2026, we recognize that cycle variability is common, but extreme irregularity is often a signal from your body that something needs attention. Whether it's stress, nutrition, or a condition like PCOS, your cycle is a vital sign of your overall health.

Tracking an irregular cycle can be frustrating if you use apps that rely on simple averages. **[LunaCycle](/tracker)** is designed to handle variability, allowing you to log what is actually happening rather than forcing you into a "standard" model.

## How Cycle Tracking Helps Manage Irregularity

1.  **Identifies the Root Cause:** By tracking symptoms alongside dates, you can see if your irregularity is linked to stress, travel, or illness.
2.  **Confirms Ovulation:** Even if your period is late, tracking your temperature can tell you if you've ovulated. If you haven't, you know the period isn't "late"—the ovulation was delayed.
3.  **Provides Data for Doctors:** Having 6 months of private, detailed cycle data in [LunaCycle](/tracker) allows your doctor to make a much more accurate diagnosis (e.g., distinguishing between PCOS and hypothalamic amenorrhea).
4.  **Reduces Anxiety:** Knowing *why* a period is late (because you tracked delayed ovulation) removes the "Am I pregnant?" or "Is something wrong?" stress.

## Quick Reference: Irregular Cycle Causes

| Condition | Common Cycle Pattern | Associated Symptoms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PCOS** | Very long cycles (35-90+ days) | Acne, hair growth, weight gain |
| **Stress/HA** | Missing or very late periods | Weight loss, high exercise, anxiety |
| **Thyroid Issues** | Very heavy or very light periods | Fatigue, cold intolerance, hair loss |
| **Perimenopause** | Unpredictable, changing lengths | Night sweats, mood shifts, sleep issues |

## What to Look for in Your Cycle Data

When reviewing your history in **[LunaCycle](/tracker)**, look for:
-   **The length of your Follicular Phase:** This is usually where the "irregularity" happens (delayed ovulation).
-   **The length of your Luteal Phase:** This should stay relatively consistent (10-16 days). If this varies wildly, it indicates a different type of hormonal issue.

**Medical Disclaimer:** Irregular cycles can be a symptom of various health conditions. Please use your [LunaCycle](/tracker) data to facilitate a conversation with a qualified healthcare professional.
