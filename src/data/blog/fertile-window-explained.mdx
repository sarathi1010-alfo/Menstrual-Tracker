Understanding the **fertile window** is the most critical step for anyone looking to conceive or understand their reproductive health. While many believe pregnancy can happen at any time, biology dictates a specific, narrow window of opportunity each month.

## Why the Fertile Window Matters for Conception

The fertile window is determined by the lifespan of both the sperm and the egg. In 2026, we have a clear scientific understanding of these timelines:
- **The Egg:** Once released during ovulation, the egg lives for only **12 to 24 hours**.
- **The Sperm:** Sperm can survive in the supportive environment of fertile cervical mucus for up to **5 days**.

Therefore, if you have intercourse 5 days before ovulation, sperm may still be present and viable when the egg is released. This 6-day span (5 days prior + the day of) constitutes your fertile window.

## How to Pinpoint Your Fertile Window (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Track Your Cycle Length
Use [LunaCycle](/tracker) to log your period start dates. Your cycle length is the number of days from the first day of one period to the day before the next.
- **Action:** Consistently log at least 3-6 cycles to find your average.

### 2. Estimate Ovulation
In a textbook 28-day cycle, ovulation often happens on Day 14. However, many people ovulate earlier or later.
- **Action:** Subtract 14 days from your average cycle length to find your approximate ovulation day.

### 3. Identify Peak Fertility Signs
Don&apos;t rely on calculations alone. Watch for:
- **Cervical Mucus:** Look for &quot;Egg White&quot; consistency.
- **LH Surge:** Use ovulation predictor kits to find the surge that precedes ovulation by 24-48 hours.
