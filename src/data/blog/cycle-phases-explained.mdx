## Why Understanding Cycle Phases Matters

Each phase of your cycle brings unique physiological and emotional changes. By understanding these phases, you can better predict your energy levels, mood shifts, and fertile windows. This knowledge allows you to 'sync' your lifestyle—including exercise and nutrition—with your body's natural rhythms.

## The Four Phases of Your Cycle

### 1. Menstruation (Days 1-5)
The cycle begins with menstruation, where the uterine lining is shed. This phase is characterized by low levels of estrogen and progesterone, often leading to lower energy.

### 2. The Follicular Phase (Days 1-13)
Overlapping with menstruation, this phase involves the maturation of follicles in the ovaries. As estrogen rises, you may feel an increase in energy and a brighter mood.

### 3. Ovulation (Day 14)
The climax of the follicular phase, ovulation is the release of a mature egg. This is the peak of fertility. Many people notice increased libido and physical energy during this brief window.

### 4. The Luteal Phase (Days 15-28)
After ovulation, the body produces progesterone. This phase is often associated with PMS symptoms like bloating or mood swings if pregnancy does not occur.
