Your cycle is a dynamic process. By understanding the four phases, you can better predict how you might feel throughout the month.

## 1. The Menstrual Phase
**Days 1-5 (approx.)**
The cycle begins with menstruation. This is when the uterine lining is shed because a pregnancy did not occur in the previous cycle. Estrogen and progesterone are at their lowest levels.

## 2. The Follicular Phase
**Days 1-13 (approx.)**
This phase starts on Day 1 and ends at ovulation. The brain releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH) to prepare eggs for release. Estrogen levels rise, which often increases energy and mood.

## 3. The Ovulatory Phase
**Day 14 (approx.)**
The climax of the follicular phase. A surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) causes the most mature egg to be released. This is the only time in the cycle when conception is possible.

## 4. The Luteal Phase
**Days 15-28 (approx.)**
After ovulation, the ruptured follicle transforms into the corpus luteum, which produces progesterone. This hormone thickens the uterine lining. If no pregnancy occurs, the corpus luteum dissolves, hormone levels drop, and the cycle starts over.
