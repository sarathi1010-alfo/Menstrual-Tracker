A period tracker app is a software application—either on a mobile device or a web browser—designed to help users record the start and end dates of their menstrual periods.

## How Does a Period Tracker Work?
Most apps use an algorithm to calculate the average length of your past cycles. By adding this average to the start date of your most recent period, the app can predict when your next period, fertile window, and ovulation day are likely to occur.

## Real-World Example of a Period Tracker
Using **LunaCycle**, you simply tap a date on the calendar to log your period. Over time, the app builds a "rhythm" of your unique cycle, showing you a visual map of your month so you can plan vacations, workouts, and events with confidence.

## Why is a Period Tracker Important?
Tracking your period is essential for reproductive health, but it's also a powerful general health tool. Changes in your cycle can be the first sign of stress, illness, or hormonal shifts. Furthermore, using a privacy-first app ensures that this sensitive data remains under your total control.
