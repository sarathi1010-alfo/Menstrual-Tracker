# What is PMDD?

**Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD)** is a severe form of premenstrual syndrome (PMS) that affects a small percentage of people of reproductive age. It is a serious medical condition that can cause significant distress and interfere with daily life, work, and relationships.

## How Does PMDD Work?

Unlike PMS, which can be uncomfortable, PMDD is often debilitating. It is thought to be an abnormal reaction to normal hormonal fluctuations. People with PMDD often have a higher sensitivity to the drop in estrogen and progesterone that happens before a period.

## Real-World Example of PMDD in Cycle Tracking

Someone with PMDD might use [LunaCycle](/tracker) to document intense emotional shifts. They might log &quot;extreme anxiety&quot; or &quot;feelings of hopelessness&quot; starting exactly 10 days before their period. Most importantly, they would notice those feelings disappear almost immediately once their period bleeding begins.

## Why is PMDD Important?

Recognition and diagnosis of PMDD are crucial because:
- It is often misdiagnosed as bipolar disorder or clinical depression.
- It requires specific treatment strategies that differ from standard PMS.
- Tracking is the **only** way to confirm the diagnosis (showing that symptoms are strictly tied to the luteal phase).

## PMDD vs. PMS

| Feature | PMS | PMDD |
|---------|-----|------|
| Severity | Moderate/Annoying | Severe/Debilitating |
| Mood | Irritability/Tearfulness | Intense anger/Suicidal thoughts |
| Physical | Bloating/Cramps | Similar, but secondary to mood |
| Resolution | Within a few days | Within 24-48 hours of period |
