# What is PMDD?

Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD) is a health condition similar to premenstrual syndrome (PMS) but is much more serious. It causes severe irritability, depression, or anxiety in the week or two before your period starts.

## How Does PMDD Work?

While researchers aren't exactly sure what causes PMDD, it is believed to be an extreme sensitivity to the normal hormonal fluctuations of the menstrual cycle. It is categorized as a depressive disorder in medical literature.

## Real-World Example of PMDD

A person with PMDD might feel fine for two weeks of the month, but then experience intense feelings of hopelessness, anger, or suicidal ideation that disappear almost immediately once their period arrives.

## Why is PMDD Important?

PMDD is a serious medical condition that can impact relationships and work. Because it is strictly tied to the cycle, maintaining a [[LunaCycle](/tracker) Daily Log](/tracker) of your mood is the only way for a doctor to distinguish it from other mental health conditions.

## PMDD vs. PMS

| Feature | PMS | PMDD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mood Impact** | Mild/Moderate | Severe/Disabling |
| **Duration** | 1-5 days | 7-14 days |
| **Frequency** | Common | ~5% of women |

### Core Principles
-   Symptoms follow a cyclical pattern.
-   Disappears during pregnancy and after menopause.
-   [LunaCycle](/tracker) history is vital for your healthcare provider.
-   Treatment may include therapy, lifestyle changes, or medication.
