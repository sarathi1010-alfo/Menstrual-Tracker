The menstrual cycle is divided into four phases, each with distinct hormonal changes and physical effects. Knowing them helps you understand your body's rhythm.

## Why Knowing Your Cycle Phases Matters

Knowing your phases helps optimize your lifestyle, diet, and exercise routines, while also being crucial for fertility tracking and health monitoring.

## How to Identify Each Phase

- **Menstruation:** Days 1-5 (approx). Bleeding occurs.
- **Follicular:** Days 1-13. Estrogen rises.
- **Ovulation:** Day 14 (approx). Egg is released.
- **Luteal:** Days 15-28. Progesterone dominates.

## Top 4 Cycle Phase Tracking Tools

### 1. Calendar
Tracking dates to estimate phase lengths.

### 2. BBT
Logging basal body temperature to confirm ovulation.

### 3. Cervical mucus
Monitoring changes in discharge to identify fertility.

### 4. Hormonal tests
Using ovulation predictor kits (OPKs) to detect hormone surges.

## Common Misconceptions About Phases

| Myth | Fact |
| ---- | ---- |
| Everyone ovulates on day 14 | Ovulation can occur on different days depending on cycle length |
| The luteal phase varies wildly | The luteal phase is usually a stable 12-14 days |
