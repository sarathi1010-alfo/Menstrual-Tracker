The menstrual cycle is divided into four phases, each with distinct hormonal changes and physical effects. Knowing them helps you understand your body's rhythm.

## Why Knowing Your Cycle Phases Matters

Awareness of your cycle phases helps you optimize your health, understand fertility signs, and adjust your lifestyle to match your natural rhythms.

## How to Identify Each Phase

- **Menstruation:** Days 1-5, shedding of the uterine lining.
- **Follicular:** Days 1-13 (overlaps with menstruation), estrogen rises.
- **Ovulation:** Day 14 (approx), release of an egg.
- **Luteal:** Days 15-28, progesterone rises to prepare for potential pregnancy.

## Top 4 Cycle Phase Tracking Tools

### 1. Calendar
Simple day-counting to estimate phases.

### 2. BBT
Basal Body Temperature tracking for identifying ovulation.

### 3. Cervical mucus
Observing changes to pinpoint the fertile window.

### 4. Hormonal tests
At-home kits to measure hormone levels directly.

## Common Misconceptions About Phases

| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ovulation always happens on day 14 | Ovulation varies greatly among women |
| The cycle is only about bleeding | The cycle is continuous hormonal changes |
