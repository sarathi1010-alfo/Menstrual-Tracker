## Why Knowing Your Cycle Phases Matters

Understanding the four phases of your menstrual cycle is key to optimizing your health, fertility, and daily lifestyle. By knowing what to expect in each phase, you can better manage symptoms and support your body.

## How to Identify Each Phase

*   **Menstruation:** The shedding of the uterine lining (Days 1-5).
*   **Follicular Phase:** Overlaps with menstruation and continues until ovulation. Estrogen rises.
*   **Ovulation:** The release of an egg from the ovary. Look for clear, stretchy cervical mucus.
*   **Luteal Phase:** Progesterone rises to support potential pregnancy. Often associated with PMS symptoms.

## Top 4 Cycle Phase Tracking Tools

### 1. Calendar
Using a digital tracker like [LunaCycle](/) to predict phases based on cycle length.

### 2. BBT (Basal Body Temperature)
Tracking daily temperature spikes that indicate ovulation.

### 3. Cervical mucus
Observing changes in vaginal discharge throughout the cycle.

### 4. Hormonal tests
Using at-home ovulation predictor kits (OPKs).

## Common Misconceptions About Phases

| Myth | Fact |
| --- | --- |
| Ovulation always happens on Day 14 | Ovulation varies greatly between individuals and cycles. |
| The luteal phase length varies a lot | The luteal phase is usually relatively consistent (12-14 days). |
