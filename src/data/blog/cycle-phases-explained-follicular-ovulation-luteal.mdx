## Why Knowing Your Cycle Phases Matters
Knowing your cycle phases is vital for health tracking, fertility, and lifestyle optimization. It helps you anticipate changes in energy, mood, and physical symptoms.

## How to Identify Each Phase
- **Menstruation:** Shedding of the uterine lining (Days 1-5).
- **Follicular Phase:** Preparation for ovulation; rising estrogen (Days 1-13).
- **Ovulation:** Release of an egg; peak fertility (Day 14).
- **Luteal Phase:** Preparation for potential pregnancy; rising progesterone (Days 15-28).

## Top 4 Cycle Phase Tracking Tools
### 1. Calendar
Tracking your period start dates helps establish your baseline cycle length. [LunaCycle](/) is great for this.

### 2. BBT (Basal Body Temperature)
Tracking BBT can help confirm when ovulation has occurred.

### 3. Cervical Mucus
Observing changes in cervical mucus provides real-time clues about your fertile window.

### 4. Hormonal Tests
Ovulation predictor kits (OPKs) detect surges in hormones like LH.

## Common Misconceptions About Phases
| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ovulation always happens on Day 14. | Ovulation day varies greatly among individuals and cycles. |
| You can get pregnant any day of the month. | Pregnancy is only possible during the fertile window (around ovulation). |
| All phases feel the same. | Hormonal shifts cause distinct physical and emotional changes in each phase. |
