## Why Knowing Your Cycle Phases Matters

Your menstrual cycle is a complex interplay of hormones that affects your physical and emotional well-being. By understanding the four distinct phases—menstruation, follicular, ovulation, and luteal—you can better interpret your body's signals, optimize your lifestyle, and make informed decisions about your health.

## How to Identify Each Phase

### 1. Menstruation
- **Signs/Symptoms**: Bleeding, cramping, fatigue.
- **Length**: 3-7 days.

### 2. Follicular Phase
- **Signs/Symptoms**: Increasing energy, rising estrogen, lighter mood.
- **Length**: ~13-14 days (overlaps with menstruation).

### 3. Ovulation
- **Signs/Symptoms**: Clear, stretchy cervical mucus, slight pelvic pain (mittelschmerz), peak libido.
- **Length**: 1-2 days.

### 4. Luteal Phase
- **Signs/Symptoms**: PMS symptoms, tender breasts, bloating, rising progesterone.
- **Length**: ~14 days.

## Top 4 Cycle Phase Tracking Tools

### 1. Calendar Tracking
Using an app like [LunaCycle](/tracker) to log your period start and end dates.

### 2. Basal Body Temperature (BBT)
Tracking your daily resting temperature to confirm ovulation.

### 3. Cervical Mucus Monitoring
Observing changes in vaginal discharge to identify your fertile window.

### 4. Hormonal Tests
Using ovulation predictor kits (OPKs) to detect the LH surge before ovulation.

## Common Misconceptions About Phases

| Myth | Fact |
| --- | --- |
| Ovulation always happens on Day 14. | Ovulation timing varies from woman to woman and cycle to cycle. |
| You can only get pregnant on the day of ovulation. | Sperm can live for up to 5 days, making the fertile window about 6 days long. |
| The luteal phase varies greatly in length. | The luteal phase is usually consistently around 14 days for most women. |
