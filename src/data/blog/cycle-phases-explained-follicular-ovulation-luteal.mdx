## Why Knowing Your Cycle Phases Matters
Knowing the different phases of your cycle helps you understand why your energy levels, mood, and body change throughout the month. It's key for lifestyle optimization and fertility tracking.

## How to Identify Each Phase
The **menstrual phase** is marked by bleeding. The **follicular phase** usually brings rising energy. **Ovulation** is brief, marked by clear cervical mucus. The **luteal phase** can bring PMS symptoms as progesterone rises. Track these effortlessly using [LunaCycle](/).

## Top 4 Cycle Phase Tracking Tools
### 1. Calendar
Tracking the dates of your period to estimate phases based on averages.
### 2. BBT (Basal Body Temperature)
Measuring your resting temperature daily to confirm ovulation.
### 3. Cervical mucus
Observing changes in vaginal discharge to identify the fertile window.
### 4. Hormonal tests
Using over-the-counter strips like OPKs to detect LH surges.

## Common Misconceptions About Phases
| Myth | Fact |
|------|------|
| Ovulation always happens on day 14 | Ovulation day varies significantly between women |
| The cycle is only about bleeding | The cycle involves continuous hormonal changes |
