## Why Knowing Your Cycle Phases Matters

Understanding the distinct phases of your cycle is essential for health tracking, fertility, and lifestyle optimization. Each phase brings different hormonal changes that affect your mood, energy, and physical well-being.

## How to Identify Each Phase

- **Menstruation (Days 1-5):** Shedding of the uterine lining. Symptoms include bleeding and cramping.
- **Follicular Phase (Days 1-13):** Overlaps with menstruation. Estrogen rises, energy increases.
- **Ovulation (Day 14):** Release of an egg. Cervical mucus becomes clear and stretchy.
- **Luteal Phase (Days 15-28):** Progesterone rises. PMS symptoms may occur if not pregnant.

## Top 4 Cycle Phase Tracking Tools

### 1. Calendar
Using a calendar or an app like [LunaCycle](/), to log the start and end dates.

### 2. BBT
Basal Body Temperature tracking helps pinpoint ovulation.

### 3. Cervical mucus
Monitoring changes in discharge provides clues about your fertile window.

### 4. Hormonal tests
Ovulation predictor kits measure hormone levels directly.

## Common Misconceptions About Phases

| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| Ovulation always happens on day 14 | Ovulation day varies greatly among women |
| You can get pregnant any day of the month | Pregnancy is only possible during the fertile window |
| PMS is all in your head | PMS is a real physical response to hormonal changes |
