# LunaCycle vs Clue: Which Period Tracker is Right for You?

When looking for a period tracker that isn't covered in flowers and butterflies, **LunaCycle** and **Clue** often top the list. Both apps pride themselves on being "grown-up" tools, but they take very different approaches to how they handle your information.

## Quick Verdict
**Clue** is fantastic for those who want a science-backed, data-rich experience with clinical research integration. **LunaCycle** is the go-to for users who want a lightweight, fast, and 100% private utility without the need for an account.

## Comparison Table

| Feature | LunaCycle | Clue | Winner |
|---------|-----------|------|--------|
| Design | Minimalist | Gender-Neutral / Science | Tie |
| Privacy | Local-Only | GDPR Compliant (Cloud) | LunaCycle |
| Account | Not Required | Required | LunaCycle |
| Research | Personal Use | Contributes to Science | Clue |
| Cost | Free | Freemium | LunaCycle |

## Deep Dive: Clue
Clue has long been the gold standard for science-based menstrual tracking. They collaborate with researchers and aim to provide users with a deep understanding of their bodies through data.

**Pros:**
- Highly reputable science-based content.
- Gender-neutral design.
- Excellent documentation of symptoms.

**Cons:**
- Data is stored on servers (though in the EU under strict GDPR).
- Many features require a "Clue Plus" subscription.

## Deep Dive: LunaCycle
LunaCycle takes a more "utility-first" approach. It's designed to be a tool you use, not a platform you live in. By eliminating the cloud, it eliminates the single biggest risk to user privacy.

**Pros:**
- Impossible to leak data from a server that doesn't exist.
- Extremely fast loading times.
- No notifications or marketing emails.

**Cons:**
- No backup if you lose your device (unless you use the manual export feature).
- Fewer symptom tracking categories than Clue.

## When to Choose Each
- **Choose LunaCycle if:** You value speed, simplicity, and the peace of mind that your data is locked to your device.
- **Choose Clue if:** You want a more comprehensive health diary and like the idea of your (anonymized) data helping advance menstrual research.
