# LunaCycle vs Clue: 2026 Comparison

LunaCycle and Clue are both known for their clean, science-based, and non-stereotypical approach to menstrual health.

## Quick Verdict
**Choose LunaCycle if:** You want the absolute maximum level of privacy possible (local storage) and a tool that requires no account.
**Choose Clue if:** You prefer a native mobile app experience and value their deep integration with clinical research.

## Comparison Table

| Feature | LunaCycle | Clue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Philosophy** | Privacy & Utility | Science & Research |
| **Data Control** | You (Local Storage) | Them (Server-Side) |
| **Location** | Global (Web) | Berlin, Germany |
| **Identity** | Anonymous | Account-Based |

## Deep Dive: LunaCycle
LunaCycle's main advantage is its "Zero-Knowledge" architecture. Because there is no central server, there is no single point of failure for your data security. It's a "set it and forget it" tool for private tracking.

## Deep Dive: Clue
Clue is a pioneer in the "Femtech" space. They are known for their commitment to science and gender-neutral design. Being based in Germany, they follow strict GDPR regulations, but they still store your data on their servers.
