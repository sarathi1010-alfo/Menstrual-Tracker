# The Ultimate Guide to Menstrual Cycle Tracking in 2026

Tracking your menstrual cycle is more than just knowing when your next period will arrive. It's a vital sign of your overall health and a powerful tool for body literacy.

## Why Tracking Matters in 2026

In an era of digital health, understanding your cycle helps you:
- Predict period start dates and fertile windows.
- Identify patterns in mood, energy, and physical symptoms.
- Detect potential health issues early by noticing irregularities.
- Take control of your reproductive health with data you own.

## The Four Phases of Your Cycle

1. **Menstruation:** The shedding of the uterine lining (Days 1-7).
2. **Follicular Phase:** The body prepares to release an egg (Days 1-14).
3. **Ovulation:** The release of a mature egg (around Day 14).
4. **Luteal Phase:** The body prepares for a potential pregnancy (Days 15-28).
