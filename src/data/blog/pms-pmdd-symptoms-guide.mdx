For many people, the week before a period is marked by a predictable set of physical and emotional changes. Understanding whether you are experiencing standard PMS or the more severe PMDD is essential for getting the right support.

## What PMS/PMDD is and how it affects the cycle

Both Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) and Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD) occur during the **luteal phase** of the cycle (after ovulation and before the period). They are thought to be caused by the brain's sensitivity to the natural drop in estrogen and progesterone that happens before menstruation.

## How cycle tracking can help manage PMS/PMDD

Tracking is the single most important tool for managing premenstrual symptoms. It allows you to:
1.  **Confirm the Timing:** Ensure your symptoms are actually linked to your cycle (occurring only in the luteal phase).
2.  **Predict the "Dip":** Anticipate when you might feel more vulnerable, allowing you to clear your schedule or increase self-care.
3.  **Document Severity:** Provide a clear, data-backed record for your doctor or therapist.

## What to look for in your cycle data

When reviewing your logs in LunaCycle, look for:
- **The "Clear" Phase:** Do your symptoms disappear within a few days of your period starting?
- **Symptom Clusters:** Do you always get the same symptoms (e.g., migraines or irritability) on the same cycle days?
- **Intensity Trends:** Are your symptoms getting better or worse over several months?

## When to consult a healthcare provider

If your premenstrual symptoms are making it difficult to function at work, school, or in your relationships, please seek professional help. A healthcare provider can use your LunaCycle data to help determine if you meet the criteria for PMDD or if there is another underlying cause for your symptoms.

## Quick Reference: PMS vs. PMDD

| Feature | PMS | PMDD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mood Symptoms** | Mild irritability or sadness. | Extreme depression, anxiety, or anger. |
| **Physical Symptoms** | Bloating, breast tenderness. | Can include all PMS physical symptoms. |
| **Impact** | Manageable inconvenience. | Debilitating; disrupts life and work. |
| **Timing** | Luteal phase only. | Luteal phase only. |
| **When to See Doctor** | If symptoms bother you. | As soon as possible. |
