# Endometriosis and Cycle Tracking

Endometriosis is a condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows in other places. For many, tracking is the first step in identifying that their pain isn't just "normal period cramps."

## Why Tracking Matters for Endo
Because endometriosis is often misunderstood, having a clear, data-backed history of your symptoms is powerful.
- **Pain Mapping:** Is your pain only during your period, or also during ovulation and throughout the month?
- **Flow Monitoring:** Tracking "Heavy" flow days can help identify issues like anemia.
- **Communication:** Showing a doctor a consistent log of symptoms helps them understand the severity of your experience.

## How to use LunaCycle for Endo
1. **Intensity Logging:** Use the flow intensity markers to record heavy days.
2. **Daily Notes:** Use the notes section to describe the type of pain (e.g., sharp, dull, radiating).
3. **Consistency:** Even if you don't have a period (due to medication), continue tracking your daily symptoms.
