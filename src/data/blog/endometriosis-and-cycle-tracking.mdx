# Endometriosis and Cycle Tracking: A Complete Guide

Endometriosis is a condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus. For those living with "Endo," the menstrual cycle isn't just a calendar event; it can be a source of significant physical and emotional challenge.

## Why Track Your Cycle with Endometriosis?
Tracking is more than just predicting your next period. For Endo patients, it's about:
1. **Identifying Patterns:** Does your pain peak during ovulation or just before your period?
2. **Symptom Management:** Tracking helps you predict "bad days" so you can plan rest and medication.
3. **Medical Advocacy:** Showing a doctor a 3-month log of daily pain levels is much more effective than trying to remember symptoms during a 15-minute appointment.

## What to Look for in Your Data
- **Cyclical vs. Non-Cyclical Pain:** Is the pain only during your period, or does it occur throughout the month?
- **Flow Heaviness:** Many with Endo experience "Menorrhagia" (excessively heavy bleeding).
- **Secondary Symptoms:** Track bloating ("Endo belly"), painful bowel movements, and fatigue.

## When to See a Doctor
If you experience "dysmenorrhea" (painful periods) that prevents you from going to work or school, or if your pain is not managed by over-the-counter medication, consult a healthcare provider. Bring your LunaCycle logs to your appointment to provide a clear picture of your experience.
