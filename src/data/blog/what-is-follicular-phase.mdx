## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The pituitary gland releases FSH (Follicle-Stimulating Hormone), which prompts the ovaries to develop several follicles. One follicle becomes dominant and produces increasing amounts of estrogen, which thickens the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example

For a woman with a 30-day cycle who ovulates on day 16, her follicular phase lasts 16 days (from day 1 of her period until ovulation). This phase is the most variable in length among women.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

This phase sets the stage for the entire cycle. The rising estrogen levels often lead to increased energy, better mood, and changes in cervical mucus that facilitate fertility.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Length** | Variable (often 10-16 days) | Stable (often 12-14 days) |
| **Dominant Hormone** | Estrogen | Progesterone |
| **Energy Levels** | Typically rising/high | Often decreasing before period |
