## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The brain releases Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH), prompting several follicles in the ovaries to grow. Usually, only one follicle becomes dominant and matures. As it grows, it produces estrogen, which thickens the uterine lining in preparation for an egg.

## Real-World Example

If a cycle is delayed and lasts 35 days instead of 28, it is usually because the follicular phase took longer (e.g., 21 days instead of 14) before ovulation occurred. You can monitor this variability in [LunaCycle](/tracker).

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

It sets the stage for ovulation. The rising estrogen during this phase also typically results in better moods, clearer skin, and higher energy levels.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Phase | Focus | Typical Energy Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Follicular | Growing an egg (Estrogen) | Increasing / High |
| Luteal | Preparing uterus (Progesterone) | Stable / Decreasing |
