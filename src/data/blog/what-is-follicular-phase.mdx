The follicular phase is the initial stage of the menstrual cycle, starting on the very first day of menstrual bleeding and concluding when ovulation occurs. Its main purpose is to prepare a mature egg to be released.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), prompting the ovaries to develop several follicles. Typically, one dominant follicle matures fully, producing rising levels of estrogen that thicken the uterine lining in preparation for pregnancy.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle, this phase lasts about 13-14 days. Women often report feeling increased energy, better mood, and higher productivity as estrogen levels climb.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

This phase sets the stage for the entire cycle. Its length can vary based on stress, illness, or travel, which is why cycle lengths can fluctuate from month to month.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
| ------- | ---------------- | ------------ |
| Timing | Before ovulation | After ovulation |
| Length | Variable | Consistent (12-14 days) |
