## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?
FSH stimulates follicles in the ovaries to grow, one of which will mature an egg.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, it usually lasts from day 1 to day 14.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?
It is responsible for egg maturation and preparing the body for potential conception.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase
| Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|
| Varies in length | Usually consistent length |
