# What is the Follicular Phase?

The follicular phase is the first part of your menstrual cycle. It starts on the first day of your period and ends when you ovulate.

## What's Happening?
The brain releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), which tells the ovaries to prepare several follicles (sacs containing eggs). Eventually, one "dominant" follicle continues to grow and produces estrogen.

## Rising Estrogen
As estrogen levels rise, you might experience:
- **Increased Energy:** Many people feel more social and productive.
- **Improved Skin:** Rising estrogen often helps with skin clarity.
- **Better Mood:** Estrogen can have a positive effect on serotonin.

## Tracking
Because the follicular phase is the most variable part of the cycle, it is the reason why cycles can be different lengths each month. Stress or lifestyle changes most often impact this phase.
