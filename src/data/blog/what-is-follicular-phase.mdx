## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

During this phase, the pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), which prompts the ovaries to develop several follicles. Usually, only one follicle becomes fully mature. As it grows, it produces increasing amounts of estrogen, which thickens the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, the follicular phase usually lasts from Day 1 to Day 13. However, if a woman experiences a lot of stress or illness, this phase might stretch to 20 days, delaying ovulation and pushing back her next period.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

This phase sets the stage for the entire cycle. The rising estrogen levels often lead to increased energy, better mood, and glowing skin.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| **Timing** | Before ovulation | After ovulation |
| **Duration** | Highly variable | Consistent (12-14 days) |
| **Energy Level**| Generally high | May decrease |
