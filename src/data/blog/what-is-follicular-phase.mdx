# What is the Follicular Phase?

The follicular phase is the first phase of the ovarian cycle. It begins on the first day of menstruation and ends with ovulation. During this phase, the body prepares an egg for release.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The pituitary gland releases follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH), which stimulates the ovaries to produce multiple follicles. One of these follicles will become dominant and mature into an egg. As the follicles grow, they produce estrogen.

## Real-World Example in Cycle Tracking

Because the follicular phase can vary significantly in length, tracking the start of your period in [LunaCycle](/) is the most reliable way to monitor the beginning of this phase.
