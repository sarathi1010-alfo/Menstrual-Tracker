The follicular phase is the first part of the menstrual cycle, beginning on the first day of menstruation and ending with ovulation, during which follicles mature in the ovaries.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The pituitary gland releases FSH, stimulating follicles in the ovary to grow. Estrogen levels rise as the follicles mature.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, the follicular phase usually spans days 1 to 14.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

It is the phase where the body prepares an egg for potential fertilization.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
| ---------------- | ------------ |
| Pre-ovulation | Post-ovulation |
| Length varies | Length is relatively fixed (~14 days) |
