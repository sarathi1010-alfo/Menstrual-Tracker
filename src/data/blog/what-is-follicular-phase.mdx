## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?
During this phase, the pituitary gland releases FSH, stimulating the ovaries to develop several follicles, each containing an immature egg. One follicle becomes dominant and starts producing high levels of estrogen, which thickens the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example
This phase starts on the day you begin bleeding (Day 1). As bleeding stops and estrogen rises (around Day 6-13), many women report feeling more energetic, focused, and social.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?
This phase lays the groundwork for the rest of the cycle. Stress, illness, or travel can easily delay ovulation by extending the follicular phase, which is why cycle lengths can vary from month to month. Tracking this variation in [LunaCycle](/tracker) is perfectly normal.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Before ovulation | After ovulation |
| Main Hormone | Estrogen | Progesterone |
| Function | Maturing the egg | Preparing for implantation |
