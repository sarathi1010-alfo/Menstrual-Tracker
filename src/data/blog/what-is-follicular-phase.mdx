# What is the Follicular Phase?

The follicular phase is the first half of your menstrual cycle. It is the time when your body is preparing an egg to be released and building up the environment for a potential pregnancy.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), which signals the ovaries to mature several follicles. As these follicles grow, they release estrogen. Estrogen rebuilds the uterine lining and eventually triggers the surge of LH that leads to ovulation.

## Real-World Example of the Follicular Phase

If you track your energy in [LunaCycle](/tracker), you might notice a steady increase during the follicular phase. You may feel more social, energetic, and resilient as estrogen levels rise toward their peak.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

A healthy follicular phase ensures that a high-quality egg is matured. Because the length of this phase varies more than the luteal phase, it is the primary reason why cycle lengths differ between individuals or from month to month.

## Follicular Phase vs. Luteal Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Hormone** | Estrogen | Progesterone |
| **Goal** | Mature an egg | Prepare for implantation |
| **Energy** | Rising / High | Waning / Reflective |
