## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?
The brain releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), telling the ovaries to prepare eggs. As follicles grow, they produce estrogen. The rising estrogen thickens the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example
This phase starts on Day 1 of bleeding. Once the period ends (e.g., Day 5), rising estrogen often brings increased energy, clearer skin, and an upbeat mood leading up to ovulation.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?
It sets the stage for a healthy ovulation. Because its length can vary, it is the primary reason why cycle lengths change from month to month. Track it using [LunaCycle](/).

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase
| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Start of period to ovulation | Ovulation to start of period |
| Length | Variable (often 10-22 days) | Consistent (usually 12-16 days) |
