The follicular phase is the first half of the menstrual cycle. It begins on the first day of menstruation and ends when ovulation occurs. During this phase, the body prepares an egg for release.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?
The pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), which prompts the ovaries to develop several follicles. Usually, only one matures fully. As the follicle grows, it produces estrogen.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, the follicular phase generally encompasses days 1 through 13.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?
It is responsible for maturing the egg and building up the uterine lining via rising estrogen levels.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase
| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Variable Length | Highly variable | Relatively constant (11-17 days) |
| Goal | Prepare the egg | Prepare the uterus |
