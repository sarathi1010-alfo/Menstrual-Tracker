## What is the Follicular Phase?

The follicular phase is the initial phase of the menstrual cycle. It starts on the very first day of your period and continues until ovulation occurs.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

During this phase, the pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), prompting the ovaries to develop several follicles. Typically, one dominant follicle matures and produces increasing amounts of estrogen, preparing the body for ovulation.

## Real-World Example

In a typical cycle, the follicular phase might last for the first 13-14 days. This length is the most variable part of the menstrual cycle and largely determines the overall cycle length.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

This phase is critical for developing a healthy, mature egg. The rising estrogen levels also stimulate the thickening of the uterine lining, creating a suitable environment for a potential pregnancy.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timing | Start of cycle to ovulation | Ovulation to next period |
| Focus | Egg maturation | Uterine preparation |
