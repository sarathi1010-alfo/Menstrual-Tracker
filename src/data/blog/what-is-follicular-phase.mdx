## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), prompting several follicles in the ovaries to grow. Usually, only one becomes the dominant follicle, producing increasing amounts of estrogen as it matures.

## Real-World Example

This phase begins on Day 1 of bleeding. As the period ends, rising estrogen levels often lead to increased energy, better mood, and a feeling of renewed focus, lasting until ovulation around Day 14 (in a 28-day cycle).

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

It lays the groundwork for ovulation. The length of the follicular phase dictates the overall length of the menstrual cycle, as the luteal phase remains relatively constant.

## Follicular Phase vs Menstruation

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Overlap** | Includes menstruation but continues after | Is a sub-phase of the follicular phase |
| **Focus** | Maturing an egg | Shedding the uterine lining |
