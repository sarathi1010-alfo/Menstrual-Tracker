The follicular phase is the first half of the menstrual cycle. It begins on the first day of your period and ends when you ovulate. Its primary biological function is to prepare and mature an egg for potential fertilization.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The phase begins when the pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH). This hormone stimulates the ovaries to produce 5 to 20 small sacs called follicles, each containing an immature egg.

As these follicles grow, they begin to secrete estrogen. Usually, only one follicle (the dominant follicle) fully matures, while the others dissolve. The rising estrogen levels not only signal the uterine lining to thicken in preparation for a pregnancy but also eventually trigger the LH surge that causes [ovulation](/what-is-ovulation), marking the end of the phase.

To see how this fits into the entire month, read our [cycle phases overview](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal).

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a delayed period and a 35-day cycle, the variation almost always happens in the follicular phase. In this scenario, her body might take 21 days to mature an egg (Follicular Phase: Day 1 to Day 21). Once she ovulates on Day 21, she enters the more consistent [luteal phase](/what-is-luteal-phase) for the remaining 14 days. During the late follicular phase (Days 14-20), she would likely notice increased energy and the appearance of fertile cervical mucus.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

Because the length of the follicular phase dictates the length of your entire cycle, it is the primary reason periods can be "late" or "early." Stress, illness, or travel can delay the maturation of the follicle, thereby extending the follicular phase. By tracking your cycle with [LunaCycle](/), you can observe the signs of rising estrogen (like energy spikes and mucus changes) to anticipate ovulation, regardless of when it occurs.

## Follicular Phase vs Menstrual Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Menstrual Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Relationship** | The broader first half of the cycle. | A sub-phase *within* the beginning of the follicular phase. |
| **Duration** | Averages 14 days, but highly variable. | Averages 2 to 7 days. |
| **Primary Action** | Maturing an egg and rebuilding the uterine lining. | Shedding the old uterine lining. |
| **Hormonal State** | Estrogen is steadily rising. | Estrogen and Progesterone are at their lowest. |
