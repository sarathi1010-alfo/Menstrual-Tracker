## What is the Follicular Phase?
The follicular phase is the longest phase of the menstrual cycle. It begins on the first day of menstruation and ends with ovulation. During this time, follicles in the ovary mature in preparation to release an egg.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?
FSH stimulates the ovaries to produce follicles, one of which will become dominant and secrete estrogen.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, this phase lasts from Day 1 to Day 14 (including menstruation).

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?
It sets the stage for ovulation and determines the overall length of your cycle.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase
| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
|---------|------------------|--------------|
| Timing | Before ovulation | After ovulation |
| Length | Variable | Consistent (12-14 days) |
