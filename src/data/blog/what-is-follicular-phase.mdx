## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The phase begins with the pituitary gland releasing FSH, which stimulates the ovaries to produce 5 to 20 small sacs (follicles). Each follicle houses an immature egg. As the healthiest follicle grows, it produces estrogen, which signals the uterus to thicken its lining in preparation for a potential pregnancy.

## Real-World Example

In a cycle that lasts 32 days where ovulation occurs on Day 18, the follicular phase lasts for the first 17 days. During the later part of this phase, many women report feeling more energetic, focused, and sociable due to the rising estrogen levels.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

The length of the follicular phase dictates the total length of your menstrual cycle. Stress, illness, or travel can delay ovulation by extending the follicular phase, which is why periods are sometimes "late."

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
|---------|------------------|--------------|
| Timing | Starts with menstruation, ends at ovulation. | Starts after ovulation, ends at menstruation. |
| Variability | Highly variable length. | Very consistent length. |
| Key Hormone | Estrogen. | Progesterone. |
