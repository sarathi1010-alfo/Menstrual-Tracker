## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The pituitary gland releases follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH), which stimulates the ovaries to produce several follicles. One of these follicles will become dominant and mature into an egg. As it grows, it produces estrogen, which thickens the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, the follicular phase is roughly days 1 to 13. You will likely feel a boost in energy and sociability in the second week as estrogen peaks.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

This phase is critical for preparing a healthy egg for ovulation and building a supportive environment in the uterus. Variations in cycle length are almost always due to variations in the follicular phase.

## Follicular Phase vs Menstruation

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Menstruation |
|---------|------------------|--------------|
| **Timing** | Days 1 to ovulation | Days 1-5 (approx.) |
| **Activity** | Follicle growth, rising estrogen | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Overlap** | Yes, menstruation is part of the early follicular phase | N/A |
