## Definition
The follicular phase is the first half of the menstrual cycle, starting on the first day of your period and ending with ovulation.

## How Does It Work?
The pituitary gland releases Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), prompting the ovaries to develop several follicles. One dominant follicle matures and produces increasing amounts of estrogen.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, this phase spans days 1 to 14. As estrogen rises during the second week, you may notice increased energy and improved mood.

## Why Is It Important?
This phase is crucial for developing a healthy egg and thickening the uterine lining in preparation for potential implantation.
