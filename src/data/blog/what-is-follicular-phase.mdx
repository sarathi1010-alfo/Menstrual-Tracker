The follicular phase is the first half of the [menstrual cycle](/blog/what-is-menstrual-cycle), starting on the very first day of your period (Day 1) and ending the moment [ovulation](/blog/what-is-ovulation) occurs. During this time, the body is focused on maturing a new egg for potential fertilization.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

The phase begins with the brain releasing Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), which signals the ovaries to start developing several fluid-filled sacs called follicles, each containing an immature egg. As these follicles grow, they produce estrogen. Eventually, one dominant follicle outpaces the others. The rising estrogen levels signal the brain to stop producing FSH and eventually trigger a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), which causes the dominant follicle to rupture, releasing the mature egg.

## Real-World Example

A user logging their cycle in [LunaCycle](/) notes that her period starts on March 1st. From March 1st until March 14th, she is in her follicular phase. During the first few days, she feels tired (menstruation), but by March 8th, she notices a significant increase in energy, sociability, and clearer skin as her estrogen levels rise toward their peak.

## Why is the Follicular Phase Important?

The follicular phase dictates the timing of the entire cycle. Because the [luteal phase](/blog/what-is-luteal-phase) is relatively fixed in length, any delay in ovulation—which happens at the end of the follicular phase—will directly result in a delayed period. Understanding this phase helps explain why stress or illness can make your period "late."

## Follicular Phase vs Menstruation

While they overlap, they are biologically distinct concepts.

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | Maturing a new egg in the ovary. | Shedding the old uterine lining. |
| **Duration** | Lasts until ovulation (highly variable). | Lasts only as long as bleeding occurs (2-7 days). |
| **Hormones** | Characterized by rising estrogen. | Characterized by low levels of all sex hormones. |
| **Overlap** | The first 2-7 days of the follicular phase *are* the menstrual phase. | Menstruation is just the beginning stage of the longer follicular phase. |
