Definition: The follicular phase is the first part of the menstrual cycle. It begins on the first day of menstrual bleeding and concludes with ovulation. During this time, the body prepares to release an egg.

## How Does the Follicular Phase Work?

Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH) prompts the ovaries to produce follicles. The dominant follicle produces estrogen, which thickens the uterine lining.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, this phase typically runs from Day 1 to Day 13, leading up to ovulation on Day 14.

## Follicular Phase vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Follicular Phase | Luteal Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | Variable | Consistently 12-14 days |
| Main Event | Maturation of the egg | Preparation of the uterine lining |
