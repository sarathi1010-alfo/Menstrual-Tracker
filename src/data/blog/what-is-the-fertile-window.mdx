## Definition

The **fertile window** refers to the days during a woman's menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. This window exists because sperm can live inside the female body for up to five days, while the egg only survives for 12-24 hours after release.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Your chances of conceiving increase steadily as you approach ovulation, peaking on the day before and the day of ovulation.

For example, if you ovulate on Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window would roughly be Days 9 through 14. Intercourse during this period ensures that sperm are waiting in the fallopian tubes when the egg is released.

## Real-World Example of Identifying the Fertile Window

Women avoiding pregnancy or trying to conceive often use the fertile window to time intercourse. Tools like [LunaCycle](/) can provide an estimated fertile window based on the calendar method, but for higher accuracy, many combine this with ovulation predictor kits or cervical mucus monitoring.

## Fertile Window vs. Safe Days

| Feature | Fertile Window | Safe Days |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pregnancy Chance** | High | Very Low |
| **Timing** | 5 days before + day of ovulation | Days furthest from ovulation (start/end of cycle) |
| **Goal** | Trying to conceive | Avoiding pregnancy |
| **Certainty** | Requires accurate tracking | Hard to guarantee with irregular cycles |
