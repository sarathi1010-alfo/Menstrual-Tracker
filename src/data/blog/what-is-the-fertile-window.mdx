## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window represents the days during your menstrual cycle when intercourse is most likely to result in pregnancy. It is determined by two main factors: the lifespan of the egg and the lifespan of the sperm.

## How the Fertile Window Works

While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after it is released during ovulation, sperm are much more resilient. In the right conditions—specifically in the presence of fertile cervical mucus—sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days.

Therefore, the fertile window spans the five days *before* ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. Having intercourse during these days ensures that sperm are waiting in the fallopian tubes when the egg is released.
