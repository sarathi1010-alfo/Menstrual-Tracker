## Understanding the Timeline

The fertile window exists because of the different lifespans of sperm and the egg. While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can survive in ideal cervical fluid for up to 5 days.

## Maximizing Conception Chances

For those trying to conceive, having intercourse during the days leading up to ovulation ensures that sperm are already present in the fallopian tubes when the egg is released.

## The Role of Cycle Tracking

Accurately identifying the fertile window requires consistent tracking. Relying solely on a calendar can be inaccurate if your cycle fluctuates. Using tools like [LunaCycle](/) to log cycle lengths and physical symptoms helps pinpoint this crucial phase.
