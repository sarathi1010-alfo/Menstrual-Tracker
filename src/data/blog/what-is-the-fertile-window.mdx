The fertile window is the period during the menstrual cycle when conception is possible, including the day of ovulation and the five days preceding it.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12-24 hours after release, sperm can live inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days in fertile cervical mucus. This means if you have intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation, the sperm can "wait" for the egg.

## Real-World Example of the Fertile Window

If you ovulate on Day 14, your fertile window actually begins around Day 9 or 10. Intercourse on Day 12 is highly likely to lead to pregnancy because the sperm will be ready and waiting when the egg is released on Day 14.

## Why is the Fertile Window Important?

Whether you are trying to conceive (TTC) or avoid pregnancy, knowing your fertile window is essential. It is the only time during the month that pregnancy can occur.

## Fertile Window vs. Ovulation Day

| Concept | Duration | Description |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Ovulation Day | 24 Hours | The actual release of the egg. |
| Fertile Window | ~6 Days | The total time pregnancy is possible. |
