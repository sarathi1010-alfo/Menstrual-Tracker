The fertile window refers to the days during a menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It is driven by the lifespan of both sperm and the egg.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only lives for 12 to 24 hours after [ovulation](/what-is-ovulation), sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (specifically, in the presence of fertile cervical mucus). Therefore, the fertile window spans the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## Real-World Example of Using the Fertile Window

If you are trying to conceive and [LunaCycle](/) predicts your ovulation day is on the 15th of the month, your fertile window would roughly begin on the 10th and end on the 15th. Having intercourse during these days maximizes the chances of conception.

## Fertile Window vs. Safe Days

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fertile Window** | The days when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. |
| **Safe Days** | Days in the cycle when pregnancy is highly unlikely (though never guaranteed without contraception). |
