The fertile window refers to the specific timeframe during a menstrual cycle when a person has the highest probability of getting pregnant if they have unprotected intercourse. It is a critical concept for both trying to conceive and natural family planning.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

The timing of the fertile window is dictated by two main biological factors: the lifespan of sperm and the lifespan of the egg. Sperm can survive in the favorable environment of the female reproductive tract (specifically in fertile cervical mucus) for up to five days. The egg, however, only survives for 12 to 24 hours after it is released during ovulation. Therefore, the fertile window generally consists of the five days preceding ovulation plus the day of ovulation itself.

## Real-World Example of Tracking the Fertile Window

If you are trying to conceive, identifying this window is paramount. You might log your period dates in [LunaCycle](/), which will then provide an estimated fertile window based on your average cycle length. If LunaCycle predicts ovulation on Day 14, your fertile window is roughly Days 9 through 14.

## Fertile Window vs. Luteal Phase (Table)

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Timing** | Leads up to and includes ovulation. | Occurs strictly after ovulation. |
| **Fertility** | High probability of conception. | Very low probability of conception. |
| **Hormonal Focus** | Rising Estrogen, LH surge. | High Progesterone. |
