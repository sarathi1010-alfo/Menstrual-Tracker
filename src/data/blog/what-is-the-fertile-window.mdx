## What is the Fertile Window?

The fertile window is the specific period during a menstrual cycle when intercourse can result in pregnancy. It encompasses the lifespan of both the sperm and the egg.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation, sperm can live inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (specifically, in the presence of fertile cervical mucus). Therefore, the fertile window spans the five days leading up to ovulation plus the day of ovulation itself—a total of about six days.

## Real-World Example of Using the Fertile Window

A couple trying to conceive might track the woman's cycle using [LunaCycle](/tracker). By observing changes in her cervical mucus (which becomes clear and stretchy approaching ovulation), they can identify the start of her fertile window and time intercourse accordingly to maximize their chances of conception.

## Fertile Window vs. Safe Days

| Feature | Fertile Window | Safe Days |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition** | Days when pregnancy is possible. | Days when pregnancy is highly unlikely. |
| **Timing** | Around ovulation (5 days before + day of). | The beginning and end of the menstrual cycle, far from ovulation. |
| **Risk of Conception** | High. | Low (though no time is guaranteed 100% "safe" without contraception). |
