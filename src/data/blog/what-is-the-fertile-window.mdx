The fertile window represents the specific days during a menstrual cycle when sexual intercourse can result in pregnancy. It is determined by the lifespan of both the sperm and the egg.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after being released during [ovulation](/what-is-ovulation), sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (specifically, in the presence of fertile cervical mucus).

Therefore, the fertile window is generally considered to be the **five days leading up to ovulation, plus the day of ovulation itself**. Intercourse on the days immediately preceding ovulation offers the highest statistical chance of conception.

## Real-World Example in Family Planning

If you are Trying to Conceive (TTC), knowing your fertile window is essential. If [LunaCycle](/) estimates your ovulation day to be Day 14 of your cycle, your fertile window would roughly span from Day 9 to Day 14.

*Note: Calendar methods provide estimations. For strict pregnancy avoidance, standard calendar predictions are not reliable enough on their own.*

## Fertile Window vs. Safe Days

| Term | Definition | Pregnancy Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fertile Window** | The ~6 days surrounding ovulation. | High probability |
| **Safe Days** | Days outside the fertile lifespan of sperm and egg. | Low probability |
