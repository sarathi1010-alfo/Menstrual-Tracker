The fertile window is the brief period during your menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. Because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days, and an egg lives for about 24 hours, the window encompasses the days leading up to and including ovulation.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Your body produces fertile-quality cervical mucus leading up to ovulation, creating a welcoming environment for sperm. Intercourse during this 6-day window provides the highest chance of conception.

## Real-World Example of Fertile Window Tracking

Couples trying to conceive often use ovulation predictor kits or track physiological signs to pinpoint this window. You can also use calendar methods provided by tools like [LunaCycle](/) to estimate these days based on your historical cycle data.

## Fertile Window vs. Safe Days

| Feature | Fertile Window | Safe Days |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pregnancy Chance | High | Low |
| Timing | Leading up to and including ovulation. | Days outside the fertile window (menstruation and luteal phase). |
