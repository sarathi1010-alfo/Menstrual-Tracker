## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

Pregnancy is only possible during a specific timeframe each cycle. This window exists because sperm can survive in the female reproductive tract for up to five days, while the egg only survives for 12-24 hours after ovulation.

## Real-World Example of the Fertile Window in Family Planning

If you are trying to conceive, timing intercourse during the fertile window maximizes your chances. Conversely, if you are avoiding pregnancy using fertility awareness methods, you would abstain or use barrier methods during this time. The [LunaCycle](/) app highlights your estimated fertile window on your calendar.

## Fertile Window vs Ovulation Day

| Feature | Fertile Window | Ovulation Day |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The days pregnancy is possible. | The single day an egg is released. |
| **Duration** | About 6 days. | 12-24 hours. |
| **Timing** | 5 days before ovulation + ovulation day. | Roughly midway through the cycle. |
