The fertile window is the specific timeframe in a menstrual cycle when intercourse is most likely to result in pregnancy. It encompasses the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

While an egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours after being released, sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days under the right conditions (aided by fertile cervical mucus). Therefore, having intercourse in the days just before ovulation means sperm are waiting when the egg is released, maximizing the chances of fertilization.

## Real-World Example

If a woman ovulates on Day 14 of her cycle, her fertile window technically spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Intercourse on Days 12, 13, or 14 yields the highest statistical chance of pregnancy. [LunaCycle](/) can help estimate these days based on your historical data.
