## What is the Fertile Window?

The **fertile window** is the specific timeframe during the menstrual cycle when unprotected sexual intercourse can result in conception. It is governed by the lifespan of both the sperm and the egg.

## How Does the Fertile Window Work?

An egg lives for only 12 to 24 hours after being released during ovulation. However, healthy sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract (in cervical mucus) for up to five days.

Therefore, the fertile window spans a total of roughly six days: the five days before ovulation occurs, plus the day of ovulation. Intercourse on any of these days provides a chance for the sperm and egg to meet.

## Real-World Example of the Fertile Window

If a user using [LunaCycle](/) estimates that they will ovulate on Day 14 of their cycle, their fertile window roughly spans from Day 9 to Day 14. Someone trying to conceive would aim to have intercourse during these specific days to maximize their chances of pregnancy.

## Fertile Window vs. Luteal Phase

| Feature | Fertile Window | Luteal Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Timing** | Precedes and includes ovulation. | Occurs immediately after ovulation until the next period. |
| **Fertility** | High (pregnancy is possible). | Low to None (egg is no longer viable). |
| **Hormone** | Driven by rising Estrogen and an LH surge. | Driven by Progesterone. |
