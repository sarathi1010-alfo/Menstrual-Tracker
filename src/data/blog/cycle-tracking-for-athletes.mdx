Athletes aren't just small men. Their physiology is unique, dynamic, and governed by a 28-day hormonal rhythm. By tracking your cycle, you can stop guessing and start training with precision.

## Why cycle tracking matters for Athletes

For an athlete, the menstrual cycle is a vital sign of health and performance. It influences everything from how you utilize fuel (carbs vs. fats) to your reaction time and susceptibility to inflammation. Tracking allows you to see these patterns and use them to your advantage.

## Key features to prioritize for Athletes

- **Phase Prediction:** Knowing when you'll be in your high-energy follicular phase helps in planning high-intensity blocks.
- **Symptom Logging:** Tracking fatigue, bloating, or muscle soreness helps differentiate between overtraining and hormonal shifts.
- **Notes for Performance:** Log how your specific training sessions felt relative to your cycle day.

## Tips and best practices for Athletes

1.  **Monitor Your 'RPE':** Note your Relative Perceived Exertion. If a standard workout feels like a 10/10 in your luteal phase, that's important data.
2.  **Focus on Recovery:** Prioritize extra sleep and mobility work during your period and late luteal phase.
3.  **Fuel Your Phase:** Don't be afraid to increase your caloric intake slightly in the week before your period to support your body's needs.
4.  **Communicate with Coaches:** Use your LunaCycle data to have informed conversations about your training load and recovery needs.
