## 2. Technical Foundation: Phases and Hormones

To effectively track your cycle, it is essential to understand the biological mechanisms at play.

### The Four Phases

1. **Menstruation (Days 1-5):** The shedding of the uterine lining, resulting in bleeding.
2. **Follicular Phase (Days 1-13):** Overlapping with menstruation, this phase involves the maturation of ovarian follicles, driven by Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH) and rising estrogen.
3. **Ovulation (Day 14):** A surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) triggers the release of a mature egg.
4. **Luteal Phase (Days 15-28):** The ruptured follicle forms the corpus luteum, producing progesterone to prepare the uterus for potential pregnancy.

### Hormonal Impacts

*   **Estrogen:** Often associated with increased energy, improved mood, and clear skin during the follicular phase.
*   **Progesterone:** Dominant in the luteal phase, it can cause a slight rise in body temperature and is sometimes linked to PMS symptoms like bloating or mood changes.

## 3. Top Tracking Strategies

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest form of tracking. By noting the start and end dates of your period, you can calculate your average cycle length. This is the foundation of [LunaCycle](/)’s prediction algorithm.

### 2. Basal Body Temperature (BBT)
Tracking your body temperature immediately upon waking. A slight spike in BBT confirms that ovulation has occurred.

### 3. Cervical Mucus Monitoring
Observing changes in cervical fluid throughout the cycle. It typically becomes clear, slippery, and stretchy (like raw egg whites) approaching ovulation.
