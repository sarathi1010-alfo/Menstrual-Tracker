# What is Cycle Length Variability?

Cycle length variability refers to the fluctuations in the number of days between your menstrual periods. While we often hear about the "perfect" 28-day cycle, very few people have a cycle that is exactly the same length every single month.

## How Does Variability Work?
The length of your cycle is determined by the timing of ovulation. The first half of your cycle (the follicular phase) can vary significantly based on stress, nutrition, or sleep. The second half (the luteal phase) is usually much more stable. Therefore, if you ovulate late, your entire cycle will be longer.

## Real-World Example
A user's history for the last 4 months:
- Month 1: 28 days
- Month 2: 31 days
- Month 3: 26 days
- Month 4: 29 days
This user has a variability of 5 days (the difference between 31 and 26). This is considered a very regular and healthy pattern.

## Why is it Important?
- **Prediction Accuracy:** Trackers like LunaCycle use your average cycle length to predict your next period. If you have high variability, predictions may be less accurate.
- **Health Indicator:** A sudden increase in variability (e.g., cycles suddenly jumping to 45 or 60 days) can be a sign of an underlying health issue like PCOS or thyroid dysfunction.

## Variation Categories

| Variation | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| 0-5 Days | Highly Regular |
| 5-9 Days | Normal |
| 10+ Days | Irregular |

*Disclaimer: If your cycle varies by more than 10 days consistently, or if you go more than 90 days without a period, consult a healthcare professional.*
