Learn the most effective ways to track your menstrual cycle, from simple calendar logging to observing biological markers.

## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for Your Autonomy

Knowledge is power. When you track your cycle, you stop being surprised by your period and start understanding your body's unique patterns. It allows for better family planning, health monitoring, and emotional self-care.

## How to Start Tracking (Step-by-Step)

1. **Mark Day 1**: As soon as you see red blood, log it in LunaCycle. This is the start of your cycle.
2. **Log the End**: Note the last day of your period to understand your flow length.
3. **Daily Check-ins**: Spend 30 seconds logging your mood, energy, and any physical symptoms.
4. **Identify Ovulation**: Look for signs like "egg white" cervical mucus or use ovulation tests.

## Top 3 Tracking Methods

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest way. You track the dates of your period to predict future ones based on your average cycle length.

### 2. Symptothermal Method
A more advanced method involving tracking your Basal Body Temperature (BBT) and cervical mucus to pinpoint ovulation.

### 3. Hormone Testing
Using Ovulation Predictor Kits (OPKs) to detect the LH surge in your urine.

## Common Tracking Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Only logging the period | Missing the "silent" phases | Log mood and energy every day |
| Forgetting to log spotting | Incorrect cycle length | Log any bleeding, even if it's light |
| Relying on "standard" apps | Data privacy risks | Use a local-first app like LunaCycle |
