## Empowering Yourself Through Data

Tracking your cycle is about more than just avoiding surprises. It's about gaining a deeper understanding of your body's unique rhythms. In a world where health data is often commodified, using a [privacy-first tracker](/features) allows you to gain these insights without compromising your personal information.

## Step 1: Log Your Period Dates
The foundation of tracking is recording the start and end of your menstruation.
-   **Start Date:** This is 'Day 1' of your cycle.
-   **End Date:** Helps determine the length of your period, which is an important health indicator.

Use the [LunaCycle Tracker](/tracker) to tap these dates as they happen.

## Step 2: Monitor Physical Symptoms
Your body provides clues about where you are in your cycle through various symptoms.
-   **Cervical Mucus:** Look for changes in consistency (dry vs. sticky vs. slippery).
-   **Basal Body Temperature (BBT):** A slight rise in waking temperature usually indicates ovulation has occurred.
-   **Energy & Pain:** Note any cramps, headaches, or energy fluctuations in your [Daily Log](/tracker).

## Step 3: Track Emotional Shifts
Hormones significantly impact mood. By logging how you feel—whether you're feeling social, irritable, or anxious—you can begin to predict these shifts in future cycles.
