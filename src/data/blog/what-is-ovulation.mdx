# What is Ovulation?

**Ovulation is the brief phase of the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from the ovary into the fallopian tube. It represents the peak of fertility and is the only time during the cycle when an egg can be fertilized.**

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a spike in luteinizing hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg. This egg travels down the fallopian tube toward the uterus. The egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours. If sperm is present in the reproductive tract (sperm can live up to 5 days), fertilization can occur.

## Real-World Example

In a standard 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs on Day 14. A woman tracking her cycle might notice a slight rise in basal body temperature and egg-white, slippery cervical mucus around Day 13 and Day 14, indicating ovulation is happening.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for reproduction. Without ovulation, natural conception is impossible. Tracking ovulation helps those trying to conceive and those practicing natural family planning.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 21-35 days (entire process) | 12-24 hours (specific event) |
| **Purpose** | Prepare body for potential pregnancy | Release an egg for fertilization |
| **Key Hormones** | Estrogen, Progesterone, LH, FSH | Luteinizing Hormone (LH) surge |
