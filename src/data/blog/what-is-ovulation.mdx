## Definition

**Ovulation** is the phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube. It is a critical event for reproduction, as this is when the egg is available to be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a sudden spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), known as the "LH surge." This surge causes the mature follicle in the ovary to rupture and release the egg.

The egg then travels down the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized. If fertilization does not occur within 12 to 24 hours, the egg disintegrates, and the uterine lining will eventually shed during menstruation.

## Real-World Example of Tracking Ovulation

If you are trying to conceive, pinpointing ovulation is crucial. Women often use methods like tracking Basal Body Temperature (BBT) or observing changes in cervical mucus to identify when ovulation is imminent. ## Ovulation vs. Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Timing** | Middle of the cycle (roughly) | Start of the cycle |
| **Hormonal Trigger** | LH Surge | Drop in Progesterone & Estrogen |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility | Low fertility (but not impossible) |
