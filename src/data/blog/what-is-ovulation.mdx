Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from one of the ovaries. It is a critical event in the menstrual cycle, marking the time when a woman is most fertile and able to conceive.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a sudden surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg then travels down the tube towards the uterus, available for fertilization by sperm. If fertilization doesn't occur within 12 to 24 hours, the egg dissolves.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around Day 14. A woman might notice her cervical mucus becoming clear, slippery, and stretchy (like raw egg whites) a few days prior. She might also experience a slight twinge of pain on one side of her lower abdomen, known as mittelschmerz.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the prerequisite for natural pregnancy. Even if you aren't trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a sign of hormonal health and a functioning endocrine system. It triggers the production of progesterone, which is essential for healthy bones, heart, and mood.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What it is** | The exact release of the egg | The days conception is possible |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | Roughly 6 days |
| **Timing** | Middle of the cycle | Includes the days leading up to ovulation |
