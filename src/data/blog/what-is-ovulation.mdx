## How Does Ovulation Work?

During the follicular phase, a follicle in the ovary matures. A surge in luteinizing hormone (LH) triggers the follicle to rupture and release the egg into the fallopian tube.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually happens around day 14. You might notice a clear, stretchy cervical mucus resembling raw egg whites a few days prior.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the defining event for fertility. Without it, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking it is essential for both trying to conceive and natural family planning methods.

## Ovulation vs Luteal Phase

| Feature | Ovulation | Luteal Phase |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | 12-16 days |
| **Hormone** | LH surge | Progesterone dominance |
| **Purpose** | Release the egg | Maintain the uterine lining |
