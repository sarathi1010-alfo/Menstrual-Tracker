Ovulation is the process in a female's menstrual cycle where a mature ovarian follicle ruptures and discharges an ovum (also known as an egg). This event marks the period of peak fertility.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube, where it awaits possible fertilization by sperm. If fertilization does not occur within 12 to 24 hours, the egg dissolves.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a textbook 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around Day 14. However, if her cycle is 32 days long, ovulation might happen around Day 18. Physical signs often include a slight rise in basal body temperature and clear, stretchy cervical mucus.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the central event for reproduction; without it, natural pregnancy is impossible. Furthermore, consistent ovulation is a strong indicator of hormonal balance and overall endocrine health.

## Ovulation vs Menstrual Cycle

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstrual Cycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What it is** | The release of an egg. | The entire monthly process of preparation. |
| **Length** | A single moment; egg lives 12-24 hrs. | Typically 21-35 days. |
| **Significance** | Determines peak fertility window. | Overall indicator of reproductive health. |
