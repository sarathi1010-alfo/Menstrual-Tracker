## Definition
Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from the ovary, moves down the fallopian tube, and is available to be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does It Work?
Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases the egg. This usually happens around the midpoint of the menstrual cycle.

## Real-World Example
If a woman has a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around day 14. During this time, she might notice egg-white cervical mucus or a slight twinge in her lower abdomen.

## Why Is It Important?
It is the defining event required for conception. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur.
