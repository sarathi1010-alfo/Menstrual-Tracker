## What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube. It is triggered by a sudden surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) and is the only time during the cycle when fertilization can occur. The egg survives for just 12 to 24 hours.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

During the follicular phase, estrogen rises, thickening the uterine lining. Once estrogen reaches a threshold, it signals the brain to release a surge of LH, which causes the dominant follicle to rupture and release the egg.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a 30-day cycle, she will likely ovulate around Day 16. In the days leading up to Day 16, she may notice clear, slippery cervical mucus resembling raw egg whites.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

It is the cornerstone of fertility. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking it is essential for both those trying to conceive and those practicing natural family planning.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| Hormone | LH Peak | Low Estrogen & Progesterone |
| Result | Egg release | Uterine lining shedding |
| Duration | 12-24 hours | 2-7 days |
