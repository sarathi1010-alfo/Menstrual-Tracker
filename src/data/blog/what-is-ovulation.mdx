## The Process of Ovulation

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. This is the culmination of the follicular phase.

## Identifying Ovulation

Many people notice physical changes around ovulation:

-   **Cervical Mucus:** Discharge often becomes clear, stretchy, and resembles raw egg whites.
-   **Basal Body Temperature:** A slight sustained rise in resting temperature occurs after ovulation.
-   **Ovulation Pain (Mittelschmerz):** Some experience a mild twinge or cramp on one side of the lower abdomen.
