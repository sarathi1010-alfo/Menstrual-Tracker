Ovulation is the process where a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube. It is a critical event in the menstrual cycle and the specific time when a woman is most fertile and can conceive.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg. This egg travels down the fallopian tube toward the uterus. It survives for only 12 to 24 hours. If it is fertilized by sperm during this time, pregnancy can occur.

## Real-World Example

In a standard 28-day cycle, ovulation usually happens around Day 14. If a woman wants to conceive, she should aim to have intercourse in the days leading up to and including Day 14.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for reproduction. Even if you aren't trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a sign of healthy hormone function and overall well-being. Tracking ovulation using tools like [LunaCycle](/) helps you understand your fertility.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Event** | Release of an egg | Shedding of the uterine lining |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle (Day 1) |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility | Lowest fertility |
