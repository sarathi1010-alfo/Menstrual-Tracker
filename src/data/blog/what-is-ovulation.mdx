Definition: Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube. It is the critical event required for conception to occur.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases an egg, which travels down the fallopian tube where it can be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs on Day 14. If a cycle is 32 days long, ovulation likely occurs around Day 18.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Without ovulation, natural pregnancy cannot occur. It also triggers the production of progesterone, vital for cycle health. Track your ovulation with [LunaCycle](/).

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 12-24 hours | Up to 6 days |
| Meaning | Release of the egg | Days when intercourse can lead to pregnancy |
