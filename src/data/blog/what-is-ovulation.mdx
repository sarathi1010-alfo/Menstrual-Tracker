Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from one of the ovaries. It is a critical, central event in the menstrual cycle, occurring roughly halfway through, and represents the absolute peak of a woman&apos;s fertility for that month.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a sharp surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg then travels toward the uterus. Once released, the egg is viable for fertilization by sperm for only about 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around day 14. You might notice physical signs like clear, stretchy cervical mucus (resembling raw egg whites), a slight increase in basal body temperature, or a mild twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Without ovulation, natural pregnancy cannot occur. Furthermore, regular ovulation is a sign of hormonal health, as it triggers the production of progesterone in the second half of your cycle, which is vital for bone health and mood regulation.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | The exact release of the egg | The days pregnancy is possible |
| Duration | 12 - 24 hours | Up to 6 days |
| Timing | Usually mid-cycle | Leads up to and includes ovulation day |
