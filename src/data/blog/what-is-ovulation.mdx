Ovulation is the process in the menstrual cycle where a mature ovarian follicle ruptures and discharges an ovum (egg). This event is triggered by a spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH).

## How Does Ovulation Work?
After the follicular phase prepares the egg, the LH surge causes it to break free from the ovary and travel down the fallopian tube.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around day 14. You might notice an increase in clear, stretchy cervical mucus around this time.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
It is the only time during the cycle when conception is possible. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window
| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 12-24 hours | About 6 days |
| Description | The release of the egg | The days leading up to and including ovulation |
