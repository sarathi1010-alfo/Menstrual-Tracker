## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg travels down the tube where it may meet sperm for fertilization.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation often occurs around day 14. However, if a cycle is 32 days long, ovulation is more likely to occur around day 18, as the luteal phase (following ovulation) is typically a stable 14 days.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for conception. Even if you are not trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a sign of healthy hormone production, particularly estrogen and progesterone.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | Start of cycle |
| **Sensation** | Sometimes mild pelvic pain (Mittelschmerz) | Cramping, bleeding |
