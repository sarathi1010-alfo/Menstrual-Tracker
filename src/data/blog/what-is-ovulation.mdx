Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from one of the ovaries. It is a critical event in the menstrual cycle and the defining moment for fertility, typically occurring around the middle of the cycle.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

During the follicular phase, a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) triggers the most mature follicle in the ovary to rupture and release an egg. The egg then travels down the fallopian tube, where it may be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation often happens on or around Day 14. If a woman wants to conceive, she would aim to have intercourse in the days leading up to and including Day 14.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for natural conception. Additionally, confirming ovulation through methods like BBT tracking can reassure a woman about her hormonal health and cycle regularity.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Event** | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | Beginning of the cycle |
