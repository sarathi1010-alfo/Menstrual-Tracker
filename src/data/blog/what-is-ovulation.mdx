Ovulation is the process in the menstrual cycle where a mature ovarian follicle bursts and releases an egg into the fallopian tube. This is the one time during the cycle when fertilization and conception are possible.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

During the follicular phase, estrogen levels rise. When estrogen reaches a certain threshold, it triggers a sudden surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) from the brain. This LH surge causes the dominant follicle in the ovary to rupture and release its egg within 24-36 hours.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around day 14. She might notice clear, stretchy cervical mucus (like raw egg whites) and perhaps a slight twinge of pain on one side of her lower abdomen (mittelschmerz) around this time.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the defining event of the menstrual cycle regarding fertility. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot naturally occur. Furthermore, ovulation triggers the production of progesterone in the luteal phase, which is essential for overall hormonal balance and bone health.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Definition | The release of the egg. | The days pregnancy is possible. |
| Duration | 12-24 hours. | Up to 6 days. |
| Key Factor | Lifespan of the egg. | Lifespan of sperm (up to 5 days) + egg lifespan. |
