## How Does Ovulation Work?
Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. It waits there for 12 to 24 hours to be fertilized by sperm. If fertilization doesn't occur, the egg disintegrates and is shed during your next period.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically happens around Day 14. In the days leading up to it, you might notice your cervical mucus becoming clear and stretchy, like raw egg whites, which helps sperm reach the egg.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Ovulation is the defining event of the menstrual cycle and the absolute requirement for natural pregnancy. Even if you aren't trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a key indicator of good hormonal and overall health.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Release of the egg | Days you can get pregnant |
| Duration | 12-24 hours | About 6 days |
| Timing | Once per cycle | Includes days before ovulation |
