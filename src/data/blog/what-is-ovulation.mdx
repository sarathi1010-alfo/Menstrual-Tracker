Ovulation is the process in the menstrual cycle where a mature ovarian follicle ruptures and discharges an ovum (also known as an egg). This is the necessary biological step for fertilization and pregnancy to occur.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg travels down the tube toward the uterus, waiting to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around Day 14. In the days leading up to it, she might notice an increase in clear, stretchy cervical mucus (similar to raw egg whites), indicating her body is ready for conception.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Without ovulation, natural pregnancy cannot occur. Furthermore, regular ovulation is a sign of hormonal balance and good endocrine health. Anovulatory cycles (cycles where ovulation does not happen) are common in conditions like PCOS.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Timing** | Middle of the cycle | Beginning of the cycle (Day 1) |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility | Lowest fertility |
