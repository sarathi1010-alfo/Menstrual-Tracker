Ovulation is the point in the menstrual cycle when an ovary releases a mature egg. It typically happens about two weeks before the start of the next period and is the most fertile time of the cycle.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

A surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) triggers the ovary to release the egg. The egg then travels down the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

If you have a regular 28-day cycle, you will likely ovulate around Day 14. You might notice physical signs like clear, stretchy cervical mucus (similar to raw egg whites) or a slight twinge on one side of your lower abdomen.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for conception. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur. It is also an indicator of balanced hormones and overall reproductive health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Action | Egg is released | Uterine lining is shed |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Beginning of cycle (Day 1) |
| Fertility | Peak fertility | Very low fertility |
