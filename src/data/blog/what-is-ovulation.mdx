## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a sharp spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the dominant follicle in the ovary ruptures, releasing a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg then travels toward the uterus, available for fertilization by sperm.

## Real-World Example

In a textbook 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs on Day 14. You might notice a slight twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz) and an increase in clear, stretchy cervical mucus resembling raw egg whites.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the absolute prerequisite for natural conception. Even if you are not trying to conceive, confirming ovulation is important because it triggers the production of progesterone, a hormone crucial for bone, heart, and mood health.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Release of the egg | Days conception is possible |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | ~6 days |
| **Timing** | Middle of cycle | Leading up to and including ovulation |
