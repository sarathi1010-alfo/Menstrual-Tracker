# What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the process in a female's menstrual cycle by which a mature ovarian follicle ruptures and discharges an ovum (also known as an egg). Ovulation is an essential part of reproduction and is required for natural conception.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

A surge in luteinizing hormone (LH) triggers the release of the egg. The egg then travels down the fallopian tube, where it may be fertilized by sperm. If fertilization does not occur, the egg disintegrates, and the uterine lining is shed during menstruation.
