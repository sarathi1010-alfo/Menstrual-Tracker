## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is the central event of the menstrual cycle. Throughout the follicular phase (the first half of your cycle), the brain produces Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH), which encourages several follicles in the ovaries to grow. Usually, only one of these follicles becomes dominant and fully matures.

As this dominant follicle grows, it produces increasing amounts of estrogen. When estrogen reaches a certain threshold, it signals the brain to release a massive surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This LH surge is the trigger that causes the mature follicle to rupture and release the egg into the fallopian tube. This is ovulation.

Once released, the egg begins its journey down the fallopian tube toward the uterus. It must be fertilized by sperm within 12 to 24 hours, or it will disintegrate.

## Physical Signs of Ovulation

Many people experience subtle physical changes as they approach and undergo ovulation. Learning to recognize these signs is a key part of cycle awareness:

*   **Cervical Mucus Changes:** As estrogen peaks just before ovulation, cervical fluid becomes clear, stretchy, and slippery, resembling raw egg whites. This fertile mucus helps sperm survive and travel.
*   **Basal Body Temperature (BBT) Shift:** Immediately *after* ovulation, the hormone progesterone causes your resting body temperature to rise slightly (about 0.5°F).
*   **Mittelschmerz:** A mild, one-sided pelvic pain or twinge that some people feel as the follicle ruptures.
*   **Increased Libido:** The hormonal shifts around ovulation often lead to a natural increase in sexual desire.

Consider a couple, Emma and David, who are trying to conceive. Because an egg only lives for 12-24 hours, timing is critical. Emma uses the [LunaCycle Ovulation Calculator](/tools/ovulation-calculator) to get a baseline estimate of when she might ovulate based on her cycle length.

## Ovulation vs. The Fertile Window

It's common to confuse these two terms, but they represent different timeframes.

| Feature | Ovulation | The Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The specific event of the egg being released. | The timeframe during which sex can lead to pregnancy. |
| **Duration** | The egg survives 12-24 hours. | Lasts about 6 days. |
| **Timing** | Typically occurs ~14 days before the next period. | Includes the 5 days *before* ovulation and the day of ovulation. |
| **Biological Factor** | Dependent entirely on the lifespan of the egg. | Dependent on the lifespan of sperm (up to 5 days) *and* the egg. |
