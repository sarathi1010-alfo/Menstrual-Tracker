## Definition

Ovulation is a phase in the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from an ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube. Once released, the egg survives for about 12 to 24 hours. If sperm is present in the reproductive tract during this time, fertilization can occur, leading to pregnancy.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

As the follicular phase progresses, estrogen levels rise, thickening the uterine lining. This estrogen peak triggers a sudden surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) from the brain. The LH surge is the direct catalyst that causes the mature follicle to rupture and release its egg.

## Real-World Example in Cycle Tracking

If you are trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy, identifying ovulation is crucial. You can use our [Ovulation Calculator](/tools/ovulation-calculator) to get an estimate. For more accuracy, users often track physical symptoms like a slight dip then rise in Basal Body Temperature (BBT) or the presence of clear, stretchy cervical mucus to pinpoint this 24-hour window.

## Ovulation vs. Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | Up to 6 days |
| **Event** | The exact moment the egg is released. | The days leading up to and including ovulation. |
| **Why it matters** | The egg must be fertilized in this brief window. | Sperm can survive in the body for up to 5 days, waiting for ovulation. |
