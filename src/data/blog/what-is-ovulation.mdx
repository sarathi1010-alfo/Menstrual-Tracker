## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. Here, it awaits possible fertilization by sperm. If not fertilized, the egg disintegrates, and the body eventually sheds the uterine lining during menstruation.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a 30-day cycle, her luteal phase is typically 14 days. Subtracting 14 from 30, she likely ovulates around Day 16 of her cycle. During this time, she might notice an increase in clear, stretchy cervical mucus.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for conception. Without it, pregnancy cannot occur naturally. Furthermore, regular ovulation is a sign of good hormonal balance and overall health, as it triggers the production of progesterone in the second half of the cycle.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Releasing an egg | Shedding the uterine lining |
| **Hormone Focus** | LH Surge, Estrogen peak | Low Estrogen and Progesterone |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility | Low fertility (usually) |
