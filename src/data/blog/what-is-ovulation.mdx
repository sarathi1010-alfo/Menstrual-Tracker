## How Does Ovulation Work?
Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. If sperm is present, fertilization can occur. If not, the egg dissolves, eventually leading to menstruation.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around day 14. A person might notice clearer, stretchier cervical mucus and perhaps a slight twinge of pain (mittelschmerz) on one side.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Ovulation is the prerequisite for pregnancy. Without it, conception cannot occur naturally. Tracking it is essential for family planning, whether trying to conceive or avoiding pregnancy.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Action | Releasing an egg | Shedding the uterine lining |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle |
| Fertility | Peak fertility | Lowest fertility |
