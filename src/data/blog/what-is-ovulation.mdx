Definition: Ovulation is a critical phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from an ovarian follicle. It usually occurs once a month and is the brief window during which fertilization can happen.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg travels down the tube towards the uterus, where it awaits potential fertilization by sperm.

## Real-World Example

If you have a consistent 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around day 14. You might notice physical signs like clear, stretchy cervical mucus (similar to raw egg whites) and a slight twinge or pain on one side of your lower abdomen.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for reproduction. Without it, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking ovulation, whether you are trying to conceive or avoiding pregnancy, is a core component of cycle tracking tools like [LunaCycle](/).

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Ovulation | Menstruation |
|-----------|--------------|
| Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| Occurs mid-cycle | Occurs at the start of the cycle |
| High estrogen/LH | Low hormones |
