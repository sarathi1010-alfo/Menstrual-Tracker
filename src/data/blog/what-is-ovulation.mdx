## Definition

Ovulation is a critical phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from one of the ovaries and travels down the fallopian tube, making it available for fertilization by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

As the follicular phase progresses, rising estrogen levels eventually trigger a sudden surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) from the pituitary gland. This LH surge causes the dominant ovarian follicle to rupture and release its egg. This event is ovulation. Following release, the egg survives for only about 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example in Fertility Tracking

Knowing when you ovulate is essential for identifying your fertile window. Because sperm can live inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, having intercourse in the days leading up to ovulation maximizes the chance of conception. Trackers like [LunaCycle](/) estimate your ovulation day based on your average cycle length, helping you pinpoint these crucial days.

## Ovulation vs. Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 12 - 24 hours | Up to 6 days |
| **What it is** | The physical release of an egg. | The timeframe when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. |
| **Timing** | ~14 days before next period. | The 5 days leading up to and including ovulation day. |
