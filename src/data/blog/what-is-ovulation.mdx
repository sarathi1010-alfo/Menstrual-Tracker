Ovulation is the biological process where a mature egg is released from one of the ovaries, making fertilization (and pregnancy) possible. It typically occurs around the midpoint of the menstrual cycle.

## How Does Ovulation Work?
Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg then travels toward the uterus, waiting to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around Day 14. You might notice changes like clear, stretchy cervical mucus or a slight twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz).

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Ovulation is the main event of the menstrual cycle. Without it, pregnancy cannot occur. Furthermore, consistent ovulation is a sign of good hormonal health, as the body requires a delicate balance of hormones to trigger the release of an egg.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| **Definition** | The release of the egg | The days when intercourse can lead to pregnancy |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | 5-6 days (including the days before ovulation) |
| **Key Factor** | Egg lifespan | Sperm lifespan (up to 5 days in the reproductive tract) |
