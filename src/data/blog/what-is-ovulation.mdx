# What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the point in your menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from one of your ovaries.

## The Process
Hormones (specifically Luteinizing Hormone or LH) surge, causing the most mature egg to be released from its follicle. It then travels down the fallopian tube toward the uterus.

## When Does it Happen?
In a 28-day cycle, it usually happens around Day 14. However, it can vary greatly from person to person and month to month.

## Signs of Ovulation
- **Changes in Cervical Mucus:** It becomes clear and stretchy.
- **Temperature Rise:** A slight increase in your resting body temperature.
- **Increased Libido:** Many people feel a surge in energy and sex drive.
