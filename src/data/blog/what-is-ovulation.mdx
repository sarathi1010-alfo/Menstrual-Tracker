## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This surge causes a mature follicle in the ovary to rupture and release an egg into the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle, the LH surge happens around day 13, and ovulation occurs on day 14. You might notice clear, stretchy cervical mucus around this time, resembling raw egg whites.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur. It is the central event of the menstrual cycle. Consistent ovulation is also a key indicator of balanced hormones and overall reproductive health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility | Low fertility |
