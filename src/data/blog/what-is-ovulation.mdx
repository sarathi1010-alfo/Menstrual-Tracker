## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. From there, it travels toward the uterus. If sperm are present, fertilization can occur. If not, the egg dissolves within 24 hours.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation often occurs around day 14. A woman might notice changes in her body a few days prior, such as cervical mucus becoming clear, slippery, and stretchy (like raw egg whites), indicating her body is preparing to release the egg.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for getting pregnant. Without it, natural conception cannot occur. Tracking ovulation helps identify the [fertile window](/what-is-fertile-window). Even if you aren't trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a sign of good hormonal health. You can use tools like [LunaCycle](/) to help estimate when it might happen based on cycle history.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| **What happens?** | Egg is released | Uterine lining is shed |
| **When does it happen?**| Mid-cycle | Beginning of cycle (Day 1) |
| **Fertility** | High | Low |
