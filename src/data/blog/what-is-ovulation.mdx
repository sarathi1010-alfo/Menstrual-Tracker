Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from one of the ovaries, occurring roughly once a month during the menstrual cycle.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Each month, hormones (FSH) cause several eggs to mature in the ovaries. Eventually, one "dominant" follicle releases its egg into the fallopian tube. This is triggered by a sudden surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH). The egg lives for about 12-24 hours.

## Real-World Example of Ovulation

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically happens around Day 14. You might notice "stretchy" cervical mucus (like egg whites) or a slight twinge of pain in your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the main event of the menstrual cycle. Without it, you cannot get pregnant naturally. Even if you aren't trying to conceive, ovulation is necessary for the production of progesterone, which supports bone, heart, and breast health.

## Ovulation vs. Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of cycle |
| Hormone | LH Peak | Progesterone Drop |
| Duration | 12-24 Hours | 3-7 Days |
