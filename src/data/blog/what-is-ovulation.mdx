Ovulation is the process in which a mature egg is released from the ovary. This event typically occurs roughly halfway through the menstrual cycle and is triggered by a surge in luteinizing hormone (LH).

## How Does Ovulation Work?
During the follicular phase, a follicle containing an egg matures. When estrogen levels peak, it triggers an LH surge, causing the follicle to rupture and release the egg into the fallopian tube.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around Day 14. You might notice physical signs like a slight twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz) or clear, stretchy cervical mucus.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Ovulation is the cornerstone of fertility; without it, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking when you ovulate is essential for those trying to conceive or trying to avoid pregnancy naturally.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Definition | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Beginning of the cycle (Days 1-5) |
| Sign | Egg-white cervical mucus | Vaginal bleeding |
