Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from one of the ovaries, typically occurring around midway through the menstrual cycle. It is the defining event of fertility during the month.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

A surge in luteinizing hormone (LH) triggers the ovary to release the mature egg. The egg then travels down the fallopian tube, where it can be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle and your period starts on the 1st of the month, ovulation will likely occur around the 14th.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is necessary for conception. Without it, pregnancy cannot occur. It also triggers the production of progesterone, which is vital for a healthy luteal phase.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of cycle |
| Primary event | Egg release | Shedding uterine lining |
