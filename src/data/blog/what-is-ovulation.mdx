## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a single mature egg. The egg travels down the fallopian tube toward the uterus. If sperm is present, fertilization can occur. If not, the egg dissolves within 24 hours.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 30-day cycle, ovulation likely happens around Day 16 (since the luteal phase post-ovulation is almost always 14 days long). You might notice clear, stretchy discharge and feel a slight twinge on one side of your lower abdomen.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the main event of the menstrual cycle. It is the only time pregnancy can occur. Furthermore, healthy ovulation triggers the production of progesterone, a hormone crucial for bone health, heart health, and mood regulation.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What happens** | Egg is released | Uterine lining is shed |
| **When it happens** | Mid-cycle (approx. 14 days before next period) | Start of the cycle (Days 1-5) |
| **Hormone Driver** | LH surge | Drop in estrogen and progesterone |
