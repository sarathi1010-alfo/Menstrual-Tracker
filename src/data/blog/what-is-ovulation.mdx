## How Does Ovulation Work?
An egg matures and is released due to a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH).

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically happens around day 14.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
It is essential for pregnancy to occur.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation
| Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|
| Release of egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
