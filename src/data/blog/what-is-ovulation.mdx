## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg travels down the tube towards the uterus. If sperm is present, fertilization can occur.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around Day 14. However, if a woman has a 32-day cycle, ovulation might occur closer to Day 18. The key is that it usually happens about 14 days before the start of the next period.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for natural conception. Even if you are not trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a sign of healthy hormonal balance and contributes to bone, heart, and breast health through the production of progesterone.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| **Event** | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility | Very low fertility |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | 2-7 days |
