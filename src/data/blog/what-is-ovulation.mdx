## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg travels down the tube where it may meet sperm for fertilization. If unfertilized within 12-24 hours, the egg dissolves.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation likely occurs around Day 14. If you have a 32-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around Day 18 (since the luteal phase post-ovulation is relatively constant at 14 days).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the central event of the menstrual cycle and is absolutely necessary for natural conception. The presence of regular ovulation is also a strong indicator of balanced hormones and good overall health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Release of an egg. | Shedding of the uterine lining. |
| **Timing** | Middle of the cycle. | Beginning of the cycle (Day 1). |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility. | Lowest fertility. |
