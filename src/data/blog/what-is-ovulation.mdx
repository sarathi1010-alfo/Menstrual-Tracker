Ovulation is the process in the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from one of the ovaries. It is the crucial window for fertilization and occurs when luteinizing hormone (LH) surges.

## How Does Ovulation Work?
An ovarian follicle ruptures and releases an egg into the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, an LH surge typically happens around day 13, triggering ovulation on day 14.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Without ovulation, natural conception is impossible. It is the centerpiece of the fertile window.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Action | Egg release | Uterine lining shedding |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of cycle |
