Ovulation is the physiological process where a mature egg is released from the ovary into the fallopian tube. It is the only time during the menstrual cycle when fertilization (and pregnancy) can occur.

## How Does Ovulation Work?
Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the dominant follicle in the ovary ruptures to release an egg. The egg survives for only 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around day 14. You might notice egg-white consistency cervical mucus or feel a slight twinge on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz).

## Why is Ovulation Important?
It is the defining event of fertility. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot happen naturally. It also triggers the production of progesterone, which is vital for bone and heart health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of cycle |
| Primary Hormone | LH Surge | Estrogen/Progesterone drop |
