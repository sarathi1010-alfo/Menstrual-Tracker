## What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from an ovary. This egg travels down the fallopian tube, where it can be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

As the follicular phase progresses, estrogen levels rise, triggering a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This LH surge prompts the ovary to release a mature egg.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, you might ovulate around day 14. You might notice signs like a slight pinch in your side, increased basal body temperature, or changes in cervical mucus.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for fertility; without it, pregnancy cannot occur. It is the defining event of the fertile window.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle |
