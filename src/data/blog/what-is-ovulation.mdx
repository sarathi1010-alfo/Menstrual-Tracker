## How Does Ovulation Work?
Ovulation is the central event of the menstrual cycle. Following the follicular phase, a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) causes a mature ovarian follicle to rupture and release an egg (ovum) into the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example
In a typical 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs on or around Day 14. However, if a woman has a longer 32-day cycle, ovulation is more likely to occur around Day 18, as the luteal phase following ovulation is generally a consistent 14 days.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Ovulation is absolutely essential for natural conception. Furthermore, regular ovulation is a strong indicator of good hormonal and reproductive health, even if a person is not actively trying to conceive.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | ~28 days (21-35 days) | 12-24 hours |
| Purpose | Prepare the entire body for potential pregnancy | The specific event of releasing a mature egg |
| Frequency | Monthly | Once per cycle |
