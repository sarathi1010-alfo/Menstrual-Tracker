Ovulation is the process where a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle. It is a critical event for reproduction and typically occurs once per menstrual cycle.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a spike in luteinizing hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. Here, it is available to be fertilized by sperm. If fertilization does not occur within 12 to 24 hours, the egg dissolves.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around Day 14. You might notice physical signs such as a slight twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen or changes in cervical mucus (becoming clear and stretchy).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Without ovulation, natural conception cannot occur. It is the defining moment of the fertile window and a key indicator of healthy hormonal function.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Ovulation | Menstruation |
|-----------|--------------|
| Release of an egg. | Shedding of the uterine lining. |
| Mid-cycle event. | Start of the cycle. |
