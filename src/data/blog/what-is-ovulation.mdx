Ovulation is a specific phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from one of the ovaries. It is the critical event that must occur for a natural pregnancy to be possible, defining the window of peak fertility.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg then travels toward the uterus. If sperm is present in the fallopian tube, fertilization can occur. If not fertilized, the egg dissolves after 12-24 hours.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around Day 14. A woman might notice clear, stretchy cervical mucus (similar to raw egg whites) and perhaps a slight twinge of pain on one side of her lower abdomen (mittelschmerz) around this time.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the only time during the menstrual cycle when a woman can conceive. Tracking it is essential for those trying to get pregnant or those practicing natural family planning.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Release of an egg. | Shedding of the uterine lining. |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle (approx. Day 14). | Start of the cycle (Days 1-5). |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility. | Lowest fertility (generally). |
