Ovulation is the critical event in the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from one of the ovaries, making it available for fertilization by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the dominant follicle in the ovary ruptures, releasing the egg into the fallopian tube. The egg survives for only 12 to 24 hours. If sperm is present, fertilization can occur.

## Real-World Example

If you have a consistent 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around day 14. You might notice physical signs like clear, stretchy cervical mucus (similar to raw egg whites) or a slight twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is strictly necessary for natural conception. Even if you aren't trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a sign of hormonal balance and endocrine health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| **Action** | Releasing an egg | Shedding the uterine lining |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | Beginning of the cycle |
| **Hormonal Trigger** | LH Surge | Drop in Progesterone/Estrogen |
