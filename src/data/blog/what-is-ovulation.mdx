# What is Ovulation?

**Ovulation** is the phase in the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from the ovary. Once released, the egg moves down the fallopian tube and stays there for 12 to 24 hours, where it can be fertilized.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), ovulation typically happens once per cycle. It marks the end of the follicular phase and the beginning of the luteal phase.

## Why is it Important?

Understanding when you ovulate is crucial for:
- **Conception:** It defines your most fertile days.
- **Cycle Health:** Regular ovulation is a sign of healthy hormonal balance.
- **Prediction:** It helps determine when your next period will start.

### Core Principles
- Occurs roughly midway through a standard 28-day cycle.
- The egg lives for 12-24 hours after release.
- Sperm can live for up to 5 days, creating a 'fertile window'.
