**Definition:** Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube, making it available for fertilization.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases an egg. The egg travels down the fallopian tube where it can be fertilized by sperm. It survives for 12-24 hours.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation likely occurs around Day 14. If you have a 32-day cycle, ovulation might occur around Day 18.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for conception. Without it, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking ovulation helps identify your fertile window.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|
| Release of an egg | Shedding of the unfertilized egg and uterine lining |
| Mid-cycle event | Start of the cycle (Days 1-5) |
| High estrogen and LH | Low hormones (estrogen and progesterone drop) |
