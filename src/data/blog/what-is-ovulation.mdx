## What is Ovulation?

**Ovulation** is the critical event in the menstrual cycle where a mature egg (ovum) is released from an ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube. It represents the peak of fertility and is essential for conception.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

During the follicular phase, a hormone called Follicle-Stimulating Hormone (FSH) causes several follicles in the ovaries to develop. Usually, only one becomes fully mature. As this follicle grows, it produces estrogen. When estrogen reaches a certain threshold, it triggers a sudden surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) from the brain.

This LH surge causes the mature follicle to rupture and release the egg. Once released, the egg travels down the fallopian tube, where it can be fertilized by sperm for about 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example of Tracking Ovulation

A user trying to avoid pregnancy might use a secure app like [LunaCycle](/) alongside temperature tracking to estimate their fertile window. By understanding when ovulation is likely to occur, they know when extra precautions are necessary, though calendar-based apps alone should not be relied upon as primary contraception.

## Ovulation vs. Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The specific event of the egg being released. | The timeframe during which intercourse can lead to pregnancy. |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours. | Up to 6 days (5 days leading up to ovulation + the day of). |
| **Significance** | The moment the egg is available for fertilization. | Accounts for the lifespan of sperm (up to 5 days) inside the reproductive tract. |
