**Definition:** Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube. It is the only time during the cycle when an egg is available to be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases an egg. The egg travels down the fallopian tube toward the uterus. If sperm is present, fertilization can occur. If not, the egg dissolves within 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually happens around day 14. You might notice your basal body temperature (BBT) dip slightly and then rise sharply. You may also observe egg-white cervical mucus. You can easily track these specific signs using [LunaCycle](/).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the cornerstone of fertility. Without it, pregnancy cannot occur. Furthermore, healthy ovulation is necessary for the body to produce progesterone, a hormone crucial for bone health, mood regulation, and heart health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| What happens | Egg is released | Uterine lining is shed |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle (Day 1) |
| Symptoms | Clear mucus, slight pain (mittelschmerz) | Bleeding, cramping |
