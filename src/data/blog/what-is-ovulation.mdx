# What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from the surface of the ovary. This usually occurs mid-cycle and is the most fertile time of a woman's menstrual cycle.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

A surge in luteinizing hormone (LH) triggers the ovary to release the most mature egg into the fallopian tube, where it awaits fertilization by sperm.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle, a woman might get a positive LH test on day 13, ovulate on day 14, and experience a basal body temperature spike on day 15.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Without ovulation, natural pregnancy cannot occur. Regular ovulation is also a sign of healthy endocrine function.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Mid-cycle (approx Day 14) | Beginning of cycle (Days 1-5) |
| Action | Egg released | Uterine lining shed |
| Sensation | Sometimes mild pelvic twinge | Often cramps, bleeding |
