Ovulation is the process in which a mature egg is released from the ovary. It is a critical event in the menstrual cycle and represents the time when a woman is most fertile. The egg survives for about 12 to 24 hours after release.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

As estrogen levels rise during the follicular phase, they eventually trigger a surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This LH surge causes the dominant follicle in the ovary to rupture and release the mature egg into the fallopian tube, where it can be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around day 14. However, if your cycle is 32 days long, ovulation might occur closer to day 18, as the luteal phase (following ovulation) is usually a consistent 12-14 days.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for conception. Even if you are not trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a sign of good health and hormonal balance. You can log ovulation symptoms in [LunaCycle](/).

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| Action | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle (Day 1) |
