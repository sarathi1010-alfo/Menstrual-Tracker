Ovulation is a phase in the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from an ovary. This is the only time during the cycle when a woman can get pregnant, making it the peak of the fertile window.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in luteinizing hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. There, it awaits fertilization by sperm. If fertilization does not occur within 12 to 24 hours, the egg disintegrates.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a regular 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around Day 14. However, if her cycle is 35 days, ovulation might occur closer to Day 21.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for reproduction. Understanding when you ovulate helps you identify your fertile window, whether you are trying to conceive or practicing natural family planning.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Release of an egg. | Shedding of the uterine lining. |
| **Timing** | Middle of the cycle. | Beginning of the cycle (Days 1-5). |
| **Fertility** | Highest fertility. | Lowest fertility. |
