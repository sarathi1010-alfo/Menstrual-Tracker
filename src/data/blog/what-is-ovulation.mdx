## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in luteinizing hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. Once released, the egg travels toward the uterus and is available for fertilization for roughly 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example

If you have a classic 28-day cycle, ovulation usually happens around Day 14. Your body will likely show signs like clear, slippery cervical mucus (resembling raw egg whites) and a slight increase in basal body temperature shortly afterward.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the only time during the menstrual cycle when conception is biologically possible. Understanding when you ovulate is crucial whether you are trying to conceive or avoiding pregnancy.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Event | Release of an egg. | Shedding of the uterine lining. |
| Timing | Mid-cycle. | Start of the cycle. |
| Hormonal Driver | LH Surge. | Drop in Estrogen and Progesterone. |
