## What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from the ovary. It typically occurs around the midpoint of the menstrual cycle and is the most fertile time of the month. Once released, the egg travels down the fallopian tube, where it can be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

A surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) triggers the ovary to release the most mature egg. The egg survives for 12 to 24 hours after release. If sperm is present in the fallopian tube during this time, fertilization can occur.

## Real-World Example

If you have a very regular 28-day cycle, ovulation likely occurs around day 14. If you have a 32-day cycle, it likely occurs around day 18 (since the luteal phase, the period after ovulation, is consistently about 14 days long).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for conception. Without it, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking ovulation also helps diagnose potential fertility or hormonal issues, such as PCOS.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Release of an egg. | Shedding of the uterine lining. |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle. | Start of the cycle. |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility. | Lowest fertility. |
