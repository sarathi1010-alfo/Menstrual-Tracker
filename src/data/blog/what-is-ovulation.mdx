## What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the process in the menstrual cycle where a mature follicle bursts and releases an egg (ovum) from one of the ovaries. Once released, the egg travels down the fallopian tube where it may be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a sudden spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH). After release, the egg survives for only 12 to 24 hours. If it is not fertilized within this window, it dissolves, and the body prepares to shed the uterine lining (menstruation).

## Real-World Example

If you have a 30-day cycle, you will likely ovulate around Day 16 (since the luteal phase post-ovulation is typically a constant 14 days). Your fertile window would open several days *before* Day 16.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for pregnancy. Without ovulation, fertilization cannot occur. Tracking ovulation is also a key indicator of hormonal health and cycle regularity.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The release of the egg. | The days a woman can get pregnant. |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours. | About 6 days. |
| **Timing** | Middle of the cycle. | Includes the days leading up to and including ovulation. |
