## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This surge causes the dominant follicle in the ovary to rupture and release a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg then travels down the tube toward the uterus, where it waits to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around Day 14. You might notice physical signs like a slight twinge or pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz) and an increase in clear, stretchy cervical mucus resembling raw egg whites.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the defining event of the menstrual cycle regarding fertility. Without it, pregnancy is impossible. Furthermore, regular ovulation ensures adequate production of progesterone during the second half of your cycle, which is essential for overall hormonal balance and bone health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| **What happens** | Egg is released | Uterine lining sheds |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility | Lowest fertility |
| **Discharge** | Clear, stretchy mucus | Blood and tissue |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle |
