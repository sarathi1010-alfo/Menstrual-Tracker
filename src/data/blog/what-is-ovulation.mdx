# What is Ovulation?

**Definition:** Ovulation is the biological process where a mature egg is released from the ovary, traveling down the fallopian tube where it can be fertilized by sperm. It typically occurs once per menstrual cycle.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

A surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) triggers the ovary to release the egg. Once released, the egg survives for only 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a regular 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around day 14. Signs might include a slight increase in basal body temperature and clear, stretchy cervical mucus.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the only time during the menstrual cycle when a woman can get pregnant. Tracking it is essential for those trying to conceive or avoiding pregnancy naturally.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Releasing an egg | Shedding the uterine lining |
| **Fertility** | Peak fertility | Lowest fertility |
