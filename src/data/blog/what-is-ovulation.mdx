Ovulation is a specific event within the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from an ovary. Once released, the egg travels down the fallopian tube and is available to be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a spike in luteinizing hormone (LH). The egg survives for about 12 to 24 hours after release. If it is not fertilized, the body eventually sheds the uterine lining, resulting in a period.

## Real-World Example of Ovulation Tracking

Understanding when you ovulate is key whether you are trying to conceive or avoid pregnancy. You can track ovulation using methods like basal body temperature (BBT), cervical mucus observations, or apps like [LunaCycle](/) to estimate your window based on past cycle lengths.

## Ovulation vs. Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Duration | ~12-24 hours. | ~6 days. |
| Definition | The actual release of the egg. | The days leading up to and including ovulation when pregnancy is possible. |
