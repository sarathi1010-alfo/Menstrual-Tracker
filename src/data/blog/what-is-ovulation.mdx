Ovulation is a phase in the menstrual cycle when an ovary releases a mature egg (ovum). It is the crucial event required for natural conception to occur.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

During the first half of your cycle, follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) causes several follicles in the ovaries to mature. One of these will become dominant. A surge in luteinizing hormone (LH) then triggers this dominant follicle to release its egg. The egg travels down the fallopian tube, where it may meet sperm and be fertilized.

## Real-World Example

If you have a regular 28-day cycle, you will likely ovulate around Day 14. This is the peak time to try to conceive if you are actively trying to get pregnant.

## Ovulation vs. The Fertile Window

| Concept | Description | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Ovulation** | The actual release of the egg. | 12-24 hours |
| **Fertile Window** | The days leading up to and including ovulation when pregnancy is possible due to sperm survival. | About 6 days |
