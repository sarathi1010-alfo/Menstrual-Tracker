## How Does Ovulation Work?
Rising estrogen levels during the follicular phase trigger a spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This LH surge causes the dominant follicle to rupture and release an egg.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around Day 14. You might notice clear, stretchy cervical mucus (like raw egg whites) during this time.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking it using [LunaCycle](/) is critical for both achieving and avoiding pregnancy.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window
| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Release of the egg | Days intercourse can lead to pregnancy |
| Length | 12-24 hours | Approx. 6 days (5 days before + ovulation day) |
