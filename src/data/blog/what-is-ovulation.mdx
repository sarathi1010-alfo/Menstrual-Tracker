## Definition
Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from the ovary. It typically occurs around the middle of your menstrual cycle and is the crucial event that makes pregnancy possible.

## How Does Ovulation Work?
Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), an ovarian follicle ruptures and releases an egg into the fallopian tube, where it awaits fertilization.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, ovulation often occurs around Day 14. You might notice a slight dip in basal body temperature followed by a rise, and your cervical mucus may become clear and stretchy, like raw egg whites.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Without ovulation, natural conception is impossible. It is the core of your fertile window. Furthermore, regular ovulation is a sign of good hormonal health.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation
