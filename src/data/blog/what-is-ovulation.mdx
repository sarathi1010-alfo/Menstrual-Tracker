# What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the biological process where a mature follicle in the ovary ruptures and releases an egg into the fallopian tube. It is the only time during the menstrual cycle when pregnancy can occur.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

As estrogen levels peak during the follicular phase, the brain releases a surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This 'LH surge' triggers the release of the egg within 24 to 36 hours. Once released, the egg travels toward the uterus, where it can be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example of Ovulation

If you have a 28-day cycle, you likely ovulate around Day 14. By using an [Ovulation Calculator](/tools/ovulation-calculator), you can estimate this date and observe symptoms like 'egg-white' cervical mucus to confirm your fertile window.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is necessary for natural conception. Additionally, the process of ovulation triggers the production of progesterone, which is essential for bone health, heart health, and mood stabilization in the second half of the cycle.

## Ovulation vs. Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 12–24 hours | ~6 days |
| **Explanation** | The actual release of the egg | The time sperm can survive + egg life |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | 5 days before ovulation + day of |
