Ovulation is the process in the menstrual cycle where a mature follicle ruptures and releases a viable egg from the ovary down into the fallopian tube, making fertilization possible.

## How Does Ovulation Work?
Prompted by a surge in luteinizing hormone (LH), the ovary releases the egg. The egg then travels down the fallopian tube where it can meet sperm. If unfertilized within 24 hours, it dissolves.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 30-day cycle, you will likely ovulate around day 16 (30 minus a typical 14-day luteal phase). Knowing this helps target the fertile window accurately.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Ovulation is the central event of the menstrual cycle for reproduction. Without it, natural conception cannot occur. Tracking signs like cervical mucus changes using [LunaCycle](/) helps identify when it's happening.
