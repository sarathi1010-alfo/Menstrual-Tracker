Ovulation is the brief phase in the [menstrual cycle](/blog/what-is-menstrual-cycle) when a mature egg is released from an ovarian follicle. It is the defining event of the cycle, as it determines fertility for that month. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a sudden spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), orchestrated by the brain after estrogen levels reach a critical peak during the late [follicular phase](/blog/what-is-follicular-phase). The LH surge causes a mature follicle on the ovary to rupture and release an egg into the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized by sperm. The egg only survives for 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a 30-day cycle, she will likely ovulate around Day 16. In the days leading up to Day 16, she might notice her cervical mucus becoming clear, stretchy, and slippery (like raw egg whites). On Day 16, a single egg is released. If she is tracking with [LunaCycle](/), the app will highlight this as her peak fertility day.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is critical for two reasons: reproduction and hormone production. It is the only time an egg is available for fertilization. Furthermore, the empty follicle left behind (the corpus luteum) begins producing progesterone, a hormone essential for maintaining a healthy uterine lining and regulating mood and energy in the second half of the cycle.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

While related, ovulation and the fertile window describe different timeframes.

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The release of the egg. | The days intercourse can lead to pregnancy. |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours. | Up to 6 days. |
| **Timing** | Happens on a single day. | Includes the 5 days before ovulation and the day of. |
| **Focus** | The lifespan of the egg. | The lifespan of sperm (up to 5 days) + the lifespan of the egg. |
