**Ovulation is a crucial phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube. It is the only time during the cycle when conception can occur.**

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Following the follicular phase, a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) triggers the ovary to release a mature egg. Once released, the egg travels down the fallopian tube, where it may be fertilized by sperm. If unfertilized within 12 to 24 hours, the egg disintegrates.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a regular 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around day 14. During this time, she might notice an increase in clear, stretchy cervical mucus and a slight dip followed by a rise in her basal body temperature.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for reproduction. It also indicates healthy ovarian function and a balanced endocrine system. Lack of ovulation (anovulation) is a primary cause of infertility and a symptom of conditions like PCOS.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What it is** | Release of the egg | Days conception is possible |
| **Length** | 12-24 hours | Up to 6 days |
| **Includes** | Just ovulation day | The days leading up to and including ovulation |
