## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the dominant follicle in the ovary ruptures, releasing a mature egg into the fallopian tube where it awaits potential fertilization by sperm.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a 30-day cycle, she will likely ovulate around day 16 (30 minus a 14-day luteal phase). She might notice stretchy cervical mucus and a slight twinge of pain on one side of her lower abdomen.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

It is the only time during the menstrual cycle when conception can occur. Even if you aren't trying to conceive, regular ovulation is a sign of good health.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | Up to 6 days |
