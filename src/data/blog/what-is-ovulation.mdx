## How Does Ovulation Work?

During the first half of your cycle, follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) causes several egg follicles to mature. Usually, only one becomes dominant. When estrogen levels peak, it triggers a sudden surge of luteinizing hormone (LH). This LH surge causes the dominant follicle to rupture, releasing the mature egg.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around Day 14. You might notice changes in your body, such as clear, stretchy cervical mucus (resembling raw egg whites) and perhaps a slight twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for natural conception; without an egg, pregnancy cannot occur. Furthermore, ovulation is the only way your body naturally produces progesterone (via the corpus luteum), a hormone vital for bone, heart, and brain health, even if you are not trying to conceive.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | Up to 6 days |
| **What happens?** | The egg is released and available. | The time sperm can survive waiting for the egg. |
| **Timing** | Typically one specific day. | Leads up to and includes ovulation day. |
