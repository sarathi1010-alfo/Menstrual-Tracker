Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from the ovary, typically occurring midway through the menstrual cycle, marking the most fertile time.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a spike in luteinizing hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually happens around day 14.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

It is essential for natural conception and indicates healthy endocrine function.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Ovulation

| Menstrual Cycle | Ovulation |
| --------------- | --------- |
| The entire 28-day process | The 1-day event of egg release |
