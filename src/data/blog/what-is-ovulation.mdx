## What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from one of the ovaries. It is a critical event in the menstrual cycle, marking the peak of fertility. The egg then travels down the fallopian tube, where it can be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Rising estrogen levels during the follicular phase trigger a sudden spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This LH surge causes the dominant follicle in the ovary to rupture and release the egg.

## Real-World Example

If you have a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around Day 14. You might notice changes like clear, stretchy cervical mucus (similar to raw egg whites) or experience a slight twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz).

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for conception. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur naturally. Regular ovulation is also a sign of a healthy endocrine system.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Release of the egg | Days when sex can lead to pregnancy |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours | Up to 6 days |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | 5 days before + day of ovulation |
