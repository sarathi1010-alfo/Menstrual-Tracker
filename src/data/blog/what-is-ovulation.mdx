## What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the main event of the menstrual cycle. It is the physiological process where a mature ovarian follicle ruptures and discharges an ovum (also known as an egg). This egg then travels down the fallopian tube, where it may be fertilized by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), which is secreted by the pituitary gland in the brain. Once released, the egg has a short lifespan—it must be fertilized within 12 to 24 hours. If it is not fertilized, it dissolves, and the uterine lining will eventually shed, resulting in a period.

## Real-World Example in Tracking

If you are trying to conceive or simply want to understand your body better, tracking ovulation is key. ### Ovulation vs. the Fertile Window

| Concept | Definition | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ovulation** | The specific release of the egg. | 12-24 hours |
| **Fertile Window** | The days leading up to and including ovulation when pregnancy is possible. | ~6 days |
