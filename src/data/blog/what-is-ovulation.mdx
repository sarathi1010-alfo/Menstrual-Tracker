Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from the ovary. It is the crucial event that defines your fertile window and makes conception possible.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

During the first half of your cycle (the follicular phase), hormones stimulate the growth of follicles in the ovaries. Typically, one follicle becomes dominant and matures into an egg. A surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH) triggers the release of this egg from the ovary and into the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized.

## Real-World Example of Identifying Ovulation

If you are tracking your cycle to understand your fertility, you might notice clear, stretchy cervical mucus around day 14 of a 28-day cycle. Using [LunaCycle](/), you can log this symptom, which often aligns with the app's predicted ovulation day, helping you pinpoint your most fertile time.

## Ovulation vs. Fertile Window

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ovulation** | The single event of the egg being released (lasts 12-24 hours). |
| **Fertile Window** | The days leading up to and including ovulation when pregnancy is possible (lasts about 6 days). |
