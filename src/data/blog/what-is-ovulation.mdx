Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature ovarian follicle ruptures and discharges an ovum (egg). This is the only time during the cycle when a woman can get pregnant. It typically occurs midway through the cycle.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

As the follicular phase progresses, estrogen levels rise. When estrogen reaches a certain threshold, it triggers a rapid spike in Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This LH surge causes the ovary to release a mature egg into the fallopian tube, where it awaits fertilization.

## Real-World Example

If you have a consistent 28-day cycle, you will likely ovulate around day 14. However, if you have a 32-day cycle, you might not ovulate until day 18, as the phase leading up to ovulation can vary in length.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for natural conception. Even if you are not trying to conceive, regular ovulation produces progesterone, a hormone crucial for bone, heart, and breast health.

## Ovulation vs Fertile Window

| Feature | Ovulation | Fertile Window |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition** | The release of the egg. | The days pregnancy is possible. |
| **Duration** | 12-24 hours. | Approx. 6 days (5 days before + ovulation day). |
