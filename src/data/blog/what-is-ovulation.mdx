Ovulation is the process where a mature egg is released from one of the ovaries. It is a critical event in the menstrual cycle and the essential requirement for pregnancy to occur.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. The egg then travels down the tube toward the uterus. The egg is viable and can be fertilized by sperm for only about 12 to 24 hours after release.

## Real-World Example

In a typical 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around Day 14. You might notice your cervical mucus becomes clear and stretchy (like raw egg whites) a few days prior, and you might feel a slight twinge of pain on one side of your lower abdomen (mittelschmerz) on the day it happens.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the core of fertility. Without it, pregnancy cannot naturally occur. Furthermore, consistent ovulation is a strong indicator of hormonal balance and good overall reproductive health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Release of an egg. | Shedding of the uterine lining. |
| **Timing** | Middle of the cycle (roughly day 14). | Beginning of the cycle (Days 1-5). |
| **Sign** | Changes in cervical mucus, basal temperature. | Vaginal bleeding. |
