Ovulation is the process where a mature egg is released from one of the ovaries. It is a critical event in the menstrual cycle and marks the window of highest fertility.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

As the follicular phase ends, estrogen levels peak, triggering a surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This LH surge causes the dominant follicle in the ovary to rupture and release the mature egg into the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a regular 28-day cycle, ovulation might occur around day 14. She might notice her cervical mucus becoming clear and stretchy, like raw egg whites, a few days leading up to this event.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur. It is the central event of the reproductive cycle. Tracking ovulation is essential for both trying to conceive and natural family planning methods.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Release of an egg | Shedding of uterine lining |
| **Timing** | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle (Days 1-5+) |
| **Primary Hormone** | LH surge | Drop in Progesterone & Estrogen |
