## What is Ovulation?
Ovulation is the process in a female's menstrual cycle where a mature ovarian follicle releases an egg. This typically happens mid-cycle and is the window of peak fertility.

## How Does Ovulation Work?
Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases an egg into the fallopian tube.

## Real-World Example
In a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually occurs around day 14.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
It is required for natural conception and indicates healthy endocrine function.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation
| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Event | Egg release | Uterine lining shedding |
| Fertility | High | Low |
