## What is Ovulation?

Ovulation is the crucial phase of the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from one of the ovaries into the fallopian tube, making it available for fertilization by sperm.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a surge in luteinizing hormone (LH). Once released, the egg survives for about 12-24 hours. If it is not fertilized during this time, it dissolves, and the uterine lining is eventually shed during menstruation.

## Real-World Example of Tracking Ovulation

If you are trying to conceive, pinpointing ovulation is key. You might use ovulation predictor kits (OPKs) to detect the LH surge or track your basal body temperature (BBT) with a digital tool like [LunaCycle](/tracker). By logging these data points, you can identify patterns and anticipate your most fertile days.
