## How Does Ovulation Work?

Ovulation is triggered by a sudden spike in luteinizing hormone (LH). This surge causes a mature follicle in the ovary to rupture and release an egg into the fallopian tube, where it awaits possible fertilization by sperm. The egg remains viable for approximately 12 to 24 hours.

## Real-World Example

A person might notice changes in their body around day 14 of their cycle, such as increased clear, stretchy cervical mucus (resembling raw egg whites) and sometimes a mild twinge of pain on one side of their lower abdomen, known as mittelschmerz.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is the central event of the menstrual cycle and an absolute requirement for natural conception. Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking it helps determine the most fertile days.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| Event | Egg release | Uterine lining shedding |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of the cycle |
