## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a peak in estrogen and a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases a mature egg into the fallopian tube. It is then available to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example

If a woman has a 30-day cycle, she is likely to ovulate around Day 16 (since the luteal phase after ovulation is consistently about 14 days). Tracking with [LunaCycle](/tracker) helps estimate this date.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Without ovulation, pregnancy cannot occur. It is the defining event of fertility and also triggers the production of progesterone, which is vital for overall health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Concept | Function | Timing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ovulation | Releasing an egg for potential fertilization. | Mid-cycle. |
| Menstruation | Shedding the unfertilized egg and uterine lining. | Start of cycle. |
