## Definition
Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle when a mature egg is released from the ovary, making it available to be fertilized by sperm. It typically occurs around the midpoint of the menstrual cycle and is the most fertile time of the month.

## How Does Ovulation Work?
During the follicular phase, hormones stimulate the development of follicles in the ovaries. When one egg matures, a surge in luteinizing hormone (LH) causes the follicle to rupture and release the egg into the fallopian tube, a process known as ovulation.

## Real-World Example
If a woman has a 30-day menstrual cycle, ovulation might occur around day 16 (since the luteal phase, which follows ovulation, is consistently about 14 days long).

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Ovulation is essential for conception. Without the release of an egg, pregnancy cannot occur. Tracking ovulation is the primary focus for those trying to conceive or those practicing natural family planning.

## Ovulation vs Menstrual Cycle
| Feature | Ovulation | Menstrual Cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 12-24 hours | 21-35 days |
| Function | Egg release | Full reproductive preparation cycle |
