## What is Ovulation?

**Definition:** Ovulation is the phase of the menstrual cycle where a mature egg is released from the ovarian follicle into the fallopian tube, making it available for fertilization.

## How Does Ovulation Work?
Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the ovary releases an egg. The egg travels down the fallopian tube where it may meet sperm. If unfertilized within 24 hours, the egg dissolves.

## Real-World Example
If you have a 28-day cycle, you might ovulate on day 14. Leading up to this day, you may notice egg-white cervical mucus and increased libido.

## Why is Ovulation Important?
Ovulation is the only time during the cycle when pregnancy can occur. It is also essential for the production of progesterone, a hormone crucial for overall health.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation
| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
|---|---|---|
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of cycle |
| Primary Action | Egg release | Shedding uterine lining |
