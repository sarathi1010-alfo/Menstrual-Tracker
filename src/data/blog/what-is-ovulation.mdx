Ovulation is a critical event in the menstrual cycle where a mature follicle bursts and releases an egg (ovum) from one of the ovaries. This process is essential for conception.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Leading up to ovulation, the brain releases a surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH). This surge acts as the trigger for the ovary to release the egg. The egg then travels down the fallopian tube, where it waits to be fertilized by sperm.

## Real-World Example of Ovulation in Fertility

If someone is trying to conceive, identifying ovulation is paramount. Because the egg only lives for 12-24 hours, timing intercourse just before or during ovulation maximizes the chances of pregnancy. Conversely, those avoiding pregnancy use ovulation tracking to identify their fertile window, though a basic calendar tracker like [LunaCycle](/tracker) is not sufficient as a primary contraceptive method.

## Ovulation vs. The Fertile Window

| Term | Meaning | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Ovulation** | The single event of releasing the egg. | Occurs on one specific day. |
| **Fertile Window** | The period when intercourse can lead to pregnancy. | Roughly 6 days (the 5 days before ovulation plus the day of). |

Recognizing the signs of ovulation, such as changes in cervical mucus or a slight twinge in the lower abdomen (mittelschmerz), can help you better understand your body's rhythm.
