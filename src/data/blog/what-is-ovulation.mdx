Ovulation is the release of a mature egg from one of the ovaries, occurring when an ovarian follicle ruptures. This critical event typically happens once per menstrual cycle and marks a woman's peak fertility window.

## How Does Ovulation Work?

Triggered by a surge in Luteinizing Hormone (LH), the mature egg travels down the fallopian tube toward the uterus. If sperm is present, fertilization can occur.

## Real-World Example

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation usually happens around day 14. Women might notice changes like clear, stretchy cervical mucus (similar to raw egg whites) or a slight twinge of pain on one side of their lower abdomen.

## Why is Ovulation Important?

Ovulation is essential for pregnancy. Tracking it helps those trying to conceive or avoid pregnancy, and its regular occurrence is a sign of healthy hormonal balance.

## Ovulation vs Menstruation

| Feature | Ovulation | Menstruation |
| ------- | --------- | ------------ |
| Action | Egg release | Shedding uterine lining |
| Timing | Mid-cycle | Start of cycle |
