Hormones like estrogen, progesterone, FSH, and LH orchestrate the menstrual cycle, affecting everything from mood to energy.

## Why Hormones Matter for Cycle Tracking

Tracking your period is actually tracking your hormones. When you log a symptom like "breakouts" or "high energy," you are seeing the direct effect of your internal chemistry. Understanding these messengers allows you to work *with* your body instead of against it.

## The Big Four Hormones (How They Work)

1. **FSH (Follicle-Stimulating Hormone)**: Sent from the brain to tell the ovaries to get an egg ready.
2. **Estrogen**: The "builder" hormone that thickens the uterine lining and boosts your mood.
3. **LH (Luteinizing Hormone)**: The trigger that tells the ovary to release the egg (ovulation).
4. **Progesterone**: The "sustainer" hormone that maintains the uterine lining and prepares for pregnancy.

## Hormonal Milestones in Your Cycle

### The Estrogen Rise (Follicular Phase)
As follicles grow, they release estrogen. You might notice clearer skin, more social energy, and higher libido as you approach ovulation.

### The LH Surge (Ovulation)
A massive spike in LH causes the strongest follicle to rupture and release the egg. This is what ovulation tests (OPKs) look for.

### The Progesterone Peak (Luteal Phase)
After ovulation, the empty follicle becomes the corpus luteum and pumps out progesterone. This can cause bloating and breast tenderness.
