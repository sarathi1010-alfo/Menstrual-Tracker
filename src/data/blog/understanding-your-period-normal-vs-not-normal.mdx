A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding your baseline allows you to spot abnormalities early, which is crucial for early detection and treatment of potential health issues.

## How to Assess Your Period

Track the length of your cycle, the duration of bleeding, the heaviness of your flow, and your pain levels. LunaCycle is a great tool for this.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding when you are not expecting your period.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Requiring frequent changes of sanitary products.

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that interferes with daily activities and isn't relieved by over-the-counter medication.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles that are consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods in a row (if not pregnant or menopausal).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Action |
|---|---|
| Unusually heavy flow | Consult doctor |
| Debilitating cramps | Seek medical advice |
| Bleeding between periods | Schedule an exam |
