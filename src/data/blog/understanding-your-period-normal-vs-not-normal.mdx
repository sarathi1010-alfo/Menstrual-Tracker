## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what is considered "normal" for a menstrual cycle is crucial for early detection of potential health issues. While every woman's cycle is unique, there are medical baselines. Knowing your personal normal—through tracking with [LunaCycle](/)—allows you to quickly spot when something changes.

## How to Assess Your Period

When tracking your cycle, pay attention to these four key factors:
1. **Length:** A normal cycle lasts between 21 and 35 days.
2. **Duration:** Bleeding typically lasts between 2 and 7 days.
3. **Flow Intensity:** Normal flow involves changing a pad or tampon every 3-4 hours.
4. **Pain:** Mild to moderate cramping is common; debilitating pain is not.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstrual window should be evaluated.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding
If you are soaking through products every hour or passing large clots, it's considered heavy menstrual bleeding (menorrhagia).

### 3. Severe Pain
Cramps that prevent you from attending work or school, or pain that radiates, could indicate conditions like endometriosis or fibroids.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
If your cycles consistently fall outside the 21-35 day range, or vary significantly in length from month to month.

### 5. Absence of Period
Going more than 90 days without a period (amenorrhea), and you are not pregnant or menopausal.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---------|--------------------|
| Missing period > 90 days | Consult doctor |
| Bleeding between periods | Consult doctor |
| Debilitating pain | Consult doctor |
| Unusually heavy flow | Consult doctor |
| Sudden, unexplained irregularity | Consult doctor |
