## Why Knowing Normal Matters

For a deeper dive, check out our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) and learn more about [Cycle Phases Explained](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal) and [How to Track Your Cycle](/blog/how-to-track-your-cycle-step-by-step).

What is "normal" can vary from person to person, but establishing your personal baseline is crucial. Knowing what is normal for *you* helps you spot when something is wrong, allowing for early detection of potential health issues.

## How to Assess Your Period

Use [LunaCycle](/) to track the key metrics of your period: cycle length (days between periods), period length (days of bleeding), flow intensity, and pain levels.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Also known as spotting, this should be evaluated if it happens consistently.
### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several consecutive hours.
### 3. Severe pain
Cramps that do not respond to over-the-counter medication and disrupt your daily life.
### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.
### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods in a row (if not pregnant or menopausal).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| Sudden change in cycle regularity | Consult a healthcare provider. |
| Debilitating pain | Seek medical evaluation for conditions like endometriosis. |
| Very heavy bleeding | Consult a doctor to prevent anemia. |
