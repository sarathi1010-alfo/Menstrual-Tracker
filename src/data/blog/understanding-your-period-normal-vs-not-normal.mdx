A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

<MedicalDisclaimer />

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Your menstrual cycle is often considered a "fifth vital sign." Knowing what is normal for your body is the key to early detection of reproductive health issues. If you don't know your baseline, it's difficult to recognize when something is wrong.

## How to Assess Your Period

To assess the health of your period, you need to track four key metrics using a tool like [LunaCycle](/):
- **Length of Cycle:** Count from the first day of your period to the day before your next period begins (normal is 21-35 days).
- **Duration of Bleeding:** How many days the bleeding lasts (normal is 2-7 days).
- **Flow Intensity:** How heavy the bleeding is (normal is changing a pad/tampon every 3-4 hours).
- **Pain Level:** Mild to moderate cramping is common; debilitating pain is not.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal period (intermenstrual bleeding) can be caused by hormonal imbalances, fibroids, or infections.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding (Menorrhagia)
If you are soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several consecutive hours, or passing clots larger than a quarter, your flow is abnormally heavy.

### 3. Severe Pain (Dysmenorrhea)
While some cramping is normal, pain that causes you to miss work or school, or that isn't relieved by standard painkillers, could indicate conditions like endometriosis.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Cycles that frequently fall outside the 21-35 day range, or vary wildly in length from month to month, warrant investigation.

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing three or more periods in a row (if you are not pregnant or menopausal) is a significant warning sign that requires medical evaluation.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No period for 90 days** | Consult a doctor (rule out pregnancy first). |
| **Bleeding > 7 days** | Consult a doctor. |
| **Severe, debilitating pain** | Consult a doctor immediately. |
| **Sudden change in cycle length** | Track for one more cycle; if it persists, consult a doctor. |
