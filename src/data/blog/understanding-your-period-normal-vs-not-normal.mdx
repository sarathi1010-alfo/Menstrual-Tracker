A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what is 'normal' for you is vital for early detection of health issues. Changes in your cycle are often the first sign that something is off balance in your body.

## How to Assess Your Period

Use [LunaCycle](/) to track:
*   **Length:** How many days from the start of one period to the start of the next?
*   **Duration:** How many days do you bleed?
*   **Flow:** How many pads/tampons do you use per day?
*   **Pain:** Rate your cramps on a scale of 1-10.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding when you're not expecting your period warrants a check-up.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through feminine hygiene products rapidly or passing large clots.

### 3. Severe pain
Cramps that over-the-counter medication cannot manage or that cause nausea/vomiting.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles that consistently fall outside the 21-35 day range or vary wildly in length month to month.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing periods (amenorrhea) when not pregnant, breastfeeding, or menopausal.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Bleeding > 7 days | Consult physician |
| Cycle < 21 or > 35 days | Consult physician if persistent |
| Severe pelvic pain | Seek medical evaluation |
