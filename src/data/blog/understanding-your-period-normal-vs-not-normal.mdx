## Why Knowing Normal Matters
Knowing normal matters for the early detection of health issues. What is 'normal' varies slightly from person to person, but understanding the clinical baseline helps you identify when something is truly wrong.

For a broader overview, check out our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) and [Menstrual Cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

## How to Assess Your Period
Track your period's length, flow, pain level, and regularity. By keeping a detailed log in a secure app like [LunaCycle](/), you can easily show your data to a healthcare provider if needed.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch
### 1. Bleeding between periods
Unexplained spotting or bleeding.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through protection rapidly.

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that medication does not relieve or that causes vomiting/fainting.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing multiple periods without being pregnant.

## When to See a Doctor
| Symptom | Action |
|---|---|
| Heavy bleeding (>1 pad/hr) | Seek medical attention |
| Sudden intense pain | See a doctor immediately |
