## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what constitutes a "normal" period is the foundation of reproductive health awareness. Because every body is different, your personal baseline is what matters most. By understanding general medical guidelines and establishing your own patterns through tracking, you can detect health issues early and seek timely medical care.

## How to Assess Your Period

To determine what's normal for you, track these key metrics in [LunaCycle](/):
- **Cycle Length:** Days between Period 1 and Period 2.
- **Duration:** How many days bleeding lasts.
- **Flow:** Light, medium, or heavy (based on product usage).
- **Pain Level:** Mild, moderate, or severe cramping.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting mid-cycle can occasionally happen during ovulation, but unexpected bleeding should be checked by a doctor.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
If you are soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several hours, seek medical advice.

### 3. Severe pain
Debilitating cramps (dysmenorrhea) that aren't relieved by over-the-counter medication are not normal.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days warrant investigation.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods (amenorrhea) when you are not pregnant or menopausal is a clear sign to consult a healthcare provider.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- |
| Missing >3 periods | Schedule a doctor's appointment. |
| Debilitating pain | See a doctor to rule out endometriosis or fibroids. |
| Soaking a pad per hour | Seek immediate medical attention. |
