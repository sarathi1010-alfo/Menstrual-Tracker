## Why Knowing Normal Matters

What is considered "normal" can vary slightly from person to person, but there are established medical baselines. Knowing these baselines helps you identify when something is wrong, allowing for early intervention and treatment of potential health issues.

## How to Assess Your Period

Use [LunaCycle](/) to track the following metrics:
- **Length:** How many days between the start of one period and the start of the next?
- **Duration:** How many days do you bleed?
- **Flow:** Is it light, moderate, or heavy?
- **Pain Level:** Do you experience severe cramps?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Unexplained spotting or bleeding should always be investigated.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
If you need to change your pad or tampon every hour for several consecutive hours.

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that causes you to miss work, school, or social activities.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods in a row (if not pregnant, breastfeeding, or menopausal).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Action |
|---|---|
| Sudden change in cycle length | Monitor for one more cycle, then consult a doctor if it persists |
| Soaking through pads/tampons hourly | Seek immediate medical evaluation |
| Debilitating cramps | Consult a doctor to check for endometriosis or fibroids |
| Bleeding after menopause | Seek immediate medical evaluation |
