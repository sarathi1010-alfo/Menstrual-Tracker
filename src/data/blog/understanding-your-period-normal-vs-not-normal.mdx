## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what is "normal" for your period is a crucial aspect of overall health awareness. Your menstrual cycle is often considered a "fifth vital sign"—a key indicator of your body's well-being. By establishing a baseline of what is typical for you, you are better equipped to notice when things change. Early detection of abnormalities can lead to quicker diagnoses and better management of underlying health conditions. Learn how to establish your baseline with our [step-by-step tracking guide](/blog/how-to-track-your-cycle-step-by-step).

## How to Assess Your Period

Assessing your period involves tracking several key factors using a tool like [LunaCycle](/):
*   **Cycle Length:** The number of days from the start of one period to the start of the next (normally 21-35 days).
*   **Period Duration:** How many days you bleed (normally 2-7 days).
*   **Flow Intensity:** Whether your flow is light, moderate, or heavy. Note if you are soaking through products unusually fast.
*   **Pain Level:** Monitor the severity of cramps or other physical discomforts.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstruation phase should be evaluated.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
If you need to change a pad or tampon every hour for multiple hours, or if you pass large clots, this is considered heavy bleeding (menorrhagia).

### 3. Severe pain
Cramps that are debilitating and interfere with your daily life (dysmenorrhea) are not just a normal part of having a [menstrual cycle](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

### 4. Irregular cycles
Consistently unpredictable cycles, or cycles that regularly fall outside the 21-35 day range, could indicate issues like [PCOS](/conditions/pcos-and-cycle-tracking-guide).

### 5. Absence of period
Missing multiple periods (amenorrhea) when you are not pregnant or menopausal is a clear sign to consult a healthcare provider.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bleeding between periods | Schedule an appointment for an evaluation. |
| Debilitating pain | See a doctor to rule out conditions like endometriosis. |
| Consistently irregular cycles | Discuss with a healthcare provider to investigate potential hormonal imbalances. |
