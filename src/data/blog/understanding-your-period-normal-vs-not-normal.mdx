## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what constitutes a "normal" period for you is crucial for early detection of potential health issues. While there's a range of what's considered typical, significant deviations from your established pattern can be a sign that something needs medical attention.

## How to Assess Your Period

To assess your period, track the following:

*   **Length:** How many days does the bleeding last?
*   **Flow:** Is it light, moderate, or heavy?
*   **Pain Level:** Are cramps mild, manageable, or severe?
*   **Regularity:** Is your cycle length consistent?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Unexplained spotting or bleeding should be evaluated.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through pads or tampons quickly indicates a need for medical consultation.

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that interferes with daily activities is not typical.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Consistently unpredictable cycle lengths can indicate hormonal issues.

### 5. Absence of period
A missed period (amenorrhea) when not pregnant warrants investigation.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- |
| Soaking a pad/tampon every hour | Seek immediate medical evaluation. |
| Debilitating cramps | Consult a healthcare provider. |
