## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what constitutes a "normal" period is crucial for early detection of health issues. Your menstrual cycle is a vital sign. When it functions smoothly, it indicates a healthy endocrine system. When things deviate significantly from the norm, it's often your body's way of signaling that something needs attention, such as hormonal imbalances, stress, or underlying medical conditions.

## How to Assess Your Period

To determine if your period is normal, track these key metrics using [LunaCycle](/):
- **Length:** How many days does the bleeding last? (Normal: 2-7 days)
- **Regularity:** How many days between the start of one period and the start of the next? (Normal: 21-35 days)
- **Flow:** How many pads/tampons are you soaking through?
- **Pain:** Is the pain manageable with over-the-counter medication?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding between normal periods (intermenstrual bleeding) should always be evaluated by a healthcare provider.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding
If you are soaking through one or more tampons/pads every hour for several consecutive hours, seek medical attention.

### 3. Severe Pain
Pain that causes you to miss work or school, or isn't relieved by standard medication, is not normal.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Consistently having cycles shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days warrants investigation.

### 5. Absence of Period
Missing three or more periods in a row (when not pregnant or menopausal) is known as amenorrhea.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| Unusually heavy flow | Consult a doctor promptly to rule out anemia or fibroids. |
| Debilitating cramps | Schedule an appointment to discuss potential endometriosis. |
| Sudden cycle irregularity | Discuss lifestyle changes and request hormonal panels. |
