A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what constitutes a "normal" period for you is vital for early detection of health issues. Because the menstrual cycle is considered a vital sign, significant changes in your bleeding patterns, cycle length, or pain levels are often your body's first way of signaling that something needs attention. By tracking what is normal, you are empowered to advocate for yourself effectively when speaking with healthcare professionals.

## How to Assess Your Period

To accurately assess your period, you need to track four key metrics consistently using a tool like [LunaCycle](/):
*   **Length:** How many days does the bleeding last? (Normal is 2-7 days).
*   **Flow:** How heavy is the bleeding? (Are you changing products frequently?).
*   **Pain Level:** Are cramps mild and manageable, or severe and debilitating?
*   **Regularity:** How many days are between the start of one period and the start of the next? (Normal is 21-35 days).

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding at times other than your period (and not related to ovulation spotting) should always be evaluated.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
If you are soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for a few hours, or passing clots larger than a quarter, your flow is considered unusually heavy (menorrhagia).

### 3. Severe pain
Pain (dysmenorrhea) that causes you to miss work or school, or that isn't relieved by standard painkillers, is a red flag.

### 4. Irregular cycles
While occasional variation is normal, cycles that consistently fall outside the 21-35 day range, or vary wildly in length from month to month, warrant investigation.

### 5. Absence of period
If you miss three or more periods in a row (and are not pregnant or going through menopause), this is known as amenorrhea and requires medical advice.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sudden, severe pain** | Seek immediate medical attention. |
| **Heavy bleeding (soaking 1 product/hr)** | Contact your doctor promptly. |
| **Consistently irregular cycles** | Schedule an appointment to discuss. |
| **Bleeding after menopause** | Contact your doctor immediately. |
