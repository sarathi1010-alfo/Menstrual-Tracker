## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Your menstrual cycle is considered a vital sign. Just like your heart rate or blood pressure, changes in your period can indicate changes in your overall health. Knowing what is "normal" for your unique body allows you to quickly identify when something is "not normal." Early detection of irregularities can lead to faster diagnosis and management of conditions ranging from hormonal imbalances to more serious health issues.

## How to Assess Your Period

The best way to understand your normal is to track it consistently. Using a tool like [LunaCycle](/), you should monitor four main aspects:

1.  **Length of Cycle:** The number of days from Day 1 of one period to Day 1 of the next. Normal is generally 21-35 days.
2.  **Duration of Bleeding:** How many days you bleed. Normal is typically 2-7 days.
3.  **Flow Intensity:** The volume of blood. While subjective, noting if it's light, medium, or heavy helps establish your baseline.
4.  **Pain Levels:** Tracking cramps and other discomforts.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding when you aren't expecting your period can be a sign of hormonal issues, fibroids, or other conditions requiring medical attention.

### 2. Extremely Heavy Bleeding
If you are soaking through pads or tampons every hour, or passing large blood clots, this is considered menorrhagia and needs medical evaluation.

### 3. Severe Pain
While some discomfort is common during the [follicular phase's](/what-is-follicular-phase) start (menstruation), pain that requires you to miss work or school is not normal.

### 4. Highly Irregular Cycles
If your cycle length varies wildly from month to month (e.g., 25 days one month, 45 the next), it could indicate anovulation or conditions like [PCOS](/conditions/pcos-and-cycle-tracking-guide).

### 5. Absence of Period
Missing a period (amenorrhea) when you are not pregnant, breastfeeding, or menopausal should be investigated, especially if you miss three or more in a row.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---------|-------------------|
| No period for 90 days | Consult a doctor immediately |
| Suddenly irregular after being regular | Schedule an appointment |
| Bleeding lasts more than 7 days | Consult a doctor |
| Severe pain not relieved by OTC meds | Seek medical advice |
