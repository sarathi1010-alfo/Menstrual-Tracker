## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what is "normal" for your body and for reproductive health in general is crucial for the early detection of health issues. When you know your baseline, you can quickly identify when something is wrong.

## How to Assess Your Period

Track these key metrics to assess your period's health:
*   **Length:** How many days between the start of one period and the next?
*   **Duration:** How many days do you bleed?
*   **Flow:** How heavy is the bleeding?
*   **Pain Level:** Are cramps manageable or debilitating?
*   **Regularity:** Is the cycle length relatively consistent month-to-month?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Unexplained spotting or bleeding should always be evaluated.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding
Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several consecutive hours.

### 3. Severe Pain
Pain that prevents you from going to work, school, or doing daily activities.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Cycles that frequently jump between very short and very long lengths.

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing three or more periods in a row (if not pregnant or in menopause).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bleeding > 7 days** | Schedule an appointment with your gynecologist. |
| **Severe, debilitating pain** | Seek medical evaluation for conditions like endometriosis. |
| **Sudden irregularity** | Consult a doctor to check thyroid and hormone levels. |
