## Why Knowing Normal Matters
Knowing what is normal for *you* matters for the early detection of health issues. Your menstrual cycle is often considered a "fifth vital sign."

## How to Assess Your Period
To assess your period, track the following with [LunaCycle](/):
-   **Cycle Length:** Number of days between period start dates.
-   **Period Length:** How many days you bleed.
-   **Flow:** Light, medium, or heavy.
-   **Pain Level:** Mild, moderate, or severe cramping.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch
### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding when you aren't expecting your period should be checked.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through feminine hygiene products quickly (every hour) or passing large clots.

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that over-the-counter medication cannot manage and interferes with daily life.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing periods (amenorrhea) when you are not pregnant or menopausal.

## When to See a Doctor
| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| Cycle > 35 days or < 21 days | Consult a healthcare provider. |
| Severe pelvic pain | Seek medical attention promptly. |
| Unexplained missed periods (3+ months) | Schedule an appointment for evaluation. |
