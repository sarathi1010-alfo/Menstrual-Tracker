## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Your menstrual cycle is often considered a "fifth vital sign." Understanding what is normal for your body helps you quickly identify when something is off, allowing for early intervention and peace of mind.

## How to Assess Your Period

Use a tool like [LunaCycle](/) to consistently track:
*   **Cycle Length:** Time between the start of one period and the start of the next (normal is 21-35 days).
*   **Period Duration:** How many days you bleed (normal is 2-7 days).
*   **Flow:** The volume of blood lost.
*   **Pain Level:** The severity of cramps or other discomfort.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Unexplained spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstruation window.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through period products rapidly or passing large blood clots.

### 3. Severe pain
Debilitating cramps that interfere with daily activities.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles that are consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days, or highly unpredictable.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods in a row (if not pregnant or menopausal).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- |
| Bleeding through products < 2 hours | See a doctor promptly |
| Sudden severe pain | Seek immediate medical care |
