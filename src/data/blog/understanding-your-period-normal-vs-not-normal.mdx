A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what is 'normal' for a menstrual cycle is critical for early detection of health issues. Your period is a vital sign. When things go off track—whether it's extreme pain, unusually heavy flow, or a missing period—it's often your body's way of signaling that something requires attention.

## How to Assess Your Period

Use [LunaCycle](/) to track the key metrics of your period:
- **Length:** How many days does the bleeding last?
- **Flow:** How heavy is the bleeding? (e.g., number of pads/tampons used).
- **Pain Level:** Track the severity of cramps or other discomfort.
- **Regularity:** How many days between the start of one period and the next?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstruation phase.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several consecutive hours.

### 3. Severe pain
Cramps that prevent you from participating in normal daily activities (possible sign of endometriosis).

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles that frequently fall outside the 21-35 day range.

### 5. Absence of period
Going 90 days or more without a period (unless pregnant, breastfeeding, or menopausal).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---------|--------------------|
| Severe, debilitating pain | Schedule a doctor's appointment. |
| Extremely heavy flow | Seek medical advice. |
| No period for >90 days | Consult a healthcare provider. |
| Sudden onset of irregularity | Discuss with your doctor at your next visit. |
