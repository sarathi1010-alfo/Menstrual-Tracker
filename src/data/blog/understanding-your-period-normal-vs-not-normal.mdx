## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what constitutes a "normal" period is crucial for your overall health. Your menstrual cycle is a vital sign, much like your blood pressure or heart rate. Recognizing when something is amiss allows you to seek medical advice early, potentially preventing or managing conditions like PCOS, endometriosis, or thyroid issues before they become severe.

## How to Assess Your Period

The best way to assess your period is through consistent tracking. Use [LunaCycle](/) to monitor these key factors over several months:

*   **Cycle Length:** Count from Day 1 of one period to Day 1 of the next. Normal is 21-35 days.
*   **Duration:** How many days you bleed. Normal is 2-7 days.
*   **Flow Intensity:** How often you change your menstrual products.
*   **Pain Level:** From mild discomfort to severe cramping.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding at times other than your period should always be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding (Menorrhagia)
Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several hours, or passing large clots, is a sign of menorrhagia.

### 3. Severe Pain (Dysmenorrhea)
Cramps that are debilitating, cause nausea, or are not relieved by over-the-counter medication require medical attention.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
While slight variations are normal, cycles that consistently fall outside the 21-35 day range, or vary wildly in length, should be investigated.

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing a period for three or more consecutive months (when not pregnant or menopausal) warrants a doctor's visit.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bleeding through 1+ pads/tampons per hour | Seek immediate medical evaluation. |
| Pain that disrupts daily life | Schedule an appointment with your gynecologist. |
| Missing periods for 3+ months | Consult a healthcare provider for hormonal testing. |
| Bleeding after menopause | Seek immediate medical evaluation. |
