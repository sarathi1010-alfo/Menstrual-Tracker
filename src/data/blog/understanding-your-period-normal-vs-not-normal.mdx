## Why Knowing Normal Matters

What is "normal" for one person might be different for another, but there are medical baselines. Understanding these baselines is crucial for the early detection of health issues. Start by learning the basics in [Menstrual Cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

## How to Assess Your Period

Use [LunaCycle](/) to track the length of your cycle, the duration of your bleeding, your pain levels, and your overall regularity. Over time, you will establish your personal baseline.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Also known as spotting, this can be normal during ovulation but should be checked if frequent.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for consecutive hours.

### 3. Severe pain
Debilitating cramps that prevent you from attending work or school.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles that are consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Going 90 days or more without a period (when not pregnant or on hormonal birth control).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| Bleeding through 1 pad/hour | Seek immediate medical evaluation |
| Cycle > 35 days consistently | Consult a doctor (evaluate for PCOS, etc.) |
| Severe pelvic pain | See a healthcare provider |
