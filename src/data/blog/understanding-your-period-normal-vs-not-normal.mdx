## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Your menstrual cycle is a vital sign. Knowing your personal "normal" is the first step in early detection of health issues like [PCOS](/conditions/pcos-and-cycle-tracking-guide), endometriosis, or thyroid dysfunction.

## How to Assess Your Period

The easiest way to assess your period is by maintaining a consistent log.
*   **Length:** Is it consistently between 21 and 35 days?
*   **Duration:** Does bleeding last between 2 and 7 days?
*   **Flow:** Can it be managed with standard products changed every few hours?
*   **Pain Level:** Is the discomfort manageable?

Use [LunaCycle](/) to easily track these metrics. If you need help getting started, read our guide on [how to track your cycle](/blog/how-to-track-your-cycle-step-by-step).

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods (Spotting)
Frequent spotting should always be evaluated to rule out infections or polyps.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding (Menorrhagia)
Soaking products hourly or passing large clots.

### 3. Severe Pain (Dysmenorrhea)
Pain that causes nausea, vomiting, or missing work/school.

### 4. Highly Irregular Cycles
Cycles that fluctuate wildly in length by more than 7-9 days month-to-month.

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing periods for 3 or more months (if not pregnant).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bleeding through products hourly | See a doctor immediately. |
| No period for 90 days | Schedule a non-urgent appointment. |
| Sudden, severe pelvic pain | Go to urgent care or the ER. |
| Cycles suddenly becoming shorter/longer | Monitor for 1-2 more cycles, then consult doctor. |
