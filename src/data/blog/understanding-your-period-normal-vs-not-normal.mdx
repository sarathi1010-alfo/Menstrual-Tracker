## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what constitutes a "normal" period is vital for early detection of health issues. Because every body is different, the most important definition of normal is what is regular *for you*. By establishing your baseline through consistent tracking, you can quickly identify deviations that might signal hormonal imbalances, structural issues, or other medical conditions.

## How to Assess Your Period

To assess whether your period is normal, track these four key metrics using an app like [LunaCycle](/):

*   **Length:** A typical cycle lasts 21-35 days (from the start of one period to the start of the next).
*   **Duration:** Bleeding typically lasts 2-7 days.
*   **Flow:** How heavy the bleeding is (e.g., how often you change menstrual products).
*   **Pain Level:** Mild cramping is common; severe pain that disrupts daily life is not.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal period (intermenstrual bleeding) can be caused by hormonal fluctuations, but it can also indicate infections or polyps.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding (Menorrhagia)
If you need to change your pad or tampon every hour for consecutive hours, or if you are passing large blood clots (larger than a quarter).

### 3. Severe Pain (Dysmenorrhea)
Pain that does not respond to over-the-counter painkillers or forces you to miss school or work.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Cycles that frequently fall outside the 21-35 day range, or cycle lengths that vary wildly from month to month (e.g., 28 days one month, 45 the next).

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing three or more periods in a row when you are not pregnant, breastfeeding, or in menopause.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bleeding lasting more than 7 days | Schedule an appointment |
| Soaking through products hourly | Seek immediate medical evaluation |
| Sudden, severe pelvic pain | Seek emergency care |
| No period for 90 days (and negative pregnancy tests) | Schedule an appointment |
