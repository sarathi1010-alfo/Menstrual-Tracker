## Why Knowing Normal Matters
Knowing what constitutes a "normal" period for you helps you quickly identify when something is wrong. Changes in your cycle can be the first indicator of issues like PCOS, thyroid disorders, or fibroids.

## How to Assess Your Period
To figure out your baseline, track your cycles closely for a few months using [LunaCycle](/). Pay attention to cycle length, the duration of bleeding, flow volume, and the severity of cramps.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch
### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or full bleeding when you are not expecting your period.
### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through feminine hygiene products rapidly.
### 3. Severe pain
Pelvic pain that isn't relieved by over-the-counter painkillers.
### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.
### 5. Absence of period
Going 90 days or more without a period (amenorrhea).

## When to See a Doctor
| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---------|--------------------|
| Sudden change in cycle length | Track for one more cycle, see a doctor if it persists |
| Pain disrupting daily life | Schedule an appointment promptly |
