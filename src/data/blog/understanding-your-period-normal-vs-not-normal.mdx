## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what is considered a "normal" period for you helps you quickly identify when something is wrong. Changes in your cycle are often the first sign of issues like thyroid dysfunction, PCOS, or fibroids.

## How to Assess Your Period

Track the length of your cycle, the number of days you bleed, the heaviness of the flow, and your pain levels. Use [LunaCycle](/) to maintain an accurate record to share with your healthcare provider if needed.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Unexplained spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstrual window.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Needing to change a pad or tampon every hour for several hours.

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that interferes with your daily activities or doesn't respond to over-the-counter medication.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles that are consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing your period for three or more consecutive months (when not pregnant).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---------|--------------------|
| Heavy bleeding (soaking pads hourly) | Seek medical evaluation promptly |
| Severe pelvic pain | Consult a doctor |
| Missing period for 3+ months | Schedule an appointment |
