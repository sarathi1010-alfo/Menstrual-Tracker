## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what is normal for your body is the first step in early detection of health issues. Your menstrual cycle is often considered a "fifth vital sign"—a key indicator of your overall health.

## How to Assess Your Period

You should track:
- **Length:** How many days from Day 1 of one period to Day 1 of the next.
- **Duration:** How many days you bleed.
- **Flow:** How heavy the bleeding is (e.g., number of pads/tampons used).
- **Pain level:** Intensity of cramps.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Unexplained spotting or bleeding outside your normal window.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through products rapidly or passing very large clots.

### 3. Severe pain
Debilitating cramps that disrupt your life (dysmenorrhea).

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles consistently outside the 21-35 day range, or highly unpredictable lengths.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods in a row (amenorrhea) when not pregnant.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---------|--------------------|
| Sudden severe pain | Seek immediate medical care |
| Consistently irregular cycles | Schedule an appointment with a gynecologist |
| Bleeding lasting > 7 days | Consult your healthcare provider |
| Missing periods | Take a pregnancy test, then see a doctor if negative |
