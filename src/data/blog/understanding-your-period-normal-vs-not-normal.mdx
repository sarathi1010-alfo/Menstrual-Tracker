## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what is normal for *your* body matters for early detection of health issues. While there's a broad range of what's considered medically normal, significant deviations from your own baseline are the most important indicators. For a complete overview, see our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide).

## How to Assess Your Period

Use [LunaCycle](/) to track the four key metrics of your period:
1.  **Length of Cycle:** Count from Day 1 of your period to Day 1 of the next.
2.  **Length of Menstruation:** How many days you bleed.
3.  **Flow Volume:** Note how often you change products (e.g., soaking a pad every hour is considered heavy).
4.  **Pain Levels:** Track cramps and their severity.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding when you're not expecting your period.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for consecutive hours.

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that isn't relieved by over-the-counter medication and disrupts your life.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods consecutively (amenorrhea).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---------|--------------------|
| Heavy bleeding (>1 pad/hr) | Seek immediate medical evaluation |
| Cycles > 35 days consistently | Schedule a non-urgent appointment |
| Bleeding post-menopause | See a doctor immediately |
