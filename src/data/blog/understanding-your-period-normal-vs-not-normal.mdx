## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what is normal for your period is vital for early detection of health issues. While every woman's cycle is unique, recognizing significant deviations from standard medical parameters can help you seek timely medical advice.

## How to Assess Your Period

You can assess your period by tracking four key metrics using [LunaCycle](/):
1.  **Length:** Cycle length (21-35 days).
2.  **Duration:** How many days you bleed (2-7 days).
3.  **Flow:** How heavy the bleeding is.
4.  **Pain Level:** The severity of cramping.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstrual phase can indicate hormonal imbalances, infections, or other issues.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding
Menorrhagia (excessively heavy bleeding) can lead to anemia and requires medical evaluation.

### 3. Severe Pain
Debilitating cramps are not just "part of being a woman" and could indicate conditions like endometriosis.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Consistent variation in cycle length (more than 7-9 days difference between cycles) suggests irregular ovulation.

### 5. Absence of Period
Amenorrhea (missing periods for 3+ months) can be caused by stress, weight loss, PCOS, or other conditions.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| Bleeding through pads every hour | Seek medical attention promptly. |
| Missing 3 or more periods | Schedule a check-up with a healthcare provider. |
| Sudden, severe pelvic pain | Seek immediate medical evaluation. |
