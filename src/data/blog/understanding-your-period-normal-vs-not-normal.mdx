A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what is normal for your body is crucial for the early detection of health issues. Your period is a vital sign. Changes in its regularity, duration, or intensity can be the first indicator of hormonal imbalances, stress, or other health conditions. Read more about cycle basics in our [Beginner's Guide](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

## How to Assess Your Period

You can assess your period by tracking four key metrics using a tool like [LunaCycle](/):
1. **Length:** How many days between periods?
2. **Duration:** How many days do you bleed?
3. **Flow:** Is it light, medium, or heavy? Are there large clots?
4. **Pain level:** Do you experience mild cramping or severe, debilitating pain?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstruation phase should be evaluated.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
If you have to change your pad or tampon every hour for multiple hours, or pass large clots.

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that prevents you from going to work, school, or doing normal activities.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles that are consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods in a row (if not pregnant or menopausal). This could be a sign of conditions discussed in our [Ultimate Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- |
| Cycles > 35 days | Consult doctor |
| Cycles < 21 days | Consult doctor |
| Bleeding > 7 days | Consult doctor |
| Severe pain | Consult doctor |
| Missing periods | Take a pregnancy test; if negative, consult doctor |
