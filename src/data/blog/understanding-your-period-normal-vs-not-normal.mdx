## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Every woman's cycle is slightly different, which is why it's crucial to establish what is "normal" for *you*. By knowing your baseline cycle length, flow intensity, and typical symptoms, you can quickly spot when something is off. Early detection of irregularities can lead to faster diagnoses of conditions like PCOS, endometriosis, or thyroid disorders.

## How to Assess Your Period

The best way to assess your period is through consistent tracking. Using [LunaCycle](/tracker), you can easily monitor:
- **Cycle Length**: The number of days between the start of one period and the start of the next.
- **Duration**: How many days you bleed.
- **Flow**: The heaviness of the bleeding.
- **Pain Level**: The severity of cramps or other discomforts.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstruation window can be a sign of infection, hormonal imbalance, or other issues.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding (Menorrhagia)
Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several consecutive hours, or passing large blood clots.

### 3. Severe Pain (Dysmenorrhea)
Cramps that are debilitating and interfere with your daily life are not normal and may indicate endometriosis or fibroids.

### 4. Consistently Irregular Cycles
Cycles that frequently vary in length by more than 7-9 days, or are shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing your period for 3 or more consecutive months when you are not pregnant or going through menopause.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- |
| Severe pelvic pain | Consult a healthcare provider immediately. |
| Bleeding through pads/tampons hourly | Seek medical attention. |
| Missing 3+ periods | Schedule an appointment for an evaluation. |
| Sudden cycle irregularity | Discuss with your doctor at your next visit. |
