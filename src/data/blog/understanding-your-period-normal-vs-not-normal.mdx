## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what is 'normal' for *your* body is crucial for the early detection of health issues. While there are clinical averages, personal baselines established through tracking are your best diagnostic tool.

## How to Assess Your Period

When tracking with [LunaCycle](/), pay attention to these four pillars:
1. **Length:** How many days does the bleeding last?
2. **Flow:** Is it light, medium, or heavy? How many products are you using?
3. **Pain Level:** Are cramps manageable with OTC medication, or are they disrupting your life?
4. **Regularity:** How consistent is the gap between Day 1 of each cycle?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your expected menstrual window should be evaluated by a doctor.

### 2. Extremely Heavy Bleeding
Soaking through products rapidly or passing large clots is a sign of menorrhagia.

### 3. Severe Pain
Debilitating pain that causes nausea, vomiting, or prevents you from working is not normal and could indicate conditions like endometriosis.

### 4. Highly Irregular Cycles
Cycles that frequently fall outside the 21-35 day range or vary wildly in length month-to-month.

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing periods for three or more months (when not pregnant or menopausal) requires investigation.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| Soaking a pad/tampon every hour | Seek medical care promptly. |
| Pain not relieved by standard medication | Schedule an appointment with a gynecologist. |
| Missing 3+ periods in a row | Consult a healthcare provider. |
| Bleeding after sex | Contact your doctor for an evaluation. |
