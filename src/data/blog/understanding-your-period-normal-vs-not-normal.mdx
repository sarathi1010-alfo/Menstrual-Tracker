A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

For early detection of health issues, you must first establish your baseline. What's normal for you might differ slightly from the average, but major deviations shouldn't be ignored.

## How to Assess Your Period

Track length, flow, pain level, and regularity. Use an app to visualize these metrics over several months to establish your personal 'normal'.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting can be benign, but persistent mid-cycle bleeding needs evaluation.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
If you're changing products every hour or passing large clots.

### 3. Severe pain
Cramps shouldn't cause you to miss work or school regularly.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days consistently.

### 5. Absence of period
No period for 3 months (if not pregnant, lactating, or menopausal).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Sudden change in cycle length | Monitor for 2 cycles, then consult a doctor. |
| Debilitating pain | See a doctor immediately. |
| Post-menopausal bleeding | See a doctor immediately. |
