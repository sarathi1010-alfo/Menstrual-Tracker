## Why Knowing Normal Matters
Understanding what is medically considered a 'normal' period is vital for early detection of health issues. While every woman's body is different, significant deviations from the standard parameters can be symptoms of conditions like PCOS, endometriosis, or fibroids.

## How to Assess Your Period
To assess your period, track the following metrics over several months using a tool like LunaCycle:
- **Length:** How many days between the start of one period and the start of the next?
- **Duration:** How many days do you bleed?
- **Flow:** How many pads/tampons do you soak through?
- **Pain Level:** Is it manageable or debilitating?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside your normal menstrual window should be evaluated.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding (Menorrhagia)
Needing to change a pad or tampon every hour for consecutive hours, or bleeding longer than 7 days.

### 3. Severe Pain (Dysmenorrhea)
Cramps that prevent you from attending work or school, or that aren't helped by standard pain medication.

### 4. Highly Irregular Cycles
Cycles that frequently fall outside the 21-35 day range, or vary dramatically in length from month to month.

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing three or more periods in a row (when not pregnant or menopausal).

## When to See a Doctor
| Symptom | Action |
|---|---|
| Bleeding through products hourly | Seek medical advice promptly. |
| Sudden change in cycle length | Schedule an appointment if it persists for >3 cycles. |
| Debilitating cramps | Consult a doctor; don't suffer in silence. |
| No period for 90 days | See a healthcare provider for evaluation. |
