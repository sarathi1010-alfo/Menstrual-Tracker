A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what is 'normal' for your period is essential for early detection of health issues. While there is a broad range of normal across the population, identifying changes from your *personal* normal is the most important factor.

## How to Assess Your Period

Use a tracker like [LunaCycle](/) to monitor key aspects of your period:
*   **Length:** How many days does the bleeding last?
*   **Flow:** How heavy is the bleeding? (e.g., number of pads/tampons used).
*   **Pain Level:** Note the severity of cramping.
*   **Regularity:** How consistent is the cycle length from month to month?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding when you don't expect your period can be a sign of infection, hormonal imbalance, or other issues.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding
Soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several consecutive hours is considered abnormally heavy.

### 3. Severe Pain
Debilitating cramps that interfere with daily life and aren't relieved by standard medication could indicate conditions like endometriosis.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Consistently having cycles shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days, or cycles that vary wildly in length.

### 5. Absence of Period
Missing three or more periods in a row (if not pregnant, breastfeeding, or menopausal) requires medical evaluation.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Bleeding through >1 pad/hour | Seek immediate medical advice. |
| Severe, unmanageable pain | Consult a healthcare provider. |
| No period for 90 days | Schedule an appointment for evaluation. |
| Bleeding between periods | Discuss with your doctor at your next visit or sooner if accompanied by pain. |
