A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Understanding what is normal helps you identify potential health issues early, allowing for timely medical intervention.

## How to Assess Your Period

Track the length of your bleeding, the intensity of your flow, your pain levels, and overall regularity using [LunaCycle](/).

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstruation phase.

### 2. Heavy bleeding
Requiring frequent pad or tampon changes (more than one per hour).

### 3. Severe pain
Pain that does not improve with over-the-counter medication.

### 4. Irregular cycles
Cycles that are consistently shorter than 21 days or longer than 35 days.

### 5. Absence of period
Missing three or more periods in a row (if not pregnant).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Action |
| ------- | ------ |
| Bleeding through >1 pad/hr | See a doctor promptly |
| No period for 90 days | Schedule an appointment |
