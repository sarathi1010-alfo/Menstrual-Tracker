## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Your menstrual cycle is often referred to as a "fifth vital sign." Just like your heart rate or blood pressure, the characteristics of your period can provide significant clues about your overall health. Knowing the difference between what is considered medically normal and what might be a sign of a problem is crucial for early detection and treatment of reproductive health issues.

## How to Assess Your Period

To understand your period, you need to track it consistently. Use an app like [LunaCycle](/) to monitor:

*   **Cycle Length:** Count from Day 1 of one period to Day 1 of the next. Normal is 21-35 days.
*   **Period Duration:** How many days do you bleed? Normal is 2-7 days.
*   **Flow Volume:** How many products do you use per day?
*   **Pain Level:** Rate your cramps on a scale of 1-10.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding when you are not expecting your period can be caused by hormonal changes, infections, or other physical issues and should be evaluated.

### 2. Extremely Heavy Bleeding
If you are soaking through a pad or tampon every hour for several hours in a row, or passing large clots, seek medical advice.

### 3. Severe Pain
Cramps shouldn't prevent you from going to work or school. If over-the-counter medication doesn't help, it could be a sign of endometriosis or fibroids.

### 4. Highly Irregular Cycles
While occasional variation is normal, cycles that consistently fall outside the 21-35 day range, or vary wildly in length month-to-month, require attention.

### 5. Absence of Period
If you miss three or more periods in a row (and are not pregnant), it's important to consult a healthcare provider.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bleeding > 7 days | Schedule an appointment to rule out fibroids or hormonal issues. |
| Debilitating pain | See a doctor to investigate potential endometriosis. |
| Sudden cycle changes | Discuss with your provider, especially if accompanied by other symptoms. |
| Bleeding after menopause | Seek immediate medical evaluation. |
