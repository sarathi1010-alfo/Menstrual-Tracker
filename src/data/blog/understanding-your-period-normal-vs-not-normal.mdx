A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

For early detection of health issues, knowing what is normal *for you* is critical. While there are general medical averages, individual cycles vary. By establishing your own baseline through tracking with [LunaCycle](/), you can quickly spot anomalies that might indicate stress, hormonal imbalances, or more serious reproductive health issues.

## How to Assess Your Period

When tracking, evaluate these four pillars:
1. **Length:** How many days between the start of one period and the start of the next?
2. **Duration:** How many days do you bleed?
3. **Flow Intensity:** How often do you need to change your hygiene products?
4. **Pain Level:** Are your cramps manageable or debilitating?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal period window can be a sign of polyps, fibroids, or hormonal issues.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding
If you are soaking through a pad or tampon every hour, or passing clots larger than a quarter, it's considered abnormally heavy (menorrhagia).

### 3. Severe Pain
Cramps that cause nausea, vomiting, or force you to miss work/school (dysmenorrhea) are not just "part of being a woman" and should be investigated.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Cycles that fluctuate wildly in length (e.g., 25 days one month, 45 the next) indicate that ovulation is not happening consistently.

### 5. Absence of Period
Missing three or more periods in a row (amenorrhea) when you are not pregnant or menopausal requires medical attention.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---------|--------------------|
| Sudden, severe pelvic pain | Seek immediate medical care |
| Bleeding through 1 pad/hr | See a doctor promptly |
| Cycles consistently > 35 days | Schedule a routine checkup |
| Missing 3+ periods | Schedule a routine checkup |
