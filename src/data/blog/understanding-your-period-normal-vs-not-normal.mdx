A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what is normal *for you* is critical. While there are general medical guidelines, every body is different. By establishing your baseline through tracking with [LunaCycle](/tracker), you can easily spot deviations. Early detection of abnormalities can lead to earlier diagnosis and treatment of potential health issues.

## How to Assess Your Period

To assess your period, track these key metrics consistently:
* **Cycle Length:** The number of days from the start of one period to the start of the next.
* **Period Length:** The number of days you experience bleeding.
* **Flow Intensity:** How heavy the bleeding is (e.g., number of pads/tampons used).
* **Pain Levels:** The severity of cramps or other physical discomforts.

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal menstrual phase can be a sign of infection, hormonal imbalance, or other issues.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding (Menorrhagia)
Soaking through products every hour or passing clots larger than a quarter.

### 3. Severe Pain (Dysmenorrhea)
Cramps that are debilitating and do not respond to over-the-counter pain medication.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Cycles that frequently fall outside the 21-35 day range or vary wildly in length from month to month.

### 5. Absence of Period (Amenorrhea)
Missing three or more periods in a row (when not pregnant or on continuous birth control).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| Bleeding through >1 pad/tampon per hour | Seek immediate medical evaluation. |
| Missing >3 periods | Consult a healthcare provider. |
| Severe pain disrupting daily life | Schedule an appointment to rule out conditions like endometriosis. |
| Unusual discharge or odor | See a doctor to check for infection. |
