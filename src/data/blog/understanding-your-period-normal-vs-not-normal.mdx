A regular period occurs every 21-35 days, lasts 2-7 days, and has moderate flow. Deviations can signal underlying conditions.

## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what is considered normal for a menstrual cycle is critical for the early detection and management of potential reproductive health issues.

## How to Assess Your Period

You can assess your period by diligently tracking its length, the heaviness of the flow, the intensity of any pain, and its overall regularity using [LunaCycle](/).

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### Bleeding between periods
Spotting or bleeding between expected periods should be investigated.

### Heavy bleeding
Unusually heavy bleeding that requires frequent product changes is a red flag.

### Severe pain
Debilitating pain (dysmenorrhea) may indicate conditions like endometriosis.

### Irregular cycles
Consistently unpredictable cycles can be a sign of hormonal imbalances.

### Absence of period
Missing periods (amenorrhea) when not pregnant requires medical evaluation.

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Action |
|---|---|
| Bleeding through a pad an hour | Seek medical attention promptly. |
