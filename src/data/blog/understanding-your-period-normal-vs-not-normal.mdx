## Why Knowing Normal Matters

Knowing what is normal for a menstrual period is essential for early detection of health issues. Because "normal" varies from person to person, understanding general baselines helps you recognize when something is wrong.

## How to Assess Your Period

Use a tool like [LunaCycle](/tracker) to track your period over a few months. Pay attention to:
- **Length:** Is your cycle consistently between 21 and 35 days?
- **Duration:** Does bleeding last between 2 and 7 days?
- **Flow:** Are you changing your product every 3-4 hours (normal), or every hour (heavy)?
- **Pain:** Is the cramping manageable, or does it interfere with daily activities?

## Top 5 Warning Signs to Watch

### 1. Bleeding Between Periods
Spotting or bleeding outside of your normal period window.

### 2. Heavy Bleeding
Needing to change menstrual products every hour or passing large clots.

### 3. Severe Pain
Cramps that do not improve with over-the-counter medication.

### 4. Irregular Cycles
Cycles that frequently vary by more than 7-9 days or fall outside the 21-35 day range.

### 5. Absence of Period
Missing multiple periods in a row (when not pregnant).

## When to See a Doctor

| Symptom | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bleeding through products every hour | See a doctor promptly |
| Sudden, severe pelvic pain | Seek immediate medical attention |
| Missing 3 or more periods | Schedule a doctor appointment |
