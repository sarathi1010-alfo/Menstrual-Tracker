When you are **Trying to Conceive (TTC)**, your menstrual cycle data becomes your most valuable tool. Understanding the timing of ovulation and the length of your fertile window can significantly increase your chances of success.

## Why Cycle Tracking Matters for TTC
Conception can only happen during a very specific window each month. Sperm can live inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, but an egg is only viable for 12-24 hours after release. Tracking allows you to time intercourse to ensure sperm are present when the egg arrives.

## Key Features to Prioritize for TTC
- **Fertile Window Visualization:** A clear calendar view of your most likely fertile days.
- **Symptom Logging:** Tracking changes in cervical mucus, libido, and energy.
- **Basal Body Temperature (BBT):** A daily log to confirm that ovulation has occurred.
- **Privacy:** TTC can be an emotional journey; you may not want this data shared with advertisers.

## Tips and Best Practices
1. **Log Every Day:** Even on non-period days, log your symptoms to see the full pattern.
2. **Observe Your Mucus:** Look for "egg-white" consistency to identify your peak fertility.
3. **Check Your Luteal Phase:** Ensure your post-ovulation phase is long enough (typically 10+ days) to support implantation.
4. **Be Patient:** It can take several months of tracking to identify your unique rhythm.
