# Cycle Tracking for TTC (Trying to Conceive)

When you're trying to conceive, timing is everything. Tracking your cycle helps you narrow down the small window of time each month when pregnancy is possible.

## Identifying Your Fertile Window
The fertile window is the timeframe when an egg is available and sperm can survive. This typically lasts about 6 days.
- **The Lead Up:** Days 1-5 of the window.
- **Ovulation Day:** The day the egg is released.

## Key Biomarkers to Track
While LunaCycle predicts your window based on your past cycles, adding these biomarkers provides more precision:
1. **Cervical Mucus:** Look for a consistency like raw egg whites, which is "fertile" mucus.
2. **Basal Body Temperature (BBT):** A sustained rise in temperature usually indicates ovulation has occurred.
3. **Physical Symptoms:** Some experience a mild twinge (Mittelschmerz) during ovulation.
