# Cycle Tracking for TTC: The Complete 2026 Guide

For many, cycle tracking is about more than just knowing when their period will arrive; it's a vital tool for **Trying to Conceive (TTC)**. By understanding your body's signals, you can pinpoint the most fertile days of your month and optimize your chances of pregnancy.

## Understanding the Fertile Window
While you can only get pregnant after an egg is released (ovulation), sperm can live inside the female reproductive tract for up to five days. This means your "fertile window" is approximately six days long: the five days before ovulation and the day of ovulation.

## Key Fertility Biomarkers to Track
1. **Basal Body Temperature (BBT):** Your temperature at rest. It typically rises slightly (about 0.5°F or 0.3°C) after ovulation.
2. **Cervical Mucus:** As you approach ovulation, your mucus becomes clear, stretchy, and slippery (like raw egg whites).
3. **Luteal Phase Length:** Knowing the length of time between ovulation and your next period helps ensure your body has enough time to support an embryo.

- **Be Consistent:** Log your data every day for the most accurate predictions.
