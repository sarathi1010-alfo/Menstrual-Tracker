Amenorrhea is the absence of menstruation—one or more missed menstrual periods. It is not a disease itself, but rather a symptom of another condition or a natural stage of life.

## How Does Amenorrhea Work?
A regular menstrual cycle requires a complex coordination between the brain (hypothalamus and pituitary gland) and the ovaries. Amenorrhea occurs when this hormonal communication is disrupted by factors like stress, excessive exercise, weight changes, or underlying medical conditions.

## Real-World Example of Amenorrhea
**Secondary Amenorrhea** is common among endurance athletes or those experiencing extreme stress; they may find their periods stop for several months. Once the stressor is removed or nutrition is improved, the cycle often returns.

## Why is Amenorrhea Important?
The menstrual cycle is often called the "fifth vital sign." A missing period can be an early indicator of hormonal imbalances, bone density issues, or nutritional deficiencies. Tracking your dates in LunaCycle helps you immediately identify when a period is late or missing.

## Primary vs. Secondary Amenorrhea

| Feature | Primary Amenorrhea | Secondary Amenorrhea |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Never started menstruating by age 15. | Period stops for 3+ months. |
| **Common Causes** | Genetic or anatomical issues. | Stress, diet, PCOS, pregnancy. |
| **Frequency** | Rare. | More common. |

**Core Principles:**
- A missing period (outside of pregnancy/menopause) warrants investigation.
- Nutrition and stress play a massive role in cycle regularity.
