## Why Understanding Your Menstrual Cycle Matters
Understanding your cycle goes far beyond just predicting when your next period will start. It allows you to anticipate mood changes, adjust your lifestyle for energy fluctuations, and even detect potential health issues early on.

## How to Start Tracking Your Cycle
Getting started is easy. Start by recording the first day of your period and its duration. Over time, you can add symptoms like mood and flow intensity. For a privacy-first approach, use [LunaCycle](/), a simple interface that securely keeps your data on your device.

## Top 5 Reasons to Track Your Cycle
### 1. Know your fertile window
Understanding your fertile days is crucial whether you are trying to conceive or avoid pregnancy.
### 2. Predict PMS
Anticipate premenstrual syndrome symptoms so you can prepare self-care routines.
### 3. Spot irregularities
Notice deviations from your normal pattern which could be an early warning sign for health conditions.
### 4. Optimize energy
Align your exercise and work schedules with the energy peaks of your follicular phase.
### 5. Prepare for TTC
If you're trying to conceive (TTC), tracking helps you time intercourse optimally.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
| Mistake | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| Irregular logging | Inaccurate predictions |
| Ignoring discharge changes | Missed ovulation signs |
| Relying only on memory | Forgetting cycle length over time |
