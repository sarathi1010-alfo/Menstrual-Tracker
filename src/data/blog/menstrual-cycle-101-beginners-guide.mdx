The menstrual cycle is a monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for pregnancy. Understanding it is the first step to taking control of your reproductive health.

## Why Understanding Your Menstrual Cycle Matters

Understanding your cycle is crucial for predicting periods and detecting health issues early. By knowing what is normal for you, you can easily spot irregularities that may require medical attention.

## How to Start Tracking Your Cycle

Getting started is simple. Using [LunaCycle](/)’s intuitive interface, you can begin by logging the first day of your period and noting any symptoms you experience throughout the month.

## Top 5 Reasons to Track Your Cycle

### 1. Know your fertile window
Identify the days you are most likely to conceive.

### 2. Predict PMS
Anticipate mood swings and physical symptoms before they happen.

### 3. Spot irregularities
Catch abnormalities early, which can indicate underlying conditions.

### 4. Optimize energy
Plan activities around your natural energy peaks and valleys.

### 5. Prepare for TTC
Gather data to share with your healthcare provider if you are trying to conceive.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Consequence |
| ------- | ----------- |
| Irregular logging | Inaccurate predictions |
| Ignoring discharge changes | Missing key fertility signs |
| Forgetting to log symptoms | Missing overall health patterns |
