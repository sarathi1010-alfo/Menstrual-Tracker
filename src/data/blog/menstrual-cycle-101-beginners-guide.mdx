The menstrual cycle is a monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for pregnancy. Understanding it is the first step to taking control of your reproductive health.

## Why Understanding Your Menstrual Cycle Matters

Understanding your menstrual cycle matters for numerous reasons, ranging from predicting periods to detecting health issues early. By knowing what is normal for your body, you can better advocate for your own health.

## How to Start Tracking Your Cycle

Starting to track your cycle is easy with [LunaCycle](/)'s simple interface. You can begin by logging the first day of your period and gradually add more details like symptoms and mood.

## Top 5 Reasons to Track Your Cycle

### Know your fertile window
Tracking helps you pinpoint when you are most likely to ovulate and conceive.

### Predict PMS
Anticipating premenstrual syndrome (PMS) allows you to prepare and manage symptoms proactively.

### Spot irregularities
By establishing a baseline, you can easily notice if your cycle becomes irregular, which may indicate an underlying health issue.

### Optimize energy
Aligning your lifestyle and activities with your cycle phases can help you optimize your energy levels.

### Prepare for TTC
If you are trying to conceive (TTC), tracking is crucial for identifying your most fertile days.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Irregular logging | Inaccurate predictions |
| Ignoring discharge changes | Missing key fertility signs |
