The menstrual cycle is a monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through to prepare for pregnancy. Understanding it is the first step to taking control of your reproductive health.

## Why Understanding Your Menstrual Cycle Matters

Understanding your cycle allows you to predict periods, manage symptoms, and detect health issues early. It empowers you to make informed decisions about your body and well-being.

## How to Start Tracking Your Cycle

Tracking is easy with LunaCycle's simple interface. Just log the start and end dates of your period, and note any symptoms or changes in flow. The more you track, the better the predictions.

## Top 5 Reasons to Track Your Cycle

### 1. Know your fertile window
Identify the days you are most likely to conceive.

### 2. Predict PMS
Anticipate mood changes and physical symptoms to manage them better.

### 3. Spot irregularities
Early detection of abnormal patterns can indicate health issues.

### 4. Optimize energy
Plan activities around your energy levels throughout the cycle.

### 5. Prepare for TTC
Tracking helps you optimize timing if you're trying to conceive.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Irregular logging | Inaccurate predictions |
| Ignoring discharge changes | Missed fertility signs |
| Forgetting to note symptoms | Difficulty identifying patterns |
