What you eat provides the building blocks for your hormones. By adjusting your nutrition to match your cycle's phases, you can support your body's natural processes and potentially reduce common symptoms like fatigue and bloating.

## Why Cycle Nutrition Matters for Health

Every phase of your cycle is driven by different dominant hormones. These hormones don't just affect your reproductive system; they influence your digestion, insulin sensitivity, and mood. Eating with your cycle isn't about restriction; it's about giving your body the specific tools it needs at the right time.

1.  **Identify Your Current Phase:** Use the LunaCycle dashboard to see where you are in your cycle today.
2.  **Audit Your Pantry:** Keep staples on hand that support different phases (e.g., seeds for seed cycling, various legumes, and grains).
3.  **Meal Plan by Phase:** When planning your week, consider if you'll need more energy-dense foods (luteal) or lighter, fresh options (ovulatory).
4.  **Listen to Cravings:** Distinguish between "emotional" cravings and your body's "functional" needs (e.g., craving chocolate might mean you need magnesium).

## Top 4 Nutritional Strategies

### 1. The Menstrual Phase: Replenish
Focus on iron-rich foods (red meat, lentils, spinach) paired with Vitamin C to support absorption. Opt for warming, easy-to-digest meals like stews and bone broths.

### 2. The Follicular Phase: Energize
As estrogen rises, support your liver's ability to metabolize hormones with cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, kale). Incorporate fermented foods to support gut health.

### 3. The Ovulatory Phase: Support
During your peak energy phase, focus on anti-inflammatory foods and plenty of hydration. Berries, citrus fruits, and light proteins like fish or quinoa are excellent choices.

### 4. The Luteal Phase: Balance
Metabolism speeds up and progesterone rises. Focus on slow-burning complex carbohydrates (sweet potatoes, brown rice) and magnesium-rich dark chocolate or pumpkin seeds to stabilize mood.

## Common Nutritional Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it's a Problem | Better Alternative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Excessive salt in luteal phase | Increases water retention and bloating. | Use herbs and spices for flavor instead. |
| Skipping meals | Can lead to blood sugar crashes and intensified PMS. | Eat regular, balanced meals with protein and healthy fats. |
| Ignoring hydration | Dehydration can make fatigue and headaches worse. | Aim for 2-3 liters of water daily, especially before your period. |
