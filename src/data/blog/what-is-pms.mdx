# What is PMS?

**Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS)** is a combination of physical and emotional symptoms that many people experience after ovulation and before their period starts. It occurs during the **luteal phase** of the menstrual cycle.

## How Does PMS Work?

After ovulation, your body produces higher levels of progesterone. Scientists believe that PMS is caused by the body&apos;s reaction to these changing hormone levels, as well as shifts in brain chemicals like serotonin. When the period begins and hormone levels drop, the symptoms usually fade away.

## Real-World Example of PMS Tracking

A user of [LunaCycle](/tracker) might notice they feel particularly irritable and have sugar cravings starting on Day 22 of their 28-day cycle. By logging these symptoms in the [Daily Log](/tracker), they can see the pattern repeat every month, helping them realize that their mood is a reflection of their hormones rather than external factors.

## Why is PMS Important?

Understanding your PMS patterns allows you to:
- **Plan Ahead:** Adjust your schedule for days when you know your energy might be lower.
- **Improve Communication:** Explain to partners or family why you might be feeling extra sensitive.
- **Target Relief:** Use specific strategies (like reducing salt for bloating) at the right time in your cycle.

## PMS vs. Normal Cycle Changes

| Feature | Mild Cycle Changes | PMS |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| Impact | Barely noticeable | Affects daily life/mood |
| Timing | 1-2 days before period | 5-10 days before period |
| Relief | Not usually needed | Lifestyle changes or OTC relief |
