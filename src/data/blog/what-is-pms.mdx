# What is PMS?

Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) is a collection of symptoms that occur in the days or weeks leading up to a woman's period. Most women experience at least one sign of PMS each month.

## How Does PMS Work?

PMS occurs during the **luteal phase** of the cycle, after ovulation. It is thought to be caused by the rapid drop in estrogen and progesterone that happens if pregnancy does not occur, which affects brain chemicals like serotonin.

## Real-World Example of PMS

Common experiences include bloating, breast tenderness, mood swings, food cravings, and increased irritability in the 3-5 days before bleeding begins.

## Why is PMS Important?

Understanding your PMS patterns helps you plan your life and reduces the stress of "feeling off." If symptoms are severe enough to interfere with your daily life, it may be a more serious condition called PMDD.

## PMS vs. Normal Luteal Phase

| Feature | Healthy Luteal Phase | PMS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mood** | Stable | Irritable or Sad |
| **Physical** | Minimal changes | Bloating/Pain |
| **Daily Life** | Unaffected | Noticeable impact |

### Core Principles
-   Symptoms resolve shortly after the period starts.
-   Influenced by diet, caffeine, and sleep.
-   Tracking in [LunaCycle](/tracker) reveals your unique "symptom map."
-   Exercise often helps reduce symptom intensity.
