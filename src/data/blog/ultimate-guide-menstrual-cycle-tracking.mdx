## Understanding the Four Phases

To track effectively, you must understand the four distinct phases of your menstrual cycle:

1.  **Menstruation:** The shedding of the uterine lining.
2.  **Follicular Phase:** Preparation for egg release and rising estrogen.
3.  **Ovulation:** The release of an egg from the ovary.
4.  **Luteal Phase:** The preparation for potential pregnancy or the next cycle.

## Advanced Tracking Strategies

For those looking to go deeper, consider tracking these biomarkers:

- **Basal Body Temperature (BBT):** Your resting temperature rises slightly after ovulation.
- **Cervical Mucus:** Changes in consistency indicate where you are in your fertile window.
- **Energy Patterns:** Map your productivity to your hormonal phases (Cycle Syncing).
