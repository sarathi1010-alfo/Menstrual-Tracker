## Why Consistent Tracking Matters
Consistent cycle tracking is essential for accurate predictions and gaining deep health insights. It transforms vague memories of 'I think my period is due soon' into clear, actionable data. Over time, this data helps you understand your unique 'normal' and spot deviations early.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle
1. **Open the App:** Navigate to the LunaCycle tracker on your device.
2. **Select the Date:** Tap on the current date (or the date your period started).
3. **Log the Start:** Confirm that your period has begun. This marks Day 1 of your new cycle.
4. **Add Details:** Note the flow intensity (light, medium, heavy) and any symptoms (cramps, headache).
5. **Review:** Check your calendar view to see predictions for your next cycle.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The most basic method. Good for predicting the next period if your cycles are highly regular.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Adding symptoms (mood, physical changes) provides a holistic view of how your cycle affects your body.

### 3. Tech App (like LunaCycle)
Combines calendar and symptom tracking with automated calculations, keeping all your data organized privately.

## Common Tracking Errors
| Error | Impact | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews all future predictions. | Make it a habit to log on day one. |
| Relying on memory | Memories are flawed; data is objective. | Use a tool (digital or physical) consistently. |
| Ignoring non-bleeding days | Misses important phase changes. | Track symptoms even when you're not bleeding. |
