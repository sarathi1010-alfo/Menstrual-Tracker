## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Tracking your cycle consistently provides a baseline for what is "normal" for your body. This allows you to accurately predict future periods, anticipate symptom patterns (like PMS), identify your fertile window, and quickly spot any irregularities that might warrant a discussion with a healthcare provider.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
The simplest form of tracking. You mark the start and end dates of your period on a physical or digital calendar to monitor cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Going beyond just dates, this involves keeping a daily log of physical and emotional changes, helping you connect symptoms to specific times in your cycle.

### 3. Tech App (LunaCycle)
Using a dedicated app like [LunaCycle](/) automates calculations, provides predictions, and allows you to easily visualize your data over time without compromising privacy.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact on Tracking | How to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews cycle length calculations | Set a monthly reminder near your expected start date |
| Logging spotting as day 1 | Can artificially shorten or lengthen cycle data | Only log day 1 when full bleeding begins |
| Using inconsistent methods | Makes it hard to see long-term patterns | Choose one tool (like LunaCycle) and stick with it |
| Ignoring abnormal symptoms | Delays potential medical care | Always mention significant changes to a doctor |
