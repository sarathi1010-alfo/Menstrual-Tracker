## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent cycle tracking is the key to unlocking accurate predictions and profound health insights. If you only log your period sporadically, the data won't reveal the true patterns of your body. Regular tracking allows you to anticipate your next period, prepare for PMS symptoms, and understand your unique hormonal rhythm.

Over time, this consistent data becomes a powerful tool. It not only helps you manage your day-to-day life but also provides a concrete record to share with your healthcare provider if you ever notice irregularities.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The foundation of all tracking. You simply mark the days you bleed on a calendar. It's best for basic prediction of cycle length and next period start dates.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Beyond bleeding, this involves noting daily physical and emotional changes. This technique is excellent for identifying PMS patterns and hormonal shifts throughout the month.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using an app combines calendar tracking, symptom journaling, and prediction algorithms. Apps provide visual charts and automated insights, making the process effortless.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Why it's a problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to Log | Leads to gaps in data and poor predictions. | Build tracking into your morning or evening routine. |
| Logging Spotting as Day 1 | Skews your cycle length calculations. | Day 1 is the first day of *full flow* bleeding. |
| Inconsistent Symptom Logging | Makes it hard to find true patterns. | Try to log symptoms daily, even on non-period days. |
