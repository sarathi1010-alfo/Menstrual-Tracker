## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Tracking your cycle is like gathering data for an experiment where you are the subject. Consistent tracking allows you to predict your periods, identify your fertile window, and spot potential health issues early. It transforms the menstrual cycle from an unpredictable event into a manageable, understood process. For a primer on the basics, read [Menstrual Cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The simplest method. Record the start and end dates of your period to calculate cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Going beyond the calendar, note daily physical and emotional changes to understand your [hormonal shifts](/blog/hormones-and-your-cycle-explained).

### 3. Tech Applications
Using an app like [LunaCycle](/) automates the math and visualizes your data without compromising your privacy.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact | Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Counting spotting as Day 1 | Skews cycle length calculation | Only log full flow as Day 1. |
| Inconsistent logging | Inaccurate predictions | Set a quick daily reminder. |
| Ignoring anomalies | Missing health signs | Note unusual pain or heavy bleeding. |
| Relying solely on apps for contraception | High risk of unintended pregnancy | Use barrier methods; apps predict, they don't prevent. |
