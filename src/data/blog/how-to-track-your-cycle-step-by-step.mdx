Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier by keeping all your data private and local.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

For accurate predictions and health insights, consistency is crucial. The more data you provide, the better you (and the algorithm) can predict future periods, fertile windows, and PMS phases. Consistent tracking also creates a reliable historical record that is invaluable if you ever need to discuss irregularities with a doctor.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

1. **Open the App:** Navigate to the main tracker dashboard.
2. **Log Period Start:** Tap the current date and select 'Start Period'.
3. **Record Flow:** Choose your flow intensity (Light, Medium, Heavy, Spotting).
4. **Add Symptoms:** Select any relevant physical (cramps, headache) or emotional (sad, anxious) symptoms.
5. **Save:** Your data is instantly saved locally on your device.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The traditional approach. Mark day 1 of bleeding and count the days until your next day 1. Best for basic prediction if your cycles are very regular.

### 2. Symptom Journal
A more detailed approach where you record daily physical feelings, moods, and discharge. Excellent for holistic health monitoring.

### 3. Tech App
Using a tool like LunaCycle combines the calendar method with symptom journaling, automatically calculating averages and providing visual insights without requiring manual math.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews average cycle length and future predictions |
| Only logging heavy days | Makes period duration appear shorter than it is |
| Ignoring spotting | Spotting can be an important sign of ovulation or hormonal shifts |
| Stopping tracking mid-cycle | Leaves gaps in symptom data for the luteal phase |
