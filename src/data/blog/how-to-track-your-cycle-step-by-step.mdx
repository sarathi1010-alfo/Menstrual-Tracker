## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Cycle tracking is only as useful as the data you put into it. Consistent logging is essential for accurate predictions of your next period, fertile window, and symptom patterns. Over time, this personalized data becomes a powerful tool for understanding your health and recognizing when something might be amiss.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The most basic form of tracking. You mark the start and end dates of your period on a calendar to calculate cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
In addition to dates, you record physical and emotional symptoms daily to identify recurring patterns.

### 3. Tech App (The Modern Way)
Using a dedicated app like LunaCycle automates calculations, visualizes trends, and keeps your data organized.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Inaccurate cycle length calculation |
| Logging spotting as day 1 | Skewed period predictions (Day 1 is full flow) |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Difficulty identifying patterns |
| Using apps that sell your data | Compromised personal privacy |
