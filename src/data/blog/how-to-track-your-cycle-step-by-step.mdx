Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. LunaCycle makes it even easier.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking provides accurate predictions for your next period and fertile window, and helps build a comprehensive picture of your health.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

1. **Open the App:** Access the LunaCycle tracker on your device.
2. **Select the Date:** Tap on the calendar to mark the first day of your period.
3. **Log Details:** Use the daily modal to record flow intensity, mood, and symptoms.
4. **Review Patterns:** Check the insights section for trends over time.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar
The traditional method of marking days on a physical calendar.

### 2. Symptom journal
Writing down daily physical and emotional feelings.

### 3. Tech app
Using a digital tracker like LunaCycle for automated insights and privacy.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log | Skewed predictions |
| Only logging bleeding | Missing the bigger picture of hormonal health |
