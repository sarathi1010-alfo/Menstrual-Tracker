## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

For accurate predictions and health insights, consistency is key. Sporadic tracking leads to faulty algorithms and missed health signals.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The traditional way—marking days on a paper calendar. Good for basics, less ideal for details.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Writing down detailed daily notes. Excellent for deep insights, but hard to quickly analyze.

### 3. Tech App (LunaCycle)
Combines the calendar with symptom logging and automatic pattern recognition, all while protecting privacy.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log | Set a daily alarm. |
| Logging 'spotting' as Day 1 | Day 1 is the first day of full flow. |
| Inconsistent BBT timing | Take temperature immediately upon waking. |
