## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking matters for accurate predictions and deeper health insights. A single data point doesn't tell a story, but several months of data can reveal patterns in your cycle length, symptom frequency, and overall health. This information is invaluable for managing your lifestyle, planning for the future, and providing accurate information to your healthcare provider if needed.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The foundational method: simply marking the days you bleed on a digital or physical calendar to track total cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Expanding beyond dates to log daily moods, energy levels, and physical sensations like cramps or headaches.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using privacy-first digital tools to aggregate data and automatically calculate averages and predict future cycles based on your unique history.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews the entire cycle length calculation. |
| Only logging when symptoms are bad | Creates a negative bias in your health history. |
| Using apps that sell your data | Compromises your personal health privacy. |
