Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier with a privacy-first approach. For a broader overview of the benefits, read our [menstrual cycle 101 guide](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide).

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the foundation of gaining actionable insights into your health. Logging sporadically will lead to inaccurate predictions for your next period and fertile window. When you track daily, you start to see patterns in your mood, energy, and physical symptoms, allowing you to [understand your period](/blog/understanding-your-period-normal-vs-not-normal) better.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest form of tracking. You mark the days you bleed on a physical or digital calendar to calculate the length of your cycle and estimate your next period.

### 2. Symptom Journal
A more detailed approach where you write down daily physical and emotional changes. This is excellent for identifying PMS patterns.

### 3. Tech App (Like LunaCycle)
Combines the calendar and journal methods, automating predictions and providing visual charts of your data.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to log the start date. | Throws off predictions for the entire cycle. |
| Inconsistent symptom tracking. | Makes it difficult to identify genuine patterns. |
| Ignoring abnormal bleeding. | Delaying medical attention for potential health issues. |

### Core Principles
*   Consistency is the key to accurate predictions.
*   Track both bleeding and secondary symptoms.
*   Use a tool that fits your lifestyle.
*   Review your data regularly for patterns.
*   Prioritize privacy when choosing tracking methods.
