## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the key to accurate predictions and deep health insights. If you only log your period sporadically, you miss the subtle patterns that indicate fertile windows, impending PMS, or potential health concerns. A complete log acts as a personal health diary.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking with [LunaCycle](/) is designed to be seamless and private.
1. **Log Day 1:** Open the app on the first day of your period and tap "Start Period."
2. **Record Flow:** Note if your flow is light, medium, or heavy.
3. **Add Symptoms:** Throughout the month, quickly toggle symptoms like cramps, headache, or bloating.
4. **Note Discharge:** Log cervical mucus changes to track fertility.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar
The traditional method. Mark a red 'X' on period days. Good for basic length tracking, but poor for symptom correlations.

### 2. Symptom Journal
Writing detailed daily notes. Excellent for deep insights into mood and diet, but can be time-consuming.

### 3. Tech App
Using a privacy-focused app like LunaCycle combines the ease of a calendar with the depth of a symptom journal, all while keeping your data safe.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Mistake | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Forgetting to log the end date | Skews your average cycle length calculations. |
| Counting spotting as Day 1 | Leads to inaccurate phase predictions. Day 1 must be full flow. |
| Ignoring lifestyle factors | Stress, travel, and illness affect the cycle and should be noted. |
