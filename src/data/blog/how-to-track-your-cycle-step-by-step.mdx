Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the key to accurate predictions and valuable health insights. The more data you provide, the better you'll understand your unique patterns.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

1.  **Open the App:** Access LunaCycle on your device.
2.  **Log Your Period:** Tap the current date and indicate if you are bleeding and the flow intensity.
3.  **Add Symptoms:** Note any pain, mood changes, or other physical signs.
4.  **Save:** Your data is securely stored locally on your device.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
Simply marking the days you bleed on a calendar. Good for basic length tracking but lacks detail.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Writing down detailed notes about how you feel each day. Excellent for identifying PMS patterns.

### 3. Tech App (LunaCycle)
Combines the benefits of both, offering automated predictions and secure data storage.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Consequence | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Forgetting to log period start | Inaccurate next period prediction | Set a phone reminder |
| Logging spotting as day 1 | Skews cycle length data | Only count full flow as day 1 |
