Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. LunaCycle makes it even easier.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters
Consistent tracking is the foundation for accurate predictions and meaningful health insights. Your body operates on patterns, but these patterns can only be recognized if data is recorded regularly over time. By tracking consistently, you can separate normal fluctuations from true irregularities, providing both peace of mind and actionable health data.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle
LunaCycle is designed for privacy and ease of use. Here is a step-by-step guide:
1. **Open the App:** Navigate to the [LunaCycle](/) homepage.
2. **Select the Date:** Tap on the current date or the date your period started on the calendar view.
3. **Log Flow:** Select the intensity of your bleeding (Spotting, Light, Medium, Heavy).
4. **Add Symptoms:** Check off any physical symptoms (like cramps or bloating) or mood changes.
5. **Save Locally:** Hit save. Your data is instantly stored on your device—never on a server.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The traditional approach. Simply mark the days you bleed on a calendar to calculate cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed diary of how you feel every day to correlate moods and physical feelings with cycle days.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using specialized software like [LunaCycle](/) to aggregate data, automate calculations, and visualize patterns over time.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews average cycle length | Set a monthly reminder |
| Logging spotting as Day 1 | Throws off phase calculations | Wait for full flow to mark Day 1 |
| Only tracking when bleeding | Misses ovulation and luteal data | Try to log at least one symptom daily |
