## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the foundation of accurate cycle predictions and meaningful health insights. It transforms vague recollections into concrete data, allowing you to anticipate your period, understand mood shifts, and provide accurate information to healthcare providers.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking is intuitive with the right tool. Here is how to use [LunaCycle](/):

1. **Start Your Cycle:** Open the app on the first day of full bleeding and tap the current date on the main calendar to log the start of your period.
2. **Log Daily Details:** Tap any date to open the daily modal. Here, you can select your flow intensity (light, medium, heavy) and any physical or emotional symptoms.
3. **Monitor Predictions:** The app will automatically calculate your expected next period based on your logged history, displaying it clearly on the dashboard.
4. **Review History:** Use the charts and history views to see patterns in your cycle length and symptom frequency over time.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
The simplest form of tracking. You only log the start and end dates of your period. It's great for basic predictions but lacks deeper health context.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Logging daily physical and emotional symptoms alongside period dates. This provides a holistic view of how your cycle affects your daily life.

### 3. Tech-Assisted Tracking
Using apps like LunaCycle that combine calendar logging, symptom tracking, and automated calculations to provide personalized insights and predictions without manual math.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews all future predictions | Set a phone reminder when you feel symptoms starting. |
| Logging spotting as Day 1 | Inaccurate cycle length | Only count full flow as Day 1. |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Missed patterns | Try to log at the same time every day (e.g., morning or bedtime). |
