## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Cycle tracking is only as good as the data you input. Consistent tracking helps you accurately predict your next period, identify your fertile window, and spot potential health irregularities. Over time, this data becomes a valuable tool for understanding your unique bodily rhythms.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The most basic method. You simply record the days you bleed to determine your average cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Adding qualitative data like mood, cervical mucus, and physical symptoms to your calendar tracking.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using privacy-first digital tools to automatically calculate averages and provide predictions based on your inputs.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews average cycle length calculations. |
| Inconsistent BBT timing | Leads to erratic temperature charts that are hard to interpret. |
| Ignoring lifestyle factors | Missing the connection between stress/diet and cycle changes. |
