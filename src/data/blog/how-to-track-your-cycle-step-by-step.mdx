## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the foundation of accurate predictions and health insights. When you log your cycle data regularly, you build a historical record of your body's unique rhythms. This helps you predict when your period will arrive, anticipate hormonal changes, and spot any unusual deviations that might require a doctor's visit.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The traditional approach: noting start and end dates on a calendar. Good for basic predictions.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Detailed daily logging of physical and emotional changes. Excellent for identifying PMS patterns.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using an app like LunaCycle combines calendar calculation with symptom journaling for automated, private insights.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log Day 1 | Throws off predictions for the current and future cycles. |
| Counting spotting as Day 1 | Inaccurate cycle length calculations. |
| Ignoring symptoms | Missing the chance to identify patterns in mood and pain. |
