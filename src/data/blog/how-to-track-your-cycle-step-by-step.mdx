## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent cycle tracking provides accurate predictions for your next period and fertile window. It also establishes a baseline for your personal "normal," making it easier to spot health issues or irregularities early on.

## How to Log Your Cycle with [LunaCycle](/tracker)

Tracking with [LunaCycle](/tracker) is designed to be simple and secure:
1. **Open the App:** Navigate to the main dashboard.
2. **Select the Date:** Tap on the current date or the first day of your period.
3. **Log Your Flow:** Select the intensity (light, medium, heavy).
4. **Add Symptoms:** Quickly check off any relevant symptoms or moods.
5. **Save:** Your data is instantly saved locally on your device.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
Basic tracking of start and end dates to estimate cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Detailed daily logs of physical and emotional changes.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using apps for automated predictions and easy visualization.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to log the start date | Inaccurate predictions for the next month |
| Tracking only when symptoms are bad | Skewed perception of the overall cycle |
| Using apps that sell your data | Loss of digital privacy |
