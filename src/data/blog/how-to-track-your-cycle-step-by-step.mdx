## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

For a deeper dive, check out our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) and learn more about [Menstrual Cycle 101](/blog/menstrual-cycle-101-beginners-guide) and [Understanding Your Period](/blog/understanding-your-period-normal-vs-not-normal).

Consistent cycle tracking empowers you with data about your own body. It improves the accuracy of predictions and provides vital information for understanding your reproductive health.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking is simple with [LunaCycle](/):
1. **Open the app:** Navigate to the calendar view.
2. **Log Day 1:** Tap the current date and mark it as the start of your period.
3. **Add details:** Note your flow intensity (light, medium, heavy).
4. **Log symptoms:** Add any symptoms you are experiencing, like cramps or headaches.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest method; tracking the start and end dates of your period.
### 2. Symptom Journaling
Recording daily physical and emotional symptoms to identify patterns.
### 3. Tech App Tracking
Using a digital tracker like LunaCycle to automate predictions and store data privately.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log Day 1 | Skews cycle length calculations. |
| Counting spotting as Day 1 | Inaccurate phase predictions. |
| Using an app that sells your data | Compromises your privacy. |
