## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Tracking your cycle is like keeping a diary of your body's vital signs. Consistency is the key to unlocking accurate predictions and profound health insights. Whether you're trying to conceive, avoiding pregnancy naturally, managing a health condition, or simply wanting to understand your body better, a reliable record of your cycle is invaluable.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
The traditional approach. Mark the start and end days of your period on a physical calendar. It's simple but lacks predictive capabilities.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed diary of physical and emotional changes. Excellent for deep self-awareness but can be time-consuming to analyze for patterns.

### 3. Tech Applications (Like LunaCycle)
Combines the benefits of calendar tracking and symptom journaling with automated predictions and visual insights, all while ensuring your data remains private.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Why to Avoid It |
| :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to Log | Inconsistent data leads to inaccurate predictions. Set a daily reminder. |
| Logging Spotting as Day 1 | Day 1 is the first day of *full flow* bleeding, not just spotting. |
| Using Apps that Sell Data | Protect your privacy by choosing local-storage apps like LunaCycle. |
| Stopping After a Few Months | Long-term tracking reveals macro-patterns and changes over time. |
