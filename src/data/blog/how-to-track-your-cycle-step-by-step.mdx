Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier by providing a clean, intuitive interface.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Your memory is not as reliable as you might think. Trying to remember exactly when your last period started or when you felt that twinge of ovulation pain is difficult. Consistent tracking removes the guesswork. Over time, the data you collect becomes a powerful tool for predicting future periods, identifying your fertile window, and discussing your health with a doctor.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

### Step 1: Log the Start of Your Period
When you experience full flow (not just spotting), open the app and mark it as Day 1.

### Step 2: Track Flow Intensity
Note whether your flow is light, medium, or heavy. This helps identify abnormally heavy bleeding.

### Step 3: Record Symptoms
Use the daily log to record physical symptoms (cramps, headaches, bloating) and emotional states.

### Step 4: Add Fertility Signs (Optional)
If you are tracking for fertility, log your Basal Body Temperature (BBT) and cervical mucus observations daily.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
Simply marking the days you bleed on a calendar. Best for women with very regular cycles who just want to know when to expect their next period.

### 2. The Symptom Journal
Keeping a detailed diary of physical and emotional changes. Excellent for managing PMS or identifying triggers for symptoms.

### 3. The Tech Approach (Apps)
Using an app like LunaCycle to combine calendar tracking with symptom logging and algorithm-based predictions.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Forgetting to log Day 1** | Throws off predictions for the entire cycle | Log it as soon as you notice bleeding. |
| **Confusing spotting with a period** | Can lead to inaccurate cycle length calculations | Only count full flow as Day 1. |
| **Quitting after one month** | Not enough data to see patterns | Commit to at least 3-6 months of tracking. |
