## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Cycle tracking is only as useful as the data you provide. Consistent, accurate logging is the foundation for reliable predictions regarding your next period, estimated fertile window, and symptom patterns. By maintaining a regular tracking habit, you transform abstract calendar dates into actionable health insights, allowing you to anticipate your body's needs rather than just reacting to them.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### Calendar
The classic method. Marking dates on a physical or simple digital calendar helps establish baseline cycle lengths and expected start dates.

### Symptom journal
A more detailed approach where you maintain a daily diary of physical and emotional feelings, helping connect symptoms to specific cycle phases.

### Tech app
Dedicated applications combine calendar tracking with symptom logging and automated algorithms to provide predictions and insights effortlessly.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log consistently | Skews averages and ruins prediction accuracy |
| Only logging periods | Misses out on understanding the full scope of the menstrual cycle and phase changes |
| Ignoring irregular bleeding | Fails to capture potential early warning signs of health issues |
| Relying solely on app predictions | Apps provide estimates; understanding your own body's signals is still crucial |
