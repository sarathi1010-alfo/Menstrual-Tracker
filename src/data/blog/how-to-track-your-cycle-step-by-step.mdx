## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Tracking your menstrual cycle is one of the most powerful ways to tune into your body. While knowing when to carry tampons is useful, the real value lies in the long-term data. Consistent tracking allows you to identify your unique normal, making it much easier to spot abnormalities that might require medical attention. It also helps you predict fertile days and manage PMS symptoms proactively.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Getting started with a privacy-first app like [LunaCycle](/) is easy.

1.  **Set Day 1:** When you experience your first day of full bleeding (not just spotting), open the app and mark it as the start of your period.
2.  **Log Flow Intensity:** Record whether your flow is light, medium, or heavy each day.
3.  **Record Symptoms:** Tap on the daily log to add physical symptoms (cramps, headaches) and emotional states (mood swings, high energy).
4.  **Add Custom Notes:** Use the notes section for anything out of the ordinary, like high stress, travel, or illness, which can affect your cycle.
5.  **Review the Calendar:** Use the visual calendar to see your predicted next period and estimated ovulation window.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
Simply marking the days you bleed on a physical or digital calendar. This is the baseline for calculating cycle length.

### 2. The Symptom Journal
Logging daily physical and emotional symptoms alongside your bleeding days to understand how your cycle affects your daily life.

### 3. The Tech App Approach
Using an application that aggregates your data to provide predictions and insights. Choosing an app with local storage ensures your sensitive data remains private.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to Log | Leads to skewed averages and inaccurate future predictions. |
| Counting Spotting as Day 1 | Throws off the calculation for cycle length and ovulation. |
| Inconsistent Symptom Tracking | Makes it hard to find true patterns vs isolated incidents. |
| Changing Time Zones | Travel can delay ovulation; noting travel helps explain irregularities. |
