## Why Consistent Tracking Matters
Regular cycle tracking provides a clear picture of your reproductive health baseline. It helps your tracking app make precise predictions so you're never caught off guard.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle
Simply open [LunaCycle](/), tap the current date on the calendar, and mark it as a period day. You can easily add details like flow level and symptoms in the daily log modal. Since it's all saved locally, your private health data stays yours.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques
### 1. Calendar Logging
Marking the start and end dates of your period to find your cycle length.
### 2. Symptom Journal
Recording daily physical and emotional feelings to identify monthly patterns.
### 3. Tech App
Using a privacy-first application to consolidate your calendar and journal seamlessly.

## Common Tracking Errors
| Error | Why to Avoid It |
|-------|-----------------|
| Calling spotting "Day 1" | Skews cycle length calculation |
| Forgetting to log symptoms | Misses the context of your cycle phases |
