## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the key to accurate predictions and valuable health insights. The more data you log, the better you understand your body's unique rhythms, allowing you to anticipate periods and identify anomalies early.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking with [LunaCycle](/tracker) is designed to be effortless and private.
1. Open the app and tap the current day.
2. Select your flow intensity (light, medium, heavy) if you are menstruating.
3. Use the daily log to select any symptoms you are experiencing.
4. Save your entry. All data stays strictly on your device.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest method: marking the start and end days of your period on a calendar.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed diary of physical and emotional changes alongside bleeding days.

### 3. Technology / Apps
Using apps like LunaCycle that analyze your logged data to provide predictions and visualizations.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Forgetting to log start dates** | Skews predictions for future cycles. |
| **Ignoring symptoms** | Misses the opportunity to predict PMS or identify issues. |
| **Stopping when not bleeding** | The luteal and follicular phases provide important data too. |
