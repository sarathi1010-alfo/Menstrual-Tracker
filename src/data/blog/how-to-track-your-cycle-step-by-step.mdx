## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the key to accurate predictions and meaningful health insights. It helps you understand what is "normal" for your body, making it easier to spot potential issues early on.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The most basic form of tracking. You simply mark the days you bleed on a calendar to calculate cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed log of physical and emotional changes throughout the month. This helps identify patterns related to hormonal shifts.

### 3. Tech App Tracking
Using a digital tool like LunaCycle combines the calendar method with symptom journaling, offering automated predictions and insights.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to log the start date | Inaccurate predictions for the next cycle. |
| Counting spotting as Day 1 | Can artificially lengthen your cycle calculation. |
| Only tracking when bleeding | Misses the opportunity to understand the full cycle's impact on your body. |
