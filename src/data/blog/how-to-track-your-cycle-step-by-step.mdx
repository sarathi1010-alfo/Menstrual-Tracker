## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

For accurate predictions and health insights, consistency is key. Your cycle history is the dataset that allows algorithms to accurately predict your future dates.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Step-by-step, it’s effortless:
1. Open the app on the first day of your period.
2. Tap the current date on the calendar.
3. Select your flow intensity.
4. Save. That's it!

The [LunaCycle](/) interface is designed to be completely frictionless, saving your data locally instantly.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest approach: marking the days you bleed on a calendar.

### 2. Symptom Journal
A more detailed approach where you record daily moods, pains, and physical changes.

### 3. Tech App Tracking
Using a digital tool to automatically calculate cycle lengths and predict future dates based on your logged history.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews average cycle length calculations |
| Logging spotting as Day 1 | Day 1 should be the first day of *full* flow |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Makes it hard to identify true patterns |
