## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the foundation of accurate predictions and deep health insights. It transforms random symptoms into understandable patterns, empowering you to anticipate your body's needs.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking with [LunaCycle](/) is designed to be intuitive and private:

1. **Open the Calendar:** Access the main dashboard.
2. **Select the Date:** Tap the current date (or the date your period started).
3. **Log Your Period:** Toggle the 'Period' switch to indicate bleeding.
4. **Add Details (Optional):** Use the daily log modal to note flow intensity, mood, energy levels, and any custom notes.
5. **Save:** Your data is instantly saved locally on your device.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
The simplest form. Mark the start and end days on a physical or digital calendar to calculate cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed diary of physical and emotional changes throughout the month. Great for identifying PMS triggers.

### 3. Privacy-First Tech Apps
Using tools like LunaCycle that automate calculations and predictions without compromising your personal data.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| Counting spotting as Day 1 | Throws off cycle length calculations and ovulation predictions. |
| Forgetting to log the end date | Makes it difficult to assess average flow duration. |
| Ignoring lifestyle factors | Stress, travel, and illness can significantly impact your cycle; note these events. |
