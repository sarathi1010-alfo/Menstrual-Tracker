## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking gives you reliable data. This data is essential for accurately predicting your next period, identifying ovulation, and spotting any potential health issues early on.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking is effortless with [LunaCycle](/).
1. Open the app and navigate to the calendar.
2. Tap the date when your period started.
3. Use the daily log feature to note flow intensity, mood, and any symptoms.
Your data stays entirely on your device for complete privacy.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The foundation of cycle tracking, noting the start and end dates of menstruation.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Recording physical and emotional symptoms helps you understand how the cycle affects you daily.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using an app like [LunaCycle](/) automates predictions and provides easy-to-read charts of your history.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews predictions for the next cycle |
| Ignoring spotting | Can confuse cycle start dates |
| Not logging symptoms | Missed opportunities to identify PMS patterns |
