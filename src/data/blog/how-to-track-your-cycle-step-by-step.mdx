## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent cycle tracking is the foundation of body literacy. Without reliable data, it's difficult to predict when your period will arrive, identify your fertile window, or communicate effectively with your healthcare provider about potential issues. When you track consistently, you transform subjective feelings into objective data, leading to more accurate predictions and deeper health insights.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
The simplest form of tracking. You simply mark the first and last day of your period on a calendar. Best for basic period prediction.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed daily log of physical and emotional symptoms. Excellent for managing PMS and understanding hormonal impacts on mood.

### 3. Tech-Assisted Tracking
Using a dedicated app like [LunaCycle](/) which combines calendar data and symptom logging to provide predictive insights without compromising privacy.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Consequence | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Forgetting to log Day 1 | Throws off predictions for the entire cycle. | Set a reminder when you expect your period. |
| Logging spotting as Day 1 | Can lead to incorrectly calculating the follicular phase length. | Wait for full flow to mark Day 1. |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Makes it hard to spot true patterns. | Build logging into your daily routine (e.g., before bed). |
