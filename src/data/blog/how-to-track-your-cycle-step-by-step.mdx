Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the foundation of gaining meaningful insights from your menstrual cycle. It allows for accurate predictions of future periods and fertile windows. Beyond predicting dates, a consistent log serves as a vital health record. If you ever experience unusual pain, irregular cycles, or severe mood swings, having a detailed history helps healthcare providers diagnose issues more effectively.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest form of tracking. You mark the days you bleed on a physical calendar to calculate cycle length. It's basic but effective for spotting major irregularities.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
This involves keeping a detailed diary of physical and emotional symptoms every day of the month. It's excellent for understanding how your cycle affects your daily life.

### 3. Tech App
Using a privacy-first app like LunaCycle combines the calendar and symptom journaling methods, automating calculations and providing visual insights.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to log | Leads to inaccurate predictions for the next cycle. |
| Only logging periods | Misses vital information about the follicular and luteal phases. |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Makes it difficult to identify genuine patterns vs. isolated events. |
