## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the key to accurate predictions and health insights. The more data you provide, the better you (and your tracking tool) can understand your unique baseline. It empowers you to know what is normal for your body, making it easier to spot when something might be wrong.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest approach. Just mark a red 'X' on the days you bleed. Good for basic period prediction if your cycle is very regular.

### 2. Symptom Journal
A more detailed approach. Write down physical and emotional changes daily to identify hormonal patterns.

### 3. Tech App
Using an app combines the calendar and journal methods, using algorithms to predict future cycles based on your logged data.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Mistake | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Forgetting to log Day 1 | Throws off predictions for the entire cycle. |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Makes it hard to identify true patterns vs isolated events. |
| Ignoring spotting | Spotting can be an important indicator of hormonal shifts or issues. |
