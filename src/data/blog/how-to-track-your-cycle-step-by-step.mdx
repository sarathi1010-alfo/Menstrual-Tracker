## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent cycle tracking is the key to unlocking accurate predictions and profound health insights. When you track daily, you transform isolated symptoms into recognizable patterns. This allows you to anticipate when your period will arrive, pinpoint your fertile window, and prepare for cyclical changes in mood and energy.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The most basic method involves marking the start and end days of your period on a calendar. It's useful for estimating cycle length but lacks detail.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a daily written log of how you feel physically and emotionally. This is great for identifying patterns but can be tedious to analyze over time.

### 3. Tech App Tracking
Using a digital tracker like LunaCycle combines the benefits of both. It tracks dates, logs symptoms easily, and uses algorithms to analyze patterns and predict future cycles.
