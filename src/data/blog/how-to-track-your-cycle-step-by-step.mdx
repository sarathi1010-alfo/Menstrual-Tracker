## Why Consistent Tracking Matters
Consistent tracking matters for accurate predictions and discovering health insights. When you log daily or whenever symptoms arise, you build a comprehensive dataset that helps you spot anomalies early.

Read more about why this matters in our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) and [Understanding Your Period](/blog/understanding-your-period-normal-vs-not-normal).

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle
Logging your cycle with [LunaCycle](/) is straightforward:
1. Open the tracker.
2. Tap the current date.
3. Select your flow intensity.
4. Add any relevant symptoms like cramps or headaches.
Your data stays entirely on your device.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques
### 1. Calendar Method
A simple paper or digital calendar to mark period days.

### 2. Symptom Journal
A dedicated notebook to detail daily physical and emotional feelings.

### 3. Tech App
Using an app for algorithmic predictions and symptom correlation.

## Common Tracking Errors
| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the first day | Throws off entire cycle prediction |
| Only logging when you bleed | Misses ovulation and luteal phase context |

Make sure you understand what you're tracking by reviewing [Cycle Phases Explained](/blog/cycle-phases-explained-follicular-ovulation-luteal).
