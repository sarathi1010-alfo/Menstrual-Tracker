Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the foundation for accurate predictions and meaningful health insights. Over time, it helps you understand what is normal for your body, making it easier to spot deviations that might warrant a discussion with a healthcare provider.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

1.  **Open the App:** Navigate to the main calendar view.
2.  **Select the Date:** Tap on the current date or the day your period started.
3.  **Log Flow:** Choose the intensity of your flow (light, medium, heavy).
4.  **Add Symptoms:** Select any symptoms you are experiencing from the daily log options.
5.  **Save:** Your data is instantly saved locally on your device.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The traditional approach of marking the start and end dates of menstruation on a calendar.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed written record of daily physical and emotional changes throughout the month.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using specialized software like [LunaCycle](/) that automates calculations and predictions based on your input.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Mistake | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Inconsistent logging | Set a daily reminder or build it into your morning/evening routine. |
| Forgetting to log the end date | Ensure you mark when bleeding completely stops to get an accurate cycle length. |
| Ignoring symptoms | Even mild symptoms can provide valuable data over time; log them all. |
