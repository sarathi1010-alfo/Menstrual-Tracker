## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistency is the key to actionable cycle tracking. A single month of data tells you very little, but months or years of data reveal your unique rhythm. This consistency is what allows tracking tools to provide accurate predictions for your next period and fertile window, and it provides a reliable history to share with your doctor if you ever have concerns.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
The simplest approach: marking the start and end days of your period on a calendar. Good for basic predictions but lacks detailed health insights.

### 2. The Symptom Journal
Writing down physical and emotional symptoms daily. Excellent for body literacy and identifying patterns like premenstrual migraines or mood drops.

### 3. The Tech App Approach
Using a digital tracker like LunaCycle combines calendar and symptom tracking, using algorithms to predict future cycles based on your logged history.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact | How to Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Logging Spotting as Day 1 | Skews cycle length calculations | Wait for full flow to mark Day 1. |
| Stopping After the Period Ends | Misses ovulation and luteal symptoms | Continue logging mood and discharge throughout the month. |
| Changing Trackers Frequently | Losing historical data | Stick with one reliable, private tool. |
