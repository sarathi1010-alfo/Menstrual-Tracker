Starting to track your cycle can feel overwhelming, but it doesn't have to be. By taking it one step at a time, you can easily build a habit that provides invaluable insights into your health.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistency is the secret to accurate predictions. Tracking regularly helps identify patterns that might indicate health issues or simply help you plan your life around your natural rhythms.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Getting started with [LunaCycle](/) is designed to be as frictionless as possible:

1. **Log Day 1:** When your period starts, open the app and mark the date. This is the foundation of your cycle data.
2. **Track Flow:** Note the intensity of your bleeding (light, medium, heavy) each day.
3. **Add Symptoms:** Use the intuitive interface to log physical symptoms (cramps, headaches) and emotional states.
4. **Review Trends:** Over time, check the calendar and insights to see your patterns emerge.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The foundational technique. Simply tracking the dates your period starts and stops.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Going deeper by recording daily physical and emotional changes to map against your calendar.

### 3. Tech App Integration
Using a privacy-first app like LunaCycle to consolidate dates and symptoms, utilizing algorithms to spot trends you might miss.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Why It's a Problem | How to Fix It |
|---|---|---|
| Forgetting to log Day 1 | Skews cycle length data | Set a phone reminder or keep the app on your home screen. |
| Only logging bleeding | Misses ovulation and PMS insights | Commit to checking the app daily to note mood or energy. |
| Ignoring irregular cycles | Missed health warnings | If your cycle is consistently outside the 21-35 day range, consult a doctor. |
