## Why Consistent Tracking Matters
Consistent tracking is the key to unlocking the benefits of understanding your menstrual cycle. A few days of missed data won't ruin your records, but logging regularly provides a much clearer picture of your body's patterns. It allows for accurate predictions of your next period, helps you anticipate PMS, and provides valuable data should you ever need to consult a healthcare professional about your reproductive health.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques
Beyond the basics, here are three techniques to deepen your tracking:

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The foundation of all tracking. Simply noting the start and end dates of your period helps calculate your cycle length and predict future dates.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Logging physical and emotional changes daily. This helps connect how you feel with where you are in your cycle, validating your experiences.

### 3. Tech App Integration
Using a privacy-first web app like LunaCycle automates the math, visualizes your patterns in charts, and provides easy-to-read predictions based on your historical data.

## Common Tracking Errors
Avoid these pitfalls for the most accurate tracking experience:

| Error | Impact | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Forgetting to log Day 1 | Throws off entire cycle prediction | Log immediately when bleeding starts |
| Confusing spotting with a period | Inaccurate cycle length | Only count full flow as Day 1 |
| Giving up after one irregular cycle | Loss of long-term data | Keep tracking! Irregularities are valuable data |
| Using apps that sell your data | Privacy risks | Use local-storage apps like LunaCycle |
