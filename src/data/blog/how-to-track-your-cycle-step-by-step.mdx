## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent cycle tracking provides accurate predictions for your next period and helps you identify long-term health trends. The more data you provide, the better the insights.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking with [LunaCycle](/) is designed to be straightforward and privacy-first.

1.  **Open the App:** Access the tracker interface.
2.  **Mark Day 1:** Tap the first day you experience full menstrual flow.
3.  **Add Details:** Use the daily log to record flow intensity (light, medium, heavy).
4.  **Log Symptoms:** Select any physical or emotional symptoms you are experiencing.
5.  **Review Patterns:** Check the calendar and charts for predictions.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
Simply marking the days you bleed on a physical or digital calendar.

### 2. Symptom Journal
Writing down daily physical and emotional changes.

### 3. Tech App
Using a comprehensive, privacy-focused app to aggregate data.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Forgetting to log | Inaccurate period predictions |
| Counting spotting as Day 1 | Skews cycle length calculations |
