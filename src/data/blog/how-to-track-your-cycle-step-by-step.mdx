Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other physical signs. Using a privacy-first tool like [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier, ensuring your data remains yours alone.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistency is key to accurate predictions and meaningful health insights. By logging your cycle regularly, you build a historical record that can help predict future periods, identify fertile windows, and spot deviations that might warrant a doctor's visit.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

1. **Open the App:** Navigate to the main tracker screen.
2. **Log Your Period:** Tap the calendar date when your bleeding started.
3. **Record Details:** Note the flow intensity (light, medium, heavy) and any symptoms like cramps or bloating.
4. **Track Daily:** Continue logging symptoms, mood, and discharge throughout the month, even when you're not bleeding.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The traditional approach. Mark the days you bleed on a physical calendar. Simple, but lacks detailed symptom tracking.

### 2. Symptom Journal
A more involved method where you keep a daily diary of your physical and emotional state. Great for detail, but harder to analyze for patterns.

### 3. Tech App (LunaCycle)
Combines the best of both worlds. Easy logging, automatic pattern recognition, and with LunaCycle, complete data privacy.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews predictions for the next cycle. |
| Only tracking period days | Misses important ovulation and luteal phase data. |
| Ignoring lifestyle factors | Stress, sleep, and diet affect the cycle; not tracking them limits insights. |
