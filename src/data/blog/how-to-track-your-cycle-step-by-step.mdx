## Why Consistent Tracking Matters
Tracking your cycle is like keeping a diary for your health. The more consistent you are, the clearer the picture becomes. Accurate tracking allows you to anticipate your periods, understand your unique symptom patterns, and provide valuable information to your healthcare provider if needed.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle
[LunaCycle](/) is designed to be intuitive and completely private.

1.  **Start your cycle:** On the first day of your period, open the app and log the start date.
2.  **Log daily details:** Use the daily modal to record flow intensity, mood, energy levels, and any symptoms you're experiencing.
3.  **Review your patterns:** Over time, check the history and patterns charts to see how your cycle lengths and symptoms trend.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques
### 1. Calendar Tracking
The foundational method of noting period start and end dates to calculate cycle length.
### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping detailed notes on physical and emotional changes throughout the month.
### 3. Tech Applications
Using an app like LunaCycle to consolidate data and generate visual insights.

## Common Tracking Errors
| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the end of a period | Skews predictions for the next cycle. |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Makes it difficult to identify real patterns versus isolated events. |
| Ignoring spotting | Spotting can be a sign of ovulation or hormonal shifts and should be noted separately from a full period. |
