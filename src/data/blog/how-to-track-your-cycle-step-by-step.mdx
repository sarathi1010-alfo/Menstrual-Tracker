## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Cycle tracking is a fundamental practice for anyone seeking to understand their reproductive health. Consistent tracking provides a historical record that helps you anticipate future periods, identify your fertile window, and recognize patterns in your physical and emotional well-being. More importantly, maintaining a consistent log allows you to quickly spot deviations from your norm, which can be crucial for early medical interventions if necessary.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking your cycle is intuitive with a privacy-first tool like [LunaCycle](/).
1. **Mark Day 1:** On the first day of significant bleeding, open the app and log it as the start of your period.
2. **Detail Your Flow:** Record the intensity of your flow (light, medium, heavy) each day.
3. **Log Symptoms:** Use the daily log to note symptoms like cramps, headaches, or mood changes.
4. **Track Extras:** If you are monitoring fertility, remember to add data on cervical mucus consistency or basal body temperature.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest approach, recording only the dates of your period to track overall cycle length and regularity.

### 2. Symptom Journal
A more detailed method that involves logging physical and emotional changes to identify patterns related to PMS or ovulation.

### 3. Tech Applications
Using a dedicated application like [LunaCycle](/) that securely stores your data and uses it to provide personalized insights.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log | Less accurate predictions and unreliable historical data |
| Inconsistent symptom tracking | Difficulty identifying true patterns over time |
| Logging spotting as Day 1 | Skewed cycle length calculations |
