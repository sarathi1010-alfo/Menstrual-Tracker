Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/tracker) makes it even easier.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking matters because the more data you provide, the more accurately you can predict your next period, fertile window, and symptom patterns. It transforms random bodily events into predictable, manageable patterns, empowering you with valuable health insights.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking with LunaCycle is designed to be intuitive and private.

1. **Open the Tracker:** Navigate to the [LunaCycle](/tracker) dashboard.
2. **Select the Date:** Tap on the current date or the day your period started.
3. **Log Your Period:** Toggle the period switch and select your flow intensity.
4. **Add Symptoms:** Check off any physical or emotional symptoms you are experiencing.
5. **Save:** Your data is saved locally on your device, completely private and secure.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The most basic method. You simply mark the days you bleed on a calendar to calculate cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed log of your mood, energy, and physical feelings alongside your bleeding days to identify patterns.

### 3. Tech App Tracking
Using an app like LunaCycle that combines calendar logging and symptom tracking, and uses algorithms to predict future phases based on your history.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews cycle length calculations |
| Inconsistent logging | Reduces accuracy of predictions |
| Ignoring unusual symptoms | Missing potential health warnings |
| Switching apps frequently | Losing historical data needed for accurate trends |
