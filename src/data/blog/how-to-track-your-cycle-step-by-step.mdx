## Why Consistent Tracking Matters
Consistent tracking is the foundation of gaining actionable insights into your menstrual health. When you log your data regularly, you provide the algorithm with the information it needs to make accurate predictions about your upcoming periods and fertile windows. Furthermore, having a comprehensive record of your cycle history is invaluable if you ever need to consult a healthcare professional about irregularities or concerning symptoms.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques
There are several ways to track your cycle, and the best one is the one you will use consistently:

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest method involves marking the days you bleed on a physical calendar. This helps you calculate cycle length but doesn't capture nuanced symptom data.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a daily journal allows for detailed notes on how you feel physically and emotionally. This is excellent for recognizing patterns but requires more time and effort.

### 3. Digital Tracking Apps
Apps like LunaCycle combine the best of both worlds, offering an easy interface for logging dates and symptoms while automatically calculating predictions. LunaCycle sets itself apart by ensuring all this data remains strictly on your device for absolute privacy.

## Common Tracking Errors
| Error | Fix |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log | Set a daily reminder |
| Logging spotting as Day 1 | Only count full bleeding as Day 1 |
| Inconsistent symptom tracking | Try to log symptoms at the same time each day |
