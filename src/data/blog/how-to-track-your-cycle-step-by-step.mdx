Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier by prioritizing your privacy.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistency is the secret to unlocking the benefits of cycle tracking. Regular logging ensures accurate predictions for your next period, helping you avoid surprises. More importantly, consistent tracking builds a reliable baseline of your health insights. Only by knowing what is "normal" for your unique body can you quickly identify deviations that might require a doctor's attention.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. The Calendar Method
This involves marking the start and end dates of your period on a physical or digital calendar to track cycle length. It's the foundation of all tracking.

### 2. The Symptom Journal
Going beyond just dates, this technique involves writing down daily physical and emotional feelings to correlate them with different phases of your cycle.

### 3. The Tech App
Using a privacy-focused app like LunaCycle automates the math and visualization, combining the calendar and symptom journal into an easily accessible tool.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Forgetting to log the start date** | Throws off cycle length calculations and future predictions. |
| **Only tracking when symptomatic** | Creates a skewed picture of your overall health and cycle phases. |
| **Ignoring spotting** | Spotting can be a crucial indicator of ovulation or early menstruation. |
| **Using apps that sell data** | Compromises your deeply personal health information. |
