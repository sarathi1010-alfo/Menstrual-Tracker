## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Tracking your menstrual cycle might seem tedious at first, but consistency is the secret to unlocking profound insights about your body. When you track consistently, you are doing more than just marking a calendar; you are building a personalized medical history. Consistent tracking matters because it allows algorithms—and you—to calculate accurate predictions, anticipate your fertile window, and recognize when something is genuinely wrong. Without consistent data, it's nearly impossible to distinguish between a one-off oddity and a persistent health issue.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The foundational technique. Simply record the start and end dates of menstruation to calculate average cycle length. Best used alongside digital tools.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Goes beyond dates to include physical and emotional feelings. Helps in diagnosing PMDD or severe PMS.

### 3. Tech App Integration
Using privacy-first digital applications to automatically calculate rolling averages and predict future fertile windows and menstruation dates.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Mistake | Why it's a problem | Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Counting spotting as Day 1** | Skews cycle length calculations. | Only log Day 1 when full flow begins. |
| **Forgetting to log the end date** | Makes period duration unclear. | Log daily until bleeding stops completely. |
| **Inconsistent symptom tracking** | Misses the 'big picture' of hormonal health. | Try to open the app at the same time each day. |
| **Using apps that sell your data** | Compromises personal privacy. | Switch to a local-storage app like LunaCycle. |
