Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the foundation of accurate cycle predictions. The more data you provide, the better you can predict your next period, fertile window, and PMS symptoms. Over time, consistent tracking creates a detailed health record that can be invaluable when discussing your reproductive health with a healthcare provider.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking with LunaCycle is straightforward.
1. **Open the App:** Navigate to the main tracker interface.
2. **Log Your Period:** Tap on the current date and select your flow intensity (e.g., light, medium, heavy) to mark the start of your period.
3. **Add Symptoms:** Scroll through the daily log options to add any physical or emotional symptoms you are experiencing, such as cramps, headaches, or mood swings.
4. **Save:** Your data is saved locally and securely.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar
The most basic method, involving marking the start and end days of your period on a calendar.

### 2. Symptom journal
Writing down daily physical and emotional changes to identify patterns related to your cycle phases.

### 3. Tech app
Using an application like LunaCycle to easily record data, view charts, and receive predictions.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Forgetting to log | Inaccurate predictions for future cycles. |
| Only logging bleeding | Missing out on understanding the full cycle's impact on health. |
| Inconsistent symptom tracking | Difficulty in identifying clear patterns or PMS triggers. |
