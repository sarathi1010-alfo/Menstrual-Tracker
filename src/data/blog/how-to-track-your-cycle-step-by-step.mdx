## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking is the key to accurate predictions and valuable health insights. It helps you understand your unique rhythm, anticipate your needs, and provides a clear record to share with healthcare providers if necessary.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The foundation of cycle tracking. Simply noting the start and end dates of your period to calculate cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Keeping a detailed daily record of physical and emotional changes to identify patterns related to different cycle phases.

### 3. Tech App Tracking
Using a privacy-focused app to consolidate calendar dates and symptom logs for easy analysis and automated predictions.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews the entire cycle length calculation. |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Makes it difficult to identify real patterns. |
| Ignoring spotting | Spotting should be noted, but full flow marks Day 1. |
