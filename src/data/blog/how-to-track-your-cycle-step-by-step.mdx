## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent cycle tracking is essential for generating accurate predictions and gaining meaningful health insights. By regularly logging your data, you build a comprehensive picture of your menstrual health, making it easier to identify patterns, anticipate your period, and spot potential irregularities.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

[LunaCycle](/) offers a simple and intuitive interface for tracking your cycle. Follow these steps:

1.  **Open the app:** Access the LunaCycle tracker.
2.  **Select the date:** Tap on the current date or the day you want to log.
3.  **Enter your data:** Log your period start date, flow intensity, and any symptoms or notes.
4.  **Save:** Ensure your data is saved securely in your local storage.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar
A simple, low-tech method for noting period start and end dates.

### 2. Symptom journal
A detailed approach for recording physical and emotional changes.

### 3. Tech app
Using an app like [LunaCycle](/) automates predictions and provides easy-to-read insights.

| Mistake | Consequence |
