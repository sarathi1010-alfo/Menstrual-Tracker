## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Cycle tracking is most effective when done consistently. Your body relies on patterns, and tracking those patterns over time allows you to predict future periods, understand your fertility window, and manage symptoms proactively. Inconsistent logging can lead to skewed data and inaccurate predictions, which defeats the purpose of tracking. Whether you are aiming to understand your mood swings or trying to conceive, consistency is your most valuable tool.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Method
The simplest approach. Note the first day of bleeding and the last. Count the days between the start of one period and the start of the next to determine your cycle length.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Go beyond the calendar. Document physical symptoms (cramps, bloating, acne) and emotional states (irritability, high energy) to correlate them with different parts of your cycle.

### 3. Tech App Integration
Combine the calendar and journaling methods using a digital tool like LunaCycle to automate calculations and securely store your history.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact | Solution |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews average cycle length calculations. | Set a reminder when you expect your period. |
| Only tracking when bleeding | Misses ovulation and luteal phase patterns. | Make logging a quick daily habit. |
| Using overly complex tools | Leads to tracking fatigue and abandonment. | Start simple; add detail only when comfortable. |
