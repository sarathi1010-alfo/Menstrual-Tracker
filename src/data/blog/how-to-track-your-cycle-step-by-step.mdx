## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistent tracking provides accurate predictions for your next period and fertile window. It also creates a historical record of your health, which can be invaluable if you ever need to consult a healthcare provider about reproductive health issues.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Tracking with [LunaCycle](/) is straightforward:
1. Open the app and navigate to the calendar view.
2. Tap the date your period started.
3. Select the flow intensity (light, medium, heavy).
4. Add any relevant symptoms like cramps or headaches.
5. Save your entry locally.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar
The most basic method, simply noting the start and end dates of your period.

### 2. Symptom journal
A more detailed approach where you record physical and emotional changes daily.

### 3. Tech app
Using a privacy-first app like LunaCycle to automate calculations and predictions based on your inputs.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact |
|---|---|
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews all future predictions |
| Confusing spotting with Day 1 | Incorrect cycle length calculation |
| Inconsistent symptom tracking | Missing patterns in your health |
