Consistent tracking matters for accurate predictions and health insights. The more data you provide, the better you understand your unique normal.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques
### 1. Calendar Method
The foundational method: tracking the start and end dates of your period.

### 2. Symptom Journal
Recording physical and emotional changes daily to identify patterns related to hormonal shifts.

### 3. Tech App

## Common Tracking Errors
| Error | Why it's a problem | How to fix it |
|---|---|---|
| Forgetting to log period start | Ruins cycle length calculations | Set a monthly reminder near your expected date. |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Misses the big picture of hormonal health | Make logging a daily habit, like brushing your teeth. |
| Ignoring spotting | Can confuse cycle start dates | Note spotting, but only count full flow as Day 1. |
