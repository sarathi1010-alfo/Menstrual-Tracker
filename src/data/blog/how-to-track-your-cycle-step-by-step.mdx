Cycle tracking is simple when you know what to log: start date, flow intensity, symptoms, and any other signs. [LunaCycle](/) makes it even easier.

## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Consistency provides the data needed for accurate predictions and meaningful health insights, allowing you to spot trends over time.

## How to Log Your Cycle with LunaCycle

Open LunaCycle, tap the current date, and log your period flow, mood, and symptoms. Your data is saved securely on your device.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar
The traditional method of marking days on a physical calendar.

### 2. Symptom journal
Writing down detailed daily observations in a notebook.

### 3. Tech app
Using a privacy-first app like LunaCycle for automated insights.
