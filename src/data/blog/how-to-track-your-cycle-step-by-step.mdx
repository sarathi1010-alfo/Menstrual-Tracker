## Why Consistent Tracking Matters

Tracking your menstrual cycle provides invaluable insights into your reproductive health. Consistent tracking allows you to accurately predict your next period, identify your fertile window, and spot any potential health irregularities early. The more data you collect, the better you'll understand your unique patterns.

## Top 3 Tracking Techniques

### 1. Calendar Tracking
The foundation of cycle tracking. You simply log the days you are menstruating to determine your cycle length and predict future periods.

### 2. Symptom Journaling
Logging daily physical and emotional symptoms to correlate them with different phases of your cycle.

### 3. Tech-Assisted Tracking
Using apps like LunaCycle that combine calendar and symptom logging to provide intelligent, localized predictions.

## Common Tracking Errors

| Error | Impact on Tracking |
| --- | --- |
| Forgetting to log the start date | Skews predictions for the entire cycle. |
| Inconsistent symptom logging | Makes it hard to identify true patterns. |
| Ignoring spotting | Spotting can be a sign of ovulation or implantation, important for full context. |
| Changing tracking methods frequently | Leads to fragmented data and inaccurate insights. |
