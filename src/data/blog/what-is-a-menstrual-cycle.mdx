The menstrual cycle is the monthly series of changes a woman's body goes through in preparation for the possibility of pregnancy. Each month, one of the ovaries releases an egg, a process called ovulation. At the same time, hormonal changes prepare the uterus for pregnancy.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is controlled by a complex interaction of hormones, including estrogen and progesterone. It consists of four main phases: the menstrual phase, the follicular phase, ovulation, and the luteal phase.

## Menstrual Cycle vs. Period

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Period (Menstruation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Definition | The entire monthly process (average 28 days). | The specific days of bleeding (average 3-7 days). |
| Function | Prepares the body for potential pregnancy. | Sheds the unneeded uterine lining. |
