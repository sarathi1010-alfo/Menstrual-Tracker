The menstrual cycle is a series of natural changes in hormone production and the structures of the uterus and ovaries of the female reproductive system. It is the body's monthly preparation for a potential pregnancy. If pregnancy does not occur, the cycle results in menstruation, commonly known as a period.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is regulated by hormones, primarily estrogen and progesterone, which are controlled by the brain and ovaries. It consists of four main phases:

1. **Menstrual Phase:** The shedding of the uterine lining.
2. **Follicular Phase:** The preparation of a new egg in the ovaries.
3. **Ovulation:** The release of the mature egg.
4. **Luteal Phase:** The preparation of the uterus for a potential pregnancy.

## Menstrual Cycle vs. Menstruation

| Term | Definition | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Menstrual Cycle** | The entire hormonal loop from the start of one period to the start of the next. | 21 - 35 days (average) |
| **Menstruation (Period)** | The specific phase of the cycle where bleeding occurs. | 3 - 7 days (average) |
