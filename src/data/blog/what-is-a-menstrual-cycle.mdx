## How the Menstrual Cycle Works

The menstrual cycle is governed by a delicate balance of hormones, primarily estrogen and progesterone, produced by the ovaries. These hormones signal the body to build up the uterine lining and mature an egg.

## The Four Phases Explained

1.  **Menstruation:** The cycle begins with the first day of your period, when the uterine lining sheds.
2.  **Follicular Phase:** Overlapping with menstruation, this phase focuses on maturing a follicle in the ovary.
3.  **Ovulation:** The release of the mature egg, typically occurring mid-cycle.
4.  **Luteal Phase:** The final phase where the body prepares for implantation. If pregnancy doesn't occur, hormone levels drop, triggering the next period.
