The menstrual cycle is a series of natural changes in hormone production and the structures of the uterus and ovaries of the female reproductive system that make pregnancy possible. It is a vital sign of overall health and well-being.

## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

The cycle is controlled by a complex interplay of hormones, primarily estrogen and progesterone. These hormones rise and fall in a specific pattern, orchestrating the four main phases of the cycle:

1.  **Menstrual Phase:** The shedding of the uterine lining (your period).
2.  **Follicular Phase:** The development of an egg in the ovary.
3.  **Ovulation:** The release of the mature egg.
4.  **Luteal Phase:** The preparation of the uterus for a potential fertilized egg.

## Menstrual Cycle vs. Period

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Menstrual Cycle** | The entire process, from the first day of bleeding to the day before the next bleeding starts. |
| **Period (Menstruation)** | The specific days within the cycle when vaginal bleeding occurs. |
