## How Does the Menstrual Cycle Work?

Each month, one of the ovaries releases an egg — a process called ovulation. At the same time, hormonal changes prepare the uterus for pregnancy. If ovulation takes place and the egg isn't fertilized, the lining of the uterus sheds through the vagina. This is a menstrual period.

The cycle is controlled by a complex interplay of hormones, primarily estrogen and progesterone, which fluctuate throughout the month.

## Menstrual Cycle vs Period

| Feature | Menstrual Cycle | Period (Menstruation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | The entire monthly process of preparation for pregnancy. | The phase where the uterine lining sheds. |
| **Duration** | Typically 21-35 days. | Typically 3-7 days. |
| **Function** | Reproductive regulation. | Shedding unneeded tissue. |
