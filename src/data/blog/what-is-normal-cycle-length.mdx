# What is a Normal Cycle Length?

A "normal" cycle length is not a single number, but a range. While the 28-day cycle is often used as the standard in medical textbooks, healthy cycles can be significantly shorter or longer.

## How is Cycle Length Calculated?
Cycle length is the total number of days from **Day 1 of your period** (the first day of full flow) to the **day before your next period starts**.

## Real-World Example
If your period started on January 1st and your next period started on January 29th, your cycle length for that month was 28 days. If the following period starts on February 26th, that cycle was also 28 days.

## Why is it Important?
Knowing your normal range helps you:
- **Predict your period:** So you aren't caught by surprise.
- **Identify pregnancy:** A "late" period is often the first sign of conception.
- **Spot health changes:** Changes in your average cycle length can be an early indicator of hormonal shifts or perimenopause.

## Cycle Length Ranges

| Category | Range (Adults) | Range (Teens) |
|----------|----------------|---------------|
| Short | 21 - 24 days | 21 - 30 days |
| Average | 25 - 30 days | 30 - 38 days |
| Long | 31 - 35 days | 39 - 45 days |
| Irregular| < 21 or > 35 days| < 21 or > 45 days|
