A **normal cycle length** is the number of days in a healthy menstrual cycle, measured from the first day of menstrual bleeding to the day before the next period begins.

## What is the Range?
Medical professionals generally define a "normal" cycle as lasting between **21 and 35 days** for adults. While 28 days is the statistical average, only a small percentage of people have a cycle that lasts exactly 28 days every single month.

## How Do I Calculate Mine?
To find your normal, track the start date of your period for several months.
- **Cycle 1:** 27 days
- **Cycle 2:** 29 days
- **Cycle 3:** 28 days
- **Average:** 28 days

## Why is Knowing Your Normal Important?
Knowing your baseline allows you to:
1. **Predict Periods:** Avoid surprises by knowing when to expect your next cycle.
2. **Identify Pregnancy:** Knowing when your period is *actually* late for your body.
3. **Spot Health Issues:** If your cycle suddenly falls outside your 21-35 day range, it may be time for a checkup.

## Cycle Length by Age

| Life Stage | Normal Range | Characteristics |
|------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Adolescence** | 21 - 45 days | Often irregular as hormones stabilize. |
| **Adulthood** | 21 - 35 days | Generally more consistent. |
| **Perimenopause** | Variable | Cycles often shorten, then lengthen. |
