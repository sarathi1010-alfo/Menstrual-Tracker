## Navigating the Perimenopause Transition in 2026

Perimenopause is often called the "second puberty," yet it remains one of the most misunderstood phases of a woman's life. In 2026, we are finally seeing a shift toward more open discussion and better data around this transition. It is not an overnight event but a gradual process that can last a decade.

## Top 3 Indicators of Perimenopause

### 1. Cycle Length Variability
The hallmark of early perimenopause is a change in cycle length of 7 days or more. In late perimenopause, you may begin skipping periods entirely, with gaps of 60 days or more between bleeds.

### 2. Hormonal "Chaos"
Unlike the steady decline people expect, perimenopause often involves "estrogen spikes." This can lead to breast tenderness, heavier periods, and increased irritability.

### 3. Sleep and Mood Disruptions
Fluctuating progesterone levels can wreak havoc on your sleep architecture. Many women find that insomnia or waking up with night sweats is their first clue that perimenopause has begun.
