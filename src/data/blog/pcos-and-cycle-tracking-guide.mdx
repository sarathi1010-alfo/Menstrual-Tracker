PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects approximately 10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and ovarian cysts. Cycle tracking is a vital tool for management.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

If you have PCOS, or suspect you might, logging your data is one of the most proactive steps you can take. By tracking cycle length and symptoms (e.g., acne, unusual hair growth, weight changes), you can identify patterns and provide concrete data to your healthcare provider. Tracking also helps you monitor if dietary changes, medications, or supplements are effectively regulating your cycle.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with [LunaCycle](/), watch for these PCOS indicators:
* **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
* **Missed periods:** Going months without a period.
* **Spotting:** Irregular bleeding without a full period flow.
* **Symptom clusters:** Noting when acne breakouts or intense cravings align with cycle phases.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should speak to a doctor if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss three or more periods in a row, or if you experience severe symptoms like debilitating pain or rapid weight gain.
