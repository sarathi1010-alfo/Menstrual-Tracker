## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder affecting approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is typically characterized by a combination of irregular menstrual cycles, elevated androgen (male hormone) levels, and sometimes the presence of small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

When you have PCOS, your cycle might not follow the standard 28-day pattern. Cycle tracking becomes less about predicting the exact day of your next period and more about **pattern recognition and symptom management**.

By logging your cycle length and daily symptoms—such as acne flare-ups, changes in body hair, or unexplained weight fluctuations—you create a comprehensive record of how PCOS affects your body over time.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), watch out for:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
*   **Spotting:** Unexpected bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

Cycle data is incredibly valuable when speaking with a doctor. You should consult a healthcare provider if:
*   Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
*   You frequently miss periods.
*   You experience severe symptoms like excessive hair growth, severe acne, or significant, unexplainable weight gain.
