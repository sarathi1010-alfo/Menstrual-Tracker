## What is PCOS?

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated levels of androgens (male hormones), and sometimes the presence of small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

Cycle tracking is particularly beneficial for individuals with PCOS. By meticulously logging cycle lengths and symptoms—such as acne, excess hair growth, or weight changes—you can identify patterns that might otherwise go unnoticed. This data is crucial for understanding how the condition affects your body personally.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with PCOS, pay close attention to:

*   **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed periods:** Frequent skipping of periods.
*   **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between periods.
*   **Symptom correlations:** Note if certain symptoms flare up at specific times in your (albeit irregular) cycle.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a healthcare provider if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you frequently miss periods, or if you experience severe symptoms like heavy bleeding or significant pain. The data you've gathered through tracking will be invaluable during these consultations.
