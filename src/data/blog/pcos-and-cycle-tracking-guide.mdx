## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age, affecting approximately 10%. Women with PCOS may have infrequent or prolonged menstrual periods or excess male hormone (androgen) levels. The ovaries may develop numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) and fail to regularly release eggs.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

Cycle tracking is an invaluable tool for managing PCOS. By meticulously logging cycle length and accompanying symptoms (such as acne flare-ups, unusual hair growth, or unexplained weight changes), you can identify patterns that might otherwise go unnoticed. This objective data is crucial when discussing your health with a doctor, moving the conversation from vague descriptions to concrete timelines.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), pay close attention to:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
- **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a healthcare provider if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss your period for several months, or if you experience severe symptoms like debilitating pain or rapid weight gain.

| Condition | Symptoms | Cycle Impact | When to See a Doctor |
|-----------|----------|--------------|----------------------|
| PCOS | Irregular cycles, high androgens | Prolonged (>35 days) or missed cycles | Consistent >35 day cycles or severe symptoms |
