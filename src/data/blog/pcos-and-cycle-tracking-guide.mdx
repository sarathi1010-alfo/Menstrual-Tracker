## What is PCOS?

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects roughly 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated androgen (male hormone) levels, and sometimes small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is an essential management tool. By logging cycle length and specific symptoms (e.g., acne flare-ups, unusual hair growth, unexplained weight changes), you can identify patterns. This data is incredibly valuable when consulting with a healthcare provider. Use [LunaCycle](/) to keep a private, detailed log.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect PCOS, look for:
- Prolonged cycles (consistently longer than 35 days).
- Missed periods (fewer than 8 periods a year).
- Unpredictable spotting.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a healthcare provider if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you regularly miss periods, or if you experience severe symptoms like debilitating pain or rapid weight gain.
