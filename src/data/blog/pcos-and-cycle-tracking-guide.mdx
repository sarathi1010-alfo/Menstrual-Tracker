## Definition
PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects ~10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and ovarian cysts.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
Log your cycle length and symptoms (e.g., acne, hair growth, weight changes) to identify patterns and provide your doctor with accurate data.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
Watch out for prolonged cycles (>35 days), missed periods, and spotting.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
If your cycles are consistently >35 days, or you have severe symptoms, consult a healthcare provider.
