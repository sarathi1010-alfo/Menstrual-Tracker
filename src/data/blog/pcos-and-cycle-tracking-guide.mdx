## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by high levels of androgens (male hormones), irregular menstrual cycles, and often, small cysts on the ovaries. Because the hormonal imbalance prevents regular ovulation, cycles become unpredictable.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

When dealing with a condition that causes irregularity, data is your best friend. By diligently logging your cycle in [LunaCycle](/), you create a concrete record of your symptoms over time. This takes the guesswork out of doctor's appointments. You can clearly show exactly how long your cycles are and how frequently you experience symptoms like acne, mood swings, or pelvic pain.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect PCOS, pay attention to these patterns in your tracking app:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
- **Spotting:** Irregular, light bleeding between expected periods.
- **Physical Symptoms:** Note days with severe acne breakouts or unusual hair growth.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your tracking data shows cycles that are consistently over 35 days, if you miss three or more periods in a row, or if your symptoms are affecting your quality of life, it is time to consult a doctor. Bring your LunaCycle data with you to help them understand your baseline.
