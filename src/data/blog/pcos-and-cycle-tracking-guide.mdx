## Definition

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal condition that affects approximately 10% of women. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated levels of androgens (male hormones), and often, polycystic ovaries (ovaries that develop small collections of fluid and may fail to regularly release eggs).

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is an invaluable management tool. By rigorously logging cycle length and daily symptoms (such as acne breakouts, excess hair growth, and unexplained weight changes), you can identify underlying patterns and triggers. This data is incredibly useful when communicating with your healthcare provider.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect PCOS or have been diagnosed, watch your tracking data for:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
*   **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your tracking app like [LunaCycle](/tracker) shows that your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, or if you are experiencing severe physical symptoms like debilitating cramps or significant weight changes, it is time to consult a doctor for a proper evaluation and treatment plan.
