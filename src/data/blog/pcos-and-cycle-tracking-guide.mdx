## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age. It affects approximately 10% of women. Symptoms often include infrequent or prolonged menstrual periods, excess androgen (male hormone) levels, and the development of numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) in the ovaries, which can prevent regular ovulation.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is a crucial tool for management. By consistently logging your cycle length and symptoms using [LunaCycle](/), you can identify patterns. Tracking symptoms like acne, abnormal hair growth, weight fluctuations, and mood changes alongside your bleeding patterns provides a comprehensive health picture.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect PCOS or have been diagnosed, pay close attention to:
-   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently longer than 35 days.
-   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
-   **Spotting:** Frequent spotting instead of a full flow.
-   **Symptom Correlation:** Notice if acne flares or fatigue worsen during specific long stretches of your cycle.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a doctor if you have missed three or more periods in a row, if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, or if you are experiencing severe symptoms like heavy bleeding or significant pelvic pain. Your tracked data will be incredibly valuable during this consultation.
