## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder affecting approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated levels of androgen (male hormones), and sometimes small fluid-filled sacs (cysts) on the ovaries. Because of these hormonal imbalances, women with PCOS often experience unpredictable periods.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

When you have PCOS, standard calendar predictions often fail because ovulation does not happen predictably. However, consistent cycle tracking is a powerful tool. By logging your cycle length and daily symptoms using a secure tool like [LunaCycle](/), you create a detailed health record. This record helps you identify your personal baselines and is invaluable when discussing treatment options with a healthcare provider.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with PCOS, pay special attention to:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles lasting longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
- **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between periods.
- **Physical Symptoms:** Log occurrences of acne, unexpected hair growth, or sudden weight changes, as these correlate with androgen levels.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss three or more periods in a row, or if you are experiencing severe symptoms that impact your quality of life, it is time to consult a doctor. Bring your tracking history; it will help your doctor make a more accurate diagnosis.
