## Definition: What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is typically characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated levels of male hormones (androgens), and the presence of small cysts on the ovaries. Because it disrupts ovulation, it is a leading cause of infertility and can lead to other metabolic issues.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

If you have or suspect you have PCOS, cycle tracking is your best tool for managing it. By meticulously logging your cycle length and symptoms in [LunaCycle](/), you can identify patterns. Tracking helps you see if lifestyle changes (like diet or exercise) or medications are helping regulate your cycle over time.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When reviewing your tracking history, look for:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
*   **Spotting:** Frequent spotting between periods without a full flow.
*   **Symptom Clusters:** Note when you experience acne breakouts, excessive hair growth (hirsutism), or unexplained weight changes.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss three or more periods in a row, or if you are experiencing severe symptoms that impact your quality of life, it is time to consult a doctor. Bring your tracked data with you to the appointment.

| Condition | Symptoms | Cycle Impact | When to See a Doctor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PCOS** | Acne, hair growth, weight gain. | Cycles >35 days, missed periods. | Consistently irregular cycles. |
