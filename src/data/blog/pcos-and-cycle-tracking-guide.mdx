# The Complete Guide to PCOS and Cycle Tracking

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects ~10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and ovarian cysts.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

Log cycle length and symptoms (e.g., acne, hair growth, weight changes) to identify patterns. Because PCOS cycles are notoriously irregular, having hard data is crucial when speaking with healthcare providers.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

- Prolonged cycles (>35 days)
- Missed periods entirely
- Frequent spotting

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, you miss periods frequently, or you experience severe symptoms like heavy bleeding or intense pain, consult a healthcare provider.
