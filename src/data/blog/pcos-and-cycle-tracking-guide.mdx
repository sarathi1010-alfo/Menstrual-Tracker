## What is PCOS?

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age. It affects approximately 10% of women. Symptoms often include irregular cycles, high levels of male hormones (androgens), and multiple small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is a crucial tool for management. By logging your cycle length and symptoms, you can identify patterns and evaluate if lifestyle changes or treatments are working.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect you have PCOS or have been diagnosed, pay close attention to:

* **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
* **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
* **Spotting:** Bleeding lightly between expected periods.
* **Associated Symptoms:** Track acne flare-ups, excess hair growth, and unexplained weight changes.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should see a doctor if:

* Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
* You are not getting a period at all.
* You experience severe symptoms like pelvic pain.
* You are trying to conceive and having difficulty.
