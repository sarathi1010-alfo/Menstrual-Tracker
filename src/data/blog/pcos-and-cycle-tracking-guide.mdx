PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects ~10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and ovarian cysts.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

Tracking your cycle is a powerful tool for managing PCOS. By logging your cycle length and symptoms—such as acne, excess hair growth, and weight changes—you can identify patterns and evaluate the effectiveness of lifestyle changes or treatments.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

Pay close attention to:
- Prolonged cycles (longer than 35 days).
- Missed periods.
- Unexpected spotting or bleeding.
- Severity of hormonal symptoms over time.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss periods frequently, or if you experience severe symptoms, you should consult a healthcare provider. The data you've tracked with [LunaCycle](/) will be invaluable for their diagnosis and treatment plan.
