## Definition
PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age. It affects roughly 10% of women and is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, high levels of androgens (male hormones), and sometimes cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
For women with PCOS, cycle tracking is a vital management tool. By logging cycle length and specific symptoms (like acne, unexpected hair growth, or sudden weight changes), you can identify patterns and triggers that might otherwise go unnoticed.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
When reviewing your logs in [LunaCycle](/), watch for:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
*   **Spotting:** Irregular bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
If your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, or if you experience severe symptoms related to high androgens, it's crucial to consult a healthcare provider. Bring your cycle tracking data; it provides valuable clinical insight for diagnosis and treatment planning.
