## What is PCOS?
PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal condition that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is often characterized by irregular cycles, elevated androgen levels, and sometimes small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
For those with PCOS, the menstrual cycle can be highly unpredictable. Tracking helps you establish a baseline. By logging cycle lengths and associated symptoms (like acne breakouts, changes in body hair, or weight fluctuations), you build a comprehensive record of your health. ## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
When reviewing your tracked data, watch out for:
- **Prolonged cycles**: Regularly going more than 35 days between periods.
- **Missed periods**: Months without any menstrual bleeding.
- **Spotting**: Unexplained bleeding between periods.
- **Anovulatory cycles**: Cycles where ovulation does not occur.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
If your tracking reveals that your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, or if you experience severe symptoms like debilitating pain or rapid weight gain, it's time to seek professional medical advice. Bring your tracked data with you to the appointment.
