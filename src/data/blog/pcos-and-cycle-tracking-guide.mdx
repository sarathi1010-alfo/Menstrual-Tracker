## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder affecting roughly 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by an imbalance of reproductive hormones, which creates problems in the ovaries. The defining features often include elevated androgen levels (male hormones), small cysts on the ovaries, and irregular menstrual cycles.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

If you have PCOS—or suspect you might—cycle tracking is one of the most powerful tools at your disposal. Tracking goes beyond just knowing when your period might arrive; it provides tangible data about your hormonal health. By meticulously logging cycle length and associated symptoms (like acne breakouts, unusual hair growth, or weight fluctuations), you can identify patterns and evaluate if lifestyle or medical interventions are working.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), pay close attention to:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
*   **Oligomenorrhea:** Having fewer than eight periods in a year.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going several months without a period.
*   **Spotting:** Unpredictable light bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

Cycle tracking data is invaluable to your doctor. You should consult a healthcare provider if:
*   Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
*   You experience severe, debilitating symptoms.
*   You are trying to conceive and having difficulty due to irregular cycles.
*   You notice sudden, significant changes in your physical appearance (rapid weight gain, severe acne).
