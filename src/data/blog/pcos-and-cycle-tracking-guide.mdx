**Definition:** PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal disorder affecting roughly 10% of women of reproductive age. It is typically characterized by irregular cycles, elevated androgen levels, and often, small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

When dealing with a condition that causes irregularity, tracking becomes even more important. By logging your cycle length and symptoms—such as acne breakouts, excess hair growth, and unexplained weight changes—you can identify patterns that you might otherwise miss. This documented history is a powerful tool when you speak with your doctor. Use [LunaCycle](/) to securely maintain this log.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect PCOS, or have been diagnosed, pay close attention to:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
- **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between periods.
- **Symptom Correlation:** Do acne or mood swings align with specific times in whatever cycle rhythm you have?

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a doctor if:
- Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
- You regularly miss periods.
- Your symptoms (pain, heavy bleeding, or androgenic signs like facial hair) are severe and impacting your quality of life.
