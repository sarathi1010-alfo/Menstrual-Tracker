**Definition:** PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is an endocrine disorder characterized by irregular cycles, elevated androgen levels, and often, small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is an invaluable tool for management. By logging cycle length, flow, and symptoms (such as acne, excess hair growth, or weight changes), you can identify patterns and provide concrete data to your healthcare provider.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you have PCOS, watch for:
- Prolonged cycles (consistently longer than 35 days).
- Missed periods (fewer than 8-9 periods a year).
- Spotting between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should seek medical advice if your cycles are consistently irregular (longer than 35 days), if you miss multiple periods, or if you experience severe symptoms like debilitating pain or heavy bleeding.
