## Definition
PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age. It affects ~10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and sometimes polycystic ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
Tracking your cycle with [LunaCycle](/) can help you manage PCOS by allowing you to log cycle length and specific symptoms (e.g., acne, hair growth, weight changes). This helps identify patterns and triggers.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
When tracking, look for:
-   **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles lasting more than 35 days.
-   **Missed periods:** Going months without a period.
-   **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
You should consult a healthcare provider if:
-   Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
-   You have severe symptoms (pain, heavy bleeding).
-   You are trying to conceive and having difficulty.
