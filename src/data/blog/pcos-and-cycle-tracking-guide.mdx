## Understanding PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder affecting approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by infrequent or prolonged menstrual periods, excess male hormone (androgen) levels, and ovaries that may develop numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) and fail to regularly release eggs.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

If you have PCOS, or suspect you might, tracking your cycle is one of the most powerful tools at your disposal. While predictive algorithms might struggle with irregular data, the historical record you create is invaluable.

By logging your cycle length and daily symptoms (such as acne flare-ups, unusual hair growth, or weight fluctuations) in an app like [LunaCycle](/), you provide concrete data to your healthcare provider, rather than relying on memory.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

| Condition | Symptoms | Cycle Impact | When to See a Doctor |
|---|---|---|---|
| PCOS | Acne, excess hair, weight gain | Prolonged cycles (>35 days), missed periods, spotting | If cycles are consistently >35 days, or symptoms are severe. |

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your tracking reveals that your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, you are missing periods entirely for months at a time, or you are experiencing severe symptoms associated with high androgens, you should schedule an appointment with an endocrinologist or gynecologist.
