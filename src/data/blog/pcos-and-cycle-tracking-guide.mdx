PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects ~10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and ovarian cysts.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

Cycle tracking is a powerful tool for managing PCOS. By diligently logging cycle length and associated symptoms (like acne, unwanted hair growth, or weight changes), you can identify patterns and triggers.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When reviewing your data, look out for prolonged cycles (lasting more than 35 days), frequently missed periods, or instances of unexplained spotting.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a healthcare provider if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss multiple periods in a row, or if you experience severe symptoms.
