## What is PCOS?
Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder affecting approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated levels of male hormones (androgens), and sometimes small fluid-filled sacs (cysts) on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
For those with PCOS, a cycle tracker is an essential management tool. Because periods are often irregular, relying on memory is ineffective. By diligently logging cycle lengths, you can provide your healthcare provider with hard data. Furthermore, tracking associated symptoms—such as hormonal acne, unusual hair growth (hirsutism), or weight fluctuations—helps paint a comprehensive picture of how PCOS affects your body month-to-month.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
When reviewing your tracked data in an app like LunaCycle, pay attention to:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going 90 days or more without a period.
- **Spotting:** Unexpected bleeding between periods.
- **Symptom Correlation:** Notice if symptom severity aligns with specific parts of your cycle.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
It is vital to seek medical advice if your tracking reveals consistent irregularities. You should consult a doctor if your cycles are persistently longer than 35 days, if you miss multiple periods, or if you experience severe PCOS symptoms that impact your quality of life.
