## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age, affecting approximately 10% of women. It is characterized by infrequent or prolonged menstrual periods, excess androgen levels (male hormones), and ovaries that may develop numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) and fail to regularly release eggs.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

When you have PCOS, cycle predictability is often the biggest challenge. Tracking your cycle with a tool like [LunaCycle](/) helps you take back control by providing concrete data.

By logging not just your bleeding days, but also daily symptoms like acne flare-ups, unusual hair growth, weight changes, and mood swings, you can begin to identify patterns that relate to your hormonal fluctuations.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect PCOS or are managing it, pay close attention to:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting more than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
- **Spotting:** Irregular bleeding between expected periods.
- **Symptom Clusters:** Notice if acne or fatigue consistently precede a period (even an irregular one).

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

Cycle data is most powerful when shared with a medical professional. You should consult a doctor if:
- Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
- You have severe symptoms (e.g., rapid weight gain, severe acne).
- You are trying to conceive and are experiencing irregular cycles.
