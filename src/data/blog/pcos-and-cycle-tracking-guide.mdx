PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects roughly 10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and ovarian cysts.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking an unpredictable cycle might seem frustrating, it is incredibly valuable for managing PCOS. Cycle tracking helps you and your healthcare provider understand your specific patterns. By logging cycle length and associated symptoms (e.g., acne flare-ups, excess hair growth, weight changes), you can identify triggers and monitor the effectiveness of lifestyle changes or treatments.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When reviewing your [LunaCycle](/tracker) data, look for:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
*   **Unpredictable Spotting:** Bleeding outside of a normal menstrual phase.
*   **Symptom Correlation:** Do mood dips or physical symptoms correlate with specific (even if irregular) cycle phases?

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your tracking reveals that your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you frequently miss periods, or if you experience severe symptoms like heavy bleeding or severe pain, it is important to consult a healthcare provider. Your tracked data will be essential for their assessment.
