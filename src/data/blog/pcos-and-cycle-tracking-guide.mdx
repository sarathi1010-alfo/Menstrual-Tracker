Definition: PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal disorder affecting roughly 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated androgen (male hormone) levels, and often, small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

Living with PCOS can make the menstrual cycle unpredictable. Cycle tracking is a vital management tool. By diligently logging cycle lengths and accompanying symptoms—such as acne breakouts, unusual hair growth, or weight changes—you can identify patterns and triggers. This data empowers you to better understand your condition and manage it proactively.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), pay special attention to:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Frequently skipping periods entirely.
- **Irregular Spotting:** Bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your tracking reveals that your cycles are consistently irregular, longer than 35 days, or if you are experiencing severe symptoms like debilitating pain or rapid weight gain, it is important to consult a healthcare provider. The data you have collected will be invaluable for diagnosis and treatment planning.
