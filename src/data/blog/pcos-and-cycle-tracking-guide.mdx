## Definition
PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age, affecting roughly 10%. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, excess androgen levels, and polycystic ovaries (ovaries developing numerous small collections of fluid).

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
Tracking is a critical tool for managing PCOS. By logging your cycle length and symptoms (such as acne, excess hair growth, or weight changes), you can identify patterns. This data is invaluable when working with a healthcare provider to find the right treatment plan.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
If you suspect you have PCOS, or are actively managing it, pay close attention to:
- **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting more than 35 days.
- **Missed periods:** Going months without a period.
- **Irregular spotting:** Bleeding that occurs outside of a normal period window.
- **Symptom flare-ups:** Noting when acne or mood changes are most severe.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
You should consult a doctor if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss periods frequently, or if you experience severe symptoms like excessive hair growth or pelvic pain.
