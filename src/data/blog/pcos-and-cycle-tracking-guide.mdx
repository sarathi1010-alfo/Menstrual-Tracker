## Understanding PCOS

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is often characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, excess androgen levels, and polycystic ovaries. Because PCOS disrupts ovulation, it is a leading cause of irregular periods and fertility challenges.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

Cycle tracking is an essential tool for managing PCOS. By meticulously logging your cycle length and symptoms, you can identify patterns that help you and your healthcare provider understand your specific condition better.

Use [LunaCycle](/tracker) to track:
- **Cycle Length**: Are your cycles consistently longer than 35 days?
- **Symptoms**: Log acne flare-ups, unusual hair growth, or weight changes.
- **Bleeding Patterns**: Note if you experience heavy bleeding or spotting between periods.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

Women with PCOS often see the following patterns:
- Prolonged follicular phases (delayed ovulation).
- Cycles lasting longer than 35 days (oligomenorrhea).
- Completely missed periods (amenorrhea).

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss three or more periods in a row, or if you experience severe symptoms like debilitating pain or extremely heavy bleeding, you should consult a healthcare provider. Your tracked data will be incredibly helpful during your appointment.
