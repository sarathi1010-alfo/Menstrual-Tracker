## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder affecting approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by an imbalance of reproductive hormones, which creates problems in the ovaries. The ovaries may develop numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) and fail to regularly release eggs, leading to irregular or prolonged menstrual cycles.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking your cycle cannot diagnose or cure PCOS, it is an essential tool for managing the condition. By logging your cycle length, bleeding intensity, and daily symptoms, you can:
*   **Identify Patterns:** Determine exactly how irregular your cycles are.
*   **Monitor Symptoms:** Track the severity of acne, hair growth, weight changes, and mood swings alongside your cycle days.
*   **Provide Data to Doctors:** A detailed log of your cycles over several months is invaluable information for your healthcare provider when creating a treatment plan.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), individuals with PCOS should pay close attention to:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going several months without a period (oligomenorrhea or amenorrhea).
*   **Unpredictable Spotting:** Irregular bleeding between periods.
*   **Lack of Ovulation Signs:** Absence of the typical basal body temperature shift or changes in cervical mucus.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should seek medical advice if:
*   Your menstrual cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
*   You have fewer than eight periods a year.
*   You experience severe symptoms like rapid weight gain, severe acne, or significant excess hair growth.
*   You are trying to conceive and have been unsuccessful for over a year (or six months if over 35).
