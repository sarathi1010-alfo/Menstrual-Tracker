PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects ~10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and ovarian cysts.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
For individuals with PCOS, the cycle is often unpredictable, making tracking both challenging and essential. Consistent logging allows you to identify patterns within the irregularity. By recording not just bleeding but also symptoms like acne, excessive hair growth, and weight changes, you can help your healthcare provider understand the severity of your hormonal imbalances.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
When reviewing your cycle history in [LunaCycle](/), watch for these PCOS-related markers:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period (oligomenorrhea).
- **Spotting:** Light bleeding between periods due to irregular hormone levels.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
Cycle data is a powerful diagnostic tool. You should bring your [LunaCycle](/) records to a doctor if:
- Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
- You have severe, unmanageable symptoms (like sudden weight gain or severe acne).
- You are trying to conceive and are experiencing highly irregular cycles.
