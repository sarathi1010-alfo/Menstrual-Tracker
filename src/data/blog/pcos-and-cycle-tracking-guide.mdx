## What is PCOS?

Start your journey with our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide).

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. Common features include irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is an essential management tool. By tracking with [LunaCycle](/), you can:
* Identify patterns in your cycle length.
* Log symptoms related to PCOS, such as acne flare-ups or mood changes.
* Provide concrete data to your healthcare provider.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you have or suspect PCOS, monitor your data for:
* **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
* **Missed periods:** Going months without a period.
* **Spotting:** Irregular bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a doctor if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss multiple periods, or if you experience severe symptoms. Cycle tracking is also useful for younger demographics, as discussed in our [Teen Guide](/use-cases/cycle-tracking-teens-guide).
