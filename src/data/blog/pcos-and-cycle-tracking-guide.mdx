## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age. It can cause infrequent or prolonged menstrual periods, excess male hormone (androgen) levels, and the ovaries may develop numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) and fail to regularly release eggs.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot diagnose or cure PCOS, it is an invaluable tool for managing it. By consistently logging cycle length and symptoms (like acne, excess hair growth, or weight changes), you can identify patterns. This detailed record is incredibly helpful to share with your healthcare provider.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect PCOS, pay attention to these patterns in your [LunaCycle](/tracker) logs:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Frequently skipping periods entirely.
- **Irregular Bleeding:** Unpredictable spotting.
- **Symptom Correlations:** Tracking if symptoms like acne worsen during specific cycle phases.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a doctor if you experience:
- Cycles that are consistently longer than 35 days.
- You have not had a period in several months (and are not pregnant).
- You experience severe symptoms like excessive hair growth or severe acne.
