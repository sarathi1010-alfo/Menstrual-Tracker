## Definition

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is typically characterized by a combination of irregular menstrual cycles, elevated androgen (male hormone) levels, and sometimes small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

For individuals with PCOS, cycle tracking is a crucial management tool. Because cycles are often unpredictable, logging the start and end dates can help identify any underlying patterns. Beyond dates, tracking specific symptoms—such as acne breakouts, excess hair growth, weight fluctuations, and mood changes—can provide a comprehensive picture of how the condition is affecting the body.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking your cycle with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), pay close attention to:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
- **Spotting:** Irregular bleeding between periods.
- **Symptom Correlation:** Notice if symptoms like acne or fatigue flare up during specific times, even without a regular period.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

Cycle data is incredibly valuable to bring to a medical appointment. You should consult a healthcare provider if:
- Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
- You have missed multiple periods.
- You experience severe symptoms like debilitating pain or heavy bleeding.
- You are trying to conceive and are struggling due to irregular ovulation.
