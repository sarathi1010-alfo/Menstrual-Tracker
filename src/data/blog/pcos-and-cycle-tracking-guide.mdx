PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects ~10% of women, causing irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and ovarian cysts.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

For those with or suspecting PCOS, cycle tracking is a vital tool. By logging cycle length, you can provide concrete data to your healthcare provider. Tracking symptoms like sudden weight changes, severe acne, or unusual hair growth alongside your cycle can reveal patterns associated with hormonal imbalances.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

Pay close attention to:
- **Prolonged cycles:** Consistently going more than 35 days between periods.
- **Missed periods:** Going months without a cycle.
- **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between expected periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

If your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss periods frequently, or if you experience severe symptoms like pelvic pain, it's time to consult a doctor. Bring your [LunaCycle](/) data with you to help provide a clear picture of your menstrual history.
