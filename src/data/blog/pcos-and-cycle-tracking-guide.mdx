## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age. It affects approximately 10% of women and is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, high levels of androgens (male hormones), and sometimes cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

When you have PCOS, your cycle might be unpredictable. Tracking your cycle length and symptoms is crucial. By keeping a log of your periods, along with symptoms like acne, excessive hair growth, or weight changes, you can begin to identify patterns and triggers.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

Pay attention to:
- **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
- **Missed periods:** Going months without a period.
- **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should see a doctor if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss periods frequently, or if you experience severe symptoms associated with PCOS. The data you've tracked with [LunaCycle](/) will be incredibly helpful for your doctor to make an accurate diagnosis.

| Condition | Symptoms | Cycle Impact | When to See a Doctor |
|-----------|----------|--------------|----------------------|
| PCOS | Acne, hair growth, weight gain | Irregular, >35 days | Consistently irregular cycles |
