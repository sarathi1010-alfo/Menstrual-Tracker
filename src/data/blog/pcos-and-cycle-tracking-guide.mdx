## What is PCOS?

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by three main features (though a diagnosis usually requires at least two): irregular or absent menstrual cycles, elevated levels of male hormones (androgens), and multiple small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

If you suspect you have PCOS or have been diagnosed, cycle tracking is one of the most effective ways to manage your health.

By meticulously logging your cycle length and daily symptoms, you can identify patterns that are characteristic of PCOS. For example, tracking can highlight exactly how irregular your periods are, or show a correlation between certain cycle days and flare-ups in acne or unusual hair growth. This data is invaluable when you visit an endocrinologist or gynecologist.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with an app like [LunaCycle](/), watch out for these specific patterns associated with PCOS:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going 90 days or more without a period (when not pregnant).
*   **Frequent Spotting:** Unpredictable, light bleeding between periods.
*   **Androgen Symptoms:** Track when you experience worsening acne, thinning hair on the scalp, or excess facial/body hair growth.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should definitely consult a healthcare provider if:
*   Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
*   Your periods are highly irregular (e.g., 20 days one month, 60 days the next).
*   You experience severe pelvic pain.
*   You are having difficulty getting pregnant.
