PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by hormonal imbalances, specifically high androgen levels, which can lead to irregular cycles, ovarian cysts, and other symptoms.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

When dealing with a condition that causes irregularity, tracking becomes even more important. By logging your cycle length and symptoms in [LunaCycle](/), you can start to identify underlying patterns that might not be immediately obvious. Track specific PCOS symptoms alongside your period, such as:
*   Acne breakouts
*   Unusual hair growth
*   Unexplained weight changes

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

If you suspect PCOS or have been diagnosed, pay close attention to:
*   **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed periods:** Going months without a period.
*   **Spotting:** Irregular bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

The data you track is invaluable for your doctor. You should consult a healthcare provider if:
*   Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
*   You miss three or more periods in a row.
*   You experience severe symptoms like heavy bleeding or intense pain.
