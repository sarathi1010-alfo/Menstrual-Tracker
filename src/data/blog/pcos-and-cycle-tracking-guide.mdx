PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular cycles, high androgen levels, and sometimes polycystic ovaries on ultrasound.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking won't cure PCOS, it is a vital management tool. By logging cycle length and symptoms (e.g., acne flare-ups, unusual hair growth, weight changes, mood swings) in [LunaCycle](/), you can identify personal patterns and provide concrete data to your healthcare provider.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with PCOS, look for:
*   **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed periods:** Going months without a period (oligomenorrhea or amenorrhea).
*   **Spotting:** Bleeding between expected periods.
*   **Anovulatory cycles:** Cycles where ovulation does not occur, often characterized by lack of typical biphasic BBT shifts or fertile cervical mucus.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a healthcare provider if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss periods frequently, or if you experience severe symptoms like heavy bleeding or significant pelvic pain.
