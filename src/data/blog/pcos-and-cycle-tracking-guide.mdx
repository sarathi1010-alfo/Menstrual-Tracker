## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age, affecting approximately 10%. It is characterized by infrequent or prolonged menstrual periods, excess male hormone (androgen) levels, and the ovaries may develop numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) and fail to regularly release eggs.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is an essential management tool. By meticulously logging your cycle length and symptoms, you can identify patterns that are crucial for a diagnosis. Key symptoms to track alongside your period include acne breakouts, unexpected weight changes, excessive hair growth, and mood fluctuations.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When reviewing your [LunaCycle](/) data, look for these common PCOS indicators:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period (when not pregnant).
*   **Irregularity:** Wide fluctuations in cycle length from month to month.
*   **Spotting:** Unexplained spotting between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

Cycle data is most powerful when shared with a medical professional. You should consult a doctor if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss multiple periods in a row, or if you experience severe symptoms like heavy bleeding or debilitating pain.
