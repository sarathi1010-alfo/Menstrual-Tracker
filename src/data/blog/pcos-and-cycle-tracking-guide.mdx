## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder common among women of reproductive age, affecting approximately 10%. It is characterized by infrequent or prolonged [menstrual cycles](/what-is-menstrual-cycle), excess male hormone (androgen) levels, and sometimes the development of numerous small collections of fluid (follicles) in the ovaries, which fail to regularly release eggs.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is an essential management tool. By logging cycle length and associated symptoms, you can identify patterns that are crucial for a diagnosis and for monitoring treatment efficacy.

If you are just starting, read our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) (coming soon) for the basics.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), watch for these common PCOS indicators:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going 90 days or more without a period.
*   **Spotting:** Unpredictable bleeding between periods.
*   **Androgen Symptoms:** Log flare-ups of acne, excess facial/body hair growth, or unexplained weight gain.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

Cycle tracking data is incredibly valuable to your doctor. You should consult a healthcare provider if:
1. Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
2. You miss three or more periods in a row.
3. You experience severe pelvic pain.
4. You are trying to conceive and have irregular cycles.
