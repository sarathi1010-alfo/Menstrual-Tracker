## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated levels of male hormones (androgens), and often the presence of small fluid-filled sacs (cysts) on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

While tracking cannot cure PCOS, it is an invaluable tool for managing it. By meticulously logging cycle dates and daily symptoms (such as acne flare-ups, excess hair growth, or unexpected weight changes), you create a detailed health record. This data is critical for healthcare providers to understand the severity of the condition and to measure the effectiveness of any prescribed treatments or lifestyle changes.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with an app like [LunaCycle](/), pay special attention to:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going several months without a period (oligomenorrhea or amenorrhea).
- **Spotting:** Irregular bleeding between periods.
- **Anovulatory Cycles:** Cycles where ovulation does not occur (indicated by a lack of temperature shift if tracking BBT, or persistently negative ovulation tests).

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should seek medical advice if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss three or more periods in a row, or if you are experiencing severe symptoms like debilitating pain or rapid weight gain.
