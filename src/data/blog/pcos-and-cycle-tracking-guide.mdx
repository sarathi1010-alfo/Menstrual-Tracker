## Definition
PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, high androgen (male hormone) levels, and sometimes small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
Because irregular cycles are a hallmark of PCOS, tracking is vital. Logging your cycle length and symptoms—such as acne breakouts, unusual hair growth, or weight changes—can help you and your healthcare provider identify patterns. It can also help you determine if and when you are ovulating, which is often delayed or absent in PCOS.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
When reviewing your tracking data, pay attention to:
*   **Prolonged cycles:** Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed periods:** Going months without a period.
*   **Unusual spotting:** Spotting that occurs outside of a normal period.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
If your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss periods frequently, or if you experience severe symptoms like rapid weight gain or excessive hair growth, you should consult a doctor for a proper evaluation.
