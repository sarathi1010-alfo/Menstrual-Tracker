## Understanding PCOS
Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal condition affecting roughly 10% of women of reproductive age. It is typically characterized by high levels of androgens (male hormones), irregular ovulation, and sometimes small cysts on the ovaries. The most common symptom women notice first is highly irregular or missed periods.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
When your cycle is irregular, tracking might seem frustrating, but it is actually more important than ever.
- **Identifying Patterns:** You might find that your cycles, while long (e.g., 45 days), still follow a specific pattern.
- **Monitoring Symptoms:** Log symptoms like acne, unwanted hair growth, or weight fluctuations. Connecting these to your cycle data can provide vital clues to your healthcare provider.
- **Evaluating Treatment:** If you start a new treatment plan (dietary changes, medication), tracking helps you see if your cycle length or symptoms are improving over time.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
When tracking with [LunaCycle](/tracker), look out for:
- Prolonged cycles (consistently longer than 35 days)
- Frequent skipped periods
- Heavy bleeding when a period does finally arrive
- Mid-cycle spotting without ovulation

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
If your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you miss periods frequently, or if you experience severe symptoms like rapid weight gain or excessive hair growth, you should consult a healthcare provider. Bring your LunaCycle tracking data with you; concrete data is incredibly helpful for a doctor when diagnosing and treating PCOS.
