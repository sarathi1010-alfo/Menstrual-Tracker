## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder that affects roughly 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, high levels of androgens (male hormones), and sometimes cysts on the ovaries. Because PCOS disrupts ovulation, it often leads to unpredictable periods.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

If you have PCOS, or suspect you might, cycle tracking is one of the most powerful tools at your disposal. Tracking doesn't cure PCOS, but it provides the data necessary to understand how the condition affects your unique body.

By logging not just the days you bleed, but also the length of your cycles and accompanying symptoms (such as acne flare-ups, unusual hair growth, or weight changes), you can identify patterns. This data is invaluable when you visit an endocrinologist or gynecologist.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), pay close attention to:

*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.
*   **Spotting:** Frequent spotting between periods without a full flow.
*   **Anovulatory Cycles:** Cycles where ovulation does not occur (often identified if you track basal body temperature and see no sustained rise).

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should consult a healthcare provider if you experience:
*   Cycles that are consistently longer than 35 days.
*   Fewer than 8 periods in a year.
*   Severe physical symptoms (excessive hair growth, severe acne, rapid weight gain).
*   Difficulty getting pregnant.
