## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder affecting approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated androgen (male hormone) levels, and often, small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

For individuals with PCOS, cycle tracking is less about pinpointing an exact 28-day rhythm and more about managing a chronic condition.
* **Identify Patterns**: Logging cycle length can highlight the frequency of irregular or absent periods.
* **Monitor Symptoms**: Tracking symptoms like acne, excessive hair growth, weight fluctuations, and mood changes helps correlate them with cycle phases or overall condition severity.
* **Provide Data for Doctors**: A detailed log is invaluable for healthcare providers when diagnosing or adjusting treatment for PCOS.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with a tool like [LunaCycle](/), pay attention to:
* **Prolonged Cycles**: Cycles consistently lasting longer than 35 days.
* **Missed Periods**: Going months without a period.
* **Spotting**: Unexplained bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should seek medical advice if:
* Your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days.
* You miss three or more periods in a row.
* You experience severe symptoms like rapid weight gain or significant hair growth.
