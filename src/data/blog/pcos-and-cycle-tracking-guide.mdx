## What is PCOS?
**Definition:** PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a common hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of reproductive-aged women. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, elevated androgen (male hormone) levels, and sometimes multiple small cysts on the ovaries.

To understand how a normal cycle operates, refer to our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) and [Understanding Your Period](/blog/understanding-your-period-normal-vs-not-normal).

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help
For those with PCOS, cycle tracking is a crucial management tool. By keeping a detailed log in a secure app like [LunaCycle](/), you can:
- Track cycle length to see if and when you are ovulating.
- Log specific symptoms (e.g., acne flare-ups, unusual hair growth, weight changes) to identify hormonal patterns.
- Provide objective data to your endocrinologist or gynecologist.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data
When reviewing your tracking history, keep an eye out for:
- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles lasting longer than 35 days.
- **Anovulatory Cycles:** Cycles where ovulation does not occur (often indicated by a lack of BBT shift).
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without a period.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider
| Condition | When to act |
|---|---|
| Cycle Length | Consistently > 35 days or < 21 days |
| Symptoms | Severe pain, heavy bleeding, or distressing physical changes |
