## What is PCOS?

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder affecting roughly 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular menstrual cycles, high androgen (male hormone) levels, and sometimes cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

Cycle tracking is an essential tool for managing PCOS. By meticulously logging cycle lengths and physical symptoms (e.g., acne, unwanted hair growth, weight fluctuations), you can identify patterns that might indicate hormonal imbalances. Read our [Ultimate Cycle Tracking Guide](/blog/ultimate-cycle-tracking-guide) for general tracking tips.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with [LunaCycle](/), women with PCOS or those who suspect they have it should look out for:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles consistently longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without menstruation.
*   **Spotting:** Bleeding between irregular periods.
*   **Anovulatory Cycles:** Cycles where ovulation does not occur (harder to track without BBT, but lack of cervical mucus changes can be a sign).
