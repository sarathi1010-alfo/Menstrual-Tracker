## Definition

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal disorder that affects approximately 10% of women of reproductive age. It is typically characterized by high levels of androgens (male hormones), irregular menstrual cycles, and the presence of small cysts on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

For those with PCOS, the cycle is often unpredictable. However, cycle tracking remains one of the most effective management tools. By meticulously logging cycle lengths, bleeding patterns, and associated symptoms (such as sudden acne flare-ups, excess hair growth, or weight fluctuations), you can identify subtle patterns over time. This data is not just for you; it is crucial evidence that helps healthcare providers formulate an accurate diagnosis and evaluate if treatments are working.

## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

When tracking with [LunaCycle](/), pay close attention to:
*   **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles that consistently last longer than 35 days.
*   **Missed Periods:** Going months without a menstrual period.
*   **Spotting:** Unexplained bleeding between expected periods.
*   **Anovulatory Cycles:** Cycles where ovulation does not occur (often indicated by a lack of temperature shift if tracking BBT).

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

You should seek medical advice if your cycles are consistently irregular (frequently longer than 35 days or completely absent), if you experience excessively heavy bleeding when periods do occur, or if you are troubled by severe physical symptoms like worsening acne or hirsutism.
