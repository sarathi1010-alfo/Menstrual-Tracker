## What is PCOS?

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a hormonal disorder that affects roughly 10% of women of reproductive age. It is characterized by irregular cycles, elevated levels of male hormones (androgens), and sometimes the presence of small fluid-filled sacs (cysts) on the ovaries.

## How Cycle Tracking Can Help

When you have PCOS, your cycle might not look like the "textbook" 28-day cycle, making tracking even more critical. By diligently logging cycle length and specific symptoms (such as acne breakouts, unusual hair growth, or sudden weight changes), you can start to identify patterns. ## What to Look For in Your Cycle Data

- **Prolonged Cycles:** Cycles lasting longer than 35 days.
- **Missed Periods:** Going months without menstruating.
- **Spotting:** Irregular bleeding between periods.

## When to Consult a Healthcare Provider

Cycle data is a powerful tool to bring to your doctor. You should seek medical advice if your cycles are consistently longer than 35 days, if you experience severe pelvic pain, or if your periods stop entirely.
