# What is Endometriosis?

**Endometriosis** is a condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus (the endometrium) grows outside the uterus. This tissue behaves like the uterine lining—thickening, breaking down, and bleeding with each menstrual cycle. However, because it has no way to exit the body, it becomes trapped, leading to inflammation and scarring.

## How Does Endometriosis Work?

The misplaced tissue reacts to the same hormonal signals as your uterine lining. During your period, these &quot;implants&quot; bleed into the pelvic cavity. This causes irritation of the surrounding organs, leading to intense pain, cysts (endometriomas), and adhesions (where organs stick together).

## Why is Endometriosis Important?

Endometriosis is often undiagnosed for years. Awareness is critical because:
- It affects roughly 1 in 10 women.
- It is a major cause of chronic pelvic pain.
- It can lead to infertility.
- Early intervention can prevent the progression of the disease.

## Endometriosis vs. Normal Period Pain

| Feature | Normal Period Pain | Endometriosis |
|---------|--------------------|---------------|
| Timing | Usually Day 1-2 | Often starts before & lasts longer |
| Severity | Manageable with OTC meds | Can be debilitating/not helped by meds |
| Other Symptoms | Mild bloating/fatigue | Pain during intercourse/bowel movements |
