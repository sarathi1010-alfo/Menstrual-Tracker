# What is Endometriosis?

Endometriosis is a chronic condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus (endometrium) grows outside the uterus, such as on the ovaries, fallopian tubes, or the tissue lining the pelvis.

## How Does Endometriosis Work?

This displaced tissue continues to act as it would inside the uterus—thickening, breaking down, and bleeding with each menstrual cycle. Because this tissue has no way to exit the body, it becomes trapped, leading to inflammation, scarring, and severe pain.

## Real-World Example of Endometriosis

A woman with endometriosis may experience "killer cramps" that don't respond to over-the-counter medication, or chronic pelvic pain that worsens during her period.

## Why is Endometriosis Important?

Endometriosis is a leading cause of chronic pelvic pain and infertility. It often takes an average of 7-10 years to receive a correct diagnosis, making symptom tracking in tools like [LunaCycle](/tracker) essential.

## Endometriosis Pain vs. Normal Cramps

| Feature | Normal Cramps | Endometriosis Pain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 1-2 days | Throughout cycle or long periods |
| **Intensity** | Manageable | Debilitating |
| **Response** | Better with Ibuprofen | Often resistant to OTC meds |
