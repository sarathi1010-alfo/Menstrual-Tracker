Menorrhagia is defined as menstrual bleeding that lasts more than seven days or is so heavy that you need to change your pad or tampon every hour for several consecutive hours.

## How Does Menorrhagia Work?
Heavy bleeding can be caused by hormonal imbalances (like estrogen dominance), uterine fibroids, polyps, or certain bleeding disorders. It often leads to iron-deficiency anemia due to the significant blood loss each month.

## Real-World Example of Menorrhagia
A person with menorrhagia might find they need to "double up" on protection (using both a tampon and a pad) or that their sleep is disrupted because they need to change their protection during the night.

## Why is Menorrhagia Important?
Chronic heavy bleeding can cause significant fatigue and impact daily life. By logging your **Flow Intensity** in LunaCycle, you can create a clear record of your bleeding patterns to show your healthcare provider, helping them determine the best course of action.

## Normal Flow vs. Menorrhagia

| Feature | Normal Flow | Menorrhagia |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | 3-7 days. | More than 7 days. |
| **Product Usage** | Change every 3-6 hours. | Change every 1-2 hours. |
| **Impact** | Manageable. | Disrupts daily activities. |

**Core Principles:**
- Heavy bleeding is not something you just "have to live with."
- Consistently logging flow intensity in LunaCycle is vital.
- Look out for signs of anemia (pale skin, exhaustion).
- Consult a doctor if you are passing large clots or bleeding through products quickly.
