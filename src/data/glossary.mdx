# Glossary of Cycle Terms

### Cycle Length
The number of days from the first day of your period up to, but not including, the first day of your next period. The average is 28 days, but a normal range is typically 21 to 35 days.

### Estrogen
A primary hormone responsible for the development and regulation of the female reproductive system. Levels rise during the follicular phase to thicken the uterine lining.

### Fertile Window
The days in a cycle when pregnancy is most likely to occur. It typically includes the 5 days before ovulation and the day of ovulation itself, as sperm can live in the body for up to 5 days.

### Luteinizing Hormone (LH)
A hormone produced by the pituitary gland. A sudden surge in LH triggers ovulation (the release of an egg).

### Menstruation
The monthly shedding of the uterine lining, commonly known as a period.

### Ovulation
The release of a mature egg from an ovary, making it available for fertilization.

### Progesterone
A hormone that prepares the endometrium (uterine lining) for the potential of pregnancy after ovulation.

### Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS)
A combination of physical and emotional symptoms that many people experience in the week or two before their period starts.
